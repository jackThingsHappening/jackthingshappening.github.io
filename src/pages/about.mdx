<div class="content text-xl">
  <span class="block pb-3">Chattanooga has a lot going on and we're here to help. We cover events, outdoor adventures, coffee shops, restaurants, live music, and history. All in one place. The calendar updates daily and the guides are built to filter, not just scroll.</span>

  <h2 class="font-jakarta font-bold text-3xl mt-4 mb-2">Simplicity<span class="text-customGreen1">.</span></h2>
  <span class="block pb-3">Finding things to do shouldn't require digging through five different websites. Events, guides, and search are all here, so you spend less time looking and more time going. One URL covers the whole city.</span>

  <h2 class="font-jakarta font-bold text-3xl mt-4 mb-2">Engaging<span class="text-customGreen1">.</span></h2>
  <span class="block pb-3">Our guides are interactive and filterable, not walls of text. We include photos, practical details, and links so you can actually plan something, not just read about it. The event calendar surfaces what's coming up so you're not finding out after the fact. You can also star events and guide items to save them for later.</span>

  <h2 class="font-jakarta font-bold text-3xl mt-4 mb-2">Straightforward<span class="text-customGreen1">.</span></h2>
  <span class="block pb-3">No fluff nor filler. An event listing tells you what it is, where it is, and what it costs. Guides have quick filtering and content items that don't waste your time. If a detail is missing, we note it rather than guess.</span>

  <h2 class="font-jakarta font-bold text-3xl mt-4 mb-2">Platform Independent<span class="text-customGreen1">.</span></h2>
  <span class="block pb-3">You shouldn't need a Facebook account to find out what's happening in your city. No login, no algorithm, no feed to scroll. Everything is public and indexed. But if you're on Facebook, we're there too — find us at Things Happening Chattanooga.</span>

  <h2 class="font-jakarta font-bold text-3xl mt-4 mb-2">Discoverability<span class="text-customGreen1">.</span></h2>
  <span class="block pb-3">Everything is tagged and searchable. Whether you're looking for a kid-friendly hike, live music this weekend, or a coffee shop to work from, tags and search make it easy to find what fits. Browse by tag on the <a href="/chattanooga/events/tags">events tags page</a> or filter inside any guide.</span>

  <h2 class="font-jakarta font-bold text-3xl mt-4 mb-2">AI Usage<span class="text-customGreen1">.</span></h2>
  <span class="block pb-3">Guides and event listings are researched and reviewed by a human. Some content is drafted or refined with AI assistance before publishing. We do not publish unreviewed AI output.</span>

  <h2 class="font-jakarta font-bold text-3xl mt-4 mb-2">Who runs this<span class="text-customGreen1">.</span></h2>
  <span class="block pb-3">Things Happening is run by Jack Byram, a local to Chattanooga since 2020. He started the site because finding out what was happening in the city required checking too many places at once. The goal is one place that covers events, guides, and local knowledge with easy to use tools.</span>

  <span class="block pb-3">We're starting in Chattanooga and expanding to more cities over time.</span>

  <h2 class="font-jakarta font-bold text-3xl mt-8 mb-2">FAQ<span class="text-customGreen1">.</span></h2>

  <h3 class="text-2xl mt-4 mb-1">How do I get my event listed?</h3>
  <span class="block pb-3">Email <a href="mailto:jack@thingshappening.com">jack@thingshappening.com</a> with the event name, venue, date, time, and a link. We review and add qualifying events within a day or two.</span>

  <h3 class="text-2xl mt-4 mb-1">What kinds of events do you cover?</h3>
  <span class="block pb-3">Live music, outdoor events, theater, comedy, food and drink events, markets, community gatherings, and sports. We do not cover private events or paid promotions.</span>

  <h3 class="text-2xl mt-4 mb-1">Do you cover events outside Chattanooga?</h3>
  <span class="block pb-3">Yes. The <a href="/chattanooga/events/nearby">nearby cities page</a> covers towns within about an hour of Chattanooga — Cleveland, Ringgold, Ooltewah, and others.</span>

  <h3 class="text-2xl mt-4 mb-1">How is this site funded?</h3>
  <span class="block pb-3">Things Happening is self-funded. There are no ads. It is a small independent project built and run by one person.</span>

  <span class="block pb-3 mt-6">Questions, tips, or feedback: <a href="mailto:jack@thingshappening.com">jack@thingshappening.com</a></span>
