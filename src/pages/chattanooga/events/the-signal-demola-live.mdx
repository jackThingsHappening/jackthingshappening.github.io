Demola is a Nigerian-born violinist and multi-instrumentalist based in Houston, Texas. He fuses classical technique with Afrobeats, hip-hop, R&B, and pop. His viral videos built a following of more than 2 million people. He also plays as the official violinist for R&B group Tony! Toni! Toné!. The show starts at 8pm. Doors open at 6:30pm. Tickets are required.
