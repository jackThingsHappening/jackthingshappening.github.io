The Outsiders is a Broadway musical based on S.E. Hinton's 1967 novel about rival gangs in 1960s Tulsa. The score blends rock and country. The show runs eight performances as part of the Tivoli's presenting season. The Tivoli Theatre is a restored 1921 venue on Broad Street that seats about 1,700. Check the venue website for ticket prices and showtimes. The Outsiders musical opened on Broadway in 2024. The novel is widely read in American schools. This touring production brings the full Broadway staging to Chattanooga.
