Disney's Beauty and the Beast is a Broadway production based on the animated film. The show features the full score including Be Our Guest and the title song, performed live. Chattanooga Theatre Centre or a touring company stages this at the Tivoli Theatre on Broad Street. The Tivoli is a restored 1921 venue that seats about 1,700. Check the Tivoli website for ticket prices and showtimes. The production runs about two and a half hours with an intermission. This is family-friendly programming suited to children and adults who grew up with the film.
