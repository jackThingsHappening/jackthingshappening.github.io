Mannheim Steamroller performs its long-running Christmas tour, led by founder and composer Chip Davis. The group plays a neo-classical take on holiday carols and original songs. The sound mixes orchestral arrangements with modern electronic elements. This stop takes place on December 17 at 7:30 PM. The Tivoli Theatre Foundation presents the show. Check the Tivoli website for ticket details and seating.
