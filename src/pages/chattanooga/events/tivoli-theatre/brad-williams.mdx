Brad Williams is a stand-up comedian known for his high-energy delivery. He turns his own life and experiences into sharp, fast jokes. The Big & Small Tour features new material from the road. Williams has released several comedy specials and hosts his own podcast. He has toured clubs and theaters across the country. His confident, crowd-working style keeps the room engaged all night.
