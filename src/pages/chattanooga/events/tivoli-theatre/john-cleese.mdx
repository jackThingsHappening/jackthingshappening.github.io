John Cleese is a founding member of Monty Python. This event marks 50 years of Monty Python and the Holy Grail. The night opens with a screening of the film. Cleese then takes the stage for a live question and answer session. He shares stories from his comedy career and responds to the audience. Fans of British comedy will find plenty to enjoy.
