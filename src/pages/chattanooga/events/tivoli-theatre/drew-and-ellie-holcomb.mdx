Drew and Ellie Holcomb are a married folk and Americana duo from Tennessee. The Rain or Shine Tour brings them together on one stage. Drew leads the band Drew Holcomb and the Neighbors. Ellie writes and performs her own songs with warm, acoustic roots. Expect tight harmonies, story-driven lyrics, and a mix of solo and shared songs across the set.
