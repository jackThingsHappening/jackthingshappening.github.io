Billy Strings plays progressive bluegrass with speed, precision, and a psychedelic edge. He won the Grammy for Best Bluegrass Album in 2021 and built one of the most devoted followings in live music. This is a two-night run on October 20 and 21. Tickets are available through the Tivoli Theatre box office.
