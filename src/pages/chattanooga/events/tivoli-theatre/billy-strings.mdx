Billy Strings plays progressive bluegrass with speed, precision, and a psychedelic edge. He won the Grammy for Best Bluegrass Album in 2021 and built one of the most devoted fan bases in the genre. His live shows are long and improvisational. Strings grew up in Michigan and relocated to Nashville. His guitar playing draws from Doc Watson and Tony Rice while moving into territory no traditional bluegrass player has covered. The Tivoli Theatre on Broad Street seats about 1,700.
