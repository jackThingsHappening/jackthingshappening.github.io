Cirque Alice is a circus production based on Lewis Carroll's Alice in Wonderland. The show features international acrobats, aerialists, and contortionists performing against a Wonderland visual world. The production combines physical performance with narrative elements drawn from Carroll's story. The Tivoli Theatre on Broad Street seats about 1,700. Check the venue website for ticket prices and showtimes. The show runs about 90 minutes. It is family-friendly and works well for children who enjoy circus performance. The Tivoli's stage accommodates the aerial rigging required for aerialist acts.
