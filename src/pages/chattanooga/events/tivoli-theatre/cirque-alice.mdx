Cirque Alice is a circus production based on Lewis Carroll's Alice in Wonderland. The show features international acrobats, aerialists, and contortionists performing to a live orchestra. Producers Simon Painter and Tim Lawson also created Circus 1903 and The Illusionists. All ages are welcome.
