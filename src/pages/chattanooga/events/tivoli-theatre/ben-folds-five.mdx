Ben Folds Five is a piano-driven alternative rock trio. The band pairs Ben Folds on piano and vocals with bass and drums. Their sound leans on sharp melodies and witty, honest lyrics. The group is known for hits such as Brick and Song for the Dumped. This tour brings the trio back to the stage together for a full live set.
