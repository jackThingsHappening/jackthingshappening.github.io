A Beautiful Noise is a Broadway musical telling the life story of Neil Diamond. It features his most beloved songs including Sweet Caroline, Cherry Cherry, and I Am... I Said. The show uses a creative structure where Diamond tells his story through a therapy session. A Beautiful Noise ran on Broadway and now tours nationally. The Tivoli Theatre is on Broad Street and seats about 1,700. Check the venue website for ticket prices and showtimes. This is a jukebox musical built for fans of Diamond's catalog across multiple generations.
