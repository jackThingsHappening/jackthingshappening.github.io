Dusty Slay is a stand-up comedian from the South. He is known for his laid-back delivery and down-to-earth stories. His signature greeting is a cheerful "we're having a good time." The Neighborhood Guy Tour features new material about everyday life. Slay has appeared on The Tonight Show and released specials on major platforms. His clean, relatable style draws a wide crowd.
