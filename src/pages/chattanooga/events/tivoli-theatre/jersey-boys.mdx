Jersey Boys is the Tony Award-winning Broadway musical about Frankie Valli and the Four Seasons. It follows their rise from the streets of New Jersey to international fame. The show includes hits like Big Girls Don't Cry, Walk Like a Man, and Can't Take My Eyes Off You. It runs eight performances from August 11 through 15.
