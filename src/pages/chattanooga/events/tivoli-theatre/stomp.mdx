Stomp is a percussion and dance show from the United Kingdom. The cast makes rhythm with brooms, trash can lids, matchboxes, and other everyday objects. The show uses no spoken words and relies on movement, sound, and physical comedy. Stomp runs two performances at the Tivoli Theatre, May 5 and May 6, both at 7:30 PM. Check the Tivoli website for available seats.
