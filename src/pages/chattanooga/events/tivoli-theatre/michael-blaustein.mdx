Michael Blaustein is a stand-up comedian and podcast host. He hosts the Stiff Socks podcast with fellow comic Trevor Wallace. His comedy leans on blunt observations and self-deprecating stories. The Bad Bad Boy tour features new stand-up material. Blaustein built a large following through viral clips and club tours. His quick, candid style keeps the pace fast throughout the set.
