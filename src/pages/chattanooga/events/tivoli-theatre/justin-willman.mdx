Justin Willman mixes sleight-of-hand magic with stand-up comedy. He is the host and creator of the Netflix series Magic for Humans. His live show pulls audience members on stage for interactive tricks. Willman builds each bit around a punchline, then lands the trick as the payoff. The style stays casual and conversational throughout the night.
