Ben Rector is a Nashville singer, songwriter, and producer. He writes warm, melodic, piano-driven pop. His songs draw on James Taylor and Paul McCartney. His album Brand New reached the top ten on the Billboard 200. Many of his songs center on the piano with full orchestral backing. This show features guests joining him on stage for the night. The set starts at 7:30 PM. The Tivoli has strong acoustics for a quieter, piano-forward show, so the balcony offers a clear view and sound.
