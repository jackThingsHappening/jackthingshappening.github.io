Beetlejuice is a Tony-nominated Broadway musical based on Tim Burton's 1988 film. It follows Lydia Deetz and a mischievous ghost in a dark comedic story filled with physical comedy and sharp songs. The show ran 587 performances on Broadway before its national tour. The Tivoli Theatre is on Broad Street and seats about 1,700. Check the venue website for ticket prices and showtimes. The show runs about two and a half hours. It is PG-13 rated. The music and design lean into the quirky, macabre tone of the original film.
