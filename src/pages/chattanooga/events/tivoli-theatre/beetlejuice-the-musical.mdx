Beetlejuice is a Tony-nominated Broadway musical based on Tim Burton's 1988 film. It follows Lydia Deetz and a mischievous ghost in a dark comedic story filled with theatrical stunts and bold design. The show runs five performances over three days, March 12 through 14. Not recommended for young children.
