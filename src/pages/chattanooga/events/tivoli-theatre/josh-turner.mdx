Josh Turner sings neotraditional country with a deep bass-baritone voice. His sound stays rooted in classic honky-tonk and gospel. He is known for number-one hits such as Your Man, Long Black Train, and Why Don't We Just Dance. This tour marks 25 years of his career on stage. Turner plays the songs that made him a country radio staple. The Tivoli seats fewer than 1,800 people, so every seat feels close to the stage. Doors open before the 7:30 PM show, so arrive early to find downtown parking.
