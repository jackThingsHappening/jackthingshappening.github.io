Waitress is a Broadway musical with music and lyrics by Sara Bareilles. It follows a small-town waitress who enters a pie-baking contest as a way forward from an unhappy life. The show ran over 1,500 performances on Broadway. Songs include She Used to Be Mine and What Baking Can Do. The Tivoli Theatre is on Broad Street and seats about 1,700. Check the venue website for ticket prices and showtimes. The show runs about two hours and 15 minutes with an intermission. It appeals to fans of Bareilles and to general musical theater audiences.
