Sonic Live in Concert marks the 35th anniversary of the Sonic the Hedgehog franchise. A live orchestra, rock musicians, and vocalists perform music from across the franchise's history. The show covers themes from Sonic the Hedgehog, Sonic Adventure, and later titles. The Tivoli Theatre on Broad Street seats about 1,700. Check the venue website for ticket prices and seating. This is a family-friendly gaming music concert. It draws fans who grew up with the games as well as younger audiences. Doors open 30 minutes before showtime.
