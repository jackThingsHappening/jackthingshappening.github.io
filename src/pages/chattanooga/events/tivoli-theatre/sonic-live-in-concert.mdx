Sonic Live in Concert marks the 35th anniversary of the Sonic the Hedgehog franchise. A live orchestra, rock musicians, and vocalists perform music from across the game series. Synchronized HD game footage plays on screen throughout the show, covering tracks from the original game through recent releases. All ages are welcome.
