Kawaii Rave is a touring anime-inspired dance party. DJs spin EDM, K-pop, hyperpop, and big pop anthems all night. The crowd comes in cosplay, and the room fills with bright lights and color. The vibe centers on anime fandom and self-expression. This show takes over The Parlour, the smaller room at The Signal. That means a tight, high-energy dance floor rather than a big hall. This event is 18+, so bring a valid ID. Doors open at 8pm and the party starts at 9pm. Tickets are required.
