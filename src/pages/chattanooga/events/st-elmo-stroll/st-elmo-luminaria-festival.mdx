Paper luminarias line the Virginia Ave Greenway as Historic St. Elmo lights up for its annual December stroll. Volunteers and local businesses build and place hundreds of candle-lit bags along the route each year. Live music, a makers market, and food trucks fill out the evening.

The event is free to attend. Luminary kits are available to purchase so your household can light a section of the route. Proceeds support a local nonprofit chosen by the St. Elmo community each year.
