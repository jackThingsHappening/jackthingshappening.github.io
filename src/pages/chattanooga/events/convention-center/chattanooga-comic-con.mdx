Chattanooga Comic Con fills the Convention Center on November 7 and 8, 2026. This is the fifth year of the show. The weekend brings comics, celebrity guests, content creators, and cosplay contests. A large vendor hall sells art, toys, and collectibles. Panels and workshops run both days. The gaming hall holds tabletop and console rooms. Saturday runs 10 AM to 6 PM, with the gaming hall open until 10 PM. Sunday runs 11 AM to 5 PM. Single-day and weekend passes are on sale online.
