SouthPaw Promotions stages a professional boxing card at the Convention Center on October 29. The company runs fight nights across the Southeast and works out of Chattanooga. Expect several bouts across weight classes on the ticket. Local fighters share the card with regional talent. Check the SouthPaw Promotions website for the full fight lineup, ticket prices, and doors time. Ringside and general seating sell separately.
