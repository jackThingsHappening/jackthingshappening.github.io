Dare to Dance is the Chattanooga Kidney Foundation's yearly fundraiser at the Convention Center on October 3. Local dancers take the floor to raise money for kidney patients and their families. Expect performances, an evening program, and a good cause behind it all. Check the Chattanooga Kidney Foundation website for tickets and table details.
