Two days of national bodybuilding at the Convention Center, June 19–20. Day one covers men's divisions; day two covers women's, with prejudging at 9am and finals at 4pm each day. The weekend also includes the IFBB Pro League World Classic. Tickets at the box office; check npcjrnats.com for the full schedule and athlete registration.
