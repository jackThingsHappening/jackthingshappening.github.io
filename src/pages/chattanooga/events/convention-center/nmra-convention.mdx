The National Model Railroad Association holds its annual convention at the Chattanooga Convention Center July 27–31. Clinics, layout tours, a trade show, and thousands of model railroad enthusiasts in one place. Whether you're deep in the hobby or just curious, it's one of the bigger gatherings of its kind. Check nmra.org for registration and schedule details.
