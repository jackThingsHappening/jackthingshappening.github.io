The National Train Show runs August 1–2 at the Convention Center — hundreds of vendors, operating layouts, and manufacturers showing off what's new. It's the consumer-facing side of the NMRA convention, open to the public. Good for collectors, hobbyists, and anyone who got a train set as a kid and never really moved on.
