The Tennessee Tow Show returns to the Convention Center from October 15 to 17. This industry event fills the whole hall with towing and recovery equipment. About 100 vendors sell products, gear, and services. Training classes run Thursday and Friday. Exhibits open Friday and Saturday. The weekend also holds a Hall of Fame ceremony and a Wall of the Fallen tribute. Admission to the exhibits is free, but you need an attendee badge to enter.
