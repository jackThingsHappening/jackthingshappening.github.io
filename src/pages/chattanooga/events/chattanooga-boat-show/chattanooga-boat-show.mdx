The Chattanooga Boat Show returns to the Convention Center over four days in February. Local dealers fill the floor with hundreds of boats. Walk the aisles and compare pontoons, fishing rigs, and ski boats in one place. Area vendors round out the show with gear and accessories. The show opens Thursday afternoon and runs through Sunday. A single day ticket costs $15. A weekend pass covers all four days for $25. Kids 6 to 12 get in for $5, and children under 5 are free. A free TWRA safety class runs Saturday at 9am. Anyone born after 1989 needs the Type 600 permit, and this class covers it. Parking is available at the Convention Center.
