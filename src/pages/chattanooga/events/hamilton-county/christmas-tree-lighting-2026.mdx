Hamilton County holds its 5th annual Christmas tree lighting on November 30 at the courthouse lawn at 625 Georgia Ave. Festivities start at 4 PM. The official program begins at 5:20 PM. Brandon Davis and Bella Lam perform live. Santa is there for photos. Family-friendly activities run throughout the evening. Mayor Weston Wamp and county officials attend the lighting at dusk. The event is free and open to the public.
