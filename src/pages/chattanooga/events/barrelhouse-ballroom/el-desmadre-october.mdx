El Desmadre brings the chaos back to Barrelhouse Ballroom on a Friday night. The name translates to the mayhem, and the event delivers on that promise. El Desmadre is a Chattanooga Latin night with live DJs spinning reggaeton, cumbia, Latin trap, and salsa. The dance floor fills early. The event has built a consistent monthly following on Long St. Doors at 8pm. Ticket prices vary - check the venue site. 18+. El Desmadre runs on the first or second Friday of each month. It is one of the few dedicated Latin music nights in Chattanooga.
