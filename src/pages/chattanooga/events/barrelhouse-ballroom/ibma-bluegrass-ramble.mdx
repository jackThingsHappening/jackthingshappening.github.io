The IBMA Bluegrass Ramble brings juried showcase artists to Barrelhouse Ballroom. It runs as part of IBMA World of Bluegrass in downtown Chattanooga. Judges pick the acts through a competitive application. The showcases spotlight rising and established bluegrass bands. Barrelhouse is one of several official Ramble venues this year. Check the venue site for the nightly lineup and tickets.
