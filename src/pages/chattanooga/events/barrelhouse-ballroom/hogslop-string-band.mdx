Hogslop String Band are a Nashville old-time band with seven members. They push old-time past its usual limits. Their sound mixes fiddle and banjo with rock, folk, and psychedelic streaks. The live show runs on stamina and sweat, and it often turns into a square dance. Hannah Marie Kelley and Roy Caudill open the night. Doors at 6pm, music at 7pm on Long St. $20 advance or $25 at the door. 18+. Come ready to dance near the stage, not sit still.
