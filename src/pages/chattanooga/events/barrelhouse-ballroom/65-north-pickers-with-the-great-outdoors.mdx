65 North Pickers are a Kentucky bluegrass quartet with a jam-band streak. They pair fast, clean picking with long improvised runs. Their originals nod to the Grateful Dead and festival-era psychedelia. The band plays Chattanooga often and draws a loyal crowd. The Great Outdoors open the show. Doors at 7pm, music at 8pm on Long St. $20 advance or $25 at the door. 18+. This is a stand-and-dance show, so plan to be on your feet.
