Holy Locust headlines Barrelhouse Ballroom on a Friday night. The five-piece acoustic band blends folk punk, gothic country, and Americana into something that sits outside easy category. Johanna Rose & The Dreamboats open. Rose is a Chattanooga-based Americana artist known for detailed, emotionally charged songwriting. This double bill brings two distinct styles of roots music to Long St. Doors at 7pm on Long St. $22 advance or $27 at the door. 18+. Check the venue site for final ticket details and start time.
