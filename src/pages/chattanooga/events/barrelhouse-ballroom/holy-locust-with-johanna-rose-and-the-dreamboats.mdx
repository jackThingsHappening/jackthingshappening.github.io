Holy Locust headlines Barrelhouse Ballroom on a Friday night. The five-piece acoustic band blends folk punk, gothic country, and New Orleans jazz. Johanna Rose & The Dreamboats open the show. Johanna Rose is a New Orleans upright bassist and singer with a smoky, gritty sound. This bill leans raw and acoustic. $15 advance or $20 at the door. 18+.
