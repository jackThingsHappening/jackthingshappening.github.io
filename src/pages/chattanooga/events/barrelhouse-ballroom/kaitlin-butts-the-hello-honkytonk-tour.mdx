Kaitlin Butts plays Barrelhouse Ballroom on a Saturday night. The Tulsa singer-songwriter tours behind her fourth album, Hello, Honky Tonk. Her style mixes classic country with humor and storytelling. She draws on Merle Haggard, Roger Miller, the Chicks, and Miranda Lambert. The album plays out like a musical set inside a honky tonk bar. Each song gives a character its moment in the spotlight. Doors open at 6:30pm. $20 advance or $25 at the door. 18+.
