Sippy is a Sydney-born bass producer now based in Los Angeles. She is known for fearless sound design and heavy, stage-commanding sets. Her music hits hard with dubstep and riddim and gritty bass. She has played Lost Lands, Bonnaroo, and Red Rocks, and she has remixed Zeds Dead and Marshmello. Her 2025 album Scars in Stereo marked her debut full-length. Doors at 9pm on Long St. 18+. Check the venue site for ticket prices and set times. Bring earplugs, since the low end runs loud.
