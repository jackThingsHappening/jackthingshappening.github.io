REMakes recreate the early R.E.M. catalog with a faithful sound and energy. The Georgia tribute band has played this material for years. Their setlist pulls from Murmur, Reckoning, and Fables of the Reconstruction - the IRS Records years before R.E.M. became stadium-sized. Barrelhouse Ballroom on Long St seats several hundred and gives the show an intimate feel suited to this era of R.E.M.'s catalog. Doors at 7pm. $22 advance or $27 at the door. 18+. Check the venue site for support acts and final ticket details.
