Sundy Best is a country-folk duo from Prestonsburg in eastern Kentucky. Nick Jamerson and Kris Bentley build a stomping back-porch sound from acoustic guitar, cajón, and tight vocal harmony. They mix Appalachian folk, bluegrass, rock, and soul. The Kentucky Music Hall of Fame named them to its Class of 2026. This is a Friday 18+ show. Doors open at 8:00pm. Tickets run $25 in advance.
