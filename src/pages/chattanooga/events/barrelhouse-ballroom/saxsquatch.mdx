Saxsquatch - the sasquatch in a suit with a saxophone - lands at Barrelhouse Ballroom on a Saturday night in September. The character-driven performance blends comedy, spectacle, and genuine saxophone playing into one wild act. Saxsquatch performs at clubs and festivals across the country and has built a loyal following online and in person. His shows are crowd-pleasing, energetic, and impossible to describe to someone who hasn't seen one. Barrelhouse Ballroom on Long St gives the act room to roam. Doors at 7pm.
