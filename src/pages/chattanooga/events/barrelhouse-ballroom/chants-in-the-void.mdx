Chants in The Void is Chattanooga's only regular Grateful Dead tribute act. They play classic Dead material but don't stick to note-for-note reproductions. The band leans into improvisational territory, stretching songs into something that shifts set to set.

This December show is their winter stop at Barrelhouse Ballroom. Doors open at 7 PM, show at 8 PM. The venue is 18+. Tickets are $15 advance and $20 at the door. Street parking is available on Long Street and the surrounding blocks near the South Broad corridor.
