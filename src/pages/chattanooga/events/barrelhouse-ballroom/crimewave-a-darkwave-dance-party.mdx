Crimewave is a darkwave dance party built for the goth and post-punk crowd. DJs spin cold synths, drum machines, and heavy bass all night. Expect tracks that pull from darkwave, EBM, and classic goth. The dress code leans black, though all are welcome. Doors open at 8pm and the floor fills later. Barrelhouse Ballroom on Long St. $15 advance or $20 at the door. 18+. This is a late one, so pace yourself and plan a ride home.
