Back 2 Mac runs the Fleetwood Mac catalog at Barrelhouse Ballroom on a Friday night in September. Doors at 7pm on Long St. $22 advance or $27 at the door. 21+. The tribute band covers all eras of Fleetwood Mac - the early British blues records, the Lindsey Buckingham and Stevie Nicks era, and the late 70s and 80s hits. Expect Rumours, Tusk, Mirage, and more. Back 2 Mac brings the full production including keyboards, multiple vocalists, and the layered guitar arrangements from the studio records.
