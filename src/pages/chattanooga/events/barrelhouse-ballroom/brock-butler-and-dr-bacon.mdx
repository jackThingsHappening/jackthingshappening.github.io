Brock Butler & Dr. Bacon bring a night of live music to Barrelhouse Ballroom on a Friday. Expect long jams and a loose, improvisational approach to the set. Butler is the frontman of Perpetual Groove, a Georgia jam band with a loyal following. Dr. Bacon is a Chattanooga-area roots and jam band. Together they play off each other in a bill that leans on improvisation and audience energy. Barrelhouse Ballroom on Long St is an ideal venue for this kind of collaborative evening. Doors at 7pm. Check the site for ticket prices.
