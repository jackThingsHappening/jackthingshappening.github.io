U4ia is a Nashville-based U2 tribute band built around four players with serious touring credentials. Members have played with Aerosmith, Ringo Starr, Sixpence None the Richer, and The Hollywood Vampires. The band started as an informal jam and grew into a full tribute act.

They play the full U2 catalog, covering the anthemic stadium-era material alongside deeper catalog picks. This is a good fit for Barrelhouse Ballroom's mid-size room, which puts you much closer to the stage than you'd ever get for the real thing.

Doors at 7 PM, show at 8 PM. The venue is 18+. Tickets are $15 advance and $20 at the door.
