The Band Feel plays Barrelhouse Ballroom on a Saturday night. The group mixes folk-rock and retro soul. Their classic rock sound sits between Led Zeppelin and The Black Crowes. Their debut LP, What of Now, came out in August 2026. Parrotfish opens the night. The Nashville quartet slides between alternative rock, pop-punk, reggae, and funk. They lean on funky riffs and three-part harmonies. Doors open at 7pm. $20 advance or $25 at the door. 18+.
