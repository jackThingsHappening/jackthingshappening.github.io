The Heavy Heavy are a Brighton, UK band who play a warm blend of psychedelic rock, blues, and sunshine pop. Their sound pulls from California 70s rock and British folk-rock. The Point is a Chattanooga-area opener. The Heavy Heavy have released recordings on independent labels and toured Europe and the United States. Their live show leans on jangly guitars, organ, and harmonies. Barrelhouse Ballroom on Long St is a good-sized room for this kind of atmospheric rock. Doors at 7pm. $22 advance or $27 at the door. 18+. Check the site for final details.
