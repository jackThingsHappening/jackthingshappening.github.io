Wolph plays Barrelhouse Ballroom on Thursday, October 29. The Virginia band mixes classic rock, blues, Southern rock, and psychedelic rock into a loose, jam-heavy set. Their sound leans on long guitar runs and steady groove. Doors open at 7pm and the show starts at 8pm. Tickets cost $15 in advance or $20 at the door. This is an 18+ show.
