Post Animal brings their psych-rock to Barrelhouse Ballroom on the Maybe You Have To Tour. Saturday night on Long St, doors at 7pm. The Chicago band blends heavy psychedelic guitar with driving rhythms and pop hooks. They have released albums on Polyvinyl Records and toured nationally. The band's members include Wyatt Shears, who also plays with The Garden. Post Animal's live show leans on volume and atmosphere. $22 advance or $27 at the door. 18+.
