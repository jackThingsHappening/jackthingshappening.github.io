Pigeons Playing Ping Pong brings their high-energy funk and jam sound to Barrelhouse Ballroom on a Sunday. They play long sets built on tight rhythmic grooves and extended improvisational sections. The band formed in Baltimore and has toured nationally for over a decade. Fans call themselves Flock members and follow the band across multiple nights. Their studio albums chart on the jam band charts. Barrelhouse Ballroom on Long St gives them room to stretch out. Doors at 7pm, show at 8pm. Check the venue site for ticket prices and support acts.
