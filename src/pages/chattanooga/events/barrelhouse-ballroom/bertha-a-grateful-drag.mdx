Bertha is a drag show built around the Grateful Dead catalog. It's exactly what it sounds like and it works. Barrelhouse Ballroom on Long St hosts this traveling production that blends drag performance with live Dead music and fan culture. Performers lip sync and dance to iconic songs while the room fills with tie-dye and dancing. The concept originated in the Northeast and has toured nationally. It appeals to Deadheads, drag fans, and anyone who wants a night that is genuinely unusual. Doors at 7pm. Check the site for ticket prices.
