Three acts share the bill at Barrelhouse Ballroom on a Sunday. OmCat is a Nashville jam group formed in 2024. The band fuses many genres and improvises on stage. Three lead singers trade vocals, and their motto is "all music proposed is fair game." Tin Roof Cats plays the Grateful Dead catalog with high energy, featuring Sugarlegg. Freedom Junction fills out the lineup. Doors open at 6pm. $15 advance or $20 at the door. 18+.
