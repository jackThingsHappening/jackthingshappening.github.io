Jeremy Would Let Me Drown brings a 90s grunge night to Barrelhouse Ballroom on a Friday. The Atlanta band pays tribute to Pearl Jam, Alice in Chains, and Soundgarden. Two singers trade lead vocals to match the styles of Eddie Vedder, Layne Staley, and Chris Cornell. Expect heavy riffs, dark energy, and the sound of an entire decade. Doors at 9pm on Long St. $15 advance or $20 at the door. 21+.
