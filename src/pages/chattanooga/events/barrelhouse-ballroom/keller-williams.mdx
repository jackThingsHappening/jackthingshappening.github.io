Keller Williams is a one-man jam band who builds full songs live. He layers acoustic guitar, bass, and vocals with looping pedals in real time. Nothing is prerecorded, so each loop stacks in front of the crowd. His sound blends bluegrass, funk, reggae, and groovy electronica. He takes requests and leans on call-and-response. Doors at 7pm, music at 8pm on Long St. $30 advance or $35 at the door. 18+. Get there early for a spot near the stage to watch the looping up close.
