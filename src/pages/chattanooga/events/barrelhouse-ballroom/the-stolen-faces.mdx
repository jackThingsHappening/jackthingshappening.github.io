The Stolen Faces are a Nashville band that has played the music of the Grateful Dead since 2014. They cover the Dead's deep catalog with the loose, soulful feel of a seasoned jam band. Expect long jams and a dancing crowd on this Friday after Thanksgiving. Doors at 8pm on Long St. $15 advance or $20 at the door. 21+.
