Magnolia and Johnson Electric Co. bring their Americana rock to Barrelhouse Ballroom on April 10, 2027. The group pairs Will Johnson of Centro-matic with surviving members of Magnolia Electric Co. Their sets honor the songs of the late Jason Molina, mixing heavy rock with dark country. Doors at 7pm. Tickets run $25 in advance or $30 at the door. 18+.
