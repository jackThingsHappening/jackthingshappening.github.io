The 5th Annual Gravedigger's Ball returns to Barrelhouse Ballroom on Friday, October 30. DJ Scuba Steve spins the night. The costume contest is one of the largest in town. Best couple or group wins $1,000 and best solo wins $500. Entry to the contest is free with a ticket, and sign-ups close at 10:30pm. Doors open at 8pm. Tickets cost $15 in advance or $20 at the door. This is an 18+ event.
