MAINx24 is a 24-hour neighborhood festival on Chattanooga's Southside. Neighbors and merchants run it every December. Barrelhouse Ballroom hosts a full day of live music during the celebration. The venue stays open from noon to midnight. The wider festival adds a parade, open studios, a chili cook-off, and late-night dance parties. The whole event is free and family-friendly. Barrelhouse sits on Long St, a short walk from the Main Street action. Expect crowds and street parking that fills up fast, so arrive early.
