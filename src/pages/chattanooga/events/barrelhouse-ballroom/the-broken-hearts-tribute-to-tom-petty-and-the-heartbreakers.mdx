The Broken Hearts are a national Tom Petty tribute band from Gainesville, Florida. Former Petty and Mudcrutch bassist Charlie Souza started the group in 2017. The set runs through the classics like "American Girl," "Free Fallin'," and "Refugee." The band aims to match the sound and feel of a real Heartbreakers show. Barrelhouse Ballroom on a Friday. $20 advance or $25 at the door. 18+.
