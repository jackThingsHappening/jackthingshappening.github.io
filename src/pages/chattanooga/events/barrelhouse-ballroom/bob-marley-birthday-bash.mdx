The Bob Marley Birthday Bash lands at Barrelhouse Ballroom on a Friday. The show marks the birthday of reggae icon Bob Marley. Fans gather each February to honor his music and legacy. Expect live reggae and classic Marley songs played through the night. These celebrations pull reggae fans from across the region. The crowd dances and sings along to songs everyone knows. Doors open at 7pm. $15 advance or $20 at the door. 18+.
