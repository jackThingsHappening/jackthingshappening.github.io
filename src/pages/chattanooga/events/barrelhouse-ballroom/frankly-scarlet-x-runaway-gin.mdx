Two jam band tributes share a bill at Barrelhouse Ballroom on a Saturday. Frankly Scarlet plays the music of the Grateful Dead with loose, improvised takes on songs like Friend of the Devil. Runaway Gin covers Phish and recreates their long, intricate jams with care. Fans of both legends get one dancing night under one roof. Doors at 8pm on Long St. $20 advance or $25 at the door. 18+.
