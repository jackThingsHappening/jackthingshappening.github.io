John Morgan headlines Barrelhouse Ballroom on Thursday, December 3. The North Carolina singer blends bluegrass roots with modern country and rock. He co-wrote 16 songs with Jason Aldean and scored a #1 with his own single "Friends Like That." Texas artist Erin Kinsey opens. She is known for viral hits like "Just Drive" and "I Ain't Crazy." Doors open at 7pm and the show starts at 8pm. Tickets cost $25 in advance or $28 at the door. This is an 18+ show.
