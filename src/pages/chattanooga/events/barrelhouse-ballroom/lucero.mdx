Lucero has been making Southern rock and country-punk out of Memphis for 25 years. They play Barrelhouse Ballroom on a Saturday - the room suits the band's size and sound. The band has released over ten albums, most recently on Liberty and Lament Records. Their music pulls from punk, country, Americana, and Southern rock into a sound that is entirely their own. Frontman Ben Nichols writes plainspoken songs about loss, drinking, and the South. Doors at 7pm on Long St. $25 advance or $30 at the door. Check the site for support acts.
