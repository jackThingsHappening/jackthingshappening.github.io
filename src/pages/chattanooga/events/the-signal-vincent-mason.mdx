Vincent Mason is a country singer-songwriter from Georgia. He pairs plainspoken, sincere lyrics with a light, soulful touch. His sound draws comparisons to Parker McCollum and John Mayer. Mason released his debut album There I Go in late 2025. This show is all ages. Doors open at 7pm and the show starts at 8pm. Tickets are required.
