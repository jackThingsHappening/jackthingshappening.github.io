John A. Patten Community Center runs daily programs at 3202 Kellys Ferry Rd. The center is open Monday through Friday from 11:30am to 8pm. Saturday hours are noon to 4pm. Activities include fitness classes, open gym time, and use of the computer lab and game room. Youth programs run throughout the year, with a summer camp in June and July. Admission is free.
