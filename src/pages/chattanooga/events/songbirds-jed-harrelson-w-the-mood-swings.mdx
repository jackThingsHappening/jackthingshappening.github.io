Jed Harrelson is a Nashville soul singer-songwriter from Texarkana, Arkansas. He blends soul, rock, R&B, and blues with Southern grit. His songs feature smooth guitar tones and raw emotion. He draws on influences like Marcus King and Allen Stone. The mood swings open the show. The set starts at 8:00 PM at Songbirds.
