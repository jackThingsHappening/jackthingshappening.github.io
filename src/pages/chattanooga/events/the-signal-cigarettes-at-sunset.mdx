Cigarettes @ Sunset play The Signal on November 6 behind their March 2026 debut EP, Possum Rock. The band from the Appalachian foothills call their sound "possum rock" — gritty blues, indie riffs, and mountain fiddle lines fused into something raw and original. "Pavement," their breakout track, has crossed 5 million Spotify streams. Show at 8pm.
