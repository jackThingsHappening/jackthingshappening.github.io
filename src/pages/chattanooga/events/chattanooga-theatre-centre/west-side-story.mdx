Two young lovers meet across a bitter street rivalry in New York. Leonard Bernstein's score carries the heartbreak, with a book by Arthur Laurents. The dancing does the rest. Chattanooga Theatre Centre stages West Side Story as a full musical production. CTC is a community theater on Brainerd Road in Chattanooga. The show requires a large cast and live music to do properly. Check the CTC website for the full run, ticket prices, and showtimes. West Side Story runs about two and a half hours with an intermission. This is one of the great American musicals.
