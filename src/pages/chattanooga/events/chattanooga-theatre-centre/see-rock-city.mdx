May and Raleigh build a marriage while World War II pulls at the seams. They face loss and hard choices side by side. This tender sequel drama lives in a specific moment in Southern history. Chattanooga Theatre Centre stages See Rock City as part of its season. CTC is a community theater on Brainerd Road in Chattanooga. The play is set in the North Georgia and Chattanooga area, which gives local audiences a particular connection to the material. Check the CTC website for the full run, ticket prices, and showtimes.
