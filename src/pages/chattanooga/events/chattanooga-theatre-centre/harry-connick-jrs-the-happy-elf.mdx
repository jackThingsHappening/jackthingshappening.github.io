Eubie the elf spreads Christmas cheer, and Harry Connick Jr. sets it all to jazz. The songs swing while the story stays warm and simple. It works as a holiday show for all ages. Chattanooga Theatre Centre stages this musical at the end of the year. CTC is a community theater on Brainerd Road in Chattanooga. The show includes live music and a full cast. Check the CTC website for the full run, ticket prices, and showtimes. The Happy Elf runs about 90 minutes and is family-friendly. Good choice for a holiday outing with kids or grandchildren.
