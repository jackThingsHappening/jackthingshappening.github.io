Six hundred years of American history, sprinted through in about 90 minutes. The pace never lets up and the jokes come fast. It is a gleeful romp that trusts the audience to keep up. Chattanooga Theatre Centre stages this condensed comic history as part of its season. CTC is a community theater on Brainerd Road in Chattanooga. The show features a small cast moving through sketches at high speed. Check the CTC website for the full run, ticket prices, and showtimes. This show works for a wide audience and plays well for groups.
