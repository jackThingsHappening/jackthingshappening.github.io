Power, betrayal, and a knife in the Senate. Conspirators cut down Caesar, and Rome slides into civil war. Mark Antony's funeral speech turns the crowd. Chattanooga Theatre Centre stages Shakespeare's Julius Caesar as part of its season. CTC is a community theater on Brainerd Road in Chattanooga. The cast includes community members and trained actors. Performances run multiple evenings and weekends. Check the CTC website for the full run, ticket prices, and showtimes. The theater seats several hundred. Julius Caesar runs about two and a half hours with an intermission.
