A farm duck gets tired of chores and wins the farm election. Then he sets his sights on the White House. He learns that running things takes real work. Chattanooga Theatre Centre stages Duck for President as part of its youth or family programming. CTC is a community theater on Brainerd Road in Chattanooga. This short, light-hearted show works well for young audiences and school groups. Check the CTC website for the full run, ticket prices, and showtimes. The show runs about 60 minutes and includes audience interaction. It is based on the popular children's book series by Doreen Cronin.
