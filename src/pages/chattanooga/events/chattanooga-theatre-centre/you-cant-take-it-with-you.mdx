Grandpa Vanderhof runs a house full of happy oddballs. Trouble lands when his granddaughter falls for her buttoned-up boss. Two very different families collide. This Pulitzer Prize-winning comedy by George S. Kaufman and Moss Hart is one of the most performed plays in American theater. Chattanooga Theatre Centre stages You Can't Take It With You as part of its season. CTC is a community theater on Brainerd Road. Check the website for the full run, ticket prices, and showtimes. The play runs about two and a half hours with an intermission.
