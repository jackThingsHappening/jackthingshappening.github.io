Thirty-eight planes get grounded in a small Newfoundland town on 9/11. The people of Gander take in thousands of stranded strangers. They answer fear with food and kindness. Chattanooga Theatre Centre stages Come From Away as a full musical production. The Tony Award-winning show has a cast of twelve who play multiple roles. CTC is a community theater on Brainerd Road in Chattanooga. Check the website for the full run, ticket prices, and showtimes. The show runs about 100 minutes without an intermission. It is one of the most emotionally affecting musicals of the past decade.
