The Martin family loses someone they love, then fights for justice. Grief and the courtroom pull them in two directions at once. This new drama weighs both. Chattanooga Theatre Centre stages The Cost of Justice as part of its season. CTC is a community theater on Brainerd Road in Chattanooga. The play examines justice, loss, and community through a family's experience with the legal system. Check the CTC website for the full run, ticket prices, and showtimes. New American dramas at community theaters are a rarer program choice - this one is worth attention.
