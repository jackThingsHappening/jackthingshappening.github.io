Off-road 5K through the farm fields at Flat Top Mountain, with the sunflower fields and mountain views as the backdrop. The course runs past grazing horses and over the farm's natural terrain. Flat Top Mountain Farm is near Ooltewah, Tennessee, east of Chattanooga. Registration is required through the Flat Top website or a third-party running registration platform. Entry fees include a post-race gathering at the farm. This is a scenic and distinctive 5K course with elevation changes throughout. Check the Flat Top website for registration details, start time, and parking information.
