Flat Top runs fall weekends through September and October - pumpkin patch, hayrides with views out to the Smoky Mountains, a corn maze, flower fields, food trucks, and live music most weekends. Flat Top Mountain Farm is near Ooltewah, Tennessee, east of Chattanooga. Admission is charged - check the Flat Top website for pricing and any timed entry requirements. The farm sits at an elevation that provides clear views toward the Smokies on clear days. Bring comfortable clothes and shoes you can walk in. Fall weekends draw large crowds - arrive early to beat the lines.
