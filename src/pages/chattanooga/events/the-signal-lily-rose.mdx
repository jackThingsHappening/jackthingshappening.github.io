Atlanta-born Lily Rose plays The Signal on October 2 behind her debut full-length I Know What I Want, released in October 2025. She blends country, pop, and hip-hop into a sound that's entirely her own — sharp and honest, not slick. Her breakout 2020 viral hit "Villain" earned her tours with Luke Bryan, Sam Hunt, and Shania Twain. Show at 8pm.
