Pub trivia twice a week at Oddstory on Campbell St, every Tuesday and Thursday at 7:30pm. Grab a pint and bring a crew with a hodgepodge of knowledge. The questions run across general knowledge, pop culture, sports, and current events. Oddstory Brewing is a craft brewery on Campbell Street in Chattanooga. No ticket required. Walk in and form a team. Tables fill early on trivia nights. The brewery pours its own craft beers alongside guest taps. Check the Oddstory social pages for any themed trivia nights or changes to the weekly schedule.
