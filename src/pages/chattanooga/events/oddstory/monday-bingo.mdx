BINGO every Monday at 6:30pm at Oddstory Brewing on Campbell St. Cold beer, free to play, and a crowd that takes the numbers surprisingly seriously. Good way to start the week. Oddstory Brewing is a craft brewery on Campbell Street in Chattanooga. The Monday BINGO night is free to play. Walk in and pick up a card. The brewery pours its own craft beers alongside guest taps. The format is traditional bingo with prizes for winners. Check the Oddstory social pages for any changes to the weekly schedule or any special themed bingo events.
