A restored 1921 theater on Broad Street seating around 1,700. Hosts touring concerts, comedy, and live events. The Walker Theatre is one of the premier mid-size venues in Chattanooga. The building underwent a full restoration and reopened as a modern concert and event venue while preserving its historic interior details. The main floor and balcony provide good sightlines from most seats. Parking is available in nearby garages and lots on Broad Street. Check the venue website for the current event calendar. The Walker Theatre books national touring acts across music, comedy, and special events.
