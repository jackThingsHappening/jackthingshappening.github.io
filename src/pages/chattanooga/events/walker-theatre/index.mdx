A 1921 theater on Broad Street that spent a century as the Tivoli before reopening as The Walker Theatre in 2024. The restored interior seats around 1,700 and hosts a mix of concerts, comedy, and the Bobby Stone Film Series — a curated run of cinema that leans toward the artful, queer, and cult.

The **Bobby Stone Film Series** is the main recurring thing here. Named for a local benefactor, it brings in films you won't catch at a multiplex — double features on Friday nights, solo screenings on Sunday afternoons. Tickets are cheap compared to concerts.
