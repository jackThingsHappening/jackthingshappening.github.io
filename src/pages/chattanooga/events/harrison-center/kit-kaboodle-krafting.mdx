Harrison Center hosts a casual paper crafting session on October 20. Crafters gather to work on projects together in the library classroom. You practice skills for card making, journaling, and memory keeping. The group also makes home decor, gifts, and party favors. The relaxed format welcomes crafters of any skill level.
