Harrison Center hosts a casual paper crafting session on October 20. Crafters gather to work on projects together in the library classroom. You practice card making, scrapbooking, or any paper-based project of your choice. Some supplies are shared. Bring your own materials if you have specific projects in mind. Harrison Center is on Dodds Avenue in Chattanooga and serves the senior community with classes and programs. Check the website for the specific time and any entry fee. Kit & Kaboodle Krafting is a relaxed, social crafting event suited to all skill levels.
