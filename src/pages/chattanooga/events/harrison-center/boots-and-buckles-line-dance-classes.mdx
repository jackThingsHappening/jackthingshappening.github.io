Line dancing at Harrison Center every Monday evening, 6 to 9 PM in the Multi Purpose Room. The class covers country and contemporary styles. Beginners start with foundational steps while returning students tackle more complex routines. No partner is needed. Harrison Center is on Dodds Avenue in Chattanooga and runs a broad schedule of programs for seniors and adults. Check the website for cost and registration. Comfortable shoes with a smooth sole work best for line dancing. The Monday night class has built a consistent group of regular attendees.
