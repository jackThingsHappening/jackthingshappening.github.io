Sewing classes for adults at Harrison Center, meeting Tuesdays and Thursdays in Classroom 2. The sessions cover hand and machine sewing basics. Seniors are especially welcome. All skill levels fit in. No prior experience needed. Harrison Center is on Dodds Avenue in Chattanooga and serves the senior community with classes, programs, and events. The sewing class runs across multiple sessions in a semester format. Check the Harrison Center website for the full schedule, enrollment details, and any materials fee. The center provides sewing machines for students who do not own one.
