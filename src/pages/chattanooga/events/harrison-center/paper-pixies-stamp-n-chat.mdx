Stamp.N.Chat30 leads a paper crafting class at Harrison Center on October 8. Vicki Sewell is an Independent Stampin' Up! demonstrator. All materials are provided for this hands-on session. Participants learn basic stamping techniques and leave with finished cards or projects. No prior crafting experience is needed. Harrison Center is on Dodds Avenue in Chattanooga. Check the website for cost and registration. The class runs about two hours in the library classroom. Stampin' Up! uses rubber stamps, ink, and paper craft supplies to create greeting cards and other paper goods.
