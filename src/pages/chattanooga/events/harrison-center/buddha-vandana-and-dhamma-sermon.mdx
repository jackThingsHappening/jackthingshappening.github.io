A Buddhist prayer recitation and dhamma sermon at Harrison Center on September 26. The session runs about 90 minutes in the Multi Purpose Room. It is a free, community-oriented religious program open to anyone interested in Buddhist teaching and practice. The dhamma sermon covers Buddhist philosophy and practical application. Harrison Center is on Dodds Avenue in Chattanooga and regularly hosts community events across a range of spiritual and cultural traditions. No prior knowledge of Buddhism is required to attend. Check the Harrison Center website for the start time and any registration details.
