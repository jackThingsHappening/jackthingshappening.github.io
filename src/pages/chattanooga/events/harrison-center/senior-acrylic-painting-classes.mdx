Acrylic painting for seniors every Wednesday morning at Harrison Center. The class covers basic techniques in Classroom 9. No prior experience is needed. Each week the instructor introduces new skills - color mixing, brush work, composition, and subject matter. Students develop individual projects at their own pace. Harrison Center is on Dodds Avenue in Chattanooga. Check the website for the specific class time and any materials fee. The class runs through the semester and builds toward a small showcase of student work. All materials are available at the center.
