The USA Dance Choo Choo Chapter runs its monthly social ballroom dance at Harrison Center on October 9. The evening pairs a group lesson with open social dancing and light refreshments. Beginners and experienced dancers are both welcome. Non-members pay $10, while members and first-time guests pay $5. The dance fills the Multi Purpose Room.
