The Country Connection Band plays the senior dance at Harrison Center on September 19. The set covers classic country, waltz, cha-cha, line dance, and ballroom styles. Harrison Center hosts monthly dances as part of its senior programming. The dances draw regulars who come for the music, the company, and the chance to move. Harrison Center is on Dodds Avenue in Chattanooga. Check the website for admission details and start time. The dances typically run a few hours in the afternoon. All skill levels and dance backgrounds are welcome.
