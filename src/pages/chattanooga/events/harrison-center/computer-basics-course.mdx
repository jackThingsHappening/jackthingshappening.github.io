Three evenings of computer training at Harrison Center, September 16 through 18. Sessions run from 5 to 8 PM in Classroom 1. The course is built for adults with little or no computer experience. Topics include navigating the desktop, using a web browser, email basics, and file management. Harrison Center is on Dodds Avenue in Chattanooga and serves the senior and adult community. Register through the center. Check the Harrison Center website for the cost and any prerequisite. The small class size allows the instructor to give individualized attention to each participant.
