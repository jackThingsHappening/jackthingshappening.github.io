The Raqetts stage a Halloween charity dance event at Harrison Center on October 24. The party runs all afternoon and evening in the auditorium. All proceeds go to McKamey Animal Center as donations. Guests can dress in costume and support local animal rescue. The long window lets attendees drop in at any time.
