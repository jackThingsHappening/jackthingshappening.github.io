Harrison Center hosts a free Medicare education seminar on October 1. A licensed presenter explains how to compare plans and maximize your benefits. The session covers Medicare Part A, B, C, and D, along with supplement and Advantage plan options. This is not a sales presentation. No obligation to purchase anything. Harrison Center is on Dodds Avenue in Chattanooga and regularly hosts free educational programming for seniors and adults. Check the website for the start time and registration details. Attendees often bring specific questions about their own coverage and situations.
