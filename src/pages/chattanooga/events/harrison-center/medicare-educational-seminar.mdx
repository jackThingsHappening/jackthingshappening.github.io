Harrison Center hosts a free Medicare education seminar on October 1. A licensed presenter explains how to compare plans and maximize your benefits. The session covers enrollment periods, coverage options, and common mistakes to avoid. Adults nearing eligibility and current Medicare members can attend. The seminar runs in Classroom 5.
