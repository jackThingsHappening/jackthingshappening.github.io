Aerobics, light weights, and Pilates stretching for women of all ages at Harrison Center. The class meets Monday afternoons in the Multi Purpose Room on Dodds Avenue. The format is low-impact and suits beginners and those returning to exercise after time away. Harrison Center serves the senior and adult community with affordable fitness programming. Check the website for the specific class time and any enrollment cost. The class runs through the semester and is led by a certified instructor. Bring comfortable clothes and a water bottle. Light hand weights are available at the center.
