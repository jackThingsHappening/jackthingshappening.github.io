Improv Chattanooga performs their free monthly comedy show at the Chattanooga Public Library's downtown branch. The troupe builds scenes entirely from audience suggestions. No two shows are the same. The Downtown Chattanooga Library is on Broad Street. This is a free, all-ages show that runs about an hour. No ticket required. Improv Chattanooga has performed in Chattanooga for years and runs classes and workshops alongside their monthly public shows. The library format makes it accessible to anyone who wants to see live comedy without a cover charge. Check their social pages for the specific date each month.
