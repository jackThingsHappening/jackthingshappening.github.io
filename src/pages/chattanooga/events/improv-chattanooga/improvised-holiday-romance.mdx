Improv Chattanooga spoofs the holiday romance movie at the Chattanooga Theatre Centre. The troupe builds a full improvised love story from audience suggestions. Expect a small town, an unexpected romance, and a holiday miracle or two. The show plays as equal parts homage and takedown of the genre. Every scene is made up on the spot, so no two nights are alike. Doors and seating start before the 7:30pm curtain. Tickets begin at $11 online or at the door.
