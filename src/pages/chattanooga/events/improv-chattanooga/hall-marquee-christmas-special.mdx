Improv Chattanooga spoofs the Hallmark holiday movie at the Historic Mars Theatre in LaFayette. The troupe builds a full improvised romance from audience suggestions. Expect small-town charm, a snowbound meet-cute, and the usual seasonal tropes played for laughs. Every scene is made up on the spot, so no two shows match. The show starts at 8pm and runs about 90 minutes. Tickets are $14.10 at the door or online.
