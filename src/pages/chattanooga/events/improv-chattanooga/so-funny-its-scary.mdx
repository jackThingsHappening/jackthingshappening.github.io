Improv Chattanooga brings their Halloween show to The Comedy Catch. The troupe performs a horror-themed set built entirely from audience suggestions. Every scene has a Halloween twist, and nothing is scripted. The Comedy Catch is on Chestnut Street in downtown Chattanooga. Check the venue website for ticket details. Improv Chattanooga performs original long-form and short-form improv. The Halloween show is a crowd-favorite annual event. The show is PG-13 to adult-rated depending on audience suggestions. It runs about 90 minutes. Good option for a Halloween week night out.
