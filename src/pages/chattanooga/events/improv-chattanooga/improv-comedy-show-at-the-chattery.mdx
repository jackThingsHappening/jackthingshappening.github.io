Improv Chattanooga curates a night of improvised comedy at The Chattery. Performers from across the Chattanooga area take the stage. Every scene is built on the spot from audience suggestions, so no two shows repeat. Doors open at 7:00pm and the show starts at 7:30pm. This show is for ages 18 and up. Tickets are $17.85 in advance or at the door.
