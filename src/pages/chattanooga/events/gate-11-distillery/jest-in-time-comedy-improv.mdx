Tuesday nights turn into a comedy showdown at Gate 11. Shellyta Chatman hosts Jest in Time, an improv competition that runs on audience calls. Signups open at 7:30pm and the show starts at 8pm. Performers build scenes on the spot from your suggestions. Grab a cocktail and heckle in good fun. The crowd helps pick who wins.
