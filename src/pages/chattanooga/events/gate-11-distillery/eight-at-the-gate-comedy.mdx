Eight comics take the stage at Gate 11 on the last Sunday of each month. Alex Hutchinson emcees the E.IGHT at the GATE showcase. Each performer gets a set, so the styles shift fast all night. The show runs from 7:30 to 10pm and costs nothing to watch. Order a craft cocktail and settle in. Local comedy fans fill the room.
