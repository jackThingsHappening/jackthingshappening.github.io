Saturday nights bring drag and bingo to Gate 11. Anastasia L'amour hosts the Distillery Divas. Bingo cards hit the tables at 8pm and the main show follows at 9pm. Gate 11 Distillery is on Brainerd Road in Chattanooga. The distillery makes spirits on-site and uses them in its cocktails. Distillery Divas and Drag Bingo is a recurring Saturday night event. Check the Gate 11 website for ticket details. The event combines the game of bingo with live drag performance for an evening that keeps everyone at the table. Adult-rated content.
