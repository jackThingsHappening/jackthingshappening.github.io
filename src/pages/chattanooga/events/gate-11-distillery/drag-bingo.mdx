Saturday nights bring drag and bingo to Gate 11. Anastasia L'amour hosts the Distillery Divas. Bingo cards hit the tables at 8pm and the main show follows at 9pm. Win rounds, cheer the queens, and sip a craft cocktail. The night is 18 and up. Arrive early to grab a seat near the stage.
