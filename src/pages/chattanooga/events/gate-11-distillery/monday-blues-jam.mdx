Blues fills the room at Gate 11 every Monday. Robin Baker and Yattie Westfield lead the jam from 7:30 to 10pm. Local players sit in and trade solos all night. The distillery pours craft spirits at the bar while you listen. Bring an instrument or just a good ear. Play starts at 7:30pm and the night runs loose.
