The Chattanooga Market sets up at the Tennessee Aquarium Plaza every Saturday from 10am to 5pm. Fresh produce, local vendors, art, and live music on the downtown waterfront. The Chattanooga Market is one of the largest weekly markets in the region. It runs every Saturday from May through November at the aquarium plaza on Broad Street. Local farmers, food makers, and artisans set up each week. No ticket required. The waterfront location makes for an easy combination with a river walk or visit to other downtown attractions. Check the market website for the current vendor list.
