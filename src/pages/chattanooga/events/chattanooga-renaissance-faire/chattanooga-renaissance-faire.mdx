Two days of medieval shenanigans spread across Coolidge Park and Renaissance Park each November. Three stages run live performances all weekend. Combat arenas host staged fights and demos between roving performers. Artisan booths show traditional crafts being made.

Add-on ticketed experiences include a King's and Queen's Feast, Royal Tea Service, Tea with the Fairies, and hands on workshops in stained glass and entomology. General admission and add-on tickets are sold separately at chattrenfaire.com.
