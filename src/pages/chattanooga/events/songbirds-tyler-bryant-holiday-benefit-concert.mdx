Tyler Bryant is a two-time Grammy-winning producer, songwriter, and guitarist from Honey Grove, Texas. He fronts the Nashville rock band Tyler Bryant and the Shakedown. The group has released six albums and toured with ZZ Top, Aerosmith, and Lynyrd Skynyrd. Bryant won his Grammys for his production work with Larkin Poe and Ruthie Foster. This show is a holiday benefit concert. The set starts at 7:00 PM at Songbirds.
