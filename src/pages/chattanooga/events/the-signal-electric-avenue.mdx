Electric Avenue recreates the sound of 1980s MTV with close accuracy. The band plays hits from Duran Duran, Wham!, Tears for Fears, Simple Minds, INXS, and Prince. They formed in 2013 and now tour across the country. Their sound tracks the original recordings so well that record labels once challenged them. This show runs the decade's biggest radio and video hits in one night. Doors open at 6:30 PM.
