Millions of lights cover the trails and formations at Rock City Gardens each winter. The route winds through Rock City's rock formations and scenic overlooks, all lit up after dark. It runs nightly from mid-November through December 31.

Tickets are required and available at seerockcity.com. The gardens sit on Lookout Mountain just across the Georgia state line, about 20 minutes from downtown Chattanooga.
