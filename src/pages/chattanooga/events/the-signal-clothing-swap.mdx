Swap Don't Shop Chatt hosts a free clothing swap at The Commons at The Signal. Bring clothes you no longer wear and trade them for something new to you. The event runs three hours and includes sections for kids and local makers. Take home as many items as you like at no cost. It is a simple way to refresh your closet and cut down on waste.
