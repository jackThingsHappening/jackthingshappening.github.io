King's X plays The Signal on November 13. The trio has recorded together for more than four decades. Their sound fuses heavy progressive rock, soul, and tight three-part harmonies. Guitarist Ty Tabor, bassist Doug Pinnick, and drummer Jerry Gaskill built a devoted following through albums like Gretchen Goes to Nebraska and Dogman. Expect a deep catalog of riffs and layered vocals from a band that shaped the genre. Doors open at 6:30pm and the show runs to 11pm.
