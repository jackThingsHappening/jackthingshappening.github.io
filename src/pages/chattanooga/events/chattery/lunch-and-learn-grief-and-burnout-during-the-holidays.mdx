Harbor Counseling clinicians Luke Sorrell and Michelle Dawson lead this free session at The Chattery on December 1. The talk covers how grief and burnout show up during the holiday season. Sorrell specializes in trauma and grief. Dawson focuses on relational patterns and healing. The session runs noon to 1pm. Bring your own lunch. The event is part of The Chattery's Giving Tuesday programming. Registration is free via Eventbrite.
