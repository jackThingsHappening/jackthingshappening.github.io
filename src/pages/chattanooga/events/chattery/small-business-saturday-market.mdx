The Chattery hosts its Small Business Saturday Market on November 28. More than 20 local vendors sell handmade goods including art, skincare, tea, and crafts. DJ MCPRO plays throughout the event. The in house Creative ReSupply thrift store stays open during the market. Free parking is available in front of the building and on surrounding streets. The market highlights vendors who are Black, Indigenous, people of color, or LGBTQIA+. Admission is free.
