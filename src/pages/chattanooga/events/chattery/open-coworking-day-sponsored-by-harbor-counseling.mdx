The Chattery opens The Fireplace coworking space for free on Giving Tuesday, December 1. Harbor Counseling sponsors the day for nonprofits and their supporters. Bring a laptop and a to-do list. The space runs 8:30am to 5:30pm. A free Lunch and Learn on grief and burnout runs at noon with Harbor Counseling clinicians. Registration is required but free. Parking is available in front of the building.
