Chattanooga Comic Con takes over the Convention Center for two days each November. The vendor hall runs comics, collectibles, and artist tables. The gaming hall stays open late on Saturday for board games and console play. Cosplay competitions run both days with categories for all ages.

Tickets run $20 to $50 depending on day and access level. Kids 12 and under get in free. The event is cash only at the door, but an ATM is on site. Weekend passes and single-day tickets are available at chattanoogacomiccon.com.
