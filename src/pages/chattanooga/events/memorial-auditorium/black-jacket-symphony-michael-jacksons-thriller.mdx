Black Jacket Symphony performs classic albums live on stage, note for note. The Thriller show re-creates Michael Jackson's 1982 record from first track to last. A live band of eight or more musicians plays every part while the visuals and production match the album's era. After the album portion, the show expands into Michael Jackson hits from the full catalog. Memorial Auditorium seats about 2,000 on McCallie Avenue. Check the venue website for ticket prices. Black Jacket Symphony produces this show alongside other album tribute productions throughout the year.
