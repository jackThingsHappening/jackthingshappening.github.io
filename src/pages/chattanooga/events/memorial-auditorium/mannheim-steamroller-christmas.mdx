Mannheim Steamroller blends orchestral and electronic arrangements of traditional Christmas music. Their holiday concerts include theatrical lighting and full production. The group was founded by Chip Davis in Omaha, Nebraska. Their Christmas albums have sold tens of millions of copies since the first release in 1984. Memorial Auditorium seats about 2,000 on McCallie Avenue. Check the venue website for ticket prices. The concert runs about two hours. This is a family-friendly holiday show that fills the building every December. Doors open 30 minutes before showtime.
