Waitress is a Broadway musical with songs by Sara Bareilles. The story follows a small-town waitress who enters a baking contest to change her life. The score mixes pop and folk with warm, personal lyrics. This North American tour marks the show's 10th anniversary. The run includes shows on February 20 and 21, with the opening matinee at 2 PM.
