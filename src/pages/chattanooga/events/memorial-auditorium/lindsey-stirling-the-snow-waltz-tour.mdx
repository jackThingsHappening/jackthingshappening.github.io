Lindsey Stirling combines classical violin technique with electronic music and choreographed movement. The Snow Waltz Tour is her annual holiday show with original material and seasonal arrangements. Stirling built her following through viral YouTube videos that blended violin performance with cinematic visuals. She has sold millions of records and toured globally. Memorial Auditorium seats about 2,000 on McCallie Avenue. Check the venue website for ticket prices. The show includes theatrical lighting, dance, and her full touring production. Doors open 30 minutes before showtime.
