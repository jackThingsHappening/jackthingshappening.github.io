The Doobie Brothers are a classic rock band formed in 1970. They are known for Listen to the Music, Black Water, and What a Fool Believes. The Walk This Road Tour marks a new chapter for the band. Memorial Auditorium seats about 2,000 on McCallie Avenue in Chattanooga. The building dates to 1924. Check the venue website for ticket prices and seating. This is a classic rock catalog show - expect hits spanning 50+ years. Doors open 30 minutes before showtime. Parking is available around the McCallie Avenue area.
