Stephen Wilson Jr. is a singer and songwriter from Seymour, Indiana. He blends country and rock, a sound he calls "Death Cab for Country." His influences range from Willie Nelson to grunge bands like Nirvana and Soundgarden. His debut album Son of Dad came out in 2023 and drew on the death of his father. This show supports his double album Impression of a Human. Memorial Auditorium seats about 2,000 on McCallie Avenue. Check the venue website for ticket prices and seating. Doors open 30 minutes before showtime. Parking is available around the McCallie Avenue area.
