Jeff Dunham is a stand-up ventriloquist known for characters like Achmed the Dead Terrorist and Walter. Inappropriate Contact is his current touring show. Dunham is one of the highest-grossing touring comedians in the United States. His shows fill arenas and theaters. Memorial Auditorium seats about 2,000 on McCallie Avenue. Check the venue website for ticket prices and seating. His material is adult-rated and leans on his long-running characters. Doors open 30 minutes before showtime. This is a larger-scale comedy production with sets, lighting, and full character costumes.
