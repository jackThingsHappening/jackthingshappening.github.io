The Nutcracker! Magical Christmas Ballet is a touring holiday production built for families. The show pairs classical ballet with large puppets, soaring birds, and grand stage sets. Dancers from ballet companies around the world perform the full story. Choreographer Viktor Davydiuk directs this North American tour. The matinee starts at 3 PM, so plan to arrive early for seating.
