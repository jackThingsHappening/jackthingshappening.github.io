Ashley McBryde writes country music rooted in Southern rock and Americana. She is known for honest, character-driven songwriting and high-energy live shows. Her albums have landed on the country charts and earned strong reviews from both country and Americana critics. The Into the Wild Tour marks recent material. Memorial Auditorium seats about 2,000 on McCallie Avenue. Check the venue website for ticket prices. McBryde plays to a wide audience - fans of modern country, traditional country, and Americana all show up for her shows. Doors open 30 minutes before showtime.
