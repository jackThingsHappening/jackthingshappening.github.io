Little Big Town is a four-member country group known for rich four-part harmonies. Their hits include Pontoon, Girl Crush, and Better Man. The For the Art of It Tour marks a new recording cycle. Memorial Auditorium seats about 2,000 on McCallie Avenue. Check the venue website for ticket prices and seating. Little Big Town has won multiple Grammy and CMA awards. Their live show leans on harmony-based arrangements and a mix of uptempo and ballad material. Doors open 30 minutes before showtime. Parking is available around the McCallie Avenue area.
