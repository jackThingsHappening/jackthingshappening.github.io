Oddstory Brewing hosts an all-day feast before the Chattanooga Renaissance Faire. Gather your crew for steins, turkey legs, and cocktail specials from noon to 10pm. Mystery trinket bags add to the fun. Oddstory is the official beer of the Ren Faire, so their new can designs pour all weekend. The faire itself runs Saturday and Sunday, November 14 and 15, at Coolidge Park and Renaissance Park. Entry to the pre party is free.
