Avatar is a Swedish heavy metal band from Gothenburg. Their sound mixes groove metal riffs with circus-dark theatrics. Frontman Johannes Eckerstrom sings in a sharp, grinning snarl and paints his face like a jester. Live, the band leans hard into costumes, staging, and showmanship. This stop is part of the Across The Underworld Tour. The band booked these headline dates between stadium shows opening for Metallica. A club this size is a rare, close-up setting for them. The Barbarians of California open the night. Doors open at 6pm and the show starts at 7pm. Tickets are required.
