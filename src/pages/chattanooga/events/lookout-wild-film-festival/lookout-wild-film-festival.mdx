Four days of outdoor and adventure film take over the UTC Fine Arts Center each January. The 14th edition runs January 14 through 17, 2027. Films cover wild places and the people who defend them, from adaptive athletes to water conservation workers. The festival draws filmmakers and outdoor enthusiasts from across the region. General admission is $43 and includes covered parking. Tickets are at lwff.org.
