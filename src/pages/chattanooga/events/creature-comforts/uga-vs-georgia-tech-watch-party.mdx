UGA and Georgia Tech have played every year since 1897. The rivalry draws strong Dawgs support in Chattanooga. Creature Comforts is pouring the official craft beer of the Dawgs for this watch party. The Beer Garden is a good spot to watch with people who actually care about the result.

The event runs 3 pm to 11 pm. Hours depend on the actual kickoff time. Check the venue's social pages before game day for any updates. The Beer Garden sits at Foundry Plaza, an open-air space in the Foundries District next to Erlanger Park. Parking in the Foundry District fills fast on busy nights. The 21st Street garage nearby is a solid backup option.
