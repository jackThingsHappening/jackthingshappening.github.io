Beer and local goods every Thursday evening. The Chattanooga Market sets up inside The Shed at the Creature Comforts Beer Garden. The Shed sits in the Foundries District, next to Erlanger Park and the Tennessee Riverwalk. Vendors bring produce, handmade goods, and local products from 4 to 8 PM. Cold beer pours from the shipping crate bar the whole time. No ticket, no cover.
