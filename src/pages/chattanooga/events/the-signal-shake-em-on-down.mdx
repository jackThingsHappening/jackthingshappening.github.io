The Shake 'Em on Down Tour is a blues caravan curated by guitarist Samantha Fish. She plays fierce, high-energy blues rock and shares the stage with two more strong acts. Cedric Burnside carries the raw North Mississippi hill country blues tradition. Daddy Long Legs mix blues, gospel, and rock into a wild live show. Together the three acts trace the past, present, and future of the blues. Doors open at 6:30 PM.
