Lucie Tiger plays a Saturday set at Puckett's downtown. Music starts at 7:30 PM. The restaurant sits on Aquarium Way near the river. Tiger is a Chattanooga-area singer-songwriter who blends pop, folk, and Americana into an intimate acoustic style. Her sets feature original material and carefully chosen covers. The restaurant serves Southern food through the whole evening. Puckett's is on Aquarium Way in downtown Chattanooga, two blocks from the Tennessee Aquarium. No separate ticket required. Walk in and find a table or book through the Puckett's website.
