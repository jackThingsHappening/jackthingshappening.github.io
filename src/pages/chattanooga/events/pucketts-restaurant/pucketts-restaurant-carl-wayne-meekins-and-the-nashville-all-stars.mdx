Carl Wayne Meekins brings the Nashville All Stars to Puckett's this Saturday. Meekins is a Virginia native who has played Nashville honky-tonks for more than two decades. His warm voice mixes traditional and modern country. The high-energy set starts at 7:30 PM near the riverfront. Puckett's charges no cover, so bring cash for the tip jar.
