The Briars fill Puckett's with live music on Saturday night. Music starts at 7:30 PM downtown. The weekend crowd packs the tables early on Aquarium Way. The restaurant serves Southern comfort food through the whole set. Puckett's is two blocks from the Tennessee Aquarium. No separate ticket required.
