Josh Driver and Kylan Rodgers split the stage at Puckett's on Saturday. Their set starts at 7:30 PM by the river. Puckett's serves Southern food and hosts live music on the weekends on Aquarium Way. Driver is a Chattanooga singer-songwriter who tells honest stories through acoustic folk and country. Rodgers is a Chattanooga-area guitarist and vocalist. The two artists share an audience and play well together as an evening bill. The room is casual and the stage is close to the tables. No separate ticket required - walk in and find a seat.
