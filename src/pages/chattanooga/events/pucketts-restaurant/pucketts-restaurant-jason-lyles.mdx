Jason Lyles is a Chattanooga singer-songwriter. He blends powerpop, Americana, and roots rock. His sound mixes The Cars pop hooks with Jason Isbell twang. Strong vocals and rhythmic acoustic guitar drive his set. He also slips in 80s pop and 90s alt-rock covers. The show starts at 7:30 PM by the river. Puckett's runs music every Friday and Saturday. The door stays free and tips go to the players.
