Jessamyn Rains plays Puckett's this Saturday. She is a Chattanooga singer-songwriter who calls her style folk impressionism. Her writing draws on Joni Mitchell, Sara Watkins, and John Denver. She sings, plays, and layers quiet, atmospheric songs. The set starts at 7:30 PM by the river. Puckett's charges no cover, so bring cash for the tip jar.
