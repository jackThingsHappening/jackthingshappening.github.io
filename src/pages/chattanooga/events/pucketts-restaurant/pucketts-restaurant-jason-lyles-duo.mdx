Friday brings the Jason Lyles Duo to Puckett's. The pair starts at 7:30 PM by the river. Puckett's runs music every Friday and Saturday. Lyles is a Chattanooga-area guitarist and vocalist who plays acoustic pop, folk, and classic rock. The duo format adds percussion or a second instrument. The restaurant serves Southern comfort food through the whole evening. Puckett's is on Aquarium Way in downtown Chattanooga, two blocks from the Tennessee Aquarium. No separate ticket required. Walk in or book a table through the Puckett's website.
