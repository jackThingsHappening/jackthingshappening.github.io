Nettie and the Delta Boy bring roots music to Puckett's on Friday. The set opens at 7:30 PM. The restaurant sits on Aquarium Way downtown. Nettie and the Delta Boy are a Chattanooga-area duo who blend folk, blues, and country into a warm acoustic sound. Their sets mix originals with well-chosen covers. The restaurant serves Southern food through the evening. Puckett's is on Aquarium Way, steps from the Tennessee Aquarium. No separate ticket required. Walk in and find a table or book a reservation through the Puckett's website.
