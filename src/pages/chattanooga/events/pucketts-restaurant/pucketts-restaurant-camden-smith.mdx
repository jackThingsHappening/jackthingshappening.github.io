Camden Smith brings live music to Puckett's on Friday. The set opens at 7:30 PM, steps from the aquarium. The room stays easy and warm on Aquarium Way. Smith is a Chattanooga singer-songwriter who plays acoustic folk, Americana, and original material. His voice is direct and his guitar work keeps things moving. He has opened for regional and national acts at Barrelhouse Ballroom and other Chattanooga venues. Puckett's serves Southern food through the evening. No separate ticket required - walk in or book through the Puckett's website.
