Friday night at Puckett's means live music near the riverfront. Bob Keel takes the room at 7:30 PM. The kitchen runs Southern comfort food and cold drinks through the whole evening. Keel is a Chattanooga-area musician who plays acoustic pop, country, and classic rock. His sets draw from multiple decades and keep the room comfortable. Puckett's is on Aquarium Way in downtown Chattanooga, two blocks from the Tennessee Aquarium. No separate ticket required - reservations can be made through the Puckett's website.
