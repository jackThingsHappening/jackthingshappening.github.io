Pan-O-Ram Club takes Puckett's on Saturday night. The set begins at 7:30 PM, two blocks from the aquarium. The kitchen serves Southern food all evening. Their sets move between original material and crowd-friendly covers. Puckett's is on Aquarium Way in downtown Chattanooga. No separate ticket required. Walk in and find a table or book a reservation through the Puckett's website.
