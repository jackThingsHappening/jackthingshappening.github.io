Feng E is a Taiwanese ukulele prodigy born in 2007. He started playing at age five and now draws nearly a million YouTube subscribers. He plays fierce lead lines and driving rhythms on a single ukulele. His sets mix originals with covers of Nirvana, Jimi Hendrix, and Michael Jackson.
