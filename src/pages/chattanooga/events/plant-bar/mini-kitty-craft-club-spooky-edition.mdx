Mini Kitty Craft Club comes back with a Spooky Edition at Plant Bar on October 15 from 5 to 10 pm. Guests make small cat-themed items with a Halloween twist. All supplies are provided. Mini Kitty Craft Club is a recurring Plant Bar event that brings cat lovers together for an evening of making things. No prior craft experience is needed.
