Crafting & Connections is a drop-in craft session at Plant Bar on the North Shore. Collage holiday cards for the people you love. You can also sign up to find a pen pal. Sit, chat, and make things with other guests. This is a low-key way to meet people over the holidays. Supplies are provided, and the format stays open. The zero-proof bar pours tea, coffee, and botanical mocktails. The event runs again on December 20. Admission is free and open to ages 21 and up. Donations are welcome.
