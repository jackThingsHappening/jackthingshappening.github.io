Harvest Party runs at Plant Bar from 2 to 7 pm. The North Shore spot celebrates the fall season with a relaxed afternoon gathering. Order a zero proof cocktail or specialty drink from the bar. Settle in among the plants and meet other Chattanooga locals. The low key setting makes this an easy way to mark the season. Admission is free.
