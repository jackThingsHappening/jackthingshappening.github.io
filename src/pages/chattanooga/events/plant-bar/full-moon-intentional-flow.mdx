Full Moon Intentional Flow runs at Plant Bar from 6:30 to 7:30 pm. The session pairs gentle movement with focused breathwork timed to the full moon. The plant-based bar serves zero-proof cocktails and botanical drinks. No prior yoga or movement experience is required. Wear comfortable clothes. This monthly session draws from moon cycle traditions and slow movement practices. It is a good reset between weeks. Check the Plant Bar website or social pages for registration and any cost for the session.
