Every Friday from 7 to 10 pm, Plant Bar lays out board games and card games on the table. The selection rotates week to week. The zero-proof bar pours mocktails, botanical drinks, and other alcohol-free options. This is a regular, free event - no ticket required. Bring your own game or play from the shelf. Plant Bar is a sober-friendly space that runs a full calendar of community events. Game Night is a good option for a Friday that doesn't center on alcohol. Check the Plant Bar website for the specific location and any updates to the weekly schedule.
