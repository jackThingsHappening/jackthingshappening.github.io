Every Thursday from 6 to 9 pm, Plant Bar sets out cardboard and mixed materials for building small scale craft projects. Bring your own ideas or work from prompts. The zero-proof bar pours mocktails, botanical drinks, and other alcohol-free options. No craft experience is needed. All materials are provided. This is a low-key, creative evening that works solo or with friends. Plant Bar is a sober-friendly space in Chattanooga. Check their website or social pages for the specific location and any changes to the weekly schedule.
