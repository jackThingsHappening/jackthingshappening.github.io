Cacao & Breathwork runs at Plant Bar from 6 to 7:30 pm. A guide leads breathwork paired with ceremonial cacao. The practice uses rhythmic breathing techniques to shift awareness. Ceremonial cacao is a traditional plant preparation used in South American ritual traditions. It acts as a mild heart opener rather than a stimulant. The zero-proof bar pours drinks before and after the session. Wear comfortable clothes. No prior breathwork experience is required.
