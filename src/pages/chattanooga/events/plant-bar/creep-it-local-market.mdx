Creep It Local Market runs at Plant Bar from 11 am to 3 pm. Local makers set up booths with handmade goods and spooky seasonal items. The zero-proof bar pours mocktails and botanical drinks through the market window. Entry is free. This is a community market event held in October with a Halloween theme. Browse original art, handmade clothing, jewelry, and food items from Chattanooga-area makers. Check the Plant Bar social pages for the vendor list and any changes to the event details. The market is family friendly and welcomes all ages.
