Coloring runs every Saturday at Plant Bar from 4 to 8 pm. Bring your own book or use what the bar has on hand. The zero-proof bar pours mocktails, botanical drinks, and other alcohol-free options. This is a free, walk-in event. No ticket required. Colored pencils and markers are available. This is a low-key Saturday afternoon option in a sober-friendly space. Plant Bar hosts a full weekly calendar of community events. Check their website or social pages for any changes to the schedule or any special themed coloring sessions.
