Just Breathe Experience runs at Plant Bar from 6 to 7:30 pm. The session blends aromatherapy, gentle movement, meditation, and breathwork into one continuous practice. A facilitator leads the group through each element. No prior experience is required. Wear comfortable clothes. The zero-proof bar pours mocktails and botanical drinks before and after the session. Check the Plant Bar website for registration, cost, and the specific date this event next runs. This is a monthly or occasional event suited to anyone looking for a restorative evening.
