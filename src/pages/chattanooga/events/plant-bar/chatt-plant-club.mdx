Chatt Plant Club is a monthly plant meetup at Plant Bar on the North Shore. The first session runs from 3 to 5 pm. Bring plants to swap with other growers. The event includes a plant giveaway. Trade cuttings, share care tips, and make new plant friends. Experienced hobbyists are on hand to answer questions. The zero-proof bar pours tea, coffee, and botanical mocktails. This event is free and open to ages 21 and up. Order a drink and settle in for the afternoon.
