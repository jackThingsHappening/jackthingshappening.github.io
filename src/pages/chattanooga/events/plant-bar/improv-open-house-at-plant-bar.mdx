Improv Chattanooga and The Chattery run a drop-in improv open house at Plant Bar from 6 to 8 pm. Watch the games or step in and play. Instructor Jason Parker leads the session. He directs Improv Chattanooga and has trained with Second City and UCB performers. All adults are welcome. The event is free, and donations support both groups. You must be 21 to attend.
