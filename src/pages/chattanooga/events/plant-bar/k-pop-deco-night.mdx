On October 28 from 5 to 9 pm, Plant Bar hosts a night of K-pop themed crafting and decorating. Guests build DIY fan projects - photocard holders, dekoden phone cases, and other K-pop fan items. All supplies are provided. The zero-proof bar pours mocktails and botanical drinks. No prior crafting experience is needed. K-pop Deco Night draws fans of Korean pop music together for a creative community evening. Check the Plant Bar website for cost and any registration requirements. Plant Bar is a sober-friendly and inclusive community space.
