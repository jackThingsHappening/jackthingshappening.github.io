Grievers Night Out is a low-pressure social gathering for people dealing with loss. Plant Bar hosts it as a space where grief doesn't have to be hidden or explained. The format is casual - show up, have a drink, talk if you want to. No agenda, no worksheets, no pressure to share. Plant Bar is a welcoming, plant-filled bar on the North Shore at 202 Manufacturers Road. The 21+ event runs from 7pm to 9pm on December 9. Check the Plant Bar events page for any updates before you go.
