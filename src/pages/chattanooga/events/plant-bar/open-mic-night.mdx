Open Mic Night runs at Plant Bar from 7 to 9 pm. Local performers sign up to share music, poetry, or comedy. Bring your own set or come to watch and cheer. The zero proof bar keeps the room relaxed and alcohol free. Order a mocktail and settle in. Admission is free.
