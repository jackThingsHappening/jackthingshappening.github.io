Fall Clothing & Plant Swap runs at Plant Bar from 2 to 6 pm. Bring clothes you no longer wear and plants you want to rehome. Trade them for new pieces and cuttings from other guests. The swap keeps good items in use and cuts waste. Browse the racks and plant tables at your own pace. Order a zero proof drink from the bar while you shop. This event is 21 and up. Admission is free.
