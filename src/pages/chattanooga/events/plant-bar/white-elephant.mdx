White Elephant is a community gift swap at Plant Bar on the North Shore. Bring one wrapped item to join the game. The item should be homemade, thrifted, or under ten dollars. Players take turns picking and stealing gifts. The game is silly and easy to follow. No experience is needed to join in. The zero-proof bar pours tea, coffee, and botanical mocktails. This event is free and open to ages 21 and up. Donations are welcome. Arrive on time so you do not miss the first round.
