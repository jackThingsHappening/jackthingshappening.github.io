The Entomology Workshop runs at Plant Bar from 6 pm. A guide walks the group through the world of insects. Learn how bugs live, move, and shape the ecosystem around us. The evening suits curious adults who want a new topic to explore. Order a zero proof drink while you learn. You must be 21 to attend. Admission is free.
