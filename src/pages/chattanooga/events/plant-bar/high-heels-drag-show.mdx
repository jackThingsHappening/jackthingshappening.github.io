Natasha Noir Nightly hosts the High Heels Drag Show at Plant Bar from 7 to 8 pm. The night brings big looks, bold laughs, and performances from local drag artists. Plant Bar is a sober-friendly and inclusive space. The zero-proof bar pours mocktails and botanical drinks. All ages are welcome unless otherwise noted. The High Heels show is part of Plant Bar's regular drag and performance programming. Check the Plant Bar website or social pages for ticket details, the full performer lineup, and whether this is a ticketed or walk-in event.
