Host Lily leads a junk journaling session at Plant Bar every Sunday from 4 to 8 pm. Junk journaling is a collage-style book-making practice that uses scraps, paper, stickers, ephemera, and found materials. Supplies are provided but you are welcome to bring your own. The zero-proof bar pours mocktails and botanical drinks through the afternoon. No prior art experience is needed. This is a relaxed, low-structure creative session. Walk in and find a spot at the table. Check the Plant Bar website or social pages for any changes to the weekly schedule or any materials fee.
