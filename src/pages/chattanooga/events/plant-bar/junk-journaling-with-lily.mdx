Host Lily leads a junk journaling session at Plant Bar every Sunday from 4 to 8 pm. Junk journaling is a collage style bookmaking craft that layers torn pages, washi tape, stamps, and found ephemera. Supplies are provided. Zero proof drinks are available. You must be 21 to attend.
