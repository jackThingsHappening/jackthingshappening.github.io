Bring your cat in their best Halloween outfit to Plant Bar on October 21 from 6 to 8 pm. Entry is free. The bar pours zero-proof cocktails and botanical drinks. Judges pick winners across categories. This is a low-key community event for cat owners who also enjoy a little friendly competition. Only well-behaved cats who can tolerate a costume and a room full of strangers should attend. Check the Plant Bar social pages for any rules around entry or handling. Photos are strongly encouraged.
