The Holiday Market runs at Plant Bar from 12 to 6 pm. Local makers and vendors set up tables with handmade goods. Browse gifts, art, and seasonal finds from Chattanooga artists. Order a zero proof drink while you shop the stalls. The North Shore spot stays warm and welcoming. Admission is free.
