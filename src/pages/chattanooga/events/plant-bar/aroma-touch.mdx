Aroma Touch runs at Plant Bar from 6 to 8 pm. A practitioner applies essential oils with light, intentional touch. The method follows a structured sequence across the back and feet. The zero-proof bar pours mocktails and botanical drinks before and after the session. Each session runs about 45 to 60 minutes. Spots are limited - check the Plant Bar website or social pages for registration and cost. This is a periodic offering at Plant Bar, not a weekly event. Wear comfortable clothing you can lie down in.
