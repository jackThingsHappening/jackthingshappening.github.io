Chattanooga Craft Circle runs a craft swap at Plant Bar on October 3 from 4 to 6 pm. Bring a finished handmade item to trade with another maker. Items can be knitted, sewn, painted, printed, or made in any other craft medium. The zero-proof bar pours mocktails and botanical drinks through the event. This is a free community event - all you need is one finished item to swap. Check the Plant Bar social pages for any swap rules or size guidelines for items. The Chattanooga Craft Circle meets regularly at different venues across the city.
