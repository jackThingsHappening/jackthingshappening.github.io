Local artists and makers take over Plant Bar on October 16 from 5 to 9 pm for Ghouls Night Out. Expect original art, handmade goods, and creative Halloween-themed items alongside the regular zero-proof bar offerings. This is a free market event - no ticket required to browse and shop. The event doubles as a Pride market, celebrating LGBTQ+ artists and community. Plant Bar is a sober-friendly and inclusive space. Check the Plant Bar social pages for the vendor lineup and any special programming during the evening.
