Every Tuesday from 7 to 9 pm, host Pigeon runs oracle card readings at Plant Bar. Pull cards, ask questions, and see what turns up. Oracle cards are used for reflection and intuitive guidance, not prediction. No prior experience with the practice is needed. The zero-proof bar pours mocktails and botanical drinks. This is a relaxed, open-format weekly event. Walk in and sit down for a reading. Suggested donation or low entry fee applies. Check the Plant Bar website or social pages for any changes to the weekly schedule.
