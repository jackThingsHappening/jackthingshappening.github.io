The Holiday Swap runs at Plant Bar from 12 to 4 pm. Give holiday items a second life. Bring ornaments, wrapping paper, and decor you no longer use. Trade them for pieces that are new to you. This cuts waste and refreshes your holiday stash for free. Browse the tables and take what you like. The zero-proof bar pours tea, coffee, and botanical mocktails. The event is on the North Shore. Admission is free and open to ages 21 and up. Come early for the best selection.
