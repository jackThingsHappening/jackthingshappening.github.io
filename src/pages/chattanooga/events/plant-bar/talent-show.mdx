The Talent Show runs at Plant Bar from 6 to 8 pm. Come show off a hidden talent of your own. Sing, play music, read a poem, or try something odd. Sign up on the day of the show. The crowd is friendly and the format is casual. You can also come just to watch and cheer. The zero-proof bar pours tea, coffee, and botanical mocktails. The event is on the North Shore. Admission is free and open to ages 21 and up.
