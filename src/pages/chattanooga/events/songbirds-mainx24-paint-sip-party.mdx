Gracie Clark leads this paint and sip party during MAINx24. She walks the room through a Christmas painting step by step. All painting supplies are included with your ticket. The event runs at noon on December 5, 2026, so you catch it early in the festival day. MAINx24 is a 24-hour street festival along Main Street in Chattanooga's Southside. Each person needs their own ticket. No painting experience is required. Arrive a few minutes early to pick a seat and set up your canvas.
