49 Winchester is a roots-rock band from Castlewood, Virginia. The six-piece blends Appalachian country, Southern rock, and raw storytelling. Frontman Isaac Gibson writes about small-town life with a weathered, honest voice. Their breakout record "Fortune Favors the Bold" put them on larger stages across the country. Fans can expect songs like "Russell County Line" and "Hillbilly Daydream." The show starts at 8pm with doors at 6:30pm. Tickets are required.
