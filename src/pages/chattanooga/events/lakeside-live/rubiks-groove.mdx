Rubiks Groove deliver a high-energy 80s and 90s dance party at Chester Frost Park. The band covers dance, rock, pop, and rap hits from the era. Costumed characters lead an interactive, over-the-top show. Admission and parking are free for all ages. Family activities start at 4:00 PM. Doors open at 6:00 PM. Pack a lawn chair or blanket and dance by the lake.
