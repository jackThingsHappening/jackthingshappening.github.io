Amber Carrington closes the Lakeside Live series at Chester Frost Park. The Chattanooga-based country singer reached the top five on The Voice in 2013. Her sound blends Texas country roots with powerful, soulful vocals. Bella Lam and the Boys open the night. Admission and parking are free for all ages. Family activities start at 4:00 PM. Doors open at 6:00 PM. Bring a chair and enjoy the lake.
