Captain Mike & The Shipwrecked play a Jimmy Buffett tribute set at Chester Frost Park. The nine-piece band covers Coral Reefer hits, deep cuts, and seafaring stories. James Snider with Fresh Mind opens the night. Admission and parking are free for all ages. Family activities start at 4:00 PM. Doors open at 6:00 PM. Bring a lawn chair or blanket and sit by the lake.
