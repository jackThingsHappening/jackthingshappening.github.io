The Read House Hotel throws its annual New Year's Eve party at 107 W MLK Blvd. The 2026 edition goes Roaring 20s. Gwen Hughes and the Retro Kats play live through midnight. The evening includes an open bar, heavy hors d'oeuvres, a champagne toast, and a dessert bar. Standalone tickets go on sale after December 16. The full package includes one night in the hotel plus party entry for two. The event is 21+. A $200 non-refundable deposit holds your spot, with the balance due December 27.
