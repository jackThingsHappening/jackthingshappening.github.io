The Silver Ballroom at The Read House opens for a free Holiday Jubilee on November 27. Live entertainment runs 4 to 7 PM. Hot chocolate, cider, and cookies are free all evening. Santa and Mrs. Claus are there for photos. No tickets required. Kids can add an Elf Workshop in the Centennial Room for $17 per child. The workshop runs the same 4 to 7 PM window. The hotel is at 107 W MLK Blvd in downtown Chattanooga.
