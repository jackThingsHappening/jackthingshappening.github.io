The National Cornbread Festival takes over downtown South Pittsburg every last weekend of April. It's a two-day outdoor festival 30 miles west of Chattanooga with cook-offs, live music, vendor markets, carnival rides, and more cornbread than you can eat. South Pittsburg is in Marion County, Tennessee, on the Tennessee River. The festival has run annually since 1997. The cornbread cook-off draws amateur and professional entries judged across multiple categories. Admission is charged. Check the National Cornbread Festival website for ticket prices, the music lineup, and the full schedule of events.
