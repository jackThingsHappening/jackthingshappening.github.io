The National Cornbread Festival takes over downtown South Pittsburg every last weekend of April. It's a two-day outdoor festival 30 miles west of Chattanooga with live music, a cornbread cook-off, family activities, a 5K, and a cycling event. Free parking with shuttle service. Rain or shine.
