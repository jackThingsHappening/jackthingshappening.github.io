Linda Davis is one of country music's most recognized voices. She is best known for "Does He Love You," her 1993 duet with Reba McEntire that hit number one on the country charts. That song won both singers a Grammy for Best Country Vocal Collaboration. She is also the mother of Hillary Scott, the lead singer of Lady A.

This December show brings her to the intimate Princess Theatre in South Pittsburg. The 1940s-era venue seats around 400 people. Sightlines are good from every seat.

Expect classic country hits alongside seasonal material for the Christmas show. Tickets are running low and are priced at $98–128. Book directly at theprincesstheatretn.com to avoid resale markups.
