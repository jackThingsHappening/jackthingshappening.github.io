Carols by Candlelight is a free holiday sing-along on Saturday, December 12 at 7:30 PM. The event is held at 75 Central Avenue NW in Cleveland. Attendees gather by candlelight to sing classic Christmas carols together. No tickets are required.

The format is traditional - this is not a ticketed concert but a community participation event. Bring the family and expect a low-key, warm atmosphere. The address puts the event in a central part of Cleveland, close to downtown. Check clevelandtnevents.com closer to the date for any additional details.
