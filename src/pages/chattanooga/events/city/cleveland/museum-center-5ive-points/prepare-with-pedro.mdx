Pedro is FEMA's Ready.gov mascot, a small dog who helps kids learn about emergency preparedness in an age-appropriate way. This free one-hour program at the Museum Center teaches children how to stay safe during storms, power outages, and other emergencies.

The event runs from 10 to 11 AM on Wednesday, December 16. It is free and designed for young children. The museum is at 200 Inman Street E in downtown Cleveland. Contact museumcenter.org to confirm whether registration is required or if walk-ins are welcome.
