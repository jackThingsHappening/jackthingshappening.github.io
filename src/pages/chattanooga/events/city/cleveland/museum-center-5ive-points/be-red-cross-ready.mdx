This free one-hour training covers household emergency preparedness. Presented by the American Red Cross, the session is designed for adults and older teens. Topics typically include emergency kit basics, communication plans, and evacuation routes.

The event runs from 5 to 6 PM on Thursday, December 10 at the Museum Center at 5ive Points. No experience or prior training is needed. The museum is at 200 Inman Street E in downtown Cleveland. Check museumcenter.org to confirm whether registration is required.
