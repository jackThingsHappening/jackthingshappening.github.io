This is the third year for the Happy Holidays Bake Off at the Museum Center at 5ive Points in downtown Cleveland. The event runs from 11 AM to 2 PM on Sunday, December 6. A $10 taste-testing ticket gets you samples from local bakers and a vote for your favorite.

Santa and Mrs. Claus will be on hand for visits throughout the afternoon. Local vendors will be selling during the event as well. Part of the proceeds support Farmers on First's indoor vending space for the winter season.

Taste-testing tickets are available at the Farmers on First booth during Sunday markets while supplies last. The museum is at 200 Inman Street E in downtown Cleveland.
