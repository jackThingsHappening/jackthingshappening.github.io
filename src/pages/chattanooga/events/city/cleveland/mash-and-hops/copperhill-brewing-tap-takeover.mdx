Copperhill Brewery takes over the taps at Mash and Hops for the night. This woman-owned brewery has brewed on the Ocoee River since 2018. The lineup runs from hop-forward IPAs to smooth, rich stouts. Come try their beers on draft and pick a few favorites. Check the site for start time.
