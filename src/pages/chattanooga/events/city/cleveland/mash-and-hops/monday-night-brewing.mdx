Monday Night Brewing takes over the taps at Mash and Hops for the night. This independent brewery started in Atlanta in 2011. They lean hard into hop-forward IPAs, barrel-aged beers, and bright fruited sours. Expect pours like their West Coast IPA and berry sour on draft. Try a flight and pick your favorites. Check the site for the start time.
