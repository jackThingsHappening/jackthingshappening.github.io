Schulz Bräu Brewing takes over the taps at Mash and Hops for the night. This German family-owned brewery opened in Knoxville in 2016. They brew authentic old-world lagers and ales. The lineup runs to Helles, Munich Dunkel, Schwarzbier, and Hefeweizen. Come try their German beers on draft and pick a few favorites. Check the site for start time.
