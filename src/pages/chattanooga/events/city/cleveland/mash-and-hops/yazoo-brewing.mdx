Yazoo Brewing brings its beers to Mash and Hops for the night. Yazoo started in Nashville in 2003 and helped shape the Tennessee craft scene. The brewery runs a wide range of styles. Expect pale ales, the Dos Perros brown, the Daddy-O pilsner, and the Hefeweizen. Try the lineup on draft and pick your favorites. Check the site for the start time.
