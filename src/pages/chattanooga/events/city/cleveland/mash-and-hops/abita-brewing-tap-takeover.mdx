Abita Brewing takes over the taps at Mash and Hops for the night. Abita is Louisiana's original craft brewery and has brewed since 1986. The lineup runs to the Amber lager, the Purple Haze raspberry lager, and the Turbodog brown ale. The Wrought Iron IPA rounds out the heavier end. Try these Louisiana beers on draft and find a new favorite. Check the site for the start time.
