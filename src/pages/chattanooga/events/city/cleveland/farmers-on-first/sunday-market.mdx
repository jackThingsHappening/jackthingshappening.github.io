Farmers on First runs every Sunday from 11am to 2pm in downtown Cleveland's First Street Square pavilion. Twenty-plus vendors show up weekly with locally grown produce, handmade goods, and food. Free to attend, year-round — moves indoors to the Museum Center at 5ive Points during the coldest winter months.
