THE PRESS in Cleveland is hosting an Ugly Christmas Sweater Karaoke Party on Friday, December 18, starting at 7 PM. The theme is simple: wear your worst Christmas sweater and sing. The more outrageous the sweater, the better you fit in.

Karaoke nights at bar venues like this tend to run late and pick up energy as the night goes on. It is a good option if you want something a little more fun and irreverent than the typical holiday event. Check clevelandtnevents.com for the exact address of THE PRESS and any cover charge details.
