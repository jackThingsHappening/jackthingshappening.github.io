Live music every Friday and Saturday at The Diving Mouse starting at 4:30pm. Local and regional acts on a real stage, with a full bar and covered patio out back. The Diving Mouse is a bar in Cleveland, Tennessee, about 30 minutes north of Chattanooga. The venue hosts live music most weekends with rotating acts covering rock, country, Americana, and blues. No ticket required for most shows - check the venue social pages for any cover charge on special events. The covered patio adds outdoor space when the weather cooperates.
