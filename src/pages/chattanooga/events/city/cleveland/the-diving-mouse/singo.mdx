Singo is music bingo - you mark off songs as they play instead of numbers. Every Tuesday at The Diving Mouse starting at 4:30pm. Free to play, open to everyone. The Diving Mouse is a bar in Cleveland, Tennessee, about 30 minutes north of Chattanooga. Singo runs at venues across the country and has a large library of songs. The game is easy to pick up and works well for groups. No ticket required. Walk in and grab a card. Check the Diving Mouse social pages for any updates to the weekly schedule or themed Singo nights.
