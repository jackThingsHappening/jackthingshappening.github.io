Karaoke every Wednesday at The Diving Mouse starting at 4:30pm. Cleveland's go-to midweek night out - smoke-free bar, covered patio, and a stage that's yours for a song. The Diving Mouse is a bar in Cleveland, Tennessee, about 30 minutes north of Chattanooga. The karaoke format covers a large song library across all genres and decades. No ticket required. Walk in and add your name to the list. The covered outdoor patio gives the venue extra capacity on busy karaoke nights. Check the social pages for any updates to the weekly schedule.
