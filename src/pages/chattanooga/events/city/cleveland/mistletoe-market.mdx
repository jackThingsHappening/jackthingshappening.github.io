The Mistletoe Market at Bradley Square Mall celebrates its 10th year in 2026. The market runs from November 27 through December 13. Over 150 food and artisan vendors fill the space, making it one of the larger holiday markets in the region.

Animal encounters and pictures with Santa are part of the draw. This is a good destination if you want to knock out holiday shopping with something for the kids built in. Bradley Square Mall is on Georgetown Road NW in Cleveland, about 25 minutes from Chattanooga. The market is free to enter. Hours vary by day - check clevelandtnevents.com for the schedule.
