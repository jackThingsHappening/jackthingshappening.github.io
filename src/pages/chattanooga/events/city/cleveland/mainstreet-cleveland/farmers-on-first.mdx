Farmers on First runs every Sunday in downtown Cleveland. The market fills the grassy First Street Square from 11am to 2pm. More than 20 local vendors sell fresh produce, baked goods, bread, chocolate, tea, and handmade artisan items. Shop straight from the farmers and food makers who grew or made it. Downtown Cleveland sits a short drive north of Chattanooga.
