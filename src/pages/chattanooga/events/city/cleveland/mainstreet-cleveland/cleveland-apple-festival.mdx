Two days of fall festival in downtown Cleveland. Apple-themed food, local vendors, live music, and the kind of crowd that shows up in flannels and stays all afternoon. The Cleveland Apple Festival is an annual community event in downtown Cleveland, Tennessee, about 30 minutes north of Chattanooga. The festival draws vendors and visitors from across the region. Live music runs on a main stage through both days. Check the MainStreet Cleveland or Cleveland Apple Festival website for the specific dates, vendor list, and performance schedule. Free to attend.
