Downtown Cleveland kicks off the holiday season with local vendors selling handmade goods, art, and gifts. Santa shows up in the afternoon and the community tree lighting closes the evening. Christmas on the Square is an annual event in downtown Cleveland, Tennessee, about 30 minutes north of Chattanooga. Local businesses and artisans set up along the downtown square. The tree lighting is the main community gathering moment of the event. Free to attend. Check the MainStreet Cleveland website for the specific vendor list, Santa arrival time, and tree lighting schedule.
