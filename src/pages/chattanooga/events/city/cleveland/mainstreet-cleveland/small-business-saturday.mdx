Skip the big-box crowds and spend the Saturday after Thanksgiving downtown instead. Cleveland's locally-owned shops and eateries open their doors for Small Business Saturday, so you can knock out holiday gifts and keep your money in town. Wander Courthouse Square, grab a coffee, and see what the local makers have out.
