The Community Tree Lighting closes out Christmas on the Square in downtown Cleveland. The town gathers at the corner of Broad and First Street to light the community Christmas tree at 6pm. It marks the start of the local holiday season. The lighting is free, outdoors, and open to everyone. Bring the family and stay warm.
