The Chattanooga Symphony and Opera performs Handel's Messiah at Collegedale Seventh-Day Adventist Church on Sunday, December 6, at 3 PM. The CSO Chorus joins the orchestra for this full performance of the oratorio. It is one of the most recognized choral works in the classical canon.

This is a separate performance from the Southern Adventist University events held on the same campus. The Collegedale SDA Church hosts the CSO for this annual holiday tradition. General admission tickets are $45. VIP tickets are $75. Student and child tickets are 50% off. Check the CSO website or the event listing for ticket links.
