The SAU Wind Symphony performs at Collegedale Church. Free and open to the public. The ensemble is part of the SAU School of Music and performs multiple times through the academic year. Wind symphonies play original compositions and arrangements for large wind and percussion forces - no strings. The program typically includes one to two large works and shorter pieces. Collegedale Church seats several thousand on the SAU campus. No ticket is required. Concerts begin at 7pm unless otherwise noted. Check the SAU School of Music website for the specific program and performance date.
