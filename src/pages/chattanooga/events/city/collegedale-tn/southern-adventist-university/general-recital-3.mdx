SAU School of Music students perform in a general recital at Ackerman Auditorium. Free and open to the public. A relaxed evening to hear the school's performers in a low-stakes recital setting. General recitals rotate through singers, pianists, instrumentalists, and small ensembles from across the music program. No ticket required. Ackerman Auditorium is on the SAU campus in Collegedale. Check the SAU School of Music website for the start time and program. These recitals typically run 60 to 90 minutes and are a good way to discover student musicians early in their careers.
