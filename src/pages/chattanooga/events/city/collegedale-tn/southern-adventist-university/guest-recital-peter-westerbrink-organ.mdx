Dutch organist Peter Westerbrink plays a free guest recital at Collegedale Church. Westerbrink has served as organist of the Der Aa-kerk in Groningen since 1992. He specializes in North German Baroque organ music and performs across Europe, South America, and the United States. The concert is open to the public and streams live for anyone who cannot attend in person.
