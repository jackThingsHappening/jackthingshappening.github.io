The Annual Christmas Tree Lighting at Southern Adventist University features a live performance by the Annie Moses Band - a Nashville-based classical ensemble that blends classical music training with contemporary arrangements. The Annie Moses Band has toured nationally and appeared on major television programs. The event is free and open to the public on the SAU campus in Collegedale. No ticket required. Collegedale Church or an outdoor campus location typically hosts the lighting. Check the SAU website for the specific location and start time. The evening includes holiday music and the official campus tree lighting ceremony.
