Student soloists compete in the live finals of the SAU Concerto Competition at Collegedale Church. Winners earn the chance to perform with the Symphony Orchestra. The competition is open to instrumentalists and vocalists from the SAU School of Music. Finals are judged by a panel of faculty and guest adjudicators. The event is free and open to the public. No ticket required. Collegedale Church is on the SAU campus. The competition final is both a performance event and a high-stakes academic milestone for the competing students. Check the SAU website for the start time.
