The Premiere Jazz Orchestra is a student big band from the SAU School of Music. The concert runs at Ackerman Auditorium on campus. The program features jazz standards, swing arrangements, and big band charts. Students fill the brass, reed, and rhythm sections. The concert is free and open to the public. No registration is required. Doors open before the 7:30pm start.
