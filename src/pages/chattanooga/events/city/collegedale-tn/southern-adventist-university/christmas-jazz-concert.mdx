The SAU School of Music Jazz Christmas Concert takes place Sunday, December 6, 2026 at 7:30 PM in Ackerman Auditorium. The jazz ensemble performs holiday standards and original arrangements. SAU jazz musicians bring strong technical training alongside an Adventist music tradition that blends reverence and swing. Admission is free and open to the public. Ackerman Auditorium is on the SAU campus in Collegedale, about 30 minutes east of downtown Chattanooga. No ticket is required — arrive a few minutes early for a good seat.
