The Honor Choir Festival closes with a full concert at Collegedale Church. Student singers perform the works prepared during the festival. High school choral singers from across the region spend the day on campus before this final performance. Free and open to the public. The concert is typically the same evening as the festival day. The SAU Honor Choir Festival is an annual event that draws strong singers from across the Southeast. Check the SAU School of Music website for the program and start time. Collegedale Church is on the SAU campus.
