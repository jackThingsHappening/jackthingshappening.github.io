SAU School of Music students perform in a general recital at Ackerman Auditorium. Free and open to the public. A relaxed evening to hear the school's performers in a low-stakes recital setting. General recitals give students performance experience outside of juries and formal concerts. The program rotates through singers, pianists, instrumentalists, and ensembles. No ticket required. Ackerman Auditorium is on the SAU campus in Collegedale. Check the SAU School of Music website for the specific start time and program details. These recitals typically run 60 to 90 minutes.
