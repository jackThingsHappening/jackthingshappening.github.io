Constellate is a student ensemble at Southern Adventist University that performs early music. This free concert features music from the 1600s. The program includes Telemann's Suite Bizarre, Biber's Battalia, English viol consorts, and anonymous French dances. Student Alison Menzmer directs the group. The concert takes place in Ackerman Auditorium and streams live for remote viewers.
