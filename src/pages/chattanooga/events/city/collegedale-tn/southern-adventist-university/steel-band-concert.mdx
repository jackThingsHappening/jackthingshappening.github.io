The SAU Steel Band closes the spring semester with a free concert in Ackerman Auditorium. The ensemble plays on tuned steel pans, an instrument rooted in the music of Trinidad and Tobago. The program mixes popular songs with classical and Disney arrangements. Past concerts have featured hits such as "Don't Stop Believing" and "Under the Sea." Director Keith Lloyd leads the group, and several student soloists take featured turns on the pans. No ticket is required. Ackerman Auditorium sits on the SAU campus in Collegedale, about 30 minutes east of Chattanooga.
