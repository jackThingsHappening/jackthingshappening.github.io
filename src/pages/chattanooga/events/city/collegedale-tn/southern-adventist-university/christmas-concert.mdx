The SAU School of Music Christmas Concert runs twice in December - December 5 at the Iles P.E. Center (8:00pm) and December 12 at Collegedale Church (7:30pm). Both performances are free and open to the public. The concert features the School of Music's choral and instrumental ensembles performing traditional and contemporary holiday music. This is one of the most attended events in the SAU music calendar. No ticket is required. The Iles P.E. Center and Collegedale Church are both on the SAU campus in Collegedale, about 30 minutes east of Chattanooga.
