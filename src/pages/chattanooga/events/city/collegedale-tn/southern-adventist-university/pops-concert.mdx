The Southern Adventist University School of Music presents its annual Pops Concert at the Iles P.E. Center. This lively evening brings together the Wind Symphony, Jazz Ensemble, and Steel Band for an accessible program of popular and orchestral favorites. Each year carries a fresh theme, mixing classical showpieces with jazz standards and steel drum grooves. The concert is free and open to the public. The event is also livestreamed for those who cannot attend in person. No registration is needed.
