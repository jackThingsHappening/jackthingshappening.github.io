The SAU Horn Ensemble performs at Ackerman Auditorium. Free and open to the public. An afternoon of brass music from the School of Music. The horn ensemble is a chamber group drawn from the SAU music program. French horns blend in ways that reward close listening - the overtones and harmonics stack differently than other brass instruments. Ackerman Auditorium is the main performance hall at Southern Adventist University in Collegedale. No ticket is required. Check the SAU School of Music website for the specific program, start time, and performer roster.
