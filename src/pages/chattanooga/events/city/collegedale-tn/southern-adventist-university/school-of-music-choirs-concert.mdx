The SAU choirs perform a spring concert from the School of Music. Student vocal ensembles share the program at Ackerman Auditorium. The concert features choral works across sacred and classical styles. Several choirs take the stage across the afternoon. The School of Music trains singers in vocal technique and ensemble skills. The concert is free and open to the public. The program starts at 3:00pm.
