The Southern Adventist University Symphony Orchestra performs full concerts twice in the fall semester at Collegedale Church. Free and open to the public. The ensemble includes string, wind, brass, and percussion sections drawn from the SAU School of Music. The program covers standard orchestral repertoire from the Baroque through 20th-century periods. Collegedale Church seats several thousand and provides excellent acoustics for large ensemble performances. No ticket is required. Check the SAU School of Music website for the specific program and performance date. Concerts typically begin at 7pm.
