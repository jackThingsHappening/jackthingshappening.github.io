The Honor Choir Music Festival brings student singers to the SAU campus for a full day of rehearsals and workshops. Events run at various times and locations across campus. High school choral singers from across the region audition for spots in the honor choir. The day culminates in a final rehearsal and concert. The festival gives participants a chance to sing alongside peers from other schools under a guest conductor. Check the SAU School of Music website for the schedule and specific venues. The final concert is free and open to the public.
