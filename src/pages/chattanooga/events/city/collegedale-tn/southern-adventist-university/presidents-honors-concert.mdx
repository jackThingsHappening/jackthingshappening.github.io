The President's Honors Concert showcases top SAU music students at Ackerman Auditorium. Free and open to the public. An afternoon spotlighting the school's strongest performers across instruments and voice. Students are selected by faculty for this honor. The concert features soloists and small ensembles performing works from the standard repertoire. No ticket required. Ackerman Auditorium is on the SAU campus in Collegedale. Check the SAU School of Music website for the specific program and start time. The event honors student achievement at the end of the academic year.
