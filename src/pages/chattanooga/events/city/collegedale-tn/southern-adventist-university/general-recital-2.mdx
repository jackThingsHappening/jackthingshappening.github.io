SAU School of Music students perform in a general recital at Ackerman Auditorium. Free and open to the public. A relaxed evening to hear the school's performers in a low-stakes recital setting. The program rotates through singers, pianists, and instrumentalists from across the music program. No ticket required. Ackerman Auditorium is on the SAU campus in Collegedale, about 30 minutes east of Chattanooga. Check the SAU School of Music website for the specific start time and program. These recitals typically run 60 to 90 minutes.
