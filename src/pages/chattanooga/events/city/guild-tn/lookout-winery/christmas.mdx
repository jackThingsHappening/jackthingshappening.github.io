Lookout Winery stays open on Christmas Day in Guild, TN. The family-owned winery pours its own local wine and fires Neapolitan-style pizza in a wood-burning oven. Gather friends and family on the mountain for a relaxed holiday meal. Doors open at 11am and close at 8pm. Settle in with a glass and a fresh pie along Hwy 41.
