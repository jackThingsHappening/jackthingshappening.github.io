Close out the year at Lookout Winery in Guild, TN. The family-owned winery pours its own local wine and bakes Neapolitan-style pizza in a wood-fired oven. Bring friends and family up the mountain for a laid-back New Year's Eve. Doors open at 11am and close at 7pm. Raise a glass to the year ahead along Hwy 41.
