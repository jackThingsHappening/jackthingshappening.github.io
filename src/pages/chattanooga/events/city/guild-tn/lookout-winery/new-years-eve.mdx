Ring in the New Year at Lookout Winery in Guild, TN. This ticketed dinner runs from 8pm to 12:30am. The buffet includes NY strip steaks, pasta, salad, and tiramisu. A DJ keeps the dance floor moving through midnight. The champagne toast at midnight looks out over the Tennessee River. Tickets are $75 per person. Call (423) 939-9334 to reserve. Guild is about 25 minutes south of Chattanooga near the base of Lookout Mountain.
