Lookout Winery stays open late on Labor Day - 11am to midnight. A good excuse to make the drive out to Guild and wind down the long weekend with local wine on the mountain. Lookout Winery is a family-owned winery in Guild, Tennessee, on Highway 41 on Lookout Mountain. The winery makes wine from locally grown and sourced grapes and also fires Neapolitan-style pizza in a wood-burning oven. The drive from downtown Chattanooga takes about 30 minutes. No ticket required. Check the Lookout Winery website for any special programming during the holiday hours.
