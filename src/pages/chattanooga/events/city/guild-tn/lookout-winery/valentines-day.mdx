Spend Valentine's Day at Lookout Winery in Guild, TN. The family-owned winery pours wine it blends and bottles on the property. Share a wood-fired pizza built with imported Italian ingredients. The tasting room overlooks the Tennessee River for a quiet date away from town. Doors open at 11am and close at 7pm. Guild is about 25 minutes south of Chattanooga near the base of Lookout Mountain. Raise a glass with your sweetheart along Hwy 41.
