Lookout Winery marks National Pizza Day on February 9th. The family-owned winery bakes Neapolitan-style pies in a wood-fired oven. Cooks build each pizza with imported Italian ingredients. Pair your slice with wine the Bordogna family blends and bottles on the property. The tasting room sits above the Tennessee River in Guild. Doors open at 11am and close at 7pm. Guild is about 25 minutes south of Chattanooga near the base of Lookout Mountain. Drive up the mountain and settle in along Hwy 41.
