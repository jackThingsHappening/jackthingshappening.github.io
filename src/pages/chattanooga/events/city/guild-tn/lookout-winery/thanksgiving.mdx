Lookout Winery stays open on Thanksgiving Day. Bring family and friends out to Guild for a relaxed feast on the mountain. The winery pours its own local wine and fires up wood-oven pizza fresh to order. Doors open at 11am and close at 7pm, so there is plenty of time before or after your own holiday plans. Skip the crowded restaurants and settle in with a glass along Hwy 41.
