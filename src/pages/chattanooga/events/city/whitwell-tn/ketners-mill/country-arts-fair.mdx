Ketner's Mill has been running this fair since 1977. Over 150 artisans set up across the historic mill grounds in Whitwell — pottery, sculpture, jewelry, woodworking, and more. Live bluegrass and country music all weekend, 20+ food vendors, sorghum molasses demonstrations, wagon rides, canoe rides, and a petting zoo. Runs two days, October 3 and 4. $8 admission, kids 8 and under free.
