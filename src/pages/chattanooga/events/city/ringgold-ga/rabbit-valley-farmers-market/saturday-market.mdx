Rabbit Valley Farmers Market runs every Saturday morning from May through October in historic downtown Ringgold. Local farmers, food producers, and artisans from across North Georgia bring produce, meats, baked goods, and handcrafted items. Free to attend, 9am to 1pm. Special themed markets throughout the season.
