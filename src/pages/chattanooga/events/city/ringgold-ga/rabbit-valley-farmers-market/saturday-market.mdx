Rabbit Valley Farmers Market runs every Saturday morning from May through October in historic downtown Ringgold. Local farmers, food producers, and artisans from the North Georgia and Southeast Tennessee region set up each week. Shop fresh produce, local meats, baked goods, honey, and handmade goods. No ticket required. Free to attend. The market runs from 8am to noon on Nashville Street in Ringgold, Georgia. Ringgold is about 20 minutes south of Chattanooga on I-75. Check the Rabbit Valley Farmers Market social pages for the weekly vendor list.
