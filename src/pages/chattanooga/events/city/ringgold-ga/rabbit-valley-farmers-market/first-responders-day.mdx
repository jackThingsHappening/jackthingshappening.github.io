First Responders Day honors the local police, firefighters, and EMS crews who serve North Georgia. Come thank them in person while you shop the regular Saturday market. The Rabbit Valley Farmers Market runs on Nashville Street in historic downtown Ringgold, Georgia. This special market day dedicates time and visibility to first responders from Catoosa County and neighboring departments. The market runs Saturday mornings from 8am to noon. No ticket required. Check the Rabbit Valley Farmers Market social pages for the specific vendor lineup and any special demonstrations or activities on First Responders Day.
