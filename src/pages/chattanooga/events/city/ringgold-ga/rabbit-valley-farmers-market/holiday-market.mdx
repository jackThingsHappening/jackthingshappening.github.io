The Holiday Market runs outside the regular season with extended hours - 11am to 4pm. Local artisans and food vendors set up for holiday shopping in downtown Ringgold. The Rabbit Valley Farmers Market returns for this special winter edition on Nashville Street in historic downtown Ringgold, Georgia. Shop handmade gifts, local food products, and seasonal items from the same makers you see at the summer market. No ticket required. Free to attend. Check the Rabbit Valley Farmers Market social pages for the specific date and vendor list. Ringgold is about 20 minutes south of Chattanooga on I-75.
