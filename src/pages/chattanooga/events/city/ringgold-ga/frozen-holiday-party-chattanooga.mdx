A Frozen-themed holiday party for kids comes to Ringgold Legacy Hall on Sunday, December 6. Two showtimes are available: 11:30 AM and 2 PM. The event is designed for young children and their families.

Ringgold Legacy Hall is on Nashville Street in downtown Ringgold, about 15 minutes south of Chattanooga. Check venunite.com for ticket pricing and to book your preferred showtime. If you have young fans of the Disney film, this is a solid December outing close to the Chattanooga area.
