The North Georgia Holiday Spectacular is a full Christmas musical variety show running December 17-23 at The Colonnade Theatre in Ringgold. Shows run at 3 PM and 7:30 PM daily. The production features professional singers and dancers, holiday production numbers, and a nativity finale.

The show is described as Pigeon Forge-style entertainment, which means large-scale staging and high production values. It runs about two hours. Tickets range from $10 to $35 and group rates are available. The Colonnade is at 264 Catoosa Circle in Ringgold, about 15 minutes south of Chattanooga. Book at northgeorgiachristmasshow.com.
