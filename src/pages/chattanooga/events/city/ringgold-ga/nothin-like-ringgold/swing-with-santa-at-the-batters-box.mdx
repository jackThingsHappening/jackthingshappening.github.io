Swing with Santa lets families and teams reserve batting cages at The Batter's Box in Ringgold on December 12. Santa shows up for photos between swings. The event runs from noon to 4pm and is open to both baseball and softball players. It's a solid option for youth teams looking to mix a holiday activity with actual practice time. The Batter's Box is at 429 Rollins Industrial Court in Ringgold, about 20 minutes south of Chattanooga. Cage reservations are required - check the Nothin' Like Ringgold events page for pricing and booking details.
