The Butterfly Festival returns to Willow Tree Farms in Ringgold. The day brings laughter, live music, and a butterfly release held in Katie Beth's memory. This community tradition means more than a fun day out. Families gather at the farm from 10am to 3pm. Admission is free and the whole community is welcome.
