The Rabbit Valley Farmers Market runs every Saturday from May through October at 7484 Nashville Street in Ringgold. Shop fresh produce, local meats, artisan goods, and seasonal items from North Georgia farmers and makers. The market is free to attend. It draws a consistent crowd of regulars and visitors from across Catoosa County and the Chattanooga area. Ringgold is about 20 minutes south of Chattanooga on I-75. The market runs from 8am to noon on most Saturdays. Check the Rabbit Valley Farmers Market social pages for any special market days or vendor announcements.
