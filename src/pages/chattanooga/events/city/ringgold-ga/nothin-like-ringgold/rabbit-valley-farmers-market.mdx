The Rabbit Valley Farmers Market runs every Saturday from May through October at 7484 Nashville Street in Ringgold. Shop fresh produce, local meats, artisan goods, and homemade breads from area growers and makers. The market opens at 9am and closes at 1pm. Entry is free and open to all.
