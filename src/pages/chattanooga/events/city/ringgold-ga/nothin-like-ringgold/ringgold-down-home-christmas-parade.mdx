Santa leads the Down-Home Christmas Parade through downtown Ringgold. Floats, lights, and holiday music fill Nashville Street for the evening. A Christmas tree lighting marks the start of the season along the parade route. The parade runs from 6pm to 8pm. Find a spot on the sidewalk early and watch the lights roll through downtown.
