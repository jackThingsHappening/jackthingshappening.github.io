Local artist Lesley Austin leads a Christmas art class for kids at The Ringgold Depot. Students in grades 3 through 6 paint a nativity scene and make handmade ornaments. The class runs from 9am to noon. Tickets cost $30 and seats are limited, so book ahead. Children take home their finished art as a keepsake for the holidays.
