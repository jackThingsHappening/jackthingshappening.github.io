The Festival of Flags fills downtown Ringgold with more than 2,000 flags and crosses. Each one honors a fallen soldier who called Catoosa County home. Volunteers raise the display from November 4 through November 18 for Veterans Day. You can view the tribute by walking or driving through the downtown streets. The flags stay up for two full weeks.
