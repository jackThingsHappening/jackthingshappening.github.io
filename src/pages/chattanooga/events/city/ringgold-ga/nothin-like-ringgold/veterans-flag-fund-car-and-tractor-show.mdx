Classic cars and tractors line up for display and judging on Robin Road. Owners and spectators vote in the people's choice awards. All proceeds support the Veterans Flag Fund, which pays for Ringgold's Festival of Flags. The show runs from 10am to 5pm. Bring the family and browse the vehicles while you back a local cause.
