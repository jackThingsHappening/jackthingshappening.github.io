The Boynton Lions Club hosts a free outdoor screening of The Polar Express at the Ringgold Depot on December 5. The depot is a National Historic Landmark - the starting point of the Great Locomotive Chase of 1862. Watching a train movie at the actual site where the Chase began adds something the typical living room screening can't match. The event starts at 7pm and is free to attend. Ringgold is about 20 minutes south of Chattanooga on I-75. Bundle up - December evenings in North Georgia get cold. Check the Nothin' Like Ringgold page for parking tips and any last-minute details.
