The Catoosa County Library brings its 7th Annual Trunk or Treat to the Colonnade. Children collect candy from decorated trunks while wearing their costumes. The event also features chalk art, bubbles, and dancing. It runs rain or shine, so families have a safe indoor backup. The Colonnade sits at 264 Catoosa Circle in Ringgold.
