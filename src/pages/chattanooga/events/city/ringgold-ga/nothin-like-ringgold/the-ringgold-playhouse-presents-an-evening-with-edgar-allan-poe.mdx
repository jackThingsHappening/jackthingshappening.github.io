The Ringgold Playhouse brings six of Edgar Allan Poe's most haunting tales to the stage. The program spans classics from The Tell-Tale Heart to The Fall of the House of Usher. This live reading suits fans of gothic horror and dark storytelling. The show runs from 7:30pm to 10pm on Nashville Street in downtown Ringgold.
