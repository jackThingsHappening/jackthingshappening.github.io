The Fall Junk Market fills downtown Ringgold with antiques, junktiques, and treasures. Browse vintage finds and one-of-a-kind pieces from local sellers and regional vendors. The market takes place on Nashville Street in historic downtown Ringgold, Georgia. This is a one-day outdoor market event. No ticket required to browse. Ringgold is about 20 minutes south of Chattanooga on I-75. The downtown area is walkable and has a few restaurants and coffee shops nearby. Check the Nothin' Like Ringgold social pages for vendor details and the specific start time.
