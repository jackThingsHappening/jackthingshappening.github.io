The Ringgold Playhouse presents Eurydice by Sarah Ruhl, which retells the myth of Orpheus through the eyes of its heroine. The run covers six performances. Sarah Ruhl's play premiered in 2003 and has become one of the most-produced contemporary American plays. It follows Eurydice as she descends to the underworld and must choose between her husband and her father. The Ringgold Playhouse is a community theater in Ringgold, Georgia, about 20 minutes from Chattanooga. Check their website for ticket details and performance schedule.
