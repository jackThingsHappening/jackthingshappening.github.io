The Ultimate Fall Fest at The Colonnade in Ringgold is a free family day with food trucks, local vendors, live music, and kids' activities. Runs 10am to 4pm. Free to attend. The Colonnade is a commercial and event space on Nashville Street in Ringgold, Georgia. The fall festival draws families from across Catoosa County and the surrounding area. Live music runs through the day on the outdoor stage. Kids' activities include games, crafts, and seasonal fall fun. Ringgold is about 20 minutes south of Chattanooga on I-75.
