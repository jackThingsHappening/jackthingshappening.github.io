The Historic Ringgold Depot turns into a haunted attraction each October. Walk through the haunted house inside the old train depot for scares and surprises. The event adds a hayride along the Chief Richard Taylor Nature Trail and ghost tours through downtown Ringgold. A bonfire, movie screenings, face painting, and live music round out the night. Local performers play throughout the run. Buy a combo pass to see the haunted house, take a hayride, and join a ghost tour together.
