Local businesses in downtown Ringgold open their doors for an afternoon of trick or treating. Grab a costume and a candy bucket, then go shop to shop along the historic streets. The event runs from noon to 3pm and welcomes families. Kids collect candy while the whole downtown turns festive for Halloween.
