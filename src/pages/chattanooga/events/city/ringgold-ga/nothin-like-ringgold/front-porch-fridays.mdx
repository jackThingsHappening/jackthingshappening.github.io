Front Porch Fridays is a free live acoustic music series at Benton Coal in downtown Ringgold, running every Friday evening through October. Local and regional singer-songwriters perform on the porch in an informal, relaxed setting. No ticket required. The series is designed to bring the community downtown on Friday evenings. Benton Coal is on Nashville Street in historic downtown Ringgold, Georgia, about 20 minutes south of Chattanooga on I-75. Check the Nothin' Like Ringgold social pages for the weekly artist lineup and start time.
