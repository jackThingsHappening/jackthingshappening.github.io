The Nightmare on Nashville St 5K runs through downtown Ringgold and down a spooky nature trail after dark. Runners dress in costume and pass spooky characters along the course. Every dollar raised supports local children and families through the Evitt Foundation. The event starts at the Ringgold Market Pavilion and includes a shorter fun run with trick-or-treat tables.
