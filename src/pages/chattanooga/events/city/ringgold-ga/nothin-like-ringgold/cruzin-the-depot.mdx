Cruzin' the Depot brings classic cars, trucks, and bikes to Nashville Street in historic downtown Ringgold on the first Saturday of each month. Free to attend and open to all makes and models. The event draws collectors and enthusiasts from across North Georgia and Southeast Tennessee. Owners park on Nashville Street near the historic Western and Atlantic Railroad depot. The depot is a National Historic Landmark - the starting point of the Great Locomotive Chase of 1862. Check the Nothin' Like Ringgold social pages for start and end times.
