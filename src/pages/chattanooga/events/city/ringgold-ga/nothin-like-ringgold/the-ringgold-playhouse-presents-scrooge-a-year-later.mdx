The Ringgold Playhouse stages Scrooge, A Year Later, a comedy by Matt Lyle. The play picks up one year after Ebenezer Scrooge changed his ways. Three spirits return to teach him about balance and setting limits. The show runs December 11 through 13 at the Playhouse's new downtown location. Performances start at 7:30pm each night.
