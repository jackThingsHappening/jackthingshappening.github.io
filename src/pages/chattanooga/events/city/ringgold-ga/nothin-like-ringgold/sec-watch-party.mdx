The Ringgold Market Pavilion hosts an all-day SEC football watch party. Fans watch three games on one large screen from 11am to 11pm. Food trucks and drinks keep the crowd fed through every kickoff. Bring a chair and settle in for a full day of rivalry games. Admission is free and open to all SEC fans.
