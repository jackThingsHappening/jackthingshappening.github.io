The Rabbit Valley Farmers Market hosts a special holiday market on Nashville Street. Local makers sell handcrafted gifts, baked goods, and seasonal treats. You can shop for presents while you support small vendors from the Ringgold area. The market runs from 11am to 4pm. Come early for the best selection of handmade items and fresh seasonal food.
