Rooted Night Market is a free monthly outdoor market hosted by Take Root Studio in Cleveland on the first Saturday of each month. Local artisans, wellness vendors, and food makers set up for an evening of community shopping. Take Root Studio is a wellness and creative space in Cleveland, Tennessee, about 30 minutes north of Chattanooga. The night market runs outdoors in the warmer months and may move inside in colder weather. No ticket required. Check the Take Root Studio social pages for the specific vendor list and any entertainment or programming during the event.
