Urban Chestnut Brewing Co. pours its beers at Mash and Hops on Thursday, November 19. The St. Louis brewery brews true-to-style German lagers and wheat beers. Its flagship Zwickel is an unfiltered Kellerbier that drinks smooth and bready. Schnickelfritz is a Bavarian Weissbier with notes of clove, banana, and nutmeg. Pint nights run from 5 p.m. to 8 p.m. Stop in to try these German-style beers on draft. Check the Mash and Hops site for pricing and the tap list.
