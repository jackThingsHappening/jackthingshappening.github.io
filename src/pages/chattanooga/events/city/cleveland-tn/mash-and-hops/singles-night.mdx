Mash and Hops runs Singles Night every Tuesday evening. Stop in, grab a craft beer, and meet other people in Cleveland's craft beer scene. Low-key, no pressure. Singles Night is an informal social event at the craft beer bar on 25th Street NW in Cleveland, Tennessee. No ticket or registration required. The bar pours a rotating selection of craft beers on draft. This is one of several recurring community events at Mash and Hops. Cleveland is about 30 minutes north of Chattanooga on US-11. Check the Mash and Hops social pages for the specific start time.
