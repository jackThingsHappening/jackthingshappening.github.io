Tailgate Brewing takes over the taps at Mash and Hops for the night. Come try their beers on draft and see what the brewery pours. TailGate Brewery has locations in Nashville and Chattanooga and brews a range of approachable craft beers. Their lineup typically includes IPAs, pale ales, and seasonal releases. Mash and Hops is a craft beer bar in Cleveland, Tennessee, about 30 minutes north of Chattanooga. Check the site for timing. These tap takeover nights are free to attend - you just pay for your pints.
