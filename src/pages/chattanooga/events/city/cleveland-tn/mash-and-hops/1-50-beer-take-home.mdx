Mash and Hops offers canned and bottled craft beers to take home for $1.50 on December 7. This is one of their periodic deal days aimed at clearing stock and giving regulars a cheap way to try new beers. Mash and Hops stocks a rotating selection of craft cans from regional and national breweries. The $1.50 price makes it an easy way to grab a variety pack on the cheap. The shop is at 168 First Street NE in downtown Cleveland, Tennessee. Check the Mash and Hops website for the specific hours for this event.
