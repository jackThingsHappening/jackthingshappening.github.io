Creature Comforts takes over the taps at Mash and Hops for the night. Come try their beers on draft and see what the brewery pours. Creature Comforts Brewing is an Athens, Georgia brewery founded in 2014 and known for clean, flavorful craft beers. Their lineup includes Tropicália IPA and Classic City Lager alongside seasonal and experimental releases. Mash and Hops is a craft beer bar in Cleveland, Tennessee, about 30 minutes north of Chattanooga. Check the site for timing. Tap takeover nights are free to attend.
