Wiseacre Brewing out of Memphis takes over the taps at Mash and Hops on December 3. Wiseacre is Memphis's largest craft brewery, founded by brothers Davin and Kellan Bartosch in 2013. They specialize in crisp lagers brewed with water from the Memphis Sands Aquifer. Their Tiny Bomb pilsner is the fourth best-selling craft pilsner in the country. This is a solid chance to try several Wiseacre beers on draft without driving to Memphis. Mash and Hops is on First Street in downtown Cleveland, about 30 minutes north of Chattanooga. No ticket or reservation required - just show up and order.
