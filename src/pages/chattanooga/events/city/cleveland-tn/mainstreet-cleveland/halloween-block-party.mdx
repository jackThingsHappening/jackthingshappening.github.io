Downtown Cleveland's Halloween tradition runs 35 years deep. Treat Street hands out Mars chocolate by the fistful, there's live music on the square, food vendors set up along the route, and the whole thing runs from 4 to 8pm. The Halloween Block Party is one of the largest trick-or-treat events in the region. Cleveland, Tennessee, is about 30 minutes north of Chattanooga. The event draws thousands of attendees across the four hours. Free to attend. Check the MainStreet Cleveland or City of Cleveland website for the specific setup and route details.
