Over 200 floats work their way through downtown Cleveland starting at 6pm. One of the bigger small-city parades in the region - worth the drive up if you have kids along. The Cleveland Christmas Parade is an annual December tradition in downtown Cleveland, Tennessee, about 30 minutes north of Chattanooga. The parade draws marching bands, decorated floats, community organizations, and Santa Claus. Free to attend. Find a spot along the route early for the best view. Check the City of Cleveland or MainStreet Cleveland website for the specific route and start location.
