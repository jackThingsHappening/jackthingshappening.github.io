This December homeschool day at the Museum Center digs into the history of Appalachian Christmas traditions. Students explore how families in the region celebrated before the era of modern retail. Lessons are hands-on. Each student creates their own Appalachian-inspired holiday craft to take home.

The Museum Center at 5ive Points focuses on the Ocoee Region's local history. Its programs for homeschool students combine artifact-based learning with craft activities. This format works well for mixed ages. Contact the museum at museumcenter.org to confirm registration requirements and start time.
