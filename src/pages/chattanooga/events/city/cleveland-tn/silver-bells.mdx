Silver Bells kicks off the holiday season in Cleveland on the evening of December 4 at Johnston Park. The event starts at 6 PM and is free to attend. Johnston Park sits in the heart of downtown Cleveland, making it easy to combine with shopping or dinner on the square.

This is an annual outdoor celebration for the community. It typically draws families and is a good low-key option for getting into the Christmas spirit early in the month. Dress for the weather - December evenings in Cleveland can be cold. Check clevelandtnevents.com for the full lineup of activities.
