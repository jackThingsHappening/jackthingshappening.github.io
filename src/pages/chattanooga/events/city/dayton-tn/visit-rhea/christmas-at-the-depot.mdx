Christmas at the Depot is in its 62nd year in 2026. The event runs November 28-29 in downtown Spring City, anchored at 390 Front Street near the historic depot. The Spring City Chamber of Commerce organizes it as the county's main holiday season kickoff.

The weekend includes a Christmas parade, the official lighting of the depot, photos with Santa, carriage rides, food trucks, live music, craft vendors, and contests. It draws families from across Rhea County.

Spring City is about 15 miles north of Dayton on US-27. Parking fills up around parade time, so plan to arrive early. Check springcitychamber.org for the full Saturday and Sunday schedule.
