Bryan Overlook sits on the rim of the Cumberland Plateau at Laurel Snow State Natural Area outside Dayton. The overlook is named for William Jennings Bryan, the prosecutor in the 1925 Scopes Trial held in Dayton. It offers a broad eastward view down into the Tennessee Valley below.

The guided hike runs from 9 AM to 5 PM on Saturday, December 12. A sack lunch is provided at the midpoint. The trail is rated hard with moderate elevation gain. Bring layers - December temperatures on the plateau can be cold, especially in the wind.

This is a full-day commitment. Wear trail shoes or boots, not sneakers. Confirm registration at visitrhea.com before heading out.
