The Civil Air Patrol's Cleveland Composite Squadron runs a Fly-In Drive-In Breakfast on the fourth Saturday of every month at Mark Anton Municipal Airport in Dayton. The December edition is December 26 from 7:30 to 11 AM. Cadets serve pancakes, eggs, sausage, and biscuits and gravy for around $10 a plate.

The format is simple. Fly in if you have a small aircraft. Drive in if you don't. The small municipal airport setting makes it an interesting breakfast spot even if you have no aviation interest. It is a rare chance to watch light aircraft come and go from the ramp while eating breakfast.

Mark Anton Municipal Airport is on Walter Squire Road off US-27 in Dayton. Payment is cash or PayPal.
