Christmas at the Courthouse is Dayton's main holiday event of the year. It runs from noon to 4 PM on Saturday, December 5 at the Rhea County Courthouse on Market Street. The event is free to attend.

The lineup includes a Christmas Critters pageant, a non-motorized holiday parade through downtown, selfie stations with Santa, artisan craft vendors, and caroling. It is a family-friendly afternoon and one of the bigger events in the Dayton area each December.

The Rhea County Courthouse is a historic building in the heart of downtown Dayton. It is famous as the site of the 1925 Scopes Trial. Street parking is available on Market Street and surrounding blocks. The event wraps up around 4 PM, followed by the It's a Wonderful Life Radio Drama at 5 PM at the same location.
