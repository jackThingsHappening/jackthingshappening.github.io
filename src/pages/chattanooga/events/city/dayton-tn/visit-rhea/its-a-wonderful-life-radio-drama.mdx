The Rhea Heritage Preservation Foundation presents a live radio theater production of It's a Wonderful Life on Saturday, December 5 at 5 PM. The performance runs until 7 PM at the Rhea County Courthouse. It is free to attend.

The format is a 1940s-style radio drama: performers stand at microphones in period dress, scripts in hand, with live sound effects. It is a faithful recreation of how the story was originally broadcast on the radio before the film version became a classic. The Rhea County Courthouse is a fitting backdrop, with its own deep history as the site of the 1925 Scopes Trial.

This event follows the Christmas at the Courthouse afternoon event at the same location. Plan to arrive by 4:45 to get a seat before the curtain. The courthouse has limited seating so early arrival helps.
