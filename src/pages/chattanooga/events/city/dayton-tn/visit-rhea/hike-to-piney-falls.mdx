The Piney Falls loop in Spring City is a 3.5 km moderate trail that leads to a waterfall in Rhea County. This guided hike runs from 10 AM to 1 PM on Wednesday, December 30. It is a half-day outing and a good option for the week between Christmas and New Year's.

Spring City is about 15 miles north of Dayton on US-27. Wear trail shoes and bring water. The trail is rated moderate - no major scrambles but some uneven terrain. Confirm registration and the exact trailhead address at visitrhea.com before heading out.
