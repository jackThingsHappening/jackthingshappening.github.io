Monkey Town Brewing Co. books live music every Friday night. The acts are mostly local and regional - acoustic acts, country, rock, and Americana. The room is small, which means good sight lines and sound from anywhere in the taproom.

Monkey Town is Dayton's original brewpub at 287 1st Avenue. The Friday crowd tends to be a mix of regulars and walk-ins. It is a solid low-key night out if you are in the Dayton area.

Artists rotate each week. Check their Facebook page for the specific performer lineup. Holiday Fridays like Christmas and New Year's may have adjusted hours or programming.
