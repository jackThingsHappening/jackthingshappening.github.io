Monkey Town Brewing Co. runs Trivia Tuesday every week at 7 PM. The format is pub trivia with rounds covering general knowledge, pop culture, sports, and local history. It is free to play. Teams form at the bar.

Monkey Town is Dayton's original brewpub. It opened at 287 1st Avenue and has been a gathering spot in town since it launched. The taproom has a relaxed neighborhood bar feel with locally brewed beers on draft.

Arrive by 6:45 to get settled before trivia starts. Holiday weeks (Christmas, New Year's) may run differently. Check their Facebook page for any schedule changes.
