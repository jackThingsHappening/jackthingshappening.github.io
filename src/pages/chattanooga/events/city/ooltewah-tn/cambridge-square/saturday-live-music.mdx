Cambridge Square hosts free live music every Saturday night from late April through October. Local musicians play classic rock, country, jazz, and bluegrass on the lawn. Cambridge Square is a commercial and residential development in Ooltewah, Tennessee, east of Chattanooga. The acts rotate each week. No ticket required. Bring a chair or blanket and find a spot on the lawn. Music runs from 6:30 to 8:30pm. Food and drink options are available from Cambridge Square businesses. Check the Cambridge Square Facebook page for the weekly artist announcement and any changes to the schedule.
