Autumn Harvest Market brings together unique and traditional craft vendors plus food options at Cambridge Square. Free to attend. Cambridge Square is a commercial and residential development in Ooltewah, Tennessee, east of Chattanooga. The fall market features handmade crafts, seasonal produce, food vendors, and artisan goods. The outdoor setting on the Cambridge Square lawn makes for a relaxed fall shopping experience. No ticket required. Check the Cambridge Square website or social pages for the vendor list, specific hours, and any entertainment scheduled during the market.
