The Lanterns and Neon Lights Festival fills the Jasper Courthouse square on Saturday, December 12. The event runs from 2 to 8 PM. Food vendors, craft sellers, and local businesses set up around the square for the afternoon.

The highlight is the parade at 6 PM, which features lanterns and neon lighting after dark. The combination makes for a good spectacle once the sun goes down. Arrive by 5 PM to get a good spot along the parade route.

The festival is free to attend. Jasper is about 45 minutes northwest of Chattanooga via US-41.
