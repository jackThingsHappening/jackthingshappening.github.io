Jasper Parks and Recreation takes over Main Street on the evening of December 4 for Santa and S'mores Night. The event runs from 5 to 9 PM. Shops stay open late with candlelight-style window displays along the street.

Live music plays at Stegall Alley and on South Main throughout the evening. The Grinch and Cindy Lou Who will be making appearances. Cowboy Santa holds court from 5:45 to 8:45 PM for photos. Disney Princesses in Christmas attire offer free face painting for kids.

S'more kits are available for purchase near the fire pit. Proceeds from s'more kits benefit the Jasper Fire Department. The event is free to attend and runs rain-or-shine.
