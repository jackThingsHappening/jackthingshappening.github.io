Chili on Main brings a chili cook-off to downtown Jasper in winter. Jasper Main Street hosts this community contest on Main Street. Local cooks compete for the best bowl of chili. Visitors taste the entries and warm up on a cold afternoon. The event runs four hours around midday. Check the Jasper Main Street website for entry rules and tasting details.
