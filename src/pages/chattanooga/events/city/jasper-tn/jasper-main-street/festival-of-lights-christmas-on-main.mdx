Festival of Lights and Christmas on Main open the holiday season in downtown Jasper. Jasper Main Street lights the Christmas tree on Courthouse Square. Trolley rides run along Main Street with volunteers dressed as elves. Santa and Mrs. Claus pose for photos with kids. Dickens-style carolers sing through the crowd. The festivities run two hours in the early evening. Check the Jasper Main Street website for the final schedule.
