FallFest fills Main Street with autumn activities in downtown Jasper. Jasper Main Street runs this community gathering each fall. Families come out for seasonal fun on the square. The event runs two hours in the afternoon. Expect local vendors and a relaxed small-town feel. Check the Jasper Main Street website for the full lineup and any weather updates.
