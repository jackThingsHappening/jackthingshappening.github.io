The Foothills Festival runs two days on Main Street in downtown Jasper. A community event organized by Jasper Main Street with local vendors, music, and food. The festival marks a key moment in the downtown Jasper calendar. Artisans, food vendors, and local organizations set up along the main street corridor. Live music runs on a stage through the afternoon. Jasper is in Marion County, Tennessee, about 40 minutes northwest of Chattanooga. Free to attend. Check the Jasper Main Street website for the full vendor and entertainment lineup, dates, and hours.
