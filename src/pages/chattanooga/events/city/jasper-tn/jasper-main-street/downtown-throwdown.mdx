The Downtown Throwdown brings the community out to Main Street in Jasper. Organized by Jasper Main Street. Check their site for details on what to expect. The event is a community street festival in downtown Jasper, Tennessee, in Marion County. Jasper Main Street runs the event as part of its downtown revitalization mission. Main Street Jasper is about 40 minutes northwest of Chattanooga on US-72. The festival format includes vendors, food, and live entertainment. Free to attend. Check the Jasper Main Street website or social pages for the full lineup and event hours.
