Apero on Main is an evening out on Main Street in downtown Jasper - drinks, conversation, and community from 5 to 8:30pm. Think of it as a downtown happy hour with a European name. Jasper Main Street organizes the event to draw people downtown on a weekday evening. Local businesses participate with specials and open doors. No ticket required. Jasper is in Marion County, Tennessee, about 40 minutes northwest of Chattanooga on US-72. Check the Jasper Main Street website or social pages for the specific date and participating businesses.
