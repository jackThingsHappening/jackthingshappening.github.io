The Holly Jolly Trolley carries riders through downtown Jasper for a holiday weekend. Jasper Main Street runs the trolley on Main Street. Families ride together and take in the seasonal sights. The event spans two days in December. Hours run from late morning through the afternoon on both days. Check the Jasper Main Street website for boarding details and any updates.
