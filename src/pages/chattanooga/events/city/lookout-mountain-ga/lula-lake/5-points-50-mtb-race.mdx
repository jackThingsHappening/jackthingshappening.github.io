The 5 Points 50 is a mountain bike race held on the trails of Lula Lake Land Trust on Lookout Mountain. Registration through BikeReg. Check the Lula Lake events page for details. The race format covers approximately 50 miles of trail and gravel on Lookout Mountain. The land trust protects over 8,000 acres. Riders register online and receive a course map before race day. The race draws competitive and recreational mountain bikers from across the Southeast. Entry fees benefit the land trust. Check the Lula Lake Land Trust website for the specific course details and registration deadline.
