The Cleverlys are a bluegrass comedy act from the Ozarks. They play pop, rock, and hip-hop hits in full Ozark bluegrass style - banjo, fiddle, mandolin, bass, and guitar - with tight vocal harmonies and absurdist family-band comedy built in. Think "Uptown Funk" or "Baby Got Back" played like a classic mountain string band, delivered completely straight.

The band has built a loyal touring following at festivals and theaters across the country. The Princess Theatre in South Pittsburg is a 400-seat restored 1940s venue. It is a small, intimate stop for a band that regularly plays much larger rooms.
