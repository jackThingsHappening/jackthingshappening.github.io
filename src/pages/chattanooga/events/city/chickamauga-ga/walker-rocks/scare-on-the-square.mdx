Scare on The Square fills downtown LaFayette with Halloween fun. Local business owners line the street and hand out candy to children. A costume contest runs at the Downtown Commons for kids and adults. The event is free to attend. Joe Stock started this tradition more than 30 years ago. Families return each year for the trick-or-treating and the downtown atmosphere.
