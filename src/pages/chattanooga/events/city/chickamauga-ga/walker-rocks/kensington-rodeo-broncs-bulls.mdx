The Kensington Rodeo brings a Broncs & Bulls night to the arena in Chickamauga. The card features bull riding, bronc riding, roping, and barrel racing. Kids can try mutton busting, where young riders hold on to a sheep. Clowns and trick riders keep the crowd going. Food booths sell burgers, barbecue, ice cream, and fry bread. Local charities run stands on the grounds.
