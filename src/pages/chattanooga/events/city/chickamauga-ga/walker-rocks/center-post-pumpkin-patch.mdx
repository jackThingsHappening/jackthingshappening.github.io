Center Post Pumpkin Patch opens each fall on GA-337 near LaFayette. Families pick pumpkins straight from the field and enjoy seasonal farm activities. The patch runs daily from mid-September through late October with wide open hours. Bring the kids for photos, hayrides, and a full afternoon in the crisp Walker County air.
