Downtown LaFayette holds its Veteran's Day Parade to honor those who served. The route runs through the heart of town at midday. Community groups, school bands, veterans organizations, and local businesses march through downtown LaFayette, Georgia. The event is free to attend. Find a spot along the parade route and watch the procession pass. LaFayette is in Walker County, Georgia, about 30 minutes from Chattanooga. Check the Walker Rocks events page for the specific start time and parade route. The ceremony following the parade typically includes a brief program and wreath-laying.
