Chickamauga celebrates its birthday at the Gordon Lee Mansion. A community afternoon on the historic mansion grounds. Check the Walker Rocks events page for details. The Gordon Lee Mansion is a National Historic Landmark in Chickamauga, Georgia. It served as a Union field hospital during the Battle of Chickamauga in 1863. The mansion stands near the Chickamauga Battlefield and is part of the city's historic identity. The birthday celebration marks the founding of the city with community programming, food, and local vendors on the grounds.
