The Kensington Rodeo runs two full days - September 5 and 6 - at the rodeo grounds in Chickamauga. Check the Walker Rocks events page for tickets and details. The event features bull riding, barrel racing, steer wrestling, and team roping. Local and regional competitors participate across multiple events over the weekend. The Kensington Rodeo is a traditional community event in Walker County, Georgia. Concessions and vendors set up around the grounds. This is about a 30-minute drive from downtown Chattanooga and draws a crowd from across the North Georgia and Southeast Tennessee region.
