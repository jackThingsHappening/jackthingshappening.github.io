The Enchanted Pumpkin Patch turns Rock City into a fall wonderland from September 18 through October 31. Master carvers cover the trails with hundreds of hand-carved pumpkins and glowing autumn scenes. Families can walk the gardens, meet costumed characters, and enjoy seasonal food and photo spots. The event runs during regular Rock City hours. Check the Walker Rocks events page for admission and ticket details.
