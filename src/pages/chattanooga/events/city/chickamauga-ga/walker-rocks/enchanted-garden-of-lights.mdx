The Enchanted Garden of Lights returns to Rock City for its 32nd year. The event runs nightly from November 13 through December 31, closed only on Christmas Eve. Millions of LED lights cover the trails with themed holiday displays and interactive scenes. Visitors meet costumed characters along the way, including elves, a toy soldier, and Santa Claus. Gates open at 4:00pm, with last entry 45 minutes before close. Tickets use timed entry and sell online only. Check the Walker Rocks events page for current admission prices.
