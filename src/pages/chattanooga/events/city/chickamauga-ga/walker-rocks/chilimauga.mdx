Chilimauga is a chili cook-off and street fair in downtown Chickamauga. Cooks of all skill levels compete with family recipes and restaurant-style chili. The cook-off runs from noon to 2:00pm, and judges announce winners at 2:45pm. Buy a tasting ticket to sample and vote. Live music and vendors fill the street. The event raises money for the Gordon Lee Marching Trojan Band.
