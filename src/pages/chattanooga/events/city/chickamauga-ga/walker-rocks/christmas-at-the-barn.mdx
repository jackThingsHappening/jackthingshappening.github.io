Christmas at The Barn brings holiday cheer to Raulston Acres in Rock Spring. The evening gathering features local vendors, handmade gifts, and sweet treats. Live music fills the barn and Santa greets visitors. The farm is a family-run choose-and-cut Christmas tree operation. The event runs into the evening.
