The Amazing Christmas Light Show runs nightly at Joe Stock Memorial Park in LaFayette from late November through December 31. The city sets up synchronized, multi-colored LED lights timed to holiday music. The show starts at 6pm each night and runs until 10pm. It is completely free. If the cold is a problem, you can tune your car radio to FM 89.5 and watch from your vehicle. The park is at 302 North Main Street in LaFayette, GA, about 30 minutes south of Chattanooga.
