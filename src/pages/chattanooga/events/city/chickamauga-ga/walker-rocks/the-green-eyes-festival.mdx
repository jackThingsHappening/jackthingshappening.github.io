The Green Eyes Festival honors the folklore of Northwest Georgia in downtown Chickamauga. The event centers on the local legend of Old Green Eyes. More than 50 arts, crafts, and food vendors line the streets. Attendees enjoy live music, a beer garden, battlefield trolley tours, and a scavenger hunt. Costumes and cosplay are encouraged, with a contest sign-up at the info tent.
