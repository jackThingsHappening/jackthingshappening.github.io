Beginagin Farm hosts an arts and craft show on its grounds in LaFayette. Vendors show and sell handmade crafts, and artists run live demos. The day also features live music, farm animals, and food. A classic car cruise-in rolls in alongside the show. Admission is free. Bring the family and spend the day on the farm.
