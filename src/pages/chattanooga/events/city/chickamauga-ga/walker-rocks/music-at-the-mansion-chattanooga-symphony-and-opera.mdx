The Chattanooga Symphony & Opera performs on the front porch of the historic Gordon Lee Mansion. This concert is part of Chickamauga's Music at the Mansion series. Admission is free for all ages. Bring a chair or picnic blanket and settle on the lawn. Music starts in the evening under the mansion's old trees.
