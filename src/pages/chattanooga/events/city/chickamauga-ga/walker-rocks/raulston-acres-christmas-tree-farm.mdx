Raulston Acres Christmas Tree Farm opens the Saturday before Thanksgiving and runs through December 22 or until inventory is gone. The farm grows more than 3,700 trees across several varieties including Murray Cypress, Leyland Cypress, and Carolina Sapphire. You can choose and cut your own or have staff help. Free hot apple cider and hot chocolate are available around a campfire. Hayrides run on Saturdays. The farm is at 2572 GA-95 in Rock Spring, GA, about 20 minutes south of Chattanooga. Weekday hours are 2pm to 5pm. Saturday hours are 10am to 6pm.
