The Marsh House runs candlelight tours on December 12, 13, 19, and 20, 2026. Tour groups depart at 6pm, 6:45pm, and 7:30pm each evening. The house is decorated in the style of the 1880s. Hosts share period Christmas customs and traditions from the 19th century. Seasonal treats include hot cider, ginger cookies, and roasted chestnuts. Admission is $10 for adults and $1 for children. No reservations required. The Marsh House is at 308 N. Main Street in LaFayette, GA, about 25 minutes south of Chattanooga.
