Fricks Cave Preserve hosts one of Walker County's most unusual evenings - live bluegrass, cold beer, and watching bats emerge at dusk from one of the largest hibernating bat colonies in the Eastern United States. Fricks Cave is managed by The Nature Conservancy. The bat emergence is a natural spectacle - thousands of gray bats and other species stream out of the cave opening at dusk. The event raises money for bat conservation. Check the Walker Rocks events page for tickets and details. The preserve is in Walker County, Georgia, about 30 minutes from Chattanooga.
