The Walker County Fall Festival fills the civic center grounds in Rock Spring for one evening. The event brings autumn entertainment, activities, and community booths together. Families gather for a few hours as the weather turns cool. Expect food, seasonal fun, and local vendors. The festival runs from late afternoon into the evening.
