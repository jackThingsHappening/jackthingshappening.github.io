Jeep Fest returns to Chickamauga for its fifth year. Owners show off custom rigs, off-road builds, and lifted trucks across the day. The event celebrates four-wheel-drive culture in a small-town setting. Expect vendors, food, and a crowd of enthusiasts. The gates run from mid-morning through late afternoon downtown.
