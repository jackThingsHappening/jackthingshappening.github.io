Jeep Fest returns to Chickamauga for its fifth year. Owners show off custom rigs, off-road builds, and lifted trucks across the day. The event celebrates Jeep culture in Walker County, Georgia. The Kensington area of Chickamauga has become the host location for this growing annual event. No ticket required to attend and view the vehicles. Walk the event grounds and talk to owners about their builds. Chickamauga is about 25 miles south of Chattanooga. Check the Walker Rocks events page for the specific location, entry details for Jeep owners, and the full schedule of activities.
