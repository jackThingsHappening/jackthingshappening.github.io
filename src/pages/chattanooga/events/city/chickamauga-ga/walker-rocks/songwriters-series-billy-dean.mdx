Billy Dean brings the Songwriter's Series to McLemore Resort for one night. Dean is a Grammy-nominated country singer-songwriter from Quincy, Florida. He wrote and recorded hits like "Somewhere in My Broken Heart," "Billy the Kid," and "Only Here for a Little While." His warm, story-driven style fits the intimate stage well. The show runs into the evening on the mountain. Check the Walker Rocks events page for tickets.
