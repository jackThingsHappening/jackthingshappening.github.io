The 5 Points 50 is Chattanooga's oldest mountain bike race. Riders start at Lula Lake Land Trust and climb into the 5 Points and Durham Mine trail systems on Lookout Mountain. Choose the full 50-mile course or the shorter 25-mile option. A Kids' Fun Ride runs for younger riders. All proceeds support trail building and maintenance at Lula Lake Land Trust. Register in advance through the Walker Rocks events page.
