Bryanna Fuquea headlines this Music on Main night on LaFayette Square. She is a North Georgia artist who blends country, bluegrass, rock, and R&B into a cross-genre performance style. Lone Cove opens the evening. Music on Main is a free live music series in downtown LaFayette, Georgia, organized by Walker Rocks. LaFayette is about 30 minutes from Chattanooga. The events take place on the downtown square with a stage set up for the evening. No ticket required. Check the Walker Rocks events page for start time and parking information.
