Bryanna Fuquea headlines this Music on Main night on LaFayette Square. She is a North Georgia artist who blends country, bluegrass, rock, and R&B into her own sound. Lone Cove joins the bill for the evening. The show runs three hours on the downtown square. Admission is free, so bring a chair and settle in.
