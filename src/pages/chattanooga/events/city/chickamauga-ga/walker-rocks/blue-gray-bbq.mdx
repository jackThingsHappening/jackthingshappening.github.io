The Blue Gray BBQ brings a full day of barbecue to the grounds of the Gordon Lee Mansion in Chickamauga. Check the Walker Rocks events page for entry and details. The event features barbecue competition alongside public tasting. The Gordon Lee Mansion is a National Historic Landmark that served as a field hospital during the Battle of Chickamauga. The name refers to the Blue and Gray of the Civil War. The event takes place in Chickamauga, Georgia, about 30 minutes from downtown Chattanooga. Local pitmasters compete across multiple barbecue categories.
