The Merry at the Mansion Market returns for its third year on Saturday, December 12, starting at 2 PM at 217 Cove Road in Chickamauga. The setting is a historic mansion property just south of the Chickamauga Battlefield. Local artisans and vendors set up for a holiday market in the grounds.

This type of event draws a mix of handmade gifts, seasonal decor, and food vendors. It is a solid option if you are already visiting the battlefield or other sites in Walker County. Chickamauga is about 20 minutes south of Chattanooga. Admission is free.
