Nolan Taylor plays Songbirds on September 10 at 7pm, with Jess Woodland opening. His songs put a voice to people who often go unheard. The room stays small, so the show feels personal. Taylor is a Nashville singer-songwriter whose Americana and folk music draws on direct, plainspoken storytelling. His songs address working-class themes with emotional honesty. Jess Woodland is a Chattanooga-area singer-songwriter who has opened at Songbirds multiple times. Songbirds is a small listening room on the North Shore. Check the venue for ticket details.
