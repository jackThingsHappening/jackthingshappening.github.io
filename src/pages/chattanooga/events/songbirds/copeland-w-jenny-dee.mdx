Copeland brings their Florida rock to Songbirds on September 11 at 7pm, with special guest Jenny Dee. The band has played together since 2001 and has six albums. Copeland plays indie rock and emo-influenced music built on piano, guitar, and Aaron Marsh's tenor voice. They have toured nationally and internationally and built a devoted following over 20 years. Jenny Dee is a Chattanooga-area singer-songwriter. Songbirds is a small listening room on the North Shore. Check the venue for ticket details.
