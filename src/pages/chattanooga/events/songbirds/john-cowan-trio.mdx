John Cowan - the New Grass Revival voice - brings his trio to Songbirds on September 4 at 7pm. Decades of high-lonesome harmony and bluegrass fire in a room small enough to feel it. Cowan was the bassist and vocalist for New Grass Revival from the band's formation in 1971. He has toured and recorded with Doobie Brothers, Tony Rice, and others in the acoustic music world. His voice is one of the most distinctive in bluegrass. Songbirds is a small listening room on the North Shore. Check the venue for ticket details.
