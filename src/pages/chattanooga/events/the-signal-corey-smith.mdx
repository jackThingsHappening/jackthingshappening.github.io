Corey Smith is a singer-songwriter from Jefferson, Georgia. He built a grassroots following by giving away music online and touring hard. His songs mix Southern rock, country, and plainspoken storytelling. A former teacher, he writes with a direct, conversational voice. Fans can expect favorites like "Twenty-One" and "If I Could Do It Again." The show starts at 8pm with doors at 6:30pm. Tickets are required.
