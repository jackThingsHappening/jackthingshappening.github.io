The Mocs host Gardner-Webb in a Southern Conference dual at Maclellan Gym. The Runnin' Bulldogs wrestle out of Boiling Springs, North Carolina. The dual starts at 7pm on Friday night. SoCon duals count toward conference standing and seeding for the league tournament. Maclellan Gym gives fans a close view of the action on the UTC campus on McCallie Avenue. Buy tickets at the door or through gomocs.com. The gym is smaller than the arena, so seats fill fast for conference matchups.
