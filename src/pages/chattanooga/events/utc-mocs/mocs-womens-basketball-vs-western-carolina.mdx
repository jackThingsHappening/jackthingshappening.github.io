The UTC Mocs women's basketball team hosts Western Carolina at McKenzie Arena. Western Carolina is based in Cullowhee, North Carolina and competes in the Southern Conference. Conference standings tighten through January and each home win matters. McKenzie Arena is on the UTC campus on McCallie Avenue and seats around 11,000. Tipoff is at 2pm. Tickets are available at gomocs.com or through the arena box office.
