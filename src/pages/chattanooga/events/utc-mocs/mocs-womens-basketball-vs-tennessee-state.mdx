The UTC Mocs women's basketball team hosts Tennessee State at McKenzie Arena. Tennessee State plays in the SWAC out of Nashville. The two in-state programs do not meet every season, making this a notable non-conference matchup. McKenzie Arena seats around 11,000 on the UTC campus on McCallie Avenue. Tipoff is at 6pm. Tickets are available at gomocs.com or through the arena box office.
