The Mocs host The Citadel at McKenzie Arena in a Southern Conference game. The Citadel Bulldogs play out of Charleston, South Carolina. Tipoff is at 7pm. Tickets are available at the arena box office or through gomocs.com. McKenzie Arena seats about 11,000 on the UTC campus on McCallie Avenue. The Citadel is a military college that competes in the SoCon. The matchup carries conference standing implications. Check the UTC athletics website for parking details and any game day promotions.
