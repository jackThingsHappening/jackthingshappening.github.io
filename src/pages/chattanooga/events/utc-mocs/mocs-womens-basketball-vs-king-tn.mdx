The UTC Mocs women's basketball team hosts King University at McKenzie Arena. King competes in NCAA Division II and plays out of Bristol, Tennessee. This early-season home game gives the Mocs a chance to build rhythm and test depth before conference play. McKenzie Arena sits on the UTC campus on McCallie Avenue and seats around 11,000. Tipoff is at 6pm. Tickets are available at gomocs.com or through the arena box office.
