The Mocs host the Furman Paladins at McKenzie Arena in a Southern Conference game. Furman is based in Greenville, South Carolina. Tipoff is at 7pm. Tickets are available at the arena box office or through gomocs.com. McKenzie Arena seats about 11,000 on the UTC campus on McCallie Avenue. Furman is one of the stronger programs in the Southern Conference and brings a competitive roster. The matchup carries SoCon standing implications. Check the UTC athletics website for parking details.
