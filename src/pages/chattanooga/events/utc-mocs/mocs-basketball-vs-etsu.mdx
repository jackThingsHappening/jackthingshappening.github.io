The Mocs host East Tennessee State at McKenzie Arena in a SoCon rivalry game. ETSU is based in Johnson City, Tennessee. The two schools have played for decades and the rivalry runs deep. McKenzie Arena seats about 11,000 on the UTC campus on McCallie Avenue. Tipoff is at 7pm. Tickets are available through gomocs.com or the arena box office. The Bucs and Mocs are both strong programs in the Southern Conference. SoCon games carry conference standing implications through the winter. This is one of the stronger home opponents on the schedule.
