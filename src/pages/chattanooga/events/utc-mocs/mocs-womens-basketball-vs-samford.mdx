The UTC Mocs women's basketball team hosts Samford at McKenzie Arena. Samford competes in the Southern Conference out of Birmingham, Alabama. This late-December home game falls during winter break but SoCon matchups carry conference standing weight. McKenzie Arena is on the UTC campus on McCallie Avenue. Tipoff is at 6pm. Tickets are available through gomocs.com or the arena box office.
