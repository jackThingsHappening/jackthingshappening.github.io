The Mocs host Alabama A&M at McKenzie Arena. Alabama A&M is an HBCU from Huntsville, Alabama, competing in the SWAC. Tipoff is at 7pm. Tickets are available at the arena box office or through gomocs.com. McKenzie Arena seats about 11,000 on the UTC campus on McCallie Avenue. This is a non-conference home game. Alabama A&M is about 90 miles from Chattanooga and may bring a contingent of fans. Check the UTC athletics website for parking details and any game day promotions.
