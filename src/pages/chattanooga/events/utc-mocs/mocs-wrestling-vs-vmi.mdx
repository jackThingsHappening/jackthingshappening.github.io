The Mocs host VMI in a Southern Conference dual at Maclellan Gym. The Keydets wrestle out of Lexington, Virginia. The dual starts at 7pm on Friday night. This match opens a home weekend that also features Campbell the next day. SoCon duals carry direct standing and seeding stakes late in the season. Maclellan Gym sits on the UTC campus on McCallie Avenue and puts fans right on top of the mat. Buy tickets at the door or through gomocs.com. Come early to get a seat for the conference dual.
