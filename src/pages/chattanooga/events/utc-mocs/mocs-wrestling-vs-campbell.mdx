The Mocs host Campbell in a Southern Conference dual at Maclellan Gym. The Fighting Camels wrestle out of Buies Creek, North Carolina. The dual starts at 7pm on Saturday night. This match closes a home weekend that opens with VMI on Friday. Campbell is a strong SoCon program and the result shapes conference seeding. Maclellan Gym sits on the UTC campus on McCallie Avenue. Buy tickets at the door or through gomocs.com. The gym seats fewer fans than the arena, so arrive early for a good spot.
