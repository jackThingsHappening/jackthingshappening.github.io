The Mocs host the Southern Scuffle at McKenzie Arena over two days. The event draws nationally ranked Division I wrestling programs from across the country. Mat action starts at 9am on Saturday, January 2. Medal matches run at 3pm on Sunday, January 3. Many of the nation's top wrestlers compete across all weight classes. McKenzie Arena sits on the UTC campus on McCallie Avenue. Buy tickets at the arena box office or through gomocs.com. Arrive early for parking, since the two-day tournament runs long sessions.
