The Mocs host Western Carolina at McKenzie Arena in Southern Conference play. Western Carolina is based in Cullowhee, North Carolina. Tipoff is at 7pm. Tickets are available through gomocs.com or the arena box office. McKenzie Arena seats about 11,000 on the UTC campus. Western Carolina competes in the SoCon and the matchup carries conference standing implications. Check the UTC athletics website for parking information and any game day promotions.
