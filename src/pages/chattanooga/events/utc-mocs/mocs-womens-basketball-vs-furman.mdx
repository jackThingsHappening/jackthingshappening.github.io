The UTC Mocs women's basketball team hosts Furman at McKenzie Arena. Furman is based in Greenville, South Carolina and competes in the Southern Conference. SoCon games matter for conference standings and postseason seeding. McKenzie Arena seats around 11,000 on the UTC campus on McCallie Avenue. Tipoff is at 6pm. Tickets are available at gomocs.com or through the arena box office.
