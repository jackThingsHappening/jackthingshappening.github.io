The UTC Mocs women's basketball team hosts Mercer at McKenzie Arena. Mercer competes in the Southern Conference out of Macon, Georgia. February SoCon games carry direct postseason implications and the standings can shift quickly late in the season. McKenzie Arena is on the UTC campus on McCallie Avenue and seats around 11,000. Tipoff is at 6pm. Tickets are available through gomocs.com or the arena box office.
