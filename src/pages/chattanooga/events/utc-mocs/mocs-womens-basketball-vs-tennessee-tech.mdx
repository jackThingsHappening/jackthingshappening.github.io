The UTC Mocs women's basketball team hosts Tennessee Tech at McKenzie Arena. Tennessee Tech plays in the ASUN Conference out of Cookeville, Tennessee. These in-state programs do not share a conference but the matchup draws fans from across the region. McKenzie Arena seats around 11,000 on the UTC campus on McCallie Avenue. Tipoff is at 2pm. Tickets are available at gomocs.com or through the arena box office.
