The Mocs host Mercer in a Southern Conference game at McKenzie Arena. The Mercer Bears play out of Macon, Georgia. Tipoff is at 7pm. Tickets are available at the arena box office or through gomocs.com. McKenzie Arena seats about 11,000 on the UTC campus on McCallie Avenue. Mercer competes in the Southern Conference and is one of the regular opponents the Mocs face in conference play. This December game comes as the SoCon schedule gets underway. Check the UTC athletics website for parking details and pregame events.
