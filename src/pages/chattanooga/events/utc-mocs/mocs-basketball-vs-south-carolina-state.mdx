The Mocs host South Carolina State at McKenzie Arena. SC State is an HBCU from Orangeburg, South Carolina, competing in the MEAC. Tipoff is at 7pm. Tickets are available at the arena box office or through gomocs.com. McKenzie Arena seats about 11,000 on the UTC campus on McCallie Avenue. South Carolina State fields a competitive Division I basketball program. This is a non-conference game in the early part of the season. It is a good opportunity to see the Mocs at home before Southern Conference play begins in January.
