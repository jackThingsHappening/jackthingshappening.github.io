The UTC Mocs women's basketball team hosts UNC Greensboro at McKenzie Arena. UNCG plays in the Southern Conference out of Greensboro, North Carolina. Weekend afternoon games at McKenzie Arena draw a mix of students and local fans. The arena is on the UTC campus on McCallie Avenue and seats around 11,000. Tipoff is at 2pm. Tickets are available through gomocs.com or the arena box office.
