The Mocs close out the Finley Stadium home schedule against Mercer. The Mercer Bears play Southern Conference football out of Macon, Georgia. This is the final home game of the regular season at Finley Stadium on Moccasin Bend Road. UTC plays FCS football as a member of the SoCon. Kickoff is at 2pm. Tickets are available through gomocs.com. Finley Stadium seats 20,490 fans. Parking is available around the stadium. This is a good opportunity to send the Mocs off in front of their home crowd before the postseason picture becomes clear.
