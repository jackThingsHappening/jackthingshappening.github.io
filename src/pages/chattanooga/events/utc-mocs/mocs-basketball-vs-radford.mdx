The Mocs host the Radford Highlanders at McKenzie Arena. Radford University plays in the Big South Conference out of Radford, Virginia. Tipoff is at 7pm. Tickets are available at the arena box office or through gomocs.com. McKenzie Arena seats about 11,000 on the UTC campus on McCallie Avenue. This is a non-conference home game. Radford brings a Division I program from the Big South. Check the UTC athletics website for parking details and any game day promotions.
