The UTC Mocs women's basketball team hosts Wofford at McKenzie Arena. Wofford plays in the Southern Conference out of Spartanburg, South Carolina. This late-February game comes near the end of the regular season, when every conference win affects tournament seeding. McKenzie Arena is on the UTC campus on McCallie Avenue and seats around 11,000. Tipoff is at 6pm. Tickets are available at gomocs.com or through the arena box office.
