The UTC Mocs women's basketball team hosts East Tennessee State at McKenzie Arena. ETSU is based in Johnson City, Tennessee and the two programs have competed in the Southern Conference for decades. In-state SoCon matchups tend to draw louder crowds and carry extra conference weight. McKenzie Arena is on the UTC campus on McCallie Avenue and seats around 11,000. Tipoff is at 2pm. Tickets are available through gomocs.com or the arena box office.
