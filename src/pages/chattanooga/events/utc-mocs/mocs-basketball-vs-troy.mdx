The Mocs host Troy on a Saturday afternoon at McKenzie Arena. Troy plays Sun Belt Conference basketball out of Troy, Alabama. Tipoff is at 2pm. Tickets are available at the arena box office or through gomocs.com. McKenzie Arena seats about 11,000 on the UTC campus on McCallie Avenue. This is a non-conference home game and a solid early-season test. Check the UTC athletics website for parking information and any game day promotions or events.
