The Mocs host Point University at McKenzie Arena. Point University is a Christian university from West Point, Georgia. Tipoff is at 7pm. Tickets are available at the arena box office or through gomocs.com. McKenzie Arena seats about 11,000 on the UTC campus on McCallie Avenue. This non-conference home game gives the Mocs a chance to build early momentum at home. Point University competes at the NAIA level. Check the UTC athletics website for parking details and any game day promotions.
