The Mocs open the 2026-27 home basketball season at McKenzie Arena. Covenant College is a small liberal arts school on Lookout Mountain, just outside Chattanooga. Tipoff is at 7pm. Tickets are available at the arena box office or through gomocs.com. McKenzie Arena seats about 11,000 on the UTC campus on McCallie Avenue. This season opener against a local opponent gives fans a first look at the new Mocs roster. Check the UTC athletics website for parking details and any opening night promotions.
