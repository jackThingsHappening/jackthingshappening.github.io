The UTC Mocs women's basketball team hosts Radford at McKenzie Arena. Radford plays in the Big South Conference out of Radford, Virginia. This mid-December game is part of a stretch of home matchups before the holiday break. McKenzie Arena is on the UTC campus on McCallie Avenue. Tipoff is at 5pm. Tickets are available through gomocs.com or the arena box office.
