The Mocs host Tennessee Tech at McKenzie Arena on Martin Luther King Jr. Day. Tennessee Tech is based in Cookeville, Tennessee. Tipoff is at 7pm. Tickets are available at the arena box office or through gomocs.com. McKenzie Arena seats about 11,000 on the UTC campus on McCallie Avenue. Tennessee Tech competes in the Ohio Valley Conference, making this a non-conference matchup. The Monday holiday game is a good family option when schools are closed. Check the UTC athletics website for parking details and any special programming for the holiday.
