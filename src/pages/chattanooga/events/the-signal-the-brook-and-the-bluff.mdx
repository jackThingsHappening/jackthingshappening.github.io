Birmingham alt-rock band The Brook & The Bluff play The Signal on October 5 behind Werewolf, their 2026 album on Dualtone Records. They build loud, melodic songs on a foundation of choir-like four-part harmonies and swamp-rock guitar — think Petty and Creedence filtered through the modern South. This is their high-voltage touring record. Music at 8pm.
