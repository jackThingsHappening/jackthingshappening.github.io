The Tennessee Aquarium sets up its fourth annual ofrenda from October 27 to November 2, 2026. An ofrenda is a memorial altar rooted in Day of the Dead tradition. This display honors aquarium animals that have passed and invites guests to remember beloved pets of their own. Staff add photos and small tokens to the altar. You can view the ofrenda during regular hours with standard admission.
