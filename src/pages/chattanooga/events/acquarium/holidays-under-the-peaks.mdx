Holidays Under the Peaks turns the Tennessee Aquarium into a winter wonderland from November 27 through December 27, 2026. The iconic glass peaks glow with candy cane lights above festive decor. An electric eel powers a twinkling holiday tree in one exhibit. Daily seasonal programs include Winter Adaptations at 12:15 pm and Let It Glow at 1:15 pm. The holiday celebration is included with regular Aquarium admission. Families can explore the exhibits amid holiday music and lights.
