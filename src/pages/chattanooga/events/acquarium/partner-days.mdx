Partner Days give employees of participating organizations a special deal at the Tennessee Aquarium. The offer runs September 10–30, 2026. Bring a workplace ID or pay stub to claim the discount. It applies to everyone in the paying party. Tickets cover the Tennessee Aquarium and the IMAX 3D Theater documentary films. Check the events page to see if your employer takes part.
