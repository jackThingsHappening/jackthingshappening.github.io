Member Night: Ooey, Gooey Science! is a members-only evening at the Tennessee Aquarium leaning into the messy, hands-on side of science. Expect gooey, tactile experiments and stations the kids can dig into after the usual daytime crowds have cleared out. It's a Tennessee Aquarium membership perk, so check the events page for details and to sign up.
