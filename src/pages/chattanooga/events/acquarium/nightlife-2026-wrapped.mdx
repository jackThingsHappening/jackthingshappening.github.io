The Tennessee Aquarium wraps up 2026 with its final Nightlife event of the year. The series turns the aquarium galleries into an adults-only evening venue with live music, local vendors, and food and drink options. Past events have drawn a mix of locals and visitors looking for something different on a Friday night.

This one carries a year-end theme, so expect a celebratory atmosphere. Tickets run $40 for members and $45 for non-members. The events typically run 6–9 PM. You must be 21 or older to attend.

Parking is available in the downtown Chattanooga garages near the aquarium. The aquarium entrance on Broad Street is easy to find from the riverfront.
