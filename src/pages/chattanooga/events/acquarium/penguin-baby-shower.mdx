The Tennessee Aquarium is throwing a Penguin Baby Shower for two new Gentoo penguin chicks. The chicks hatched weeks apart in the summer of 2026, on June 25 and July 17. This event celebrates them before they receive their names. A public naming auction opens on September 15, so visitors can bid on naming rights and animal-made art. Come meet the aquarium's newest floofy arrivals.
