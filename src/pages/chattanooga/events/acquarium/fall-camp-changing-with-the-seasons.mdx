Changing with the Seasons is the Tennessee Aquarium's fall camp for kids, running October 12-21, 2026. The week focuses on seasonal changes in nature and wildlife. Each day covers a different animal or ecosystem. The camp runs full days from 8:30am to 4pm. It is designed for children ages 6 to 12. Tennessee Aquarium camps combine animal encounters, educator-led lessons, and hands-on activities. Registration is required through the aquarium website. Space is limited and sessions fill early. The aquarium is on Broad Street at the downtown Chattanooga riverfront.
