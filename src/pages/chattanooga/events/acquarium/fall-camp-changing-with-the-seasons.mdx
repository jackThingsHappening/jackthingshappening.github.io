Changing with the Seasons is the Tennessee Aquarium's fall camp for kids, running October 12–21, 2026. The week focuses on seasonal changes in nature and wildlife — expect hands-on activities, time with live animals, and exploration inside both aquarium buildings. Registration details are on the Aquarium's events page.
