The Polar Express returns in 3D to the Tennessee Aquarium IMAX theater this holiday season. Daily showings run from November 27 through December 27, 2026. The film follows a boy's train ride to the North Pole on Christmas Eve. The giant IMAX screen puts you inside the snowy journey. Staff recommend you wear pajamas and bring a blanket for the show. An IMAX ticket is required to attend.
