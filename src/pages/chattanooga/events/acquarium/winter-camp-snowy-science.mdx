Snowy Science is a two-day winter camp at the Tennessee Aquarium for kids ages 5 to 12. Campers run frosty experiments and explore the science behind cold-weather conditions. They also tour the aquarium to find animals with winter adaptations. Each day includes an up-close animal encounter. The camp runs January 4 and 5, 2027, from 9:00 am to 3:30 pm.
