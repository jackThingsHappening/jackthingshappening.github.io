Saint Nicholas trades his sleigh for scuba gear during Holidays Under the Peaks. SCUBA Claus dives into the River Journey exhibits each weekend from November 28 through December 20, 2026. He waves to guests from underwater at 2:00 pm on Saturdays and Sundays. The dive rotates between different exhibits across the holiday weekends. Kids can watch Santa swim among fish and other aquarium animals. The dives are included with regular Aquarium admission.
