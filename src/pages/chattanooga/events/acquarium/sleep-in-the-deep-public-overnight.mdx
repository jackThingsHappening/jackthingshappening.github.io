Sleep in the Deep lets guests spend the night inside the Tennessee Aquarium on December 11, 2026. Guests explore both the River Journey and Ocean Journey buildings after hours with no crowds. The overnight includes a late-night snack and breakfast. You get a behind-the-scenes look at how the aquarium cares for its animals. Guests must be 6 or older, and anyone under 18 needs an adult. Standard tickets cost $75 and VIP tickets cost $95. This date is sold out.
