Tennessee River Rescue is the longest-running clean-up effort in the Tennessee River basin. Volunteers gather each fall to remove trash from the river and its tributaries. The event spans more than twenty sites across four counties. Freshwater scientists from the Tennessee Aquarium Conservation Institute join the effort. Anyone can sign up to help clean the water and protect local wildlife. Registration details are on the Aquarium's events page.
