Aquascarium is the Tennessee Aquarium's yearly trick-or-treat night. The event brings scaly, spooky, family-friendly fun to the galleries. Themed decorations fill the halls. Costumed SCUBA divers swim in the tanks. Pirates, lemurs, and mad scientists hand out candy and share aquatic facts. Music plays throughout the night. Children must stay with an adult. This event often sells out, so buy tickets early.
