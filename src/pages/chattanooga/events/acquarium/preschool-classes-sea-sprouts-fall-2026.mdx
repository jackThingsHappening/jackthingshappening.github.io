Sea Sprouts is the Tennessee Aquarium's fall preschool class series for young kids. Sessions run from September 18 through November 20, 2026, with hands-on activities focused on ocean and river life. The classes are designed for children ages 3 to 5. Each session runs about an hour. The aquarium is on Broad Street at the downtown Chattanooga riverfront. Registration is required through the aquarium website. Each session covers a different aquatic theme. Check the website for cost, session dates, and registration details. This is a popular program - register early to secure your spot.
