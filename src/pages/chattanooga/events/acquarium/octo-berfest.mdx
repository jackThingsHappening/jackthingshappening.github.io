Octo-berfest blends Oktoberfest customs with a tribute to the octopus. This 21+ event runs after hours inside the Ocean Journey building. Guests sip Oktoberfest-style drinks and eat a German pretzel while they tour the galleries. Fall decor fills the space for the night. Bring a valid government-issued photo ID to enter. The event often sells out, so buy tickets in advance.
