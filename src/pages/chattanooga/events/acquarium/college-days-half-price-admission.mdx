College Days bring half-price admission to the Tennessee Aquarium for students. The offer runs September 10–30, 2026. Show a valid college ID at the door to get the discount. Explore both aquarium buildings, from the river to the ocean, at a lower price. It is a good chance for students to see live animals and exhibits on a budget. Details are on the Aquarium's events page.
