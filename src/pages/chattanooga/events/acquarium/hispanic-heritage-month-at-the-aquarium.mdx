The Tennessee Aquarium marks Hispanic Heritage Month from September 15 to October 15. The observance honors the culture, history, and contributions of Latino communities. The Aquarium ties the celebration to its exhibits on rivers and species from across the Americas. Visitors can explore galleries during regular hours throughout the month. Check the Aquarium's events page for any special programs or dates tied to the observance. Admission covers access to the full building.
