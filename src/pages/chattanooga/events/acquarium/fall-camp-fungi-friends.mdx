Fungi Friends is a one-day fall camp at the Tennessee Aquarium on October 19, 2026. Kids dig into the world of fungi - decomposition, mycelium, and the role mushrooms play in forest ecosystems. The camp runs from 8:30am to 4pm and is designed for children ages 6 to 12. Tennessee Aquarium camps combine classroom learning with hands-on activities. Registration is required through the aquarium website. Space is limited. The aquarium is on Broad Street at the downtown Chattanooga riverfront. Check the website for the specific age range, cost, and registration deadline.
