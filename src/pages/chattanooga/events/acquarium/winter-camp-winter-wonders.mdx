Winter Wonders is a holiday-week camp at the Tennessee Aquarium for kids ages 5 to 12. Campers learn how animals adapt to cold weather and winter environments. The week includes guided tours, winter crafts, and close encounters with ambassador animals. Campers also help with enrichment activities for the aquarium's animals. The camp runs December 28, 2026 through January 1, 2027, from 9:00 am to 3:30 pm each day.
