Jose Hairston is a rising Chattanooga saxophonist with a strong local following. He leads a quartet on guitar, bass, and drums for this midweek Wednesday Jazz set. The band plays warm, straight-ahead jazz in the Barking Legs lounge. Music runs from 7:30 to 9:30. Tickets are $10 in advance or $12 at the door.
