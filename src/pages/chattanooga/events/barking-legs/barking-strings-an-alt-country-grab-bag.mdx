Barking Strings is a monthly acoustic series at Barking Legs Theater. This night gathers local musicians for an alt-country grab bag. They pull from Ryan Adams, Jason Isbell, Sturgill Simpson, the Jayhawks, and Jackson Browne. The sound stays loose, warm, and rooted in roots rock and country songwriting. There is no cover charge, but the tip jar supports the performers.
