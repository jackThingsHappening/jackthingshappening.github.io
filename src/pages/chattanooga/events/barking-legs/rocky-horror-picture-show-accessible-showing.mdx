Seed Theatre stages a neurodivergent-friendly Rocky Horror Picture Show. The team adjusts lighting, sound, and audience interaction for comfort. ASL interpreters sign the full performance. This showing skips prop bags to keep everyone safe. The show runs at $30 general admission on the Dodds Avenue stage.
