Seed Theatre stages a neurodivergent-friendly Rocky Horror Picture Show. The team adjusts lighting, sound, and audience expectations to make the experience accessible to sensory-sensitive attendees. The classic 1975 film plays with live shadowcast performance. Audience props are available but participation is never required. Barking Legs Theater on Dodds Avenue seats about 100. Check the venue website for ticket details and start time. This showing is intended for anyone who wants the full Rocky Horror experience in a lower-stimulation environment. It runs before the Halloween weekend performances.
