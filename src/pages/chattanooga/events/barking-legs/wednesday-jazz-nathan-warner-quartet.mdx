Trumpeter Nathan Warner leads his quartet for this Wednesday Jazz set. Warner moved to Chattanooga from New York City and plays with regional symphonies. His range runs from screaming lead lines to tender ballads. The quartet features Shawn Perkinson on guitar, David Schwab on bass, and Matt Turnure on drums. Music runs from 7:30 to 9:30.
