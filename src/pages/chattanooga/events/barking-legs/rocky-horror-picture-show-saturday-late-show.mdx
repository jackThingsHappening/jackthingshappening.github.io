Seed Theatre closes Saturday with a late-night Rocky Horror Picture Show. The cast shadowcasts the film live in full Broadway-style costumes and choreography. Audience props bags are sold at the door. The late show draws the most participatory crowd of the Halloween run. Barking Legs Theater on Dodds Avenue seats about 100. Check the venue website for ticket details and start time. This is the rowdiest edition of Seed Theatre's Halloween season - arrive ready to participate. The cast expects and encourages audience interaction throughout the film.
