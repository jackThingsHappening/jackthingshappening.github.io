Jason B. Schmidt performs Call Me Ursula at Barking Legs Theater. The musical digs into Disney's Sea Witch and her origins. Schmidt sings cut songs that never reached the screen. He also traces the casting choices behind the character. The show plays on the Dodds Avenue stage.
