Jason B. Schmidt performs Call Me Ursula at Barking Legs Theater. The musical digs into Disney's Sea Witch and her origin story. Schmidt writes and performs this one-person show that reframes the villain from The Little Mermaid. The production combines original songs with monologue and theatrical character work. Seed Theatre produces original and experimental theater at Barking Legs on Dodds Avenue. Check the venue website for ticket details and performance dates. The show runs multiple evenings. It is part of Seed Theatre's ongoing commitment to new and boundary-pushing work in Chattanooga.
