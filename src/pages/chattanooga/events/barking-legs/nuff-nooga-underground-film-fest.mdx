The Nooga Underground Film Fest celebrates counterculture and cult cinema. NUFF screens films you rarely find elsewhere. The lineup leans into horror, exploitation, extreme violence, and crime thrillers. The festival runs across two days with film blocks, filmmaker Q&As, and craft beer. It also folds in special programming such as a comedy show. Doors open at noon each day and screenings continue into the evening.
