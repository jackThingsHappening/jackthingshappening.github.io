This party closes out the four-year Cultural Cross Ties program. The project built creative exchanges with Chattanooga's Sister Cities. Partner cities include Givatayim in Israel, Hamm in Germany, Tono in Japan, and Accra in Ghana. The night screens all the films made through the program. Come see the work and meet the artists behind it. The screening starts at 6:00 on Dodds Avenue.
