One Hit Wonders packs a set with the songs everyone half remembers. The band plays them back to back at Barking Legs on a Friday. The concept is simple and works every time - the room lights up for songs that defined a year and then disappeared. The band covers decades of one-hit wonders from pop, rock, and R&B. Barking Legs Theater on Dodds Avenue seats about 100. Check the venue website for ticket details and start time. This is an easy, fun night out that works for a wide range of music fans.
