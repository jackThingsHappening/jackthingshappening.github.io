The format asks the audience to settle in and pay close attention to the music. The artist or artists performing shift with each edition of the event. Check the Barking Legs website for the current lineup and ticket details. The room seats about 100 people in an intimate arrangement. This is a rare format for Chattanooga - a performance environment built entirely around attentive, focused listening.
