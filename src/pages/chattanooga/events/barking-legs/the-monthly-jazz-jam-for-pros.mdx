The Monthly Jazz Jam brings working pros to the Barking Legs lounge on Dodds Avenue. A rotating house trio anchors the night. Players sit in and swap standards across the set. The music runs from 7:30 to 9:30. Tickets are $10 in advance or $12 at the door.
