The Alan Wyatt Quartet returns to the Barking Legs lounge for Wednesday jazz. Settle in near the band on Dodds Avenue for an evening of jazz standards and original compositions. Wyatt is a Chattanooga-based pianist and bandleader who has performed in the region for years. The quartet format builds on the interplay between piano, bass, drums, and a horn or guitar. Barking Legs Theater on Dodds Avenue is an intimate jazz venue that seats about 100. Suggested donation at the door. This is a weekly series - arrive early for a table close to the stage.
