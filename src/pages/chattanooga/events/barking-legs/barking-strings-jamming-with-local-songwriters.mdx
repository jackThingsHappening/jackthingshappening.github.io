Barking Strings is a monthly acoustic collaboration at Barking Legs Theater. Rotating local musicians jam with songwriters Steve Smith and Zach Suede. The format stays loose and open, so sets shift from month to month. There is no cover charge, but a tip jar supports the players. Music runs from 7:30 to 9:30 in the lounge.
