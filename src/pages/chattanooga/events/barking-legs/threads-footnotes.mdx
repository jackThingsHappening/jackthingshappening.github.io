Threads: Footnotes premieres a new skateboarding video at Barking Legs Theater. The film features local skaters from across the Chattanooga area. Footage was shot at parks and street spots in and around the city over the past year. The premiere includes a live event with the filmmakers and skaters present. Barking Legs on Dodds Avenue provides the screening space. Check the Barking Legs website for ticket details and start time. This is a celebration of the local skate community and a document of the streets and parks they use.
