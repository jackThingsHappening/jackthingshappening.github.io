Seed Theatre stages Tennessee Williams' Cat on a Hot Tin Roof at Barking Legs. Director Mixy Mixon reads the play through the lens of family silence, desire, and truth-telling. The production features Seed Theatre's core ensemble. Cat on a Hot Tin Roof is a 1955 Pulitzer Prize-winning drama set on a Mississippi plantation during a birthday celebration. The play centers on Brick, Maggie, and Big Daddy as long-buried family tensions explode over one night. Barking Legs Theater on Dodds Avenue seats about 100.
