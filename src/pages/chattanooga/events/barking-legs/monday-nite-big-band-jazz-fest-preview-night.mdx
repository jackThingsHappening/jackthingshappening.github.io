The Chattanooga Monday Nite Big Band previews its set for the Second Annual Chattanooga Jazz Fest. The full ensemble plays before the festival opens. This preview night gives fans a close look at the band before the main event. The Monday Nite Big Band is a Chattanooga institution that has played weekly since the 1970s. The ensemble covers the classic big band repertoire including swing, bebop, and jazz standards. Barking Legs Theater on Dodds Avenue is a close, intimate venue for a 17-piece ensemble. Check the venue website for ticket details and start time.
