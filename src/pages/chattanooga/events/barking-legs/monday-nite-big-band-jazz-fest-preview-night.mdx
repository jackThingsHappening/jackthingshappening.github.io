The Chattanooga Monday Nite Big Band previews its set for the Second Annual Chattanooga Jazz Fest. The full ensemble plays live on the Dodds Avenue stage. Vocalist Paige Denton joins the band for the night. The music celebrates local jazz talent and runs from 7:30 to 9:30.
