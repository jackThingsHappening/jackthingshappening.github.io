Josh Couts is a Huntsville-based guitarist with a warm, agile touch. He leads a quintet for this special Wednesday Jazz set. His playing draws on bebop roots and Brazilian classical music. The blend gives the set both drive and delicate melody. Music runs from 7:30 to 9:30 in the Barking Legs lounge. Advance tickets are available online.
