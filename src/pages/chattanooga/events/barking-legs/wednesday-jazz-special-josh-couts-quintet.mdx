Huntsville guitarist Josh Couts leads his quintet on the Barking Legs stage. Couts teaches jazz at the University of Alabama in Huntsville and performs across the Southeast. The quintet format gives the music room to breathe with interlocking parts across guitar, bass, drums, and horns. This is a special edition of the weekly Wednesday Jazz series. Barking Legs Theater on Dodds Avenue is an intimate venue where jazz sounds close and detailed. Suggested donation at the door. The room holds about 100 people. Arrive early to get a good seat near the stage.
