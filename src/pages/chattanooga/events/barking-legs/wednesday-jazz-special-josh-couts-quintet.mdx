Huntsville guitarist Josh Couts leads his quintet on the Barking Legs stage. Couts teaches jazz at the University of Alabama in Huntsville. His playing leans on bebop and hard bop roots. The band includes Gary Wheat, Choko Aiken, Nick Walker, and CJ Brown. Doors open at 7:00pm.
