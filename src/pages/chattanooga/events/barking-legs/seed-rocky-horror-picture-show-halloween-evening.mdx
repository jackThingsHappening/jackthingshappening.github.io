Seed Theatre marks Halloween night with a live Rocky Horror Picture Show. The cast shadowcasts the cult classic in full Broadway-style costumes and choreography. Audience props bags are sold at the door. This is the evening performance on Halloween night - a separate late show follows later. Barking Legs Theater on Dodds Avenue seats about 100. Check the venue website for ticket details and start time. This performance marks the peak of Seed Theatre's Halloween season, which includes multiple Rocky Horror runs across the final week of October.
