The Silent Light plays live heavy metal beneath two classic silent films. The program pairs Faust with A Page of Madness, two expressionist films from the 1920s. The band performs original metal compositions timed to the films in a live synchronized performance. This is an unusual and genuinely compelling art event at Barking Legs Theater on Dodds Avenue. The combination of early film and live heavy metal creates a contrast that works in a 100-seat intimate venue. Check the Barking Legs website for ticket details and start time.
