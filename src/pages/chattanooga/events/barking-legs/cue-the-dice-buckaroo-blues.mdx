Cue the Dice blends tabletop roleplaying with live improvised comedy. Buckaroo Blues is part sci-fi adventure and part stage show. Expect bounty hunters, system failures, and plenty of questionable dice rolls. The cast builds the story live, so no two shows play the same. The afternoon session runs from 3:00 to 4:00.
