Seed Theatre hosts a drag benefit show to raise money for its productions. River Abrasax hosts a night of drag performances from local artists. Proceeds go directly to Seed Theatre's production budget for upcoming shows. The company produces original and experimental theater at Barking Legs Theater on Dodds Avenue. Check the venue website for ticket details and start time. Seed Theatre has become a consistent force in Chattanooga's independent theater scene. This benefit gives audiences a fun night out while directly supporting new work in the city.
