Seed Theatre returns for a Saturday evening Rocky Horror Picture Show. The cast performs the cult classic live beside the film in full shadowcast style. Audience props bags are sold at the door. This is the evening show - the late show runs later the same night. Barking Legs Theater on Dodds Avenue seats about 100. Check the venue website for ticket details and start time. The Saturday evening show is typically the most family-accessible time slot in the Halloween run. The late show on the same night draws a different, more raucous crowd.
