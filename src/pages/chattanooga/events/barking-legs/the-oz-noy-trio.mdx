Guitarist Oz Noy brings his trio to Barking Legs for a special Wednesday jazz night. His playing bends jazz toward funk and rock, with unusual chord choices and percussive attack. Noy is an Israeli-born New York guitarist who has played with Dave Weckl, Chris Potter, and others in the jazz and fusion world. His studio recordings blend jazz, funk, and blues into a distinctive solo sound. The trio format keeps the focus on his guitar while bass and drums provide the rhythmic foundation. Barking Legs on Dodds Avenue is an intimate space for this kind of guitar-centric jazz. Check the venue for ticket details.
