Dukes Up presents a benefit concert built around heart transplant recipients and their stories. The evening features vocal performances, instrumental sets, and personal testimony from transplant recipients. Dukes Up is a Chattanooga-area organization that supports heart transplant awareness and patient resources. The event raises funds and builds community around the cause. Barking Legs Theater on Dodds Avenue is an intimate venue well suited to this kind of personal, story-driven evening. Check the venue website for ticket details and the full program lineup. Expect an emotional and uplifting event.
