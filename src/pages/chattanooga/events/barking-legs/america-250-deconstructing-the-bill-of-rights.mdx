Local artists take apart the Bill of Rights for this America 250 program. Dancer Ann Law leads the movement side of the show. Poets Marcus Patrick-Ellsworth and Christian Collier bring spoken word. Together they read the founding text through dance and verse. The night asks what these rights mean today. The performance starts at 7:00 on Dodds Avenue. Seating is limited, so arrive early.
