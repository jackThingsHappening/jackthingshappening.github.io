New Threads is a staged reading of new theatrical work at Barking Legs Theater. Spit in Your Face is witty, provocative, and honest. The play asks who gets to tell whose stories and at what cost. A staged reading keeps the focus on the script with a minimal set. The reading starts at 7:00 and runs about an hour. See the theater website for tickets.
