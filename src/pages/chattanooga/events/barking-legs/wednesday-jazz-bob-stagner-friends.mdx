Percussionist Bob Stagner leads a quartet in the Barking Legs lounge for Wednesday jazz. Stagner is a longtime fixture of the Chattanooga music scene. His friends fill out a loose, inventive set on Dodds Avenue. The music runs from 7:30 to 9:30. Tickets are available at the door.
