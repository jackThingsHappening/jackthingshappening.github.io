Frankie Gavin plays traditional Irish fiddle on the Barking Legs main stage. Gavin founded the celebrated band De Dannan in 1975 and has been a central figure in Irish traditional music for over 50 years. He plays fiddle and flute and has recorded with artists including Stephane Grappelli and Yehudi Menuhin. His playing sits in the Connaught style of the west of Ireland. Barking Legs Theater on Dodds Avenue is an intimate space where traditional Irish music carries with full detail. Check the venue website for ticket details and start time.
