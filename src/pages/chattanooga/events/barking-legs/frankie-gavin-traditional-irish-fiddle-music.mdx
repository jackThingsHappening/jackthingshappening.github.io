Frankie Gavin plays traditional Irish fiddle on the Barking Legs main stage. Gavin founded the celebrated band De Dannan in 1973. He ranks among the most renowned living Irish musicians. His fiery, fast playing draws on old masters of the form. A guest musician joins him for the evening. The set runs from 7:30 to 9:30.
