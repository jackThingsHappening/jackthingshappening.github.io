Flamy Grant is a drag Americana singer and songwriter from Asheville. She blends folk, gospel, and roots into theatrical, personal storytelling. Her debut album topped the iTunes Christian chart in 2023. Randy Steele opens the night on Dodds Avenue. The music runs from 7:30 to 9:30. Tickets are available through Barking Legs.
