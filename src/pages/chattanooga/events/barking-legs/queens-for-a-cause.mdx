Queens for a Cause is a drag benefit at Barking Legs Theater. Performers take the Dodds Avenue stage to raise money for a Chattanooga nonprofit or community cause. The specific beneficiary changes each edition. Local drag artists perform sets across the evening while the audience shows support through ticket purchases and tips. Barking Legs is an intimate venue of about 100 seats. Check the venue website for the current beneficiary, performing artists, and ticket details. This is one of several community-benefit drag shows that Barking Legs hosts through the year.
