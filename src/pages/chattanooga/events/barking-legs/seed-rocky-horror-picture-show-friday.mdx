Seed Theatre opens Halloween weekend with a Friday Rocky Horror Picture Show. The cast performs the film live in full Broadway shadowcast costumes and choreography. Audience props bags are sold at the door. This is the first of multiple performances across the Halloween weekend. Barking Legs Theater on Dodds Avenue seats about 100. Check the venue website for ticket details and start time. Friday's show is usually the first to sell out as fans plan their Halloween weekend around the Seed Theatre run.
