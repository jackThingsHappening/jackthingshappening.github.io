Crys Matthews is a Nashville singer-songwriter and award-winning lyricist. She blends Country, Americana, Folk, Blues, and Bluegrass into a bold sound. Her lyrics tackle social justice issues with honesty and craft. Her title track Changemakers won Song of the Year at the 2022 International Folk Music Awards. The show runs from 7:30 to 10:00.
