Ben Stephens is a Chattanooga pianist and songwriter. His music mixes funk, psychedelic sounds, and playful lyrics. He draws on the storytelling style of Elton John, Billy Joel, Randy Newman, and Ben Folds. The show starts at 9:00 PM in the lounge at Barking Legs Theater. Cover is $5 at the door. Stephens plays piano-driven songs from his own catalog, including work from his album "Hello World, This Is The Inside Of My Mind." Expect a loose, late-night set built around keys and sharp lyrics.
