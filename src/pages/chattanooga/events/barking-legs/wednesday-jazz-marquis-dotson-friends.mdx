Marquis Dotson steps up as band leader for this Wednesday Jazz set. Dotson is a mainstay on keys in the Chattanooga jazz scene. He has anchored Wednesday Jazz at Barking Legs for years and released his album Keybridge in 2017. The performance moves to the lounge for the night. Music runs from 7:30 to 9:30.
