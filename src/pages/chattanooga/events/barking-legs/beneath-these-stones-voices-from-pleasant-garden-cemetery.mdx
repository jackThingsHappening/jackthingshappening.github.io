Original poetic monologues bring the people buried at Pleasant Garden Cemetery back to life. The historic African American cemetery in Chattanooga holds the remains of community members spanning generations. The performers write and deliver original dramatic monologues in first person, voice by voice. The production runs at Barking Legs Theater on Dodds Avenue. Check the Barking Legs website for ticket details and start time. This is a thoughtful, literary performance that blends theater, history, and community memory into one evening. Seating is limited at the intimate 100-seat venue.
