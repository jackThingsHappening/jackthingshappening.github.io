Seed Theatre stages Larissa FastHorse's sharp satire. FastHorse is the first Native American playwright with one of the most produced plays in America. Four well-meaning white artists try to build a culturally sensitive Thanksgiving play for schoolchildren. Their good intentions fall apart fast. The comedy skewers performative activism and liberal guilt. Doors and seating are limited, so arrive early for this run.
