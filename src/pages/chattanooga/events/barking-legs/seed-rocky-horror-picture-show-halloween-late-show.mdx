Seed Theatre closes Halloween with a late-night Rocky Horror Picture Show. The cast performs the film live in full Broadway shadowcast style - costumes, choreography, and crowd interaction. Audience props bags are sold at the door. The late show runs after the earlier evening performance. Barking Legs Theater on Dodds Avenue seats about 100. Check the venue website for ticket details. The late show traditionally draws the most enthusiastic audience of the Halloween run. This is the final performance of Seed Theatre's Rocky Horror season.
