Seed Theatre performs The Rocky Horror Picture Show live beside the film. This Broadway-style shadowcast adds costumes, choreography, lighting, and staging. Your ticket includes a prop bag for the interactive moments. The show runs 18 and up. General admission is $30 on the Dodds Avenue stage.
