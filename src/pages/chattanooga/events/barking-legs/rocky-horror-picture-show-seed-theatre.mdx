Seed Theatre performs The Rocky Horror Picture Show live beside the film. This Broadway-style shadowcast adds costumes, choreography, and crowd participation to the classic 1975 cult film. Audience props bags are sold at the door. The cast performs every move while the original film plays on screen. This is an early show - the late show on the same night is a separate event. Barking Legs Theater on Dodds Avenue seats about 100. Check the venue website for ticket details. This Halloween season run includes multiple performances across the week.
