Fiddler Haley Richardson is a multiple All-Ireland champion. She learned the lilting Sligo style from master Brian Conway and has toured with Riverdance. Guitarist Don Penzien backs her with driving DADGAD accompaniment. The pair have played together since 2017. Expect fast jigs and reels alongside slow, haunting airs. The music runs from 7:30 to 9:30. Buy advance tickets to be sure of a seat.
