Eric Vaughn is a high-energy pianist with a spry, joyful touch at the keys. He leads a trio with bass and drums for this Wednesday Jazz set. His playing blends swinging standards with his own bright compositions. Music runs from 7:30 to 9:30 in the Barking Legs lounge. Tickets are $10 in advance or $12 at the door.
