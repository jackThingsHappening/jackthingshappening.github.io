Sfyria Trio plays pure free improvisation. The band pairs saxophone, bass, and drums into moody, textured sound. They build music from small motifs and shifting timbres. The Chatt-Town Unsound Collective improvises dance alongside the music. Nothing is scored, so every moment unfolds live. The two groups follow a free, dreamlike logic together. Music runs from 7:30 to 9:30. Advance tickets are available.
