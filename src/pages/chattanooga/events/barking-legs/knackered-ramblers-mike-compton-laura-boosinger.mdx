The Knackered Ramblers pair mandolinist Mike Compton with singer Laura Boosinger. Their set blends traditional country, bluegrass, old-time, and shaped-note hymns. Compton won a Grammy and learned directly from Bill Monroe. Boosinger champions shaped-note singing and plays clawhammer banjo. Both are seasoned touring artists. The music runs from 7:30 to 9:30.
