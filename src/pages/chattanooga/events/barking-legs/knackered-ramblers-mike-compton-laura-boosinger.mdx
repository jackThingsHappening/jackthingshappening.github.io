The Knackered Ramblers pair mandolinist Mike Compton with singer Laura Boosinger. Their set blends traditional country, old-time, and early bluegrass. Compton is widely regarded as one of the finest traditional mandolinists working today, with a career rooted in Nashville and deep old-time repertoire. Boosinger is an Asheville-based singer and old-time fiddler with a strong connection to Appalachian vocal traditions. Together they present pre-commercial country and folk music in a close, seated listening format. Barking Legs Theater on Dodds Avenue is well suited to this kind of traditional acoustic performance.
