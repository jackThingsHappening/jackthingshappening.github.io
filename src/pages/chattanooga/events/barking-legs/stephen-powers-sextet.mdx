Guitarist Stephen Powers leads a strong new generation of Chattanooga jazz players. His sextet features saxophonists Grady Dickinson and Matt Roehrich up front. A full rhythm section drives the band behind them. Powers is known for sharp technique and steady growth as a player. The group plays regularly around the city. Music runs from 7:30 to 9:30 on Dodds Avenue. Advance tickets are available.
