The Chattanooga Monday Night Big Band returns for a holiday show. Vocalist Paige Denton joins the band for this set. The full horn section swings through Christmas standards and big band classics. These players carry decades of experience on the Barking Legs stage. Expect a warm, festive night of live jazz. Music runs from 7:30 to 9:30 on Dodds Avenue.
