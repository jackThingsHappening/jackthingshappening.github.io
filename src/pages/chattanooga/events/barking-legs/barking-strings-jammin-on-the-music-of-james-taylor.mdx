Barking Strings is a monthly acoustic collaboration at Barking Legs Theater. This edition celebrates the songs of James Taylor. Musicians from across the Chattanooga area join for an informal set of Taylor's catalog. Songs include Fire and Rain, Carolina in My Mind, and You've Got a Friend. The Barking Strings format encourages participation and collaboration rather than a polished performance. The event takes place at Barking Legs Theater on Dodds Avenue. Check the venue website for ticket details and start time. Bring your instrument if you want to sit in.
