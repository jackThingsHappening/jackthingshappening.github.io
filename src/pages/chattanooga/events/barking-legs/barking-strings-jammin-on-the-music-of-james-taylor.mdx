Barking Strings is a monthly acoustic collaboration at Barking Legs Theater. This edition celebrates the songs of James Taylor. Local musicians gather to play folk and Americana favorites. The audience is welcome to sing, play along, or just listen. Music starts at 7:30 in the lounge. Grab advance tickets online through the theater.
