Walk the woods with a guide and learn to name the trees around you. Staff point out leaves, bark, and shape so you can tell a white oak from a red oak, or a beech from a hornbeam. The Woodland Walk runs on the trail system at Reflection Riding Arboretum and Nature Center on Garden Road. The event is free for members and low cost for non-members. Check the website for registration and start time. Wear comfortable shoes and bring water. The walk moves at a relaxed pace suited to all fitness levels. It runs about 90 minutes to two hours.
