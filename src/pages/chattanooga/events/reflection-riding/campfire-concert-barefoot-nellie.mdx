Spread a blanket and settle in for live music under the open sky. Barefoot Nellie plays as the sun drops behind the ridge at Reflection Riding. Barefoot Nellie is a Southern folk and roots music project known for warm harmonies and acoustic arrangements. The campfire concert series runs outdoors at the nature center on Garden Road. Admission is charged - check the Reflection Riding website for ticket details. Food and drinks are available on site. Bring layers for the evening temperatures. The nature center grounds provide a natural amphitheater setting with the forest as the backdrop.
