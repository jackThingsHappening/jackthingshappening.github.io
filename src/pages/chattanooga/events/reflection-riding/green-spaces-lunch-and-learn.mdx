Bring your lunch and learn how Reflection Riding cares for its land. Staff share the site's mission and the daily work behind maintaining the arboretum, wildlife habitats, and educational programs. Green|Spaces leads this midday session on sustainable land stewardship. The event is free or low cost. Check the Reflection Riding website for registration details. The nature center is located on Garden Road at the base of Lookout Mountain. This is a good event for anyone curious about native plant conservation, wildlife corridors, or the practical work of running a nature center in an urban setting.
