Bring your dog and hit the trails together. This race day lets you run with your four-legged friend across the grounds of Reflection Riding. The event pairs trail running with a post-race beer from a local brewery. Dogs run leashed alongside their owners. Entry fees benefit the nature center. Distances vary - check the Reflection Riding website for race options and registration. Trail shoes and water for both you and your dog are essential. The nature center trails wind through forest and open ground on Garden Road near Lookout Mountain.
