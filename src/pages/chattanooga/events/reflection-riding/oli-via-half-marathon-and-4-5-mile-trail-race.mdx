Choose the half marathon or the shorter 4.5 mile course. Both routes run through the trails and open ground at Reflection Riding Arboretum and Nature Center on Garden Road. Entry fees benefit the nature center. Registration is required through the Reflection Riding website. This event honors the memory of Oli Via, a beloved member of the Reflection Riding community. Bring trail shoes and water. The trails wind through forest and cross open meadow sections. The race draws runners from across the Chattanooga region. Post-race food and beverages are provided for participants.
