This evening program covers local birds of prey and includes ASL interpretation. Everyone is welcome to attend. You learn about the owls, hawks, and falcons that live in and around Reflection Riding Arboretum and Nature Center. Staff bring live birds to the program. The ASL interpreter makes the content fully accessible to deaf and hard-of-hearing visitors. The event is free for members and low cost for non-members. Check the Reflection Riding website for ticket details and program start time. The nature center is on Garden Road in Chattanooga near the base of Lookout Mountain.
