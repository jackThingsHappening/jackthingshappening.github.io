The Chattanooga Symphony & Opera brings music straight into the woods. The program draws on "Peter and the Wolf" and fits Prokofiev's score to the natural setting of Reflection Riding. Musicians pop up along the trail before the main outdoor performance. The event is family-friendly and free for Reflection Riding members. Non-member tickets are available on the Reflection Riding website. This is part of the CSO's outdoor programming series. The nature center is on Garden Road near Lookout Mountain. Wear comfortable shoes and bring a blanket or low-back chair for the main performance.
