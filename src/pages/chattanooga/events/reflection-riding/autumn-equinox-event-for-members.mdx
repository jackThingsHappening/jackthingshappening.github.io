Members gather to mark the turn of the season. The evening brings native plant activities, herbal teas, and a guided yoga session on the grounds of Reflection Riding. This annual event is for current members of the arboretum and nature center. It runs at dusk on the fall equinox. Staff lead participants through the garden and forest with seasonal interpretive content. The yoga session is gentle and suited to all experience levels. Bring a mat and layers. Check the Reflection Riding website for event details and member sign-up. Not yet a member - membership starts at a low annual fee.
