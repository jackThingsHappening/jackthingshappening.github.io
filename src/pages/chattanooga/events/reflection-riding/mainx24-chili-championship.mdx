Taste chili and pick a winner at this MAINx24 cook-off on East Main Street. Cooks go head to head while you sample the pots and vote. The MAINx24 Chili Championship is part of the annual MAINx24 event that closes Main Street to cars for 24 hours. Reflection Riding participates in the event as a community partner. Admission to the chili championship varies - check the MAINx24 website for details. East Main Street fills with food, music, and activities across the full 24-hour window. The chili competition runs during daylight hours.
