Local historian Jim Ogden leads a guided hike across Reflection Riding to mark the 163rd anniversary of the Battle of Lookout Mountain. People call this fight the Battle Above the Clouds. The roughly two-mile walk covers the same ground where the Union Army crossed Lookout Creek and assaulted the mountain in 1863. Ogden explains the key moments of one of the most important campaigns of the Civil War. The land is now permanently protected through a conservation easement with the American Battlefield Trust. Expect uneven terrain and about two and a half hours on the trail. Check the Reflection Riding website for ticket details.
