Stock up on native plants for the fall planting season. Crabtree Farms brings a big selection across the whole weekend. Shoppers find perennials, shrubs, and grasses suited to the Chattanooga region at the Reflection Riding fall plant sale. Staff from both organizations help you match plants to your site conditions and sun exposure. The plant sale runs at Reflection Riding Arboretum on Garden Road. Admission to the grounds is free. Plants are priced individually. Fall is an ideal time to plant in the Southeast - root systems establish before winter without the stress of summer heat.
