Bring a chair and enjoy live music in the open air. Yarn plays deep into the evening on the grounds at Reflection Riding. Yarn is an Americana and roots band from the Northeast with a loyal touring following. They blend acoustic instruments with folk and rock influences and are known for energetic, fan-driven live shows. The campfire concert series runs outdoors at the Reflection Riding nature center on Garden Road. Admission is charged - check the website for ticket details. Food and drinks are available. Bring layers for the evening temperatures.
