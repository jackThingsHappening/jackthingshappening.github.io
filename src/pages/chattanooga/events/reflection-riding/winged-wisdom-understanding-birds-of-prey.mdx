Learn why birds of prey matter to the woods and fields around Chattanooga. A guide covers the hawks, owls, and vultures that live at the nature center as animal ambassadors. You hear how each bird hunts and how it keeps rodent numbers in check. The talk shows why healthy raptor populations signal a healthy ecosystem. Staff bring a bird out for a close look.
