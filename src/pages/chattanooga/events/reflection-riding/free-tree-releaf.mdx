Pick up free trees to plant in your own yard. City residents can take home up to two trees per household. Staff help you choose the right species for your site conditions. Reflection Riding hosts this giveaway as part of its urban reforestation efforts. Trees are native species suited to the Chattanooga region. Supplies are limited and distributed on a first-come basis. Check the Reflection Riding website for the species available and pickup details. The nature center is on Garden Road at the base of Lookout Mountain. This event typically runs one morning and ends when supplies are gone.
