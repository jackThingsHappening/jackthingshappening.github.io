Step outside and let the pace drop. This session with The Chattery uses the trails and gardens to settle the mind and body. The Chattery is a Chattanooga-based lifelong learning organization that offers workshops and classes across a wide range of topics. This program pairs gentle movement on the Reflection Riding trails with mindfulness practices. The session runs about 90 minutes. Check the Chattery website for registration and pricing. The nature center is on Garden Road near Lookout Mountain. No outdoor fitness level is required. Comfortable walking shoes are all you need.
