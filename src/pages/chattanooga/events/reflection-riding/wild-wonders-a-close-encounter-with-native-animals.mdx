Tour the Native Animal Area and meet the resident animal ambassadors of East Tennessee. The nature center cares for red wolves, a bobcat, owls, hawks, and a sandhill crane. These animals cannot survive in the wild, so they help staff teach visitors. A guide explains the role each species plays in the local ecosystem. The event also runs on Saturday with two time slots.
