Learn how to grow new plants from cuttings and seed. The nursery team shows you the same methods they use every day. You leave with propagated cuttings to grow at home. This workshop runs at the Reflection Riding nursery on Garden Road. Space is limited and registration is required through the Reflection Riding website. The session covers stem cuttings, division, and direct seeding for native plants. All materials are provided. The nursery grows native species used in the arboretum and sold at plant sales. This is a hands-on session suited to gardeners of any experience level.
