Eat tacos and help the land. White Duck Taco Shop hosts this giveback night and sends a share of sales to Reflection Riding Arboretum and Nature Center. White Duck Taco has locations in Chattanooga including the Southside and North Shore. Any qualifying purchase during the event window counts. This is a casual night out that doubles as a fundraiser for the nature center. No ticket required - just show up and order. Check the Reflection Riding website for which White Duck Taco location is participating and the hours of the giveback window.
