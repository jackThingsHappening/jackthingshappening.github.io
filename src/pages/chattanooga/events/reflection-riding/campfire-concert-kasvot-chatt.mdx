Close out the concert season with Kasvot Chätt. Bring a blanket and enjoy live music in the cool night air. Food and drinks available on site. Kasvot Chätt is a Chattanooga-based electronic and experimental music duo. Their sound blends ambient textures with live instrumentation and electronic production. The campfire concert series at Reflection Riding runs outdoors on the grounds of the nature center. Admission is charged - check the Reflection Riding website for ticket details. The nature center is on Garden Road near the base of Lookout Mountain.
