This evening owl program includes ASL interpretation and welcomes everyone. Learn how barn owls hunt in the dark and raise their young in the hollow trees of Reflection Riding. Staff bring live owls to the program. The ASL interpreter makes the content fully accessible to deaf and hard-of-hearing visitors. The event is free for members and low cost for non-members. Check the Reflection Riding website for ticket details and program start time. The nature center is on Garden Road at the base of Lookout Mountain. Barn owls are resident at the center and are part of their wildlife education program.
