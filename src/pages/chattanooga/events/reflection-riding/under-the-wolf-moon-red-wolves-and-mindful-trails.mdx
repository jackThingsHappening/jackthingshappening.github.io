Walk the trails under the January Wolf Moon. City of Chattanooga Parks and Outdoors leads this program with a Reflection Riding naturalist. You meet the resident red wolves and hear their conservation story. Red wolves are critically endangered, and the nature center breeds them for release. A slow, mindful walk follows along scenic trails. The pace helps you start the year with a clear, calm mind.
