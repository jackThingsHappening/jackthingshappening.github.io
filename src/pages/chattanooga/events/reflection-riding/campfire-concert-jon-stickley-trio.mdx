The Jon Stickley Trio plays fast, sharp acoustic music under the open sky. Bring a chair and settle in for the evening. Jon Stickley is an Asheville-based flatpick guitarist who leads a trio with violin and drums. Their music blends bluegrass, jazz, and world music at high speed. They have toured nationally and played major acoustic music festivals. The campfire concert series at Reflection Riding runs outdoors on the nature center grounds. Admission is charged - check the website for ticket details. Food and drinks are available on site. The nature center is on Garden Road.
