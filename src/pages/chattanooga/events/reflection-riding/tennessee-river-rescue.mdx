Join the yearly cleanup that keeps local waterways clear of trash. Volunteers gather at Reflection Riding to work a stretch of the Tennessee River and nearby waterways. The event provides bags, gloves, and water. No experience needed. Volunteers have removed thousands of pounds of trash in past years. Register through the Reflection Riding website. This is a free volunteer event open to adults and families with older children. The event runs a few hours in the morning. It is part of a larger Tennessee River cleanup effort coordinated across multiple sites along the river.
