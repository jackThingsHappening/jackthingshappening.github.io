Join the yearly cleanup that keeps local waterways clear of trash. Volunteers gather at Reflection Riding to work a stretch of the creek and river bank. You pull out litter and help protect the water for wildlife and people. Bring gloves, wear shoes you can get muddy, and plan for a hands-on morning.
