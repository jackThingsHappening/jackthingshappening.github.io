Grab a beer and put it toward a good cause. Oddstory's Central taproom hosts the night and gives back a share of sales to Reflection Riding Arboretum and Nature Center. The nature center relies on fundraising and community support to maintain its trails, wildlife habitats, and educational programs. This is a low-key evening out that benefits a Chattanooga institution. Oddstory Central is on McCallie Avenue. No ticket required - just show up and order a pint. The give-back percentage is noted on the Reflection Riding website.
