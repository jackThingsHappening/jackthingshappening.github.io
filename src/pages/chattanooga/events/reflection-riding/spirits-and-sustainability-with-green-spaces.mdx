Learn how Reflection Riding protects native land and wildlife. Green|Spaces leads the talk over light refreshments. You leave knowing more about what it takes to run a working arboretum and nature center inside a city. Green|Spaces is a Chattanooga nonprofit focused on sustainable building and land stewardship. The evening combines conversation about environmental issues with a tour of the Reflection Riding property. Admission is free or low cost. Check the Reflection Riding website for registration and start time. The nature center is on Garden Road near the base of Lookout Mountain.
