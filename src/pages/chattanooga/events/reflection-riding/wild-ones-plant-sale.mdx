Find native plants that feed local birds and pollinators. The Wild Ones group brings a strong selection to this one-day sale at Reflection Riding. Wild Ones is a national native plant society with a Tennessee Valley chapter. Their plant sales focus on locally appropriate species that support biodiversity. Staff help you choose plants for your specific conditions. The sale takes place at Reflection Riding Arboretum on Garden Road. Admission to the grounds is free. Plants are priced individually. Fall is an excellent time to plant native perennials and shrubs in the Chattanooga region.
