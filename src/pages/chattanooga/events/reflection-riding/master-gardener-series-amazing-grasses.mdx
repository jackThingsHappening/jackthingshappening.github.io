Native grasses add texture and ask for almost no care. This talk covers which grasses grow well here and how to use them in a home garden. The presenter is a Hamilton County Master Gardener Extension Volunteer. The Master Gardener Series runs periodically at Reflection Riding and covers practical gardening topics relevant to the region. Admission is free or low cost. Check the Reflection Riding website for registration and start time. The nature center is on Garden Road near Lookout Mountain. Attendees often bring questions from their own gardens.
