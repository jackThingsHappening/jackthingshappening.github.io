Help restore native habitat at this hands-on volunteer workshop. Staff teach you how to spot common invasive plants and pull them the right way. You then plant native species that feed and shelter local wildlife. Take the skills home and rewild your own yard. Volunteers earn a discount in the native plant nursery. The workshop repeats on Saturdays through December.
