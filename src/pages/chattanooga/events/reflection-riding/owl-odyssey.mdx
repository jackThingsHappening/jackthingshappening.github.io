Meet the barn owls of Reflection Riding and learn why people call them the ghosts of the night woods. A guide explains how these birds hunt in near silence and find prey by sound alone. The nature center keeps barn owls as animal ambassadors because they cannot return to the wild. You watch one up close during the hour.
