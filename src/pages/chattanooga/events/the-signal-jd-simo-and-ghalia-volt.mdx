Nashville guitarist JD Simo and Belgian blues-rock force Ghalia Volt share the bill at The Signal on September 3. Simo is a session ace who has played with Jack White and Beyoncé — his own music fuses Motown soul with Woodstock-era guitar fire. Volt's new 2026 album Burn the House Down, which Simo produced, is the spine of the night. Music at 8pm.
