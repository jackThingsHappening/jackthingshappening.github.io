Ben Sollee is a Kentucky cellist, singer, and songwriter. He built his own alt-Americana style from folk, bluegrass, jazz, and R&B roots. His playing weaves unusual cello techniques with banjo, guitar, and soulful vocals. His 2024 album Long Haul draws on tones from the American and global south.
