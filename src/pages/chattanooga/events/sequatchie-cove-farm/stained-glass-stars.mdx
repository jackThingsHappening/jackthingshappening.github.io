Learn Tiffany method stained glass with Tammy O'Connor and build a three-dimensional glass star over the course of the day. No experience required - the farm's workshop has all tools and materials. Sequatchie Cove Farm is in Sequatchie Valley, Tennessee, about an hour from Chattanooga. The Tiffany method uses copper foil to wrap glass pieces before soldering them together. The star you build takes several hours to complete. Register through the Sequatchie Cove website. Check the site for cost and start time. All tools, glass, and safety equipment are provided.
