The Cumberland Folk School runs this hands-on class at the farm. You dip tapers in warm beeswax, building each candle layer by layer. Beeswax burns clean and smells faintly of honey. The finished pairs make good holiday gifts for teachers, hosts, and family. No experience is needed, and the farm supplies the wax and tools. Space is limited, so sign up through the farm events page before the class fills.
