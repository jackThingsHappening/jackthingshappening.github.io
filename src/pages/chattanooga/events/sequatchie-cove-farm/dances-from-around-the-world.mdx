Anna Claire Camp teaches community dances from Russia, Germany, Scotland, Denmark, and Appalachia. No partner and no experience needed - these are group dances that everyone joins. Sequatchie Cove Farm is in Sequatchie Valley, Tennessee, about an hour from Chattanooga. The farm hosts workshops, classes, and events in a rural setting surrounded by ridge and farmland. Register through the Sequatchie Cove website. Check the site for cost and start time. These folk dance forms are designed for community participation and are accessible to all ages and fitness levels.
