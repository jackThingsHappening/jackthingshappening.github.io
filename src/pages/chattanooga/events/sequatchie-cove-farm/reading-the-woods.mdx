A four-hour morning walk through the Sequatchie Valley with Cooper Breeden, focused on identifying the trees, shrubs, and vines that grow here. Good for anyone who wants to read the landscape. Sequatchie Cove Farm is in Sequatchie Valley, Tennessee, about an hour from Chattanooga. The workshop covers identification techniques, field marks, and the ecology of common woody plants in the region. No prior botany knowledge is required. Register through the Sequatchie Cove website. Bring comfortable shoes, water, and a hand lens if you have one. Check the site for cost.
