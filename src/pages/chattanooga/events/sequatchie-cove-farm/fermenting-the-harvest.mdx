Sandor Katz literally wrote the book on fermentation. This is a full-day hands-on session at Sequatchie Cove - kraut, kimchi, and other techniques demystified by the author of The Art of Fermentation. Katz is one of the foremost educators in the fermentation revival movement. His books are used as references by home fermenters and professional chefs worldwide. Sequatchie Cove Farm is in Sequatchie Valley, Tennessee, about an hour from Chattanooga. Register through the Sequatchie Cove website. This workshop fills fast - book as soon as registration opens. Check the site for cost.
