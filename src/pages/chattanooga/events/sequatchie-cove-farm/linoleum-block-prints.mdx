Laurel York walks you through carving a design into linoleum and pulling your own block prints. You learn how to transfer a design, carve it safely, and ink and print on paper or fabric. Sequatchie Cove Farm is in Sequatchie Valley, Tennessee, about an hour from Chattanooga. The farm hosts craft and art workshops alongside agricultural and food classes. All materials are provided. Register through the Sequatchie Cove website. No prior printmaking experience is needed. Check the site for cost and the specific start time. The workshop runs about four hours.
