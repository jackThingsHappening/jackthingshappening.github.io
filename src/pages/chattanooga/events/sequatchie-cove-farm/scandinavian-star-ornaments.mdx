The Cumberland Folk School teaches this Nordic holiday craft at the farm. You weave folded paper strips into three-dimensional woven stars. The technique comes from a long Scandinavian winter tradition. Each finished star hangs on a tree or in a window. The class suits beginners, and the folk school brings the paper and shows every fold. This session was listed as sold out, so check the farm events page for openings or a waitlist.
