Learn to make an Appalachian floor broom with Ali Davenport - local broomcorn, hand-carved handles, bound the traditional way. Timed for the fall equinox. Half-day workshop. Sequatchie Cove Farm is in Sequatchie Valley, Tennessee, about an hour from Chattanooga. The farm hosts traditional craft workshops that connect participants to Appalachian material culture. All materials are provided. No prior craft experience is needed. Register through the Sequatchie Cove website. Check the site for cost and start time. The broomcorn is grown locally and the handles are shaped on site. You leave with a working floor broom.
