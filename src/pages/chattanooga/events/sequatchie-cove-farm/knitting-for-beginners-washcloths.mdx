Tracy Knutson teaches the basics of knitting through one small, useful project - a washcloth or dishcloth. You learn to cast on, knit, and bind off, and you leave with something finished. Sequatchie Cove Farm is in Sequatchie Valley, Tennessee, about an hour from Chattanooga. The farm hosts craft and skill workshops alongside agricultural classes. All materials are provided. No prior knitting experience is needed. Register through the Sequatchie Cove website. Check the site for cost and start time. The class runs about three hours and is well suited to complete beginners.
