Sequatchie Cove Farm hosts a casual evening to support its move to nonprofit status. Stones River Reelers play live old-time string music while you eat. The farm serves a farm-to-table dinner outdoors. A silent auction of handmade crafts raises money for the nonprofit application. Bring family and blankets for seating on the grass. BYOB is welcome. The rain date is Saturday, October 24. Adult tickets cover ages 12 and up, and children under 2 enter free.
