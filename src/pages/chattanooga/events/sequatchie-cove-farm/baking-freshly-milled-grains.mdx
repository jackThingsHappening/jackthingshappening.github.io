Erik Zilen of Neidlovs Bakery walks through milling and baking with freshly milled wheat. Three and a half hours on a Saturday afternoon - good intro if you have never worked with home-milled flour. Sequatchie Cove Farm is in Sequatchie Valley, Tennessee, about an hour from Chattanooga. The workshop covers grain selection, milling technique, and the differences between fresh-milled and commercial flour in bread making. All equipment and ingredients are provided. Register through the Sequatchie Cove website. Check the site for cost.
