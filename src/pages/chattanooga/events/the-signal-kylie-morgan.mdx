Country singer-songwriter Kylie Morgan plays The Signal on September 11. She writes confessional, unguarded songs — her 2025 single "Then You Happened" is one of the rawest breakup ballads in recent country memory. She went independent in 2024 and has been putting her whole self into every performance since. Show at 8pm.
