Rewind: 2000s Rave is a full club night built around Y2K music. DJs rework the biggest 2000s hits as edits, remixes, and mashups. The set leans on songs you already know from the radio and the club. Expect a packed dance floor and nonstop throwback energy. This event is 18+, so bring a valid ID. It shares the night with NIGHTLITE, a party aimed at the 30-and-up crowd earlier in the evening. Doors open at 8pm and the rave starts at 9pm. Tickets are required.
