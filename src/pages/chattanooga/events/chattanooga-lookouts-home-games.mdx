Double-A ball at AT&T Field, right on the Tennessee River. Lookout Mountain sits directly behind the outfield wall. Get there before first pitch and the concourse view earns it.

Recurring promos to know: **Thirsty Thursday** runs drink specials at the Right Field Bar every Thursday home game. **Fireworks Friday** follows every Friday night game. **Saturday Live Music** puts a pregame band near the stadium bar. **Family Sunday Funday** opens the field after the game. Kids run the bases and everyone plays catch.
