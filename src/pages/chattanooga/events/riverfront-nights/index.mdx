Free outdoor concerts every Saturday night at Ross's Landing, May through Labor Day weekend. Now in its 19th season, TVFCU Riverfront Nights brings in tribute acts covering classic rock, country, R&B, and beyond - all free, all ages, right on the Tennessee River.

Vendors and food trucks open at 6:00 PM. Opening act at 7:00, headliner at 8:30. Everything wraps by 10:00 PM. Extended Evening dates open up the Chattanooga Green for more vendors and more space.

No outside food, drinks, or coolers. Lawn chairs and blankets are welcome. No concert on August 15 - Ross's Landing is taken over by River Games that week.
