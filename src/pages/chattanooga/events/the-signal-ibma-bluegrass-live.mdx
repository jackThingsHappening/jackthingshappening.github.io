IBMA Bluegrass Live! takes over The Signal for two nights of string band music. Friday, October 23 features Michael Cleveland & Friends with Leftover Salmon. Saturday, October 24 brings Della Mae and Sierra Ferrell to the main room. The lineup mixes hard-driving traditional bluegrass with newgrass and roots songwriting. Buy a single night or a weekend pass. VIP weekend passes are limited to 200 and add priority seating and early entry. Doors open at 6:30pm Friday and 7pm Saturday.
