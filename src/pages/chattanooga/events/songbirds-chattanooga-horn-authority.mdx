Chattanooga Horn Authority pack Songbirds with big-band horn-driven rock on Saturday, September 5. The eight-piece local ensemble covers Chicago, Steely Dan, Van Morrison, and Huey Lewis with genuine firepower — their members range from 40 to 70 and have deep roots in the Scenic City's music scene. This is a Chattanooga crowd favorite. Music starts at 7pm.
