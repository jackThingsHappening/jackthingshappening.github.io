Garrison Keillor hosted A Prairie Home Companion for more than forty years. He is known for his slow, dry News from Lake Wobegon monologues. This solo show mixes stories, poems, limericks, and songs. He also leads the room in a sing-along. The set is one man, a microphone, and a long memory. This stop is part of his four-month solo tour across the country. Fans of old-time public radio will feel at home here. Doors open at 5:30pm and the show starts at 7pm. Tickets are required.
