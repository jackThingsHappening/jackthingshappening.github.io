Cuffing Season is the stage name of Chandler Monroe, a soul and blues singer-songwriter from southern Mississippi. His sound mixes delta blues with modern soul and R&B. Sarah Mootz is an independent singer-songwriter from Athens, Georgia. She leans on rock and indie influences and honest storytelling. The two artists have recorded together, pairing his deep voice with her lighter tone. They bring both pop-forward and blues sounds to this double bill.
