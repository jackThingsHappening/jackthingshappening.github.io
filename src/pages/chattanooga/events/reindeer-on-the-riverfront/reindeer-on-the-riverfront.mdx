River City Company brings the annual Reindeer on the Riverfront celebration to Coolidge Park on November 27. The event moves from Ross's Landing this year while riverfront park renovations continue. Admission is free.

Live reindeer are on view from 4:00pm to 6:30pm. Free photos with Santa run from 4:30pm to 7:30pm. The 46th Annual Lighted Boat Parade kicks off at 7:00pm on the Tennessee River. Fireworks close the night at 8:00pm.

Between those anchors, families can ride the Ellis Carousel, hop on the mini train, get face painting and balloon animals, and warm up with hot cocoa. Local food and artisan vendors line the park throughout the evening.
