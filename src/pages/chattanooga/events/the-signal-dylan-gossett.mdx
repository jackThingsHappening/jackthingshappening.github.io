Dylan Gossett brings The Ramblin' Tour 2027 to The Signal on March 30. The Austin singer-songwriter broke out with his 2x Platinum anthem "Coal." His music blends Americana and Red Dirt country with lyrics rooted in a Southern upbringing. The show supports his sophomore album Ramblin'. Doors open at 6:30pm.
