Kameron Marlowe is a country singer-songwriter from North Carolina signed to Columbia Nashville. His debut single "Giving You Up" built his name and still anchors his live set. He pairs a smoky baritone with heartfelt ballads and gritty country rock. Fans can expect favorites like "Steady Heart" and "Burn 'Em All" alongside newer material. The show starts at 8pm with doors at 6:30pm. Tickets are required.
