Now in its 10th year, the Mistletoe Market takes over Bradley Square Mall in Cleveland for three full weekends leading up to Christmas. Craft and commercial vendors fill the indoor space. Animal encounters and Santa photos run all three weekends. Food vendors set up outside.

The market runs Nov 27-29, Dec 5-6, and Dec 12-13, 2026. Admission and parking are free. Black Friday hours run until 9pm.
