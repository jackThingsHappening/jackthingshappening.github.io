The Chattanooga Strawberry Festival runs May 7 through 9 at Camp Jordan in East Ridge. Friday starts at 2pm, Saturday and Sunday open at 10am. More than 150 vendors are on-site alongside food trucks and fresh strawberries from MB's Farm and Produce. A free petting zoo, pony rides, inflatables, and a funnel cake eating contest round things out. Admission is $10 per person. Kids 10 and under get in free. Parking is free. Camp Jordan sits at 323 Camp Jordan Pkwy, East Ridge, TN, right off Exit 1 on I-75.
