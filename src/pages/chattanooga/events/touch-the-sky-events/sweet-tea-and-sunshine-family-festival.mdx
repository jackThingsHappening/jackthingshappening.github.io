The Sweet Tea & Sunshine Family Festival runs April 30 through May 2 at Greenway Park and Pavilion in Cleveland, TN. This is the 11th annual run of the festival. More than 100 craft vendors line the park alongside 500+ antique vendors. Food trucks cover the meals. A free petting zoo, inflatables, and a sweet tea drinking contest fill out the day. Admission pricing is TBA. Kids 10 and under get in free. The venue is at 755 Raider Dr NW, Cleveland, TN, about 30 minutes north of Chattanooga on I-75. The event runs rain or shine.
