Over 140 vendors take over Camp Jordan Arena in East Ridge for three days of indoor holiday shopping. Craft vendors, commercial booths, and specialty goods fill the floor. Free photos with Santa, the Grinch, and Cindy Lou Who run throughout the weekend.

Admission is $10. Kids 10 and under get in free. The arena has climate control and free parking on site.
