The Pickle Patch runs June 12 and 13 at Camp Jordan in East Ridge. It is a full convention built around pickles and pickle-flavored everything. Vendors bring pickle products, pickle-adjacent snacks, and specialty foods. Food trucks are on-site. General admission tickets are $10 at the gate. VIP tickets are also available for purchase. Camp Jordan sits at 323 Camp Jordan Pkwy, East Ridge, TN. Saturday hours run 10am to 5pm and Sunday runs 11am to 5pm. Parking is free.
