The World of Chocolate Expo takes over the Forum River Center in Rome for a full weekend. Vendors bring chocolate, savories, and gifts under one roof. The first 100 adults each day get a free full-size candy bar. Kids get fun-size bars while supplies last.

Admission is $10 per person. Children 3 and under enter free. Enter the Golden Basket giveaway and grab free photos with Willy. Free samples run throughout the day. The venue is indoors, so plan for a warm visit regardless of the weather.
