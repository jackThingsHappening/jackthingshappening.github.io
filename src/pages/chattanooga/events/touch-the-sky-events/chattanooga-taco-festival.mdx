The Chattanooga Taco Festival runs September 25 and 26 at Camp Jordan in East Ridge. More than 35 food vendors and food trucks compete for your attention with tacos and Mexican-inspired food. Ensamble de Mexico Dancers perform on-site. Artisan vendors and a Kids Zone fill the rest of the grounds. Admission is free and parking is free. Camp Jordan sits at 323 Camp Jordan Pkwy, East Ridge, TN, right off Exit 1 on I-75. Saturday hours run 11am to 7pm. Sunday runs 11am to 5pm.
