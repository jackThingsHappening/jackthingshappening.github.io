Claus and Co. Holiday Market runs November 13 and 14 at the OmniPlex in Cullman, AL. The OmniPlex is a large multi-use facility that hosts regional events. This is a holiday shopping market with vendors selling gifts, handmade goods, and seasonal items. Touch the Sky Events organizes the market as a family-friendly event with no alcohol and a clean, festive atmosphere. Admission pricing is TBA. The OmniPlex is at 1634 Field of Miracles Dr SW, Cullman, AL, about 90 minutes south of Chattanooga on I-65.
