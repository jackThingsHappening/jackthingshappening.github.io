Christmas at the Colonnade runs November 6 and 7 at the Colonnade Event Center in Ringgold, GA, about 15 minutes south of Chattanooga. The venue is an upscale event space that works well for a Christmas market. Handmade and commercial vendors fill the floor alongside food vendors. Free photos with Santa are included. Admission is $8 per person. Kids 12 and under get in free. Parking is free. The Colonnade is at 264 Catoosa Circle, Ringgold, GA. Saturday hours run 10am to 5pm and Sunday runs 10am to 4pm.
