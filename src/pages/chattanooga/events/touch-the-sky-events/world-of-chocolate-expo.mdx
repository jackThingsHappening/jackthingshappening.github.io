Chocolate vendors fill the Catoosa Colonnade in Ringgold for one day of sweets and gifts. Expect truffles, fudge, baked goods, and savory treats from regional makers. The first 100 attendees get a free full-size chocolate bar. Kids get fun-size bars while supplies last.

Admission is $10 at the door. Children 3 and under enter free. Parking is free. The event runs indoors, so weather is not a concern. Arrive early for the best vendor selection and the free candy giveaway.
