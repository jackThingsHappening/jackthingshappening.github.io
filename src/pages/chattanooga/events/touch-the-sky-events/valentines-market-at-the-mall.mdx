Over 125 vendors set up inside Bradley Square Mall for a day of Valentine's shopping. Browse handmade crafts, gifts, and specialty goods for the people you love. Food trucks park outside. Animal encounters give kids something to do between booths.

Admission and parking are both free. The market runs indoors with climate control. Come early for the widest vendor selection. The mall sits off Paul Huff Parkway in Cleveland, about 30 minutes north of downtown Chattanooga.
