Better with Age Vintage Market runs August 21 and 22 at Camp Jordan Arena in East Ridge. More than 140 vendors show up with vintage items, antiques, farmhouse pieces, repurposed goods, home decor, and handmade accessories. It is one of the better stops in the region if you hunt vintage regularly. Admission is $10. Kids 10 and under get in free. Parking is free. Camp Jordan Arena sits at 323 Camp Jordan Pkwy, East Ridge, TN. Saturday hours run 10am to 5pm and Sunday runs 10am to 4pm.
