The Peachy Patch Melon Fest runs May 22 and 23 at the Tri-State Exhibition Arena in McDonald, TN. The event takes place under a large covered pavilion. More than 150 vendors sell crafts, handmade goods, and specialty items alongside fresh watermelons and peaches from MB's Farms and Produce. A free petting zoo and food trucks round out the day. Admission is $10 per person. Kids 10 and under get in free. The venue is at 200 Natures Trl SW, McDonald, TN, about 20 minutes south of Chattanooga.
