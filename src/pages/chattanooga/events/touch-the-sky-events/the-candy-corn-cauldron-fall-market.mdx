The Candy Corn Cauldron Fall Market runs October 9 and 10 at Camp Jordan Arena in East Ridge. The event is built around Halloween. Kids trick-or-treat with each vendor at their booth. Free photos with the Sanderson Sisters are available on-site. A free petting zoo and inflatables round out the family options. Costumes are welcome. Admission is $10 per person. Kids 10 and under get in free. Parking is free. Camp Jordan Arena sits at 323 Camp Jordan Pkwy, East Ridge, TN.
