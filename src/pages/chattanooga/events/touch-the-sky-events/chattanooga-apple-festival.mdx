The Chattanooga Apple Festival runs October 16 and 17 at Camp Jordan in East Ridge. Craft and commercial vendors line the outdoor grounds. A petting zoo, pony rides, and inflatables give families plenty to do beyond shopping. Admission is free and parking is free. If it rains, the event moves inside Camp Jordan Arena. Camp Jordan sits at 323 Camp Jordan Pkwy, East Ridge, TN, right off Exit 1 on I-75. Saturday hours run 10am to 5pm. Sunday runs 10am to 4pm.
