The Mother's Day Market runs Saturday, May 1 at Bradley Square Mall in Cleveland, TN. More than 125 vendors show up with gifts, handmade goods, and specialty items timed for the holiday. Food trucks handle the food. Animal encounters add something for kids. Admission is free and parking is free. The mall is at 200 Paul Huff Pkwy NW, Cleveland, TN, about 30 minutes north of Chattanooga. Hours run 10am to 5pm. It is a solid stop if you want to find something different for the day.
