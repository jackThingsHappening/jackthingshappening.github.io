Chicago indie rockers RATBOYS bring their sixth album, Singin' to an Empty Chair, to The Signal on October 22. The band blends sauntering, reflective indie rock with a touch of alt-country — distortion-laced but never harsh. Guitarist Dave Sagan and vocalist Julia Steiner have been building this sound since 2010. Show at 8pm.
