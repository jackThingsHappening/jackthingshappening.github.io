Izzy hosts trivia every Tuesday at 7:30pm inside the Tavern at Feed Tavern & Table Co. on the Southside. Teams compete across multiple rounds of general knowledge, pop culture, and themed questions. Feed Tavern & Table Co. is a Southside Chattanooga restaurant and bar. No ticket required. Walk in and form a team. Table size limits vary - check the social pages. The kitchen serves food through the trivia session. The Tavern is the indoor bar section of the restaurant. Check the Feed social pages for any changes to the weekly schedule or any special themed trivia nights.
