Local musicians play live on the patio every Thursday starting at 6:30pm at Feed Tavern & Table Co. Artists rotate weekly and the current lineup posts on the venue social pages. Feed Tavern & Table Co. is a Southside Chattanooga restaurant and bar. The patio provides outdoor seating during the warmer months. No cover charge for patio music nights. Walk in and find a seat. The kitchen serves food through the evening. Check the Feed social pages for the weekly artist announcement and any changes to the schedule.
