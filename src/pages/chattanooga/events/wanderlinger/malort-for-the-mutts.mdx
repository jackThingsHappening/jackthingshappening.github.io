WanderLinger raises money for local animals from December 18 to 20. For every shot of Malört sold, the brewery donates $1 to McKamey Animal Center. The center cares for homeless dogs and cats across Chattanooga. Malört is a bitter Chicago liqueur with a reputation for strong reactions. That is the point. Order a shot, pull a face, and help a shelter animal. There is no cover charge. Stop in across all three days on Station Street in the Southside.
