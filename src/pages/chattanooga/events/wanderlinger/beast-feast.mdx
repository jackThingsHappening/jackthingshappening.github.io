Chef Carolyn fires up the smoker at WanderLinger on October 3. She smokes an entire hog, plus lamb ribs, frog legs, and smoked sausage. The feast starts at 1pm and the food keeps coming. Mud wrestling adds to the spectacle through the afternoon. Live music plays all day and into the night. Pour a fresh beer on Station Street and stay for the whole event.
