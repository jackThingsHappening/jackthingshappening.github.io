Karaoke comes to WanderLinger on September 5 from 7:30 to 10:30pm. Pick the song you sing best and take the mic with your friends. There is no cover, and WanderLinger pours craft beer through the evening. The craft brewery is on the Choo Choo property in downtown Chattanooga. Karaoke here is casual and fun - the room is set up for participation over performance. All skill levels welcome. Check the WanderLinger social pages for any updates to the karaoke schedule. This is a weekly or regular event - check the page for recurring dates.
