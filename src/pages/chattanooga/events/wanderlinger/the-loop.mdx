The Loop rolls into WanderLinger on September 6 from 2 to 8pm with nearly 70 vintage motorcycles built between 1930 and 1959. Vintage vendors and an artisan market fill out the day. Gino Fanelli plays live at 5pm, so grab a beer on Station Street and take it all in.
