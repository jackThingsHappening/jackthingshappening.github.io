The Halem Albright Band plays WanderLinger on November 27. Doors open at 8pm and the cover is $10. The Atlanta five-piece blends jazz, funk, jangle pop, indie rock, and folk. Halem Albright writes the songs and leads on guitar and vocals. The band draws on classic jam influences and leans into high-energy live sets. Catch them on Station Street in Chattanooga's Southside.
