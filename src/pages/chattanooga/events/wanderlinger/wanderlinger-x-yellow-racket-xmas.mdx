Yellow Racket Records sets up a holiday pop-up inside WanderLinger on December 11. Yellow Racket is one of Chattanooga's best independent record stores. They bring their vinyl crates to the taproom for a Christmas shopping night. Tattoo artist Alex Faulkner is also on-site for walk-in flash tattoos. WanderLinger pours its full tap list throughout the night. The event starts at 7pm. The taproom is at 55 Station Street in Southside Chattanooga, with street parking nearby on Station Street and East 11th Street.
