DJ Kris Kash runs the decks for WanderLinger's Ugly Christmas Sweater Party on December 19. The party starts at 10pm and runs until 2am. Wear your worst holiday sweater. A competition for the best ugly sweater runs through the night. Cover is $10. WanderLinger is at 55 Station Street in Southside Chattanooga. Street parking is available on Station Street and nearby side streets. The taproom pours its full craft beer lineup all night.
