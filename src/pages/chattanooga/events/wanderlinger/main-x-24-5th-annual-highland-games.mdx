WanderLinger runs its 5th Annual Highland Games on December 5 during MAINx24. The festival takes over Chattanooga's Southside for 24 hours, and this is the brewery's corner of it. The day features games and challenges for everyone, including the popular Stein Holding Competition. Live music starts at 6pm. The Bohannons play from 6 to 8pm, bringing their heavy Southern rock built from punk, metal, and country roots. The Chattanooga band has toured for more than 20 years. Danimal & Friends take over from 9pm to 1am. Admission is free, so arrive early if you want a spot near the stage.
