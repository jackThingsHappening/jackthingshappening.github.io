Needle Breather plays WanderLinger on December 26. The band plays deathcore with a heavy underground metal sound. WanderLinger's taproom has hosted metal nights before and the stage setup works well for this kind of show. December 26 is a good night for it — the holiday crowd has thinned and the people who show up want to be there. Doors open at 10pm. WanderLinger is at 55 Station Street in Southside Chattanooga. Street parking is available on Station Street and East 11th Street.
