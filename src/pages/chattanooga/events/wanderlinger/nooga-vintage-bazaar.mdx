The Nooga Vintage Bazaar fills WanderLinger with vintage finds. Vendors sell vintage clothing, handmade goods, jewelry, and accessories. DJ Minks spins throughout the event. WanderLinger is a craft brewery on the Choo Choo property in downtown Chattanooga. The bazaar draws local and regional vendors. No ticket required to attend and browse. Check the WanderLinger social pages for the vendor list and specific event hours. The indoor and outdoor spaces at WanderLinger give vendors room to set up without crowding. Bring cash - many vintage vendors prefer it.
