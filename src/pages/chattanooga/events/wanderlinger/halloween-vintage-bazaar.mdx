The Halloween Vintage Bazaar brings local vendors to the Choo Choo Gardens next to WanderLinger. Shop vintage clothing, handmade goods, and one-of-a-kind finds. Wear a costume and make a day of it on Station Street. The market runs from 1 to 7pm. Entry is free.
