The Halloween Vintage Bazaar brings local vendors to the Choo Choo Gardens next to WanderLinger. Shop vintage clothing, handmade goods, and one-of-a-kind Halloween items. The outdoor setting at the Choo Choo property gives the event a festive atmosphere. No ticket required to browse. WanderLinger pours craft beer nearby. Check the WanderLinger social pages for vendor details and event hours. Costumes are welcome. The bazaar draws sellers and shoppers from across Chattanooga. This is a good option for finding unique Halloween pieces or early holiday gifts from local makers.
