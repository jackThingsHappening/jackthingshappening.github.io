Dead Cover Office brings the Grateful Dead songbook to WanderLinger on November 7 from 6 to 9pm. Expect fan favorites and plenty of improvisational jams. Dead Cover Office is a Chattanooga-area band dedicated to the Dead catalog. Their sets pull from the full breadth of the Grateful Dead discography - early psychedelic material, country-influenced songs, and the extended jam pieces from the band's touring years. WanderLinger is a craft brewery on the Choo Choo property in downtown Chattanooga. Check the venue for ticket details and start time.
