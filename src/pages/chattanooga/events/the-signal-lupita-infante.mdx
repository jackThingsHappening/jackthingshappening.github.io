Two-time Latin Grammy nominee Lupita Infante plays The Signal on November 28. Her grandparents are Mexican music legends, and she carries that lineage into modern mariachi, norteño, and ranchera. Her 2025 album Las de Infante reimagines 23 of her grandfather Pedro Infante's most iconic songs. Show at 8pm.
