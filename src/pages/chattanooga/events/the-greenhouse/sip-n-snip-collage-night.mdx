Wednesday afternoons turn into collage hour at The Greenhouse. Snipping starts at 4pm. You cut, paste, and layer paper into your own collage. Oddstory beer stays cold and the taproom keeps the doors open. The Greenhouse Barrel + Taproom is a taproom and event space in Chattanooga associated with Oddstory Brewing. The collage format is casual and social - bring magazines, printed photos, or found materials. No artistic experience is needed. Check The Greenhouse social pages for any changes to the weekly schedule. This is a free event beyond the cost of your drinks.
