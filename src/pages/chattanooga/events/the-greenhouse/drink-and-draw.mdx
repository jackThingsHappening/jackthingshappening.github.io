Once a month, Drink & Draw sets up at The Greenhouse. Artist Jessica Schulman leads the Sunday session at 2pm. You sketch and draw while Oddstory pours craft beer. Beginners and regular artists both fit right in. Bring your own sketchbook or use the supplies on hand. The afternoon stays loose and unhurried.
