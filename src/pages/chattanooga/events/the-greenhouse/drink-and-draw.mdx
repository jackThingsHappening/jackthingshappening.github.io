Once a month, Drink & Draw sets up at The Greenhouse. Artist Jessica Schulman leads the Sunday session at 2pm. You sketch and draw while Oddstory pours craft beer. The Greenhouse Barrel + Taproom is a craft beer taproom in Chattanooga associated with Oddstory Brewing. Drink & Draw is an open-format drawing session - bring your own supplies or use what is provided. No experience is needed. Check The Greenhouse social pages for the specific monthly date and any registration requirements. The session runs about two hours.
