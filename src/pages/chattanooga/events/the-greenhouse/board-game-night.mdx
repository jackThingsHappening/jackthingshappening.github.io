Thursday nights fill The Greenhouse with board games. Play kicks off at 6pm. Pull something from the shelf of tabletop games or bring your own. Oddstory pours craft beer at the taproom bar while you strategize. The Greenhouse Barrel + Taproom is a craft beer taproom in Chattanooga associated with Oddstory Brewing. Board game nights draw a mix of regulars and new faces. No ticket required - walk in and find a table. This is a free weekly event beyond the cost of your drinks. Check The Greenhouse social pages for any updates to the weekly schedule.
