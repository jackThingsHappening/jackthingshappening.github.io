The Local Honeys play Songbirds on Thursday, October 29. The Kentucky duo pairs Linda Jean Stokley on guitar and fiddle with Montana Hobbs on banjo. They root their sound in old-time Appalachian folk and write original songs about mountain life. Hunter Blalock opens the show. Music starts at 7pm.
