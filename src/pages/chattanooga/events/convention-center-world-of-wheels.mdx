World of Wheels returns to the Chattanooga Convention Center for its 59th year. The indoor show fills 110,000 square feet with hot rods, race cars, antique cars, custom imports, and motorcycles. Judges score each vehicle on condition, design, finish, and workmanship. Celebrity guests include Kevin Mack from Count's Kustoms and Courtney Hansen from Overhaulin'. The Golden Riders perform stunts all weekend. Kids race on a free Hot Wheels drag track and meet Star Wars cosplayers. Contests award prizes such as a Harley Davidson motorcycle and an electric bike. Buy tickets early at O'Reilly Auto Parts stores for the lowest price. Passes cover a single day with no re-entry.
