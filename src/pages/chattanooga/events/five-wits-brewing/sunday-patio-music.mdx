Sundays slow down on the Five Wits patio on Long Street. Local musicians play from 1 to 4pm while the taps stay busy. Claim a table outside, bring a friend, and settle in. Five Wits Brewing is a craft brewery on Long Street in downtown Chattanooga. The Sunday patio music is a free event - walk in and find a spot. Acts rotate weekly and cover acoustic rock, folk, country, and Americana. Check the Five Wits social pages for the weekly artist and any changes to the schedule. The patio is covered and stays comfortable in most weather.
