Thursday nights mean trivia at Five Wits on Long Street. Chatt Trivia hosts from 7 to 9pm. Answer well and your team lands in the top three for prizes. Grab a pint and find a table. Five Wits Brewing is a craft brewery on Long Street in downtown Chattanooga. No ticket required - walk in and form a team. The trivia format covers general knowledge, pop culture, sports, and current events. Check the Five Wits social pages for any themed trivia nights or changes to the weekly schedule.
