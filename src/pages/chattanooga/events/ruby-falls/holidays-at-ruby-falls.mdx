The cave waterfall tours keep running through the holiday season at Ruby Falls. Outside, the castle and plaza fill with lights and decorations. Visitors can add a handwritten wish to the Forest of Wishes. Santa visits on select days. The Castle Cafe stays open for food, local craft beer, and hot cocoa by the fireplace.

Cave adventure tickets ($19.95 to $42.95) are required for the underground waterfall tour and must be bought online. The plaza, castle, and cafe are free to visit without a ticket. Book ahead as availability fills up during peak weekends.
