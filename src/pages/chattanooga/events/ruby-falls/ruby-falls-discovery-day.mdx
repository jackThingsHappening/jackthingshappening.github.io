Ruby Falls turns 98 on December 30, 2026. Leo Lambert discovered the underground waterfall on this date in 1928. The anniversary event honors that discovery with a rare deal: 98 Cave Walk tickets for select morning tours go on sale December 18 at 10 AM for the original $2 price. Those tickets sell fast and are nonrefundable. All visitors on December 30 get a complimentary keepsake postcard while supplies last. The cave is on Lookout Mountain at 1720 S. Scenic Highway — book timed tickets online before you go.
