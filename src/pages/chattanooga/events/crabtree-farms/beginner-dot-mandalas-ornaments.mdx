Crabtree Farms hosts a beginner dot mandala class. You learn to paint ornaments using simple dotting tools. The instructor teaches patterns, designs, and color choices step by step. No art experience is needed to join. The class runs from 6:00pm to 7:30pm on the farm. Tickets cost $39.19 and supplies are included. You take your finished ornament home. Register early because the class fills fast.
