Crabtree Farms serves a multi-course dinner built from the fall harvest. Chefs cook with produce grown on the farm and from local partners. The evening runs from 6:00pm to 9:00pm in an outdoor farm setting. Tickets cost $225 per person. Seats are limited for this intimate dinner, so book ahead. The meal supports the farm's education programs.
