Crabtree Farms hosts this monthly community book club. This session covers "The Accidental Ecosystem: People and Wildlife in American Cities." Readers discuss urban wildlife, sustainability, and community themes. The group meets from 5:30pm to 7:30pm on the farm. Admission is free and open to everyone. Bring your own copy of the book. Arrive early to settle in before the talk.
