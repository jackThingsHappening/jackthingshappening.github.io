Crabtree Farms and Gaining Ground teach the basics of home canning. This class opens a three-part autumn food preservation series. Students learn safe methods for canning fruits and vegetables. The class runs from 12:00pm to 2:00pm. Admission is free, but space fills quickly. Register in advance to reserve a spot. Beginners will leave ready to can at home.
