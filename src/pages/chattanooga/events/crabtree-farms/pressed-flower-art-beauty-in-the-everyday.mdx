Crabtree Farms hosts a relaxing art workshop. Students arrange pressed flowers and natural materials into finished pieces. The class invites you to slow down and notice small beauty in nature. The session runs from 6:00pm to 7:30pm on the farm. Tickets cost $39.19 and include all supplies. You take your artwork home at the end. Register early because workshops fill fast.
