Second run of the microscope workshop if August filled up. Same hands-on look at what lives in healthy soil - with the fall season giving the samples a different character than summer. Crabtree Farms leads this workshop in their learning space on Jersey Pike. Participants use microscopes to view live microorganisms from soil samples. No science background required. The class covers what healthy soil needs and why microbial life matters for growing food. Register through the Crabtree Farms website. Space is limited.
