Crabtree Farms hosts a community plant and seed swap. Bring any extra seeds or plants you have to share with others. The event includes demonstrations on seed saving with real examples. You can trade your surplus and learn how to collect and store seeds. The swap runs from 5:00pm to 7:00pm. Admission is free and open to all gardeners. Come to meet other growers and build your garden.
