Crabtree Farms screens "The Extraordinary Caterpillar" with the Wild Ones Tennessee Valley Chapter. The film explores insect life cycles and the role of native plants. The screening runs from 6:00pm to 7:30pm. A short discussion follows the film. Admission is free and open to all ages. Seating is first come, first served, so plan to arrive early.
