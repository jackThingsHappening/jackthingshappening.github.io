This is a food swap for people who make things. Bring homemade jams, ferments, baked goods, garden preserves, or pantry extras. Leave with something new from someone else. The swap is free and runs about an hour. It is capped at 12 participants so the exchange stays personal. RSVP is required by January 8 -- the event fills quickly. Crabtree Farms sits at the east end of MLK Boulevard near downtown Chattanooga. Street parking is available along 30th Street. Arrive close to the start time since the swap begins promptly.
