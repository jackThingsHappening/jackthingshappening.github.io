Crabtree Farms and Gaining Ground teach holiday cooking with fall produce. This class is part of the autumn food preservation series at the farm. Students learn to turn seasonal harvests into festive dishes. The session shows simple ways to cook and store fall vegetables. The class runs from 12:00pm to 2:00pm. Admission is free, but space fills quickly. Register in advance to reserve a spot.
