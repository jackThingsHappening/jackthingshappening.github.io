Crabtree Farms hosts a guided acrylic painting class with a cozy winter theme. You will paint a snowy cabin scene step by step. The instructor walks the whole room through each stage. Beginners and advanced painters are both welcome. No experience is needed to finish a canvas you can take home. The class runs from 6:00pm to 8:00pm. Tickets cost $53.07. This evening class pairs well with a relaxed night out. Arrive a few minutes early to settle in and claim a good seat. Register ahead, since hands-on art classes at the farm fill up fast.
