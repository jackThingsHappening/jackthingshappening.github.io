Crabtree's baking workshops fill fast. This one covers sourdough bagel technique from scratch - mixing, shaping, boiling, baking. Beginners welcome, experienced bakers will still find it worth the trip. Crabtree Farms runs this class in their learning kitchen on Jersey Pike. Students work with live sourdough starter and leave with fresh bagels they made themselves. All ingredients and equipment are provided. Register through the Crabtree Farms website. Space is limited. Check the site for the specific cost and start time. The workshop runs about three to four hours.
