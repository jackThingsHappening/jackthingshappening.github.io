Crabtree Farms runs this three-day fall tradition from September 25 to 27. Shoppers browse native plants, pollinator-friendly flowers, and cool-season vegetables. Staff and volunteers answer gardening questions on site. The festival mixes plant shopping with community activities and pollinator education. Hours run from 9:00am on Friday through 3:00pm on Sunday. Bring a wagon for hauling your finds.
