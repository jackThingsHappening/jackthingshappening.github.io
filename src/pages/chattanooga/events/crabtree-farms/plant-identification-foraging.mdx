A guided walk through the farm property covering foraging fundamentals and plant ID. Good for beginners who want to learn what's edible and what isn't. Crabtree Farms leads this walk on Jersey Pike with a knowledgeable guide who points out edible plants, common look-alikes, and safe harvesting practices. No experience is needed. Wear comfortable shoes and bring water. Register through the Crabtree Farms website. Space is limited. Check the site for the specific cost and start time. The walk covers two to three hours and ends at the farm.
