The fall seating of Crabtree's 100 Dinner Series lands at the Evelyn Center. Local chefs build a multi-course meal around seasonal, farm-grown ingredients. Between courses, staff share the story of the farm's food-access and education work. Seats are limited and tickets sell fast, so book early through the farm's site.
