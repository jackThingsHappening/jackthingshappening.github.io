Crabtree Farms hosts a monthly mindfulness meditation group. The practice uses the farm landscape as a focus for attention. A facilitator guides the session through simple, nature-based exercises. The group meets from 10:00am to 11:00am. Admission is free and open to all skill levels. Wear comfortable clothes and shoes for walking outdoors. No meditation experience is required.
