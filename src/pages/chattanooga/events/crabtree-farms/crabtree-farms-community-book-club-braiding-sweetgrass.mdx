Crabtree Farms hosts its monthly community book club. This session covers "Braiding Sweetgrass: Indigenous Wisdom, Scientific Knowledge and the Teachings of Plants." Readers discuss food, land, sustainability, health, and community. The group meets from 5:30pm to 7:30pm on the farm. Admission is free and open to everyone. Bring your own copy of the book. Arrive early to settle in before the talk.
