LA synth-pop trio Cannons bring Everything Glows, their fifth album, to The Signal on October 14. Shimmering analog production, glossy guitars, and Michelle Joy's sultry vocals are the calling cards — the band landed on Columbia Records in 2026. They co-headlined a US tour with Bob Moses earlier this year to strong reviews. Show at 8pm.
