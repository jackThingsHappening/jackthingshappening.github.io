The Chattanooga Holiday Market moves indoors to the Convention Center for two December weekends. Shop on December 12, 13, 19, and 20, 2026. More than 200 local vendors fill the hall with handmade gifts and holiday food. Browse pottery, custom art, handcrafted jewelry, and local apparel. Find natural bath products, home decorations, and woodworking from area makers. Pick up fresh local food for your holiday table. The market stays warm and dry no matter the weather outside. Admission is free, so bring the whole family. Come early for the best selection before popular items sell out.
