Reel Conversations screens the film TILL, the story of Mamie Till-Mobley and her son Emmett. The movie night pairs the screening with a guided discussion. Guest panelists lead the conversation after the film. TILL was released in 2022 and directed by Chinonye Chukwu. It tells the story of Mamie Till-Mobley's fight for justice after her son Emmett was murdered in Mississippi in 1955. The Bessie Smith Cultural Center is on Martin Luther King Jr. Boulevard in Chattanooga. Check the BSCC website for ticket details.
