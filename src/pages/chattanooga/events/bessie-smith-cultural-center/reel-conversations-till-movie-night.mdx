Reel Conversations screens the film TILL, the story of Mamie Till-Mobley and her son Emmett. The movie night pairs the screening with a guided discussion. Guests reflect on the film's history and meaning together. The event runs from 6pm to 9pm at the center. Buy tickets through the center's website.
