Voices That Shaped the Story celebrates Black journalism. The night gathers writers and reporters to share their work and history. Panelists trace how Black journalists shaped the news and the nation. The program runs from 6pm to 8pm at the center. Buy tickets through the Bessie Smith Cultural Center website.
