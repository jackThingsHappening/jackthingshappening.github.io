Voices That Shaped the Story celebrates Black journalism. The night gathers writers and reporters to share their work and history. Panelists trace how Black journalists documented their communities and shaped national narratives. The Bessie Smith Cultural Center is on Martin Luther King Jr. Boulevard in Chattanooga. The center serves as the city's primary institution dedicated to African American history and culture. Check the BSCC website for ticket details and start time. This is a panel and discussion format event that runs about two hours.
