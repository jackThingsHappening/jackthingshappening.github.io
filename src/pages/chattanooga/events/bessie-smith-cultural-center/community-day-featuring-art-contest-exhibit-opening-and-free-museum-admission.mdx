Community Day opens the center's doors for free. The day features the opening of the annual art contest exhibit. Local artists display their work across the galleries. Free museum admission means the whole collection is accessible to anyone who comes through the door. The Bessie Smith Cultural Center is on Martin Luther King Jr. Boulevard in Chattanooga. The center holds exhibits, artifacts, and programs dedicated to African American history and culture. Check the BSCC website for the schedule of activities and the art contest exhibit opening time.
