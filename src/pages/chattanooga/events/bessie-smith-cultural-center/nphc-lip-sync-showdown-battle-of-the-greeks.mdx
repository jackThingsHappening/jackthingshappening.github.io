The NPHC Lip Sync Showdown pits Chattanooga's Black Greek-letter organizations against each other. Members of the Divine Nine perform choreographed lip sync routines. The crowd cheers on the strolls, steps, and stage antics. The battle runs from 6pm to 8pm at the center. Buy tickets through the Bessie Smith Cultural Center website.
