The Velvet Chair Experience brings its final show of 2026 to the Bessie Smith Cultural Center on December 13. Karen Collins leads the evening with her fusion of jazz and uptown soul, drawing from a catalog that spans Bessie Smith and Billie Holiday to Sade and Anita Baker.

This is the last Velvet Chair show of the year, billed as a holiday edition. Dexter Bell joins Collins, along with surprise guests. The show runs 6 PM to 9:30 PM. The setting is intimate with seating at the Bessie, so tickets tend to go early. Parking is available on E Martin Luther King Boulevard and in the surrounding Bluff View area lots.
