Chattanooga State students screen two short films at Songbirds. The lineup includes "Blue after Gold" and "Vandycorn." The event celebrates local student filmmakers and their work. Live music from Habitual Strangers rounds out the night. The premiere runs from 6:00 PM to 8:00 PM. Admission is free.
