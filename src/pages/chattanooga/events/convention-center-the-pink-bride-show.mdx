The Pink Bride Show is one of Chattanooga's go-to bridal expos, bringing together local photographers, caterers, florists, venues, and more under one roof at the Convention Center. If you're planning a wedding, it's a solid way to meet vendors and get inspired in an afternoon. Check the Pink Bride Show's website for ticket prices and vendor lineup.
