Tremont Tavern opens its stage every Tuesday night. Host Mike McDade runs the show from 8pm until close. Sign-ups start around 7pm when Mike arrives. Bring a guitar, harmonica, or whatever you play. All skill levels are welcome. The bar is at 1203 Hixson Pike on the North Shore.
