Pierre Bensusan plays Songbirds on Monday, September 29. The French guitarist is known worldwide as a master of the DADGAD tuning. His playing blends Celtic, folk, world music, and chamber jazz into one voice. Fellow guitarists from Mark Knopfler to Pat Metheny have praised his work. The show starts at 7pm.
