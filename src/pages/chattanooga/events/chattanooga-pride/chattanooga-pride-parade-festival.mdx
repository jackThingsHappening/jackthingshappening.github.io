Chattanooga Pride takes over Ross's Landing on the downtown riverfront each October for a free all-day parade and festival. The parade steps off in the morning and winds through downtown before the festival opens at noon. Live music, drag performances, vendors, food stalls, and informational booths run through the evening.

The event is free and family friendly. Full schedule and entertainment lineup are posted at chattanoogapride.com as the date approaches.
