Adults become campers for one night at Drink & Discover: Camp Fear on October 23. This 21+ after-hours event runs from 7 to 10pm at the Creative Discovery Museum. Solve a mystery inspired by classic 1980s summer camp adventures while you play games and work puzzles. General admission is $35 and includes one drink ticket. Bring valid ID for entry.
