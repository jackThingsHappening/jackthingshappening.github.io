Size stops making sense at Out of Scale, the Creative Discovery Museum's play day on August 1. From 11am to 2pm, kids mess around with perspective through hands on activities scattered across the museum, sorting out how big and small stack up. It's members only, so bring your card and let the little buckaroos lose track of what's actually to scale.
