Cheers to the New Year is the museum's family New Year's party. Kids get to ring in the celebration a night early on December 30. The evening runs the museum after hours for two hours of play. Tickets cost $22, and members pay just $10. This event works well for families who want to toast the new year but still get the kids to bed. Buy tickets ahead of time, since holiday evenings fill up fast. Park on Chestnut Street or in a nearby downtown garage.
