Countdown to FUN celebrates a full year of play on New Year's Eve. The museum runs its normal daytime hours with a festive countdown. This daytime party suits young kids who cannot stay up until midnight. Families get to mark the new year early and still make it home for naps. Museum admission covers the day's activities. Come in the morning for the calmest exhibits before the afternoon crowd. Parking is easiest before 11 AM on Chestnut Street.
