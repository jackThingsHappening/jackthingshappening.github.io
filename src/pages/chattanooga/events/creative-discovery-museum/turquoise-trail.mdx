Turquoise Trail is an inclusive trick-or-treat night at the Creative Discovery Museum on October 26, from 6 to 7:30pm. The event uses reduced sensory stimulation - lower lights, lower sound levels, and a quieter environment for children who find standard Halloween events overwhelming. The museum is on Chestnut Street in downtown Chattanooga. Check the CDM website for ticket details. Costumes are welcome. Turquoise Trail is designed to give sensory-sensitive kids and families a Halloween experience that works for them. The event typically sells out - register early through the museum website.
