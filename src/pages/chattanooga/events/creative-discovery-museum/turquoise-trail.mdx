Turquoise Trail is an inclusive trick-or-treat night at the Creative Discovery Museum on October 26, from 6 to 7:30pm. The event uses reduced sensory stimulation and allergy-conscious treats. Accommodations help every child take part comfortably. Admission is free, but you must register in advance.
