Chef in Residence brings hands-on cooking to the Creative Discovery Museum on Saturdays. Dr. Luronda Jennings leads the sessions from 1:30 to 2:30pm. The program runs from November 7 through January 2. Kids learn simple kitchen skills and explore how food is made. Each Saturday offers a fresh activity in the museum's Culinary Corner. Museum admission covers the program, so no separate ticket is needed. The Creative Discovery Museum is on Chestnut Street in downtown Chattanooga. Check the CDM website for the full Saturday schedule.
