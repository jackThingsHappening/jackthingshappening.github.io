STEAM Week kicks off October 10 as the Creative Discovery Museum joins the Tennessee STEAM Festival. The museum fills with hands-on activities across science, technology, engineering, art, and math. Kids build, experiment, and test ideas in the inventor's workshop and across the galleries. The festival runs statewide through late October. This event is for members.
