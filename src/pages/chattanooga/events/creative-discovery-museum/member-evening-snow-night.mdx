Snow Night opens the Winter Playland exhibit at the Creative Discovery Museum. The new exhibit shows how people play in cold weather. Kids try ice climbing, ice skating, and snowman decorating at hands-on stations. The museum serves hot chocolate throughout the evening. The night ends with the first indoor snowfall and a Frosty the Snowman dance party. This event is for members only, and you must register in advance.
