Preschool Adventure: Snow Fun turns the museum into a winter playground for young children. Kids explore the season through hands-on activities and creative play. They make a little pretend snow and dig into sensory discoveries. The program suits toddlers and preschoolers who learn best by touching and doing. This event runs all day, so you can come when it fits your child's nap schedule. Museum admission covers the activities. Arrive early on a weekend to find parking near the Chestnut Street entrance.
