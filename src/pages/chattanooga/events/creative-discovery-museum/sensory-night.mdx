Sensory Night gives children with sensory sensitivities a calm way to explore the Creative Discovery Museum. The museum runs this evening on December 4 from 5:30 to 8pm. Staff dim the overhead announcements and reduce food smells and other strong sensory inputs. Guests can check out headphones at Guest Relations. Admission is free, but you must register in advance. The museum caps attendance at 200 visitors to keep the space quiet and comfortable.
