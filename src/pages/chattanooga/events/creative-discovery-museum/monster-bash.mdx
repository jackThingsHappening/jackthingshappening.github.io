Get in on the spooky-not-scary fun at Monster Bash on October 9, from 6 to 9pm. The Creative Discovery Museum fills with Halloween games and hands-on activities. Monster Bash is designed for young children - the scares are minimal and the focus is on play and creativity. The museum is on Chestnut Street in downtown Chattanooga. Check the CDM website for ticket prices. Members receive a discount. The event runs with extended hours compared to regular admission. Costumes are welcome and encouraged. Expect craft stations, themed games, and character appearances through the evening.
