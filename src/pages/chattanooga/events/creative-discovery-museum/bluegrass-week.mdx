Bluegrass Week fills the Creative Discovery Museum with music starting October 18, from 10am to 5pm. The museum's renovated music area shows off instruments and sounds from different cultures. Kids pick, strum, and learn where bluegrass comes from across the week. Hands-on activities run all day. This event is for members.
