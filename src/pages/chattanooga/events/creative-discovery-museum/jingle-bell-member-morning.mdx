Jingle Bell Member Morning gives CDM members the museum before the public arrives. Families celebrate the holiday season with a festive morning of play. The early start means shorter lines and quieter exhibits for young kids. This event is free, but only current members can attend. Bring your membership card to check in at the door. The 8:30 AM start makes this a good fit for early-rising toddlers. Grab coffee nearby first, since the morning ends at 10 AM.
