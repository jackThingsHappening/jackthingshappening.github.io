Member Morning gives Creative Discovery Museum members early access to the galleries. The next one runs November 22 from 9 to 10am. Members explore the hands-on exhibits before the museum opens to general admission guests. The quiet hour lets families play and build without the usual crowds. This perk is free for members, so bring your card. The Creative Discovery Museum is on Chestnut Street in downtown Chattanooga. Check the CDM website for the full schedule of member mornings and other member benefits.
