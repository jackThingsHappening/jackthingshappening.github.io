Club Discovery is a Tuesday evening respite program at the Creative Discovery Museum. It welcomes children of all abilities in kindergarten through 5th grade. The next session runs November 17 from 5:30 to 7:30pm. Trained volunteers guide the group through a predictable routine of play and activities. Kids make friends, spark curiosity, and explore the museum together. Each night includes snacks and plenty of peer interaction. The cost is $25 per session. The Creative Discovery Museum is on Chestnut Street in downtown Chattanooga.
