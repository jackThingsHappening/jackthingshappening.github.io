Head to the Creative Discovery Museum on September 26 for the National Park City PLAY Street, a free block party from 9am to 3pm. The museum teams up with Chattanooga National Park City to celebrate outdoor play and recreation. Expect hands-on activities, games, and ways to explore the outdoors right in the heart of downtown. Everyone is welcome, and admission is free.
