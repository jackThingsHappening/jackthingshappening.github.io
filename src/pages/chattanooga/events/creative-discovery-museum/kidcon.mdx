Suit up for KIDCON at the Creative Discovery Museum on September 18, from 6 to 8pm. The whole family can dress as their favorite superheroes for a night of games, crafts, and heroic fun. KIDCON is an extended hours event at the CDM on Chestnut Street in downtown Chattanooga. Members receive a discount on admission. The event runs the museum as a convention-themed experience with stations and activities across the building. Check the CDM website for ticket details. Costumes are encouraged and many families come in full superhero gear.
