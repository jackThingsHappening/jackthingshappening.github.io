Jackson Dean plays The Signal on November 7. The Maryland-born country rocker broke through with the number-one hit "Don't Come Lookin'" from his debut album Greenbroke. His sound leans on twangy slide guitar, rock-edged electric riffs, and a deep, weathered voice. Expect uptempo, freewheeling songs built for a loud room. Doors open at 6:30pm.
