SPANKthe80s is a tribute band that recreates the sound and style of the 1980s. The group has performed this show since 1999. Their sets pull hits from Michael Jackson, Prince, Bon Jovi, Journey, and more. Expect bold fashion, a bright light show, and nonstop energy. The band bills itself as the nation's premiere 80s tribute act. The show starts at 8:00 PM.
