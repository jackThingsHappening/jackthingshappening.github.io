The Ooltewah Farmers Market turns festive through December. Vendors bring Christmas foods, baked goods, and seasonal treats for your holiday table. Local makers set up arts and crafts shows alongside the usual farm stands. The market runs every Thursday afternoon on winter hours. Gates are open from 3pm to 5pm. The market sits at Ooltewah Nursery on Main Street, east of Chattanooga. Shop for handmade gifts and stock up on fresh ingredients in one trip. Parking is on site and free. Bring cash, as some small vendors do not take cards. Check the market social pages for the full December date list and guest vendors.
