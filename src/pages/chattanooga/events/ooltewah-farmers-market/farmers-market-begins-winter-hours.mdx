Starting November 5, the Ooltewah Farmers Market shifts to winter hours. The market closes at 5pm each Thursday instead of the usual 6pm. Hours return to 3-6pm in April. The market runs year round with 35+ local farms. Vendors sell produce, meats, baked goods, and dairy every Thursday.
