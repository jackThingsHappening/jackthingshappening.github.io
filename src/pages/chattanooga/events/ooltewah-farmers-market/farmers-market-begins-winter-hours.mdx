Starting November 5, the Ooltewah Farmers Market shifts to winter hours. The market closes at 5pm each Thursday instead of the usual 6pm. Hours return to 3-6pm in spring. The Ooltewah Farmers Market runs every Thursday afternoon on Hunter Road in Ooltewah, Tennessee, east of Chattanooga. The winter hour shift reflects shorter daylight as the season changes. The vendor lineup continues through the winter months with seasonal produce and preserved goods. Check the Ooltewah Farmers Market social pages for any additional changes to the schedule during the fall and winter transition.
