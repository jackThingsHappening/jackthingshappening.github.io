The Ooltewah Farmers Market reopens for the new year on January 7. The market took a short break over the holidays. It now resumes its regular Thursday afternoon schedule. Winter hours run from 3pm to 5pm. Vendors bring cold-weather produce, preserved goods, meats, eggs, and baked items. The market sits at Ooltewah Nursery on Main Street, east of Chattanooga. This first market of the year is a good time to restock after the holidays. Parking is on site and free. Bring cash for the smaller farm stands. Check the market social pages for the January vendor lineup.
