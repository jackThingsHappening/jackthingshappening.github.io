Local beekeepers take over a corner of the market for the day. The Tennessee Valley Beekeepers Association is based in Hamilton County and runs a mentorship program for new beekeepers. They bring displays, honey samples, and education about bee biology, hive management, and local pollinator health. The Ooltewah Farmers Market runs every Thursday afternoon on Hunter Road in Ooltewah. No separate ticket required. The market is free to attend. Check the Ooltewah Farmers Market social pages for the specific time and any special programming scheduled alongside the beekeepers.
