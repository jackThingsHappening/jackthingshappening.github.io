The market normally runs on Thursdays. Thanksgiving week is the exception. Vendors bring farm fresh meats, produce, pies, breads, and dairy timed for holiday cooking. The market has been running since 2013 and features more than 35 local farms. This is the only Tuesday market all year. Free to attend.
