Silver Adventures serves adults aged 50 and above. This winter trip visits Blowing Wind Falls, a 30-foot cascade in the Tennessee River Gorge. Hikers follow the Ritchie Hollow Trail, which climbs through mature hardwood forest. The route passes creek crossings and historic moonshine stills. Winter brings stronger water flow over the falls. The hike runs about 2.8 miles round trip with steady elevation gain. An Outdoor Chattanooga guide leads the group at a steady pace. Wear sturdy shoes and bring water and a snack.

Registration opens three weeks prior and closes two days before the event.
