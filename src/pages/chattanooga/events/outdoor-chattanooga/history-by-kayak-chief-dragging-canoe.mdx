This guided paddle through the Tennessee River Gorge traces the life and history of Chief Dragging Canoe, a Cherokee leader who resisted colonial expansion in the 18th century. Outdoor Chattanooga leads this free kayak tour from Ross's Landing. Guides interpret the history along the waterfront and gorge. All equipment is provided. The paddle runs about two hours at a relaxed pace. Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website. No prior kayaking experience is required. The tour is free but space is limited. Bring water, sunscreen, and clothes that can get wet.
