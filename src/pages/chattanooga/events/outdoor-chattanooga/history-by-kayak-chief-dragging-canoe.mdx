This guided paddle through the Tennessee River Gorge traces the life and history of Chief Dragging Canoe, a Cherokee leader who fought to protect this land. Guides share oral histories and landmark context as the group moves downstream.

No kayak experience is required. All gear is provided.

Registration opens three weeks prior and closes two days before the event.
