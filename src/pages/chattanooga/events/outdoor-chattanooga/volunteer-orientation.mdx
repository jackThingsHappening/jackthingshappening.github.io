Outdoor Chattanooga runs this orientation for anyone interested in volunteering with their programs. Staff walk through available roles, from leading hikes to assisting with paddle events.

Attendance is required before joining any volunteer crew. The session is free and held at the Coolidge Park center.

Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website.
