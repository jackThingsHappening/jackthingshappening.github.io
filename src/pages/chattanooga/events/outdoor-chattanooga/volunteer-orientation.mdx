Outdoor Chattanooga runs this orientation for anyone interested in volunteering with their programs. Staff walk through the types of volunteer roles available, the commitment expected, and how to sign up for specific events. The orientation takes about an hour at the Outdoor Chattanooga facility in Coolidge Park. Volunteers support everything from cleanup events to instructional programs. No outdoor experience is required for most roles. Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website. This is a free orientation open to adults. It is the required first step before participating as a volunteer on any Outdoor Chattanooga event.
