Outdoor Chattanooga hosts this Halloween-themed archery session at Coolidge Park. Participants shoot at zombie targets in a supervised range while learning basic bow form. Equipment is provided. No experience needed. This is the first of three sessions offered across the Halloween weekend. The session runs about an hour. Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website. It is free or low cost. Bring comfortable clothes and closed-toe shoes. The event works for beginners of all ages. Check the registration page for the specific time slot and any age restrictions for participants.
