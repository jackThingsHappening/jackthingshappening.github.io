Outdoor Chattanooga hosts this Halloween-themed archery session at Coolidge Park. Participants shoot at zombie targets in a try-archery format open to all ages and skill levels.

Staff handle all safety instruction and provide every piece of equipment. No experience is needed.

Costumes are encouraged. Registration opens three weeks before the event.
