Toddler Treks brings young children and their grownups outdoors at Renaissance Park. This session looks at winter adaptations in local plants and animals. Guides lead short walks, simple games, and hands-on nature play. The program builds early curiosity about the natural world. It suits toddlers and preschool-age children with a parent or caregiver. The pace stays slow and the trails stay flat. Dress for cool weather and wear shoes that can get muddy. Space is limited, so register early through Outdoor Chattanooga.

Registration opens three weeks prior and closes two days before the event.
