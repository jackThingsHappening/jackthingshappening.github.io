Outdoor Chattanooga's beginner mountain biking class covers core skills on a flat grass field. Instructors use progressive drills to build braking, cornering, and balance.

Loaner bikes and helmets are available at no cost. No prior mountain bike experience is needed.

Registration opens three weeks before the event. Space is limited.
