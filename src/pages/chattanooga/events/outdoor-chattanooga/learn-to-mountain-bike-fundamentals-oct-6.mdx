Outdoor Chattanooga's beginner mountain biking class covers core skills on a flat grass field. Instructors use progressive drills to teach body position, braking, and balance before guiding riders onto a beginner trail. Bikes and helmets are provided. No prior experience is required. This October 6 session runs about two hours at Enterprise South Nature Park. Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website. The class is part of a recurring beginner series that runs through fall. Space is limited. Bring water and closed-toe shoes.
