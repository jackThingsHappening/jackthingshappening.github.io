Silver Adventures is Outdoor Chattanooga's program for adults 60 and older. This plein air session invites participants to paint outdoors at Coolidge Park along the river.

Bring your own supplies or use basic materials available on-site. No formal art training is needed.

Registration opens three weeks before the event.
