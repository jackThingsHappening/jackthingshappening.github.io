Silver Adventures is Outdoor Chattanooga's program for adults 60 and older. This plein air session invites participants to sketch or paint outdoors at a local natural area. Supplies are provided. No prior art experience is needed. The session is relaxed and focused on the experience of making something in nature. Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website. The specific site is shared in the registration confirmation. The Silver Adventures program runs monthly throughout the year with activities tailored to older adults who want to stay active outdoors.
