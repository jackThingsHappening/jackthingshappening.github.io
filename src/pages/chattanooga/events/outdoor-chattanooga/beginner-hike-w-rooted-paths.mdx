Rooted Paths leads this relaxed hike along Big Soddy Creek Gulf. The route follows a flat, wide gravel trail that works for all skill levels.

The pace stays slow so new hikers can enjoy the creek and fall scenery. Wear closed-toe shoes with good traction and bring water.

Registration opens three weeks before the event.
