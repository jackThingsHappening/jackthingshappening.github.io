Rooted Paths leads this relaxed hike along Big Soddy Creek Gulf. The route follows a flat, wide gravel trail that works well for new hikers. Guides cover trail basics and introduce participants to the geology and plant life of the area. Outdoor Chattanooga co-presents this event. The hike is free and open to adults. Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website. Wear comfortable shoes and bring water. Big Soddy Creek Gulf is in Soddy-Daisy, about 20 minutes north of downtown Chattanooga. The area features a gorge carved by the creek through sedimentary rock.
