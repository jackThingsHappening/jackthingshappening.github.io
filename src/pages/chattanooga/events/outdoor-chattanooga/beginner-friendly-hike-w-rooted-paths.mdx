Rooted Paths leads this 1-2 mile walk designed for people new to hiking. The pace is relaxed and the terrain stays accessible throughout.

You will build trail confidence without feeling rushed or overwhelmed. Dress in layers and wear closed-toe shoes with traction.

Registration opens three weeks before the event.
