Rooted Paths leads this 1-2 mile walk designed for people new to hiking. The pace is relaxed and the terrain stays accessible. Guides cover basic trail etiquette and introduce participants to the natural features along the route. Outdoor Chattanooga co-presents this event. The hike is free and open to adults. Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website. Wear comfortable shoes and bring water. The specific trail is shared in the registration confirmation. Rooted Paths is a Chattanooga-based organization focused on making outdoor spaces accessible to new hikers and underrepresented communities.
