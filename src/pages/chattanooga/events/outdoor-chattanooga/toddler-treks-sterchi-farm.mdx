Toddler Treks is Outdoor Chattanooga's nature series for families with young children. This session visits Sterchi Farm for a guided walk suited to small legs and short attention spans. Staff lead the group through the farm property, introducing basic animal and plant identification along the way. The event is free or low cost. Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website. Children must be accompanied by an adult. Bring snacks, water, and comfortable clothes. Sterchi Farm is located in the North Chattanooga area. The Toddler Treks series runs monthly and features different outdoor sites across the region.
