Toddler Treks is Outdoor Chattanooga's nature series for families with young children. This session visits Sterchi Farm for a short walk and hands-on outdoor exploration.

The program is designed for children ages 1-5 with a parent or caregiver. Walk-ups are welcome but registration is encouraged.

Dress kids for the outdoors and bring water.
