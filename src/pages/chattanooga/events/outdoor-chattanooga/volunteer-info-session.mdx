Outdoor Chattanooga hosts this info session for new and returning volunteers. Staff walk through upcoming volunteer opportunities and explain how to sign up. Roles range from supporting paddle trips to helping with guided hikes and community events.

The session is free and runs at the Coolidge Park center. New volunteers learn what each role involves before they commit. Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website.
