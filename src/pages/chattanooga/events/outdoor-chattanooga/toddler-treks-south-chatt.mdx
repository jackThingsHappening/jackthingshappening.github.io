This program pairs young children with a parent or caregiver for a short nature walk. Guides lead easy activities that help toddlers explore the outdoors. The walk moves at a slow pace built for small legs. Children learn about plants, bugs, and the sights along the trail. The program builds early comfort with nature and outdoor play.

Registration opens three weeks prior and closes two days before the event.
