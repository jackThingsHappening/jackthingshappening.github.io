Outdoor Chattanooga's beginner mountain biking series continues through late October. This session uses a progressive drill format on a grass field, covering braking, balance, and cornering for first-time riders.

Loaner bikes and helmets are available. No gear is needed.

Registration opens three weeks before the event. Space is limited.
