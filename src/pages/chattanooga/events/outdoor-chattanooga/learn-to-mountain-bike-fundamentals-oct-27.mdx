Outdoor Chattanooga's beginner mountain biking series continues through late October. This session uses a progressive drill format on a flat grass field, then transitions to a beginner trail loop. Bikes and helmets are provided. Instructors focus on body position, braking, and turning control. No prior experience is required. This late October session is one of the final opportunities in the fall program. Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website. Space is limited. Bring water and closed-toe shoes. Enterprise South Nature Park is about 15 minutes from downtown Chattanooga.
