The Cherokee Chapter of the Tennessee Sierra Club meets monthly at Outdoor Chattanooga in Coolidge Park. Members plan upcoming outings, discuss local conservation issues, and hear from guest speakers on environmental topics. The meeting is free and open to the public. No membership is required to attend. The chapter organizes regular hikes, paddles, and advocacy efforts in the Chattanooga region. Meetings typically run about 90 minutes. Check the Cherokee Chapter's website for the specific agenda and any scheduled speakers. New members and curious newcomers are welcome.
