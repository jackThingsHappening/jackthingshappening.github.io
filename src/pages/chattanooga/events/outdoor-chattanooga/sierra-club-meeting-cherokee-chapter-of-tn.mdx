The Cherokee Chapter of the Tennessee Sierra Club meets monthly at Outdoor Chattanooga in Coolidge Park. Members plan upcoming outings, discuss local conservation campaigns, and welcome new participants.

No membership is required to attend. The meeting is free and open to the public.

Visit sierraclub.org/tennessee/cherokee for more information about the chapter.
