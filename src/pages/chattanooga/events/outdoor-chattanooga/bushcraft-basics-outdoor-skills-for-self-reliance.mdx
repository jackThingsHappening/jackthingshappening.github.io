Bushcraft Basics teaches the core skills that build confidence outdoors. The hands-on class covers fire craft, shelter ideas, and basic tool use. Students practice each skill with guidance from Outdoor Chattanooga staff. The goal is simple self-reliance in a backcountry setting. The session suits beginners who want a solid foundation. No prior experience is needed to take part. Dress for the weather and bring water and a snack. Register through Outdoor Chattanooga, since the class size stays small.

Registration opens three weeks prior and closes two days before the event.
