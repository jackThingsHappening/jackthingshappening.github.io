Outdoor Chattanooga leads this guided 8-mile out-and-back ride on the South Chickamauga Creek Greenway. The trail is paved and flat, making it accessible for most fitness levels. Bikes are provided. The group rides together at a conversational pace. Guides point out natural features along the creek. Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website. Bring water and wear comfortable clothes. The ride starts at Outdoor Chattanooga in Coolidge Park. The South Chickamauga Creek Greenway connects multiple Chattanooga neighborhoods and is one of the most popular cycling routes in the city.
