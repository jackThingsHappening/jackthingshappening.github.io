Outdoor Chattanooga leads this guided 8-mile out-and-back ride on the South Chickamauga Creek Greenway. The trail is paved and flat, making it a good fit for casual riders.

Staff lead the group and set a pace that works for beginners. Bring your own bike and helmet.

Registration opens three weeks before the event.
