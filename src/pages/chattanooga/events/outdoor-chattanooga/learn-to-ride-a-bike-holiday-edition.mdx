This holiday edition of Learn to Ride a Bike runs over winter break. The class helps adults and children ride a bicycle in one session. Instructors start with balance and work up to pedaling and steering. Bikes are provided in several sizes, so you can come empty-handed. No experience is assumed, and the class builds from zero. The session takes place on flat, open ground. Two time slots run that day at 10:30 AM and 12:00 PM. Wear comfortable clothes and closed-toe shoes, and register early.

Registration opens three weeks prior and closes two days before the event.
