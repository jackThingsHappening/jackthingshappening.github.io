The Chattanooga Grotto is a local caving club that meets the first Monday of each month at Outdoor Chattanooga in Coolidge Park. Members share trip reports, plan upcoming cave visits, and discuss gear.

No caving experience is needed to attend. The meeting is free and open to anyone curious about underground exploration.

The Grotto is affiliated with the National Speleological Society.
