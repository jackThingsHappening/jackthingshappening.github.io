The Chattanooga Bike Club holds its monthly meeting the third Monday of each month at Outdoor Chattanooga in Coolidge Park. Members plan upcoming group rides, share route recommendations, and welcome cyclists of all skill levels.

The club focuses on recreational riding around the Chattanooga region. No membership is required to attend a meeting.

Meetings are free and open to the public.
