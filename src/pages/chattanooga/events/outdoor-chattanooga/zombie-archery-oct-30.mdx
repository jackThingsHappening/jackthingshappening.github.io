The final session of Outdoor Chattanooga's Halloween archery weekend at Coolidge Park. Staff run try-archery drills with zombie-themed targets in a safe, supervised range. No experience needed. Equipment is provided. The session runs about an hour. This is the last of three sessions offered October 28-30. Participants shoot recurve bows at paper zombie targets while learning basic form. Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website. The event is free or low cost. Check registration for the specific time slot. Bring comfortable clothes and prepare to have fun.
