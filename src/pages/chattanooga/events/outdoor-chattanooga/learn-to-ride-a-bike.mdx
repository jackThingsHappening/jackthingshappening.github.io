Outdoor Chattanooga's bike riding class is for adults and children who have never learned or want a refresher. Instructors start with balance and work up to pedaling and steering on flat ground. Bikes are provided in multiple sizes. No experience is assumed - the class builds from zero. The session runs about 90 minutes. Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website. Bring comfortable clothes and closed-toe shoes. The class takes place in a flat, open area. This is one of Outdoor Chattanooga's most inclusive programs and fills quickly. Book your spot as soon as registration opens.
