This guided hike marks National Take a Hike Day. The group walks the trails at Walden's Ridge Park, a 200-acre park near Red Bank. A guide leads the route and points out features along the way. The hike suits people who want a relaxed morning outdoors. Bring water and wear sturdy shoes. The pace stays comfortable for most fitness levels.

Registration opens three weeks prior and closes two days before the event.
