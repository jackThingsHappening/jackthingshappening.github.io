This hands-on workshop introduces the basics of home fermentation. Matt Cone of Chattanooga Culture leads the session. You will learn how to brew kombucha using simple, traditional methods. The class also covers lacto-fermented foods you can make at home. Cone shares the steps, tools, and tips for safe fermenting. The workshop suits beginners who want to start brewing their own drinks. No prior experience is needed to take part. Register through Outdoor Chattanooga, since space is limited.

Registration opens three weeks prior and closes two days before the event.
