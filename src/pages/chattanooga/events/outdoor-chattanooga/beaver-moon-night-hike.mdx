This guided hike explores the trails by the light of the full Beaver Moon. Outdoor Chattanooga leads the walk with the Tennessee Aquarium. Experts share facts about nocturnal wildlife and local ecosystems. They also explain the meaning behind the Beaver Moon. The program suits adults who want to experience nature at night. Bring water and dress for cool evening weather.

Registration opens three weeks prior and closes two days before the event.
