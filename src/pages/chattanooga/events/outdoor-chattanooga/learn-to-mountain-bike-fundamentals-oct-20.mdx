Outdoor Chattanooga's beginner mountain biking class runs progressive drills on a flat grass field. Instructors focus on braking, cornering, and body position for riders who have never been on a mountain bike.

Loaner bikes and helmets are available at no cost. No equipment is needed.

Registration opens three weeks before the event. Space is limited.
