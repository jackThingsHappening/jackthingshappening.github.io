Outdoor Chattanooga's beginner mountain biking class runs progressive drills on a flat grass field. Instructors focus on body position, braking, and turning before guiding the group to a beginner trail loop. Bikes and helmets are provided. No prior experience is needed. This October 20 session runs about two hours at Enterprise South Nature Park. Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website. Space is limited - register early. Bring water and closed-toe shoes. This is the third of four FUNdamentals sessions offered in October as part of the fall beginner mountain biking series.
