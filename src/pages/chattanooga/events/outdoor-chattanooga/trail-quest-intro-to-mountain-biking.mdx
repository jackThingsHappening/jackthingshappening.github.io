Trail Quest is Outdoor Chattanooga's beginner mountain biking program. Classes start on a grass field where instructors teach balance, braking, and turning before moving to packed dirt trails. The program is designed for people with little or no mountain biking experience. Outdoor Chattanooga provides bikes and helmets. The program runs at Enterprise South Nature Park. Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website. Space is limited and sessions fill early. Bring water and closed-toe shoes. The full multi-week program builds to independent trail riding.
