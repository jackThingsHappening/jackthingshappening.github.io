Trail Quest is Outdoor Chattanooga's beginner mountain biking program. Classes start on a grass field where instructors cover braking, balance, and cornering before you move to light trail riding.

Loaner bikes and helmets are available. No prior mountain bike experience is needed.

Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website. Space is limited.
