Outdoor Chattanooga leads this free bicycle tour through Chickamauga and Chattanooga National Military Park. Guides stop at key positions and monuments to explain the two-day battle of September 1863, the bloodiest battle of the Western theater. The route covers about 10 miles on paved park roads. Bikes are provided. No prior cycling fitness is required. Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website. Bring water and dress for the weather. The park is 10 miles south of downtown Chattanooga. The tour runs about three hours total.
