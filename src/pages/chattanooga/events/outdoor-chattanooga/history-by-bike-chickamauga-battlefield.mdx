Outdoor Chattanooga leads this free bicycle tour through Chickamauga and Chattanooga National Military Park. Guides stop at key Civil War positions and monuments to explain the battle's history and significance.

Bring your own bike in good working order. Helmets are required.

Registration opens three weeks before the event.
