Outdoor Chattanooga and the Chickamauga and Chattanooga National Military Park co-lead this free guided kayak tour to Patton Island in the Tennessee River. Guides explain the island's Civil War history during the paddle. All equipment is provided. No prior paddling experience is required. The tour runs about two hours at a relaxed pace. Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website. Space is limited. Bring water, sunscreen, and clothes that can get wet. The launch point is at Ross's Landing downtown. This is one of Outdoor Chattanooga's most popular history-by-water programs.
