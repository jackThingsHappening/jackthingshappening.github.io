Outdoor Chattanooga and the Chickamauga and Chattanooga National Military Park co-lead this free guided kayak tour to Patton Island in the Tennessee River. Educators share Civil War-era history connected to the river's military crossings.

No kayak experience is required. All gear is provided.

Registration opens three weeks prior and space is limited.
