Adult Archery: Aim Your Game is an indoor class for grown-up archers. It meets at the Philip Grymes Outdoor Chattanooga Center in Coolidge Park. Instructors help you sharpen stance, aim, and a smooth release. All equipment is provided, so you do not need your own bow. The class welcomes both new shooters and those wanting to improve. Groups stay small for direct coaching. Wear closed-toe shoes and clothing you can move in. Register through Outdoor Chattanooga, since spots fill quickly.

Registration opens three weeks prior and closes two days before the event.
