Trail Quest is Outdoor Chattanooga's multi-session beginner mountain biking program. Classes begin on a grass field where instructors cover braking, balance, and cornering before moving to the trail.

Loaner bikes and helmets are available. No prior mountain bike experience is needed.

Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website. Space is limited.
