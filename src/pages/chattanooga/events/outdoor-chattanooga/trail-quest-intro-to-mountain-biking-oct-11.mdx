Trail Quest is Outdoor Chattanooga's multi-session beginner mountain biking program. Classes begin on a grass field where instructors teach balance, braking, and turning before moving to packed dirt trails. This October 11 session is part of the ongoing fall program at Enterprise South Nature Park. Bikes and helmets are provided. No prior experience is needed. Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website. Space fills quickly. Bring water and closed-toe shoes. The program builds rider confidence across multiple sessions and prepares participants for independent trail use in the park.
