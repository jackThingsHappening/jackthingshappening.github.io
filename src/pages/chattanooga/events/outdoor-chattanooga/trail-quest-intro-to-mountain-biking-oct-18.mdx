Trail Quest is Outdoor Chattanooga's recurring beginner mountain biking program running through the fall. This Saturday session at Enterprise South Nature Park starts on a grass field and moves to a beginner trail loop. Bikes and helmets are provided. Instructors teach balance, braking, and body position from the start. No prior experience is needed. Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website. The program is part of a multi-session series that builds riding skills over time. Space fills fast. Bring water and closed-toe shoes.
