Trail Quest is Outdoor Chattanooga's recurring beginner mountain biking program running through the fall. This Saturday session builds on field fundamentals and progressively introduces trail riding skills.

Loaner bikes and helmets are available. Enrollment is limited and registration opens three weeks before the event.

No prior mountain bike experience is needed.
