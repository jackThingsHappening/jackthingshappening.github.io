Outdoor Chattanooga leads this guided paddle along the downtown waterfront at Ross's Landing. Guides trace the city's industrial heritage, historic river crossings, and waterfront transformation as the group paddles through calm water.

No kayak experience is required. All gear is provided.

Registration opens three weeks before the event.
