Outdoor Chattanooga leads this guided paddle along the downtown waterfront at Ross's Landing. Guides trace the city's industrial and military history while the group paddles the Tennessee River shoreline. All equipment is provided. No prior kayaking experience is required. The tour runs about two hours at a relaxed pace. Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website. Space is limited. Bring water, sunscreen, and clothes that can get wet. The tour departs from Ross's Landing and covers the downtown waterfront from the river. This is a popular introductory tour for visitors and longtime residents alike.
