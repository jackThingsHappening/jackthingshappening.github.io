Day two of Outdoor Chattanooga's Halloween archery weekend at Coolidge Park. Zombie targets, basic technique instruction, and equipment provided. No experience needed. The session runs about an hour. This is the second of three sessions offered October 28-30. Participants shoot recurve bows at paper zombie targets while learning basic form from staff instructors. Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website. The event is free or low cost. Bring comfortable clothes and closed-toe shoes. The weekend format lets families or groups attend different days. Check the registration page for the specific time slot.
