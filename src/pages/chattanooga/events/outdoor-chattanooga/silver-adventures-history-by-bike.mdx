This free program serves adults 50 and older. Riders follow a guide through downtown Chickamauga on bicycles. The route covers local history at an easy pace. Outdoor Chattanooga provides bikes and helmets. The ride suits people who want gentle exercise and local stories. Previous cycling experience helps but is not required.

Registration opens three weeks prior and closes two days before the event.
