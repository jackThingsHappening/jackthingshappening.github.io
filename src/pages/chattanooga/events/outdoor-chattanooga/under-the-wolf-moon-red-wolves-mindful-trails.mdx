This winter program ties the January full moon to a guided trail walk. The January moon is known as the Wolf Moon, named for howling wolves in winter. The outing pairs a mindful walk with lessons about red wolves. Guides share facts about this rare and endangered species. The slow pace gives time to notice the quiet winter landscape. The program suits adults and older children who enjoy nature walks. Dress warmly and wear sturdy shoes for the trail. Register through Outdoor Chattanooga ahead of time.

Registration opens three weeks prior and closes two days before the event.
