The Appalachian Conservation Institute leads this guided hike at Little Sequatchie Cove, a privately held natural area in the Sequatchie Valley. The hike covers about four miles and focuses on forest ecology, land conservation, and the natural history of the cove. The event is free. Outdoor Chattanooga co-presents. Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website. Bring water, hiking boots, and layers. The drive to Little Sequatchie Cove takes about an hour from downtown Chattanooga. This is a rare opportunity to access a privately held conservation site with expert guides leading the way.
