The Appalachian Conservation Institute leads this guided hike at Little Sequatchie Cove, a privately held natural area in the Cumberland Plateau foothills. Guides cover conservation topics tied to the surrounding land as the group walks.

Expect 3-4 miles of moderate terrain. Sturdy footwear and water are required.

Space is limited and registration closes two days before the event.
