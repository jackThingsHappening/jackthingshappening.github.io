Try Archery is a free indoor class for people new to the sport. It runs at the Philip Grymes Outdoor Chattanooga Center in Coolidge Park. Instructors cover safety, stance, and how to aim and release. All equipment is provided, so no gear is needed. The class welcomes beginners of all ages and skill levels. Staff keep groups small for hands-on help. Wear closed-toe shoes and clothing you can move in. This session fills fast, so sign up as soon as registration opens.

Registration opens three weeks prior and closes two days before the event.
