Outdoor Chattanooga hosts this volunteer paddling cleanup on the Tennessee River. You kayak or canoe while picking up litter from the water and banks.

All skill levels are welcome. Kayaks and safety gear are provided. Dress for the water and bring sunscreen and water.

Registration opens three weeks before the event and closes two days prior.
