Outdoor Chattanooga hosts this volunteer paddling cleanup on the Tennessee River. You kayak or canoe while picking up litter and debris along the shoreline and in the water. All equipment is provided. The event runs a few hours and covers a stretch of river near Chattanooga. Volunteers have cleared tons of trash from local waterways in past years. Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website. No prior paddling experience is required. Bring clothes that can get wet, sunscreen, and water. This is a free volunteer event open to adults and older teens.
