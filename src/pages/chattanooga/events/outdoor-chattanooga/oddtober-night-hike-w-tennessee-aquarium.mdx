Outdoor Chattanooga and the Tennessee Aquarium co-lead this guided night hike. A naturalist walks the group through nocturnal animal behavior and helps participants spot owls, bats, and insects active after dark. The hike covers a local trail at an easy pace. Flashlights or headlamps are recommended. Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website. The specific trail is shared in the registration confirmation. The event is part of the annual ODDtober series. It is free or low cost. This is a popular fall program - register early. The hike works well for families with older children.
