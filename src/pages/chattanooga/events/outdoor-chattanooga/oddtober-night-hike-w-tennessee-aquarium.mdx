Outdoor Chattanooga and the Tennessee Aquarium co-lead this guided night hike. A naturalist walks the group through nocturnal ecology, covering what animals are active after dark and why darkness matters for wildlife.

Bring a headlamp and dress for cool evening temperatures. No prior hiking experience is needed.

Registration opens three weeks before the event.
