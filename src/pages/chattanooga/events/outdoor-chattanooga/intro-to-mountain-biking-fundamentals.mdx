This FUNdamentals class introduces new riders to mountain biking in a controlled field environment. Instructors cover balance, braking, maneuvering, body position, and bike control.

Progressive drills build your skills before you ever reach a trail. Loaner bikes and helmets are available if you need them.

Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website. Space is limited.
