This FUNdamentals class introduces new riders to mountain biking in a controlled field environment. Instructors cover balance, braking, shifting gears, and cornering before guiding the group onto a beginner trail. Bikes and helmets are provided by Outdoor Chattanooga. No prior mountain biking experience is needed. The class runs about two hours at Enterprise South Nature Park. Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website. Space is limited and sessions fill quickly. Bring water and wear closed-toe shoes. This class is the foundation for the Trail Quest program and other beginner cycling events.
