Historian Greg Grant leads this easy 2-mile walk along South Chickamauga Creek Greenway. The route passes five Civil War-era railroad bridges. Grant explains how these railroads shaped the war and Chattanooga's growth as a transportation hub. The walk blends outdoor exercise with local history. Bring comfortable walking shoes and water. A shuttle supports the route.

Registration opens three weeks prior and closes two days before the event.
