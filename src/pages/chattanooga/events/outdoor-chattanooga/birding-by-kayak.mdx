This guided kayak tour explores Hiwassee Wildlife Refuge near Birchwood. Paddlers circle Hiwassee Island to view migrating Sandhill Cranes and American White Pelicans. The early morning trip offers the best chances to see and hear the birds. Outdoor Chattanooga provides all kayak gear and a guide. The tour suits ages 16 and up. Previous kayak experience helps in the colder season but is not required.

Registration opens three weeks prior and closes two days before the event.
