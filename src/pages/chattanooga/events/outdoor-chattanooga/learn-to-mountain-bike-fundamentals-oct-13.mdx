Outdoor Chattanooga's beginner mountain biking class uses progressive drills on a flat grass field. Instructors cover braking, weight transfer, and cornering for riders with no prior mountain bike experience.

Loaner bikes and helmets are available. This session is a strong starting point before any trail riding programs.

Registration opens three weeks before the event.
