Outdoor Chattanooga's beginner mountain biking class uses progressive drills on a flat grass field. Instructors cover braking technique, body position, and turning fundamentals before riders move to a beginner trail loop. Bikes and helmets are provided. The class runs about two hours. No prior mountain biking experience is needed. This session meets at Enterprise South Nature Park. Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website. Space fills fast - book early. Bring water and wear closed-toe shoes. The skills covered transfer directly to any trail in the region.
