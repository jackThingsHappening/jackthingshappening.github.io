This guided hike covers about 4 miles on the TVA trail at Little Cedar Mountain. The trail borders Nickajack Lake and offers water views. The ground can be uneven with rocks and roots. The route rates as moderate, so hikers need good footwear. A guide leads the group and shares details about the area. Bring water and dress for the weather.

Registration opens three weeks prior and closes two days before the event.
