Outdoor Chattanooga marks National Take a Hike Day with a group hike led by the organization's Director. The outing celebrates the region's trails and the simple reward of time outside.

The trail location is set closer to the date. Wear sturdy shoes and bring water for the walk.

Check the Outdoor Chattanooga website for the meeting spot and start time.
