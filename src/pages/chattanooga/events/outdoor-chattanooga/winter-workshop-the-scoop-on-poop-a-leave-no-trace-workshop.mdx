This Leave No Trace workshop covers a topic every hiker faces outdoors. It explains proper bathroom practices on trails and in the backcountry. Good habits protect trails, waterways, and local wildlife. Outdoor Chattanooga staff keep the session fun and clear. You will learn where to go, how to pack out waste, and why it matters. The workshop suits hikers, paddlers, and campers of all levels. No experience is needed to take part. Register through Outdoor Chattanooga to save a seat.

Registration opens three weeks prior and closes two days before the event.
