Adult Archery is an indoor class built for grown-up archers. It meets at the Philip Grymes Outdoor Chattanooga Center in Coolidge Park. Instructors cover safety, form, and aiming at indoor targets. All equipment is provided, so you do not need your own bow. The class suits both first-time and returning adult shooters. Staff give hands-on coaching in a small group setting. Wear closed-toe shoes and clothes you can move in freely. Register through Outdoor Chattanooga, since spots are limited.

Registration opens three weeks prior and closes two days before the event.
