Outdoor Chattanooga hosts this guided evening paddle timed for the Hunter's Moon on Chickamauga Lake. Participants watch the moon rise over the water while paddling a calm stretch of the lake. All equipment is provided. No prior kayaking experience is required. The paddle runs about two hours at a relaxed pace. Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website. Space is limited. Bring layers and clothes that can get wet. The paddle launches from a site on Chickamauga Lake. Full Moon Kayak events sell out quickly - book your spot as soon as registration opens.
