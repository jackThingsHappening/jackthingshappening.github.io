Outdoor Chattanooga hosts this guided evening paddle timed for the Hunter's Moon on Chickamauga Lake. Participants watch the moonrise over the water and navigate around lake islands by moonlight.

Guides handle all safety and route-finding. All gear is provided.

Registration opens three weeks before the event. This trip fills quickly, so register early.
