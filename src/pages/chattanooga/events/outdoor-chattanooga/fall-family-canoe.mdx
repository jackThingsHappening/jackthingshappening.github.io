Outdoor Chattanooga leads this relaxed family canoe trip on North Chickamauga Creek. The flat water is ideal for beginners and young paddlers. All equipment is provided. Guides instruct basic paddling strokes and help families navigate the creek. The trip covers a few miles at an easy pace. Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website. Children must be old enough to wear a life jacket correctly. Bring water, sunscreen, and clothes that can get wet. North Chickamauga Creek is a clean, calm waterway that cuts through the northern suburbs of Chattanooga.
