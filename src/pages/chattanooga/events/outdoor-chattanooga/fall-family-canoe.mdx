Outdoor Chattanooga leads this relaxed family canoe trip on North Chickamauga Creek. The flat water is ideal for beginners, and instructors handle safety briefings and basic paddle strokes.

All canoes and gear are provided. Children must be accompanied by a registered adult.

Registration opens three weeks before the event.
