Outdoor Chattanooga leads this guided hike through Shakerag Hollow at Sewanee, Tennessee. The trail follows a creek through a deep hollow lined with hardwoods at peak fall color.

The hike covers approximately 4 miles. Sturdy footwear and water are required.

This event fills quickly. Register early through the Outdoor Chattanooga website.
