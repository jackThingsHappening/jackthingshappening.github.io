Outdoor Chattanooga leads this guided hike through Shakerag Hollow at Sewanee, Tennessee. The trail follows a creek through old-growth forest known for exceptional fall color. The hollow is on the campus of the University of the South. The route covers about four miles round trip at a moderate pace. Guides identify trees and explain the geology of the Cumberland Plateau. Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website. Bring water, hiking boots, and layers for changing temperatures. The drive to Sewanee takes about 45 minutes from downtown Chattanooga.
