Outdoor Chattanooga runs this beginner disc golf session at a local Chattanooga course. Instructors cover grip, stance, and throwing form before the group plays a few holes together. Equipment is provided for participants who do not own discs. The session runs about two hours. No prior disc golf experience is needed. The class introduces the sport in a relaxed, low-pressure format. Register through the Outdoor Chattanooga website. Bring water and wear comfortable shoes. The course used for this session varies - check the registration page for the specific location.
