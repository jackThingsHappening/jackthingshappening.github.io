Outdoor Chattanooga runs this beginner disc golf session at a local Chattanooga course. Instructors cover grip, stance, and basic throwing technique before the group plays.

Discs are provided. No experience is needed. The session ends with a short round on a beginner-friendly layout.

Registration opens three weeks before the event.
