The Lonesome Wave plays land-locked surf rock from Chattanooga. The band leans on the sound of The Ventures, Duane Eddy, and Booker T and the MGs. For this show they rework holiday songs in that twangy, reverb-heavy style. Randy Steele joins them. Steele is a Chattanooga banjo player and firefighter who won an Independent Music Award for his bluegrass writing. Two local acts split one stage, so you get surf guitar and bluegrass banjo in the same night. The set starts at 7:00 PM at Songbirds. Come early if you want a spot up front.
