Hannah Wicklund is a rock guitarist from Hilton Head Island, South Carolina. She started gigging at age eight and built her sound on classic rock. Her riffs draw on Jimi Hendrix, Fleetwood Mac, and Tom Petty. Expect blistering solos and searing vocals. Slow Funeral and Leah Blevins open the show. The set starts at 7:00 PM at Songbirds.
