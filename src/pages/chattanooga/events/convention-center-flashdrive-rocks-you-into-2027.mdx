FlashDrive rings in the new year at the Chattanooga Convention Center on December 31, 2026. The band starts at 8:00 PM and plays until the countdown to 2027. FlashDrive is a Chattanooga party band that covers Top 40 dance hits. Their sets span 12 genres and seven decades of music. Dress up, dance, and toast midnight with a live band on stage. Book your table early because early bird pricing ends November 30.
