Two Chattanooga artists share a stage for an evening of original work. Laura Candler reads from Common Name, her 2026 Poets Corner Chapbook Contest winner, with poems published in Gulf Coast, SWING, and Variant Literature. Alva Leigh plays songs from Wilderness, her album about motherhood and identity made after years performing in London with Lewis and Leigh. The conversation between sets is part of the format. The Reading Room fits around 40 people. Get there early.
