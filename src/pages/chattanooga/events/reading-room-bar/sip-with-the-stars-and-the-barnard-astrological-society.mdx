Chattanooga's amateur astronomy club takes over the bar for an evening. The Barnard Astronomical Society normally meets at UTC's Clarence T. Jones Observatory near Brainerd Road. At the Reading Room, the format goes casual. Expect conversations about recent sky observations, upcoming celestial events, and the basics of backyard astronomy. No telescope or prior experience is needed. Free and open to newcomers and longtime stargazers alike.
