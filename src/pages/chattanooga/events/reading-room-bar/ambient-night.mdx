Slow, texture-driven music fills the Reading Room once a month for Ambient Night. The format shifts. Some evenings bring a solo performer playing live drones, loops, or slow instrumentals. Other nights lean toward curated listening sets. The room is small and warmly lit, with used books on the walls. Music and bookshelves work well together. Doors open at 7pm, music at 7:30.
