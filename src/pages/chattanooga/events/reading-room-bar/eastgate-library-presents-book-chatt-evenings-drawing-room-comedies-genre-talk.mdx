Eastgate Library brings its Book Chatt Evenings series to the Reading Room for a talk on drawing room comedies. The genre covers witty, dialogue driven fiction and theater set in upper class domestic spaces. Oscar Wilde and Noel Coward wrote the best known examples. The session is conversational and open to everyone. No prior reading is required. Come with thoughts on the form or just come curious. Free, about 90 minutes.
