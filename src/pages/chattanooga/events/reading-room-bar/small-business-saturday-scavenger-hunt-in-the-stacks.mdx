Small Business Saturday falls on November 28, the first Saturday after Thanksgiving. The Reading Room celebrates with a scavenger hunt through its used book stacks. Players search for titles matching a list of clues. It is a low pressure, all ages activity. The shop carries fiction, nonfiction, and local interest books. You will likely walk out with something you were not looking for. Stop in, hunt for some titles, and support a local bookshop.
