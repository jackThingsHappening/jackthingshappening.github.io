The Young Dubliners are a Celtic rock band with Irish roots and a Los Angeles home base. Frontman Keith Roberts leads a high-energy mix of rock guitars and traditional fiddle. The band has toured for decades and built a loyal live following. Their sound joins driving anthems with Irish folk melodies. Fans can expect a fast, loud show packed with sing-along moments. The show starts at 8pm with doors at 6:30pm. Tickets are required.
