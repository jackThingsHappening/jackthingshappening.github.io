Sloppy Street Tacos is a Chattanooga funk rock band that improvises hard. They pull from Vulfpeck, Umphrey's McGee, Talking Heads, and the Grateful Dead. Their sets mix funk, rock, jazz, blues, and folk into long jams. The lineup runs guitar, synth, saxophone, drums, and bass. This is a New Year's Eve show, so the music runs past midnight into 2027. A jam band suits the night because the sets stretch out and build. The music starts at 9:00 PM at Songbirds. Pace yourself and plan a ride home for the early hours.
