The Arcadian Wild is an indie folk and pop trio from Nashville. The group blends confessional pop, folk, progressive bluegrass, and formal vocal harmony. Members Isaac Horn, Lincoln Mick, and Bailey Warren first sang together in a university choir. Fort Wayne singer-songwriter Addison Agen opens the show. Agen was a finalist on NBC's The Voice at age 16. This stop is part of the Make It Out Alive Tour.
