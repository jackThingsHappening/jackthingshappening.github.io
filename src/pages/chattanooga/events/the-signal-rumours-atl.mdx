Rumours ATL is an Atlanta tribute band devoted to Fleetwood Mac. The group recreates the classic lineup fronted by Stevie Nicks and Lindsey Buckingham. Their set draws heavily from the "Rumours" album and other hits across the catalog. Expect to hear "Go Your Own Way," "Dreams," "Landslide," and "The Chain." The show starts at 8pm with doors at 6:30pm. Tickets are required.
