Vickie Vaughn plays Songbirds on Friday, September 26. She is a Nashville bassist and vocalist who fronts her own progressive bluegrass band. Her sound mixes roots-driven bluegrass with a touch of pop country. She was voted IBMA Bass Player of the Year in 2023 and 2024. The show starts at 7pm.
