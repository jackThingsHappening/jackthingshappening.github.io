MAINx24 is Chattanooga's annual 24-hour, neighbor-made festival along Main Street. Songbirds runs programming all day on December 5. The day opens with yoga at 9:30 AM, then the come-as-you-are holiday parade at 10:30 AM. Live music runs through the afternoon with Lonesome Wave at 1 PM, Josh Wheeler at 2 PM, Lew Card at 7 PM, and The Varden at 8:30 PM. The night closes with a karaoke and silent disco afterparty with DJ Ledford from 10 PM to midnight. Specials include $3 mimosas and $5 Bloody Marys until 2 PM, happy hour from 1-5 PM, and $1 wings from 4-7 PM. Admission is free.
