Songbirds serves as an official venue for the IBMA Bluegrass Ramble. The Ramble runs October 20 and 21 as part of IBMA World of Bluegrass in Chattanooga. Multiple showcase bands play each night across downtown rooms. The 2026 lineup spans 30 official showcase artists, from Missy Raines & Allegheny to The Slocan Ramblers. Doors and music start at 6:30pm. Each set runs short, so acts rotate through the night. Check the venue site for the nightly bill and tickets.
