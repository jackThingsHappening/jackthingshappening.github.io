Custom cars, hot rods, and motorcycles fill the Chattanooga Convention Center floor each January for the World of Wheels. The 59th edition runs January 8 through 10, 2027, across 110,000 square feet. Friday hours are 3 to 9pm, Saturday 10am to 9pm, and Sunday 10am to 6pm. Cars are judged for condition, design, and craftsmanship under ISCA rules. Advance tickets are $14 for adults and $5 for kids ages 6 through 11. Buy at worldofwheels.net to save over gate prices.
