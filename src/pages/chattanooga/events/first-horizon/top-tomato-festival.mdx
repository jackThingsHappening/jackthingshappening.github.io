The Chattanooga Market's annual tomato celebration takes over First Horizon Pavilion on June 7. The headliner is a Bloody Mary sampling event, featuring bold, spicy, and tangy variations from local makers. All the usual market vendors, fresh produce, and food are there too. Free, 11am–4pm.
