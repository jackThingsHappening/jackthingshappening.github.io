Bella Lam is a Chattanooga country artist playing a hometown Christmas show with her band. She just released a holiday single called "Slow As Christmas." The song features five-time CMA Musician of the Year Jenee Fleenor on fiddle. Expect new Christmas material alongside her country catalog. This show benefits the Forgotten Child Fund, a local toy drive. Lam has built a loyal following around Chattanooga, so hometown crowds tend to sing along. The set starts at 7:00 PM at Songbirds. Come ready for a festive, sentimental night rather than a loud one.
