Bo Bice plays Songbirds on Thursday, September 25. He finished runner-up on the fourth season of American Idol in 2005. Since then he has leaned into southern rock, blues, and gritty classic rock. His powerful voice made "Inside Your Heaven" a gold-selling single. The show starts at 7pm.
