Bronwyn Keith-Hynes is a bluegrass fiddler from Charlottesville, Virginia, now based in Nashville. She won IBMA Fiddle Player of the Year in 2021 and 2022. She spent years as the fiddler for Molly Tuttle and Golden Highway. Now she fronts her own band and sings lead. Her 2024 album I Built A World earned a Grammy nomination. Expect fiery fiddle playing and rootsy vocals. The set starts at 7:00 PM at Songbirds.
