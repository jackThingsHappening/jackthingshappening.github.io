Artisan vendors close out the 2026 holiday season at The Commons in Collegedale with handcrafted gifts, homemade treats, and locally made goods. Live music plays throughout both days. Hours are Saturday 11am to 5pm and Sunday 12pm to 5pm. Free admission. The Commons is at 4950 Swinyar Dr, about 20 miles east of downtown Chattanooga.
