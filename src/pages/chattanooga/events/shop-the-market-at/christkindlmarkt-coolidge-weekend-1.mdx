Local artisans line Coolidge Park's riverside walkways with handcrafted gifts and seasonal goods. Food vendors serve hot drinks and festive dishes alongside the shopping. Hours are Saturday 10am to 7pm and Sunday 11am to 6pm. Free admission. This is the first of two back-to-back weekends at Coolidge Park before the holiday markets shift to other venues.
