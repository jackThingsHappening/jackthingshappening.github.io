Local artisan vendors set up across the Choo-Choo's outdoor grounds for the first of two December holiday market weekends. Handcrafted gifts and seasonal goods fill the historic hotel's spaces while live holiday music plays throughout the day. Hours are Saturday 10am to 6pm and Sunday 10am to 4pm. Free admission.
