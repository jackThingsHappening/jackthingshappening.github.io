More than 100 makers and artisan vendors fill historic downtown Chickamauga for the 53rd annual Down Home Days. Two live music stages and a kid zone round out the day. Hours run Saturday 11am to 8pm. Free admission. Chickamauga is about 25 miles south of Chattanooga via US-27 South. The scale here is bigger than most markets in the organizer's lineup.
