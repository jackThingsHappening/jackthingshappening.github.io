Coolidge Park gets a Paris treatment for the fourth annual Springtime in Chattanooga. Vendors offer handcrafted goods and local products while an Eiffel Tower photo setup and floral displays fill the park. Hours run Saturday 10am to 5pm. Free admission. The park is on the North Shore at 1600 River St, walkable from downtown via the Walnut Street Bridge.
