Regional artisans fill the Choo-Choo Gardens with handcrafted rings, necklaces, bracelets, and earrings for the second annual Twilight Jewelry Market. Live music plays throughout. Hours run Saturday 10am to 5pm. Free admission. This is one of the more focused markets in the lineup, built around a single craft rather than a general artisan mix.
