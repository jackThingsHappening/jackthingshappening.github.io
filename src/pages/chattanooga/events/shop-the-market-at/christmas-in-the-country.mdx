McDonald Farm in Sale Creek hosts a one-day rural holiday market with artisan vendors and handcrafted gifts spread across the farm property. The country setting offers a quieter alternative to the urban market events. Hours run Saturday 10am to 4pm. Free admission. Sale Creek is about 25 miles north of downtown Chattanooga via US-27 North.
