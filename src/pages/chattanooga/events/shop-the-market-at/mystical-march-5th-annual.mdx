Mystical March hits the Chattanooga Choo-Choo for a fifth year with a St. Patrick's Day theme and a hidden leprechaun hunt across the market grounds. Vendors sell handmade goods and local produce. Hours run Saturday 10am to 5pm. Free admission. The event lands two weeks before St. Patrick's Day and draws strong family turnout.
