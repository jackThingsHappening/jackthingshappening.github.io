Wyatt Ellis brings young bluegrass and country talent to the free Nightfall stage at Miller Plaza. He is a skilled multi-instrumentalist with a bright, technically impressive approach to acoustic music. Ellis grew up in a musical family and began performing early. His live show moves between fiddle, guitar, and banjo. He has toured nationally and appeared at major acoustic music festivals. This free concert at Miller Plaza is open to all ages. Music starts at 7pm. Bring a chair or blanket and find a spot on the lawn.
