Sweet Magnolia headlines this free Nightfall concert at Miller Plaza. The non-traditional brass band blends jazz, pop, and New Orleans influences. Their horns bring a loud, joyful energy to the plaza. Chattanooga Sheiks open the night. The concert is free and open to all ages. Come early, bring a chair, and dance along.
