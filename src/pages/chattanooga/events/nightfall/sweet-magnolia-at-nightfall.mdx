Sweet Magnolia headlines this free Nightfall concert at Miller Plaza. The non-traditional brass band blends jazz, pop, and New Orleans influences. Their arrangements take familiar songs and reshape them through the lens of a horn-driven ensemble. The group has performed at festivals and stages across the Southeast. This free concert at Miller Plaza is open to all ages. Music starts at 7pm. Bring a chair or blanket. Miller Plaza fills quickly for brass-heavy acts - arrive by 6:30 for a good spot on the lawn. The concert is free with no ticket required.
