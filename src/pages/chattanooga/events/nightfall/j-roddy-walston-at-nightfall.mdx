J. Roddy Walston headlines this free Nightfall concert at Miller Plaza. His high-octane mix of vocals and piano pulls from rockabilly, soul, and classic rock. Walston leads J. This free concert at Miller Plaza is open to all ages. Music starts at 7pm. Bring a chair or blanket and find a spot on the lawn. Miller Plaza fills quickly for high-energy headliners.
