The Spooklights headline this free Nightfall concert at Miller Plaza. The band merges roots music with electronic elements. Fans describe their sound as somewhere between folk and future. The Spooklights play guitar, keys, and rhythm alongside processed and looped sounds. Their live show is atmospheric and builds in intensity. This concert is free and open to all ages at Miller Plaza in downtown Chattanooga. Music starts at 7pm. Bring a chair or find space on the lawn. Miller Plaza fills early on busy Nightfall nights - arrive by 6:30 to get a good spot.
