Link and Chain headline this free Nightfall concert at Miller Plaza. The Jamaican reggae group plays spiritual, danceable music shaped by the Rastafari tradition and Caribbean rhythms. Their live show moves between steady one-drop rhythms and rootsy ballads. Link and Chain have toured internationally and recorded multiple albums. This free concert at Miller Plaza is open to all ages. Music starts at 7pm. Bring a chair or blanket and find a spot on the lawn. Miller Plaza is on Market Street in downtown Chattanooga between 7th and 8th Street.
