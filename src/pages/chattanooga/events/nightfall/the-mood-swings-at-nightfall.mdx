The Mood Swings headline this free Nightfall concert at Miller Plaza. The Nashville soul-pop band draws on Motown and features a prominent horn section. Their live show leans on call-and-response vocals, tight brass arrangements, and an energetic rhythm section. The band has played festivals and clubs across the Southeast and Midwest. This free concert at Miller Plaza is open to all ages. Music starts at 7pm. Bring a chair or blanket and find a spot on the lawn. The Nightfall series runs every other Friday through the season at Miller Plaza downtown.
