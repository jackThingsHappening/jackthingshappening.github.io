Robert Lee headlines this free Nightfall concert at Miller Plaza. He won the 2026 Road to Nightfall competition and earned this headline spot. The show is the prize for the annual competition that selects local and regional acts for the Nightfall stage. Robert Lee plays a style of music shaped by the competition-winning set that earned him the spot. This free concert at Miller Plaza is open to all ages. Music starts at 7pm. Bring a chair or blanket and find a spot on the lawn. Check the Nightfall website for support acts and the full evening schedule.
