Holy Roller headlines this free Nightfall concert at Miller Plaza. The band plays Southern rock that is riff-heavy yet deeply intimate. Their songs pair loud guitar arrangements with lyrical writing rooted in the South. Holy Roller has toured the Southeast and built a consistent following in the listening room and outdoor festival circuit. This free concert at Miller Plaza is open to all ages. Music starts at 7pm. Bring a chair or find space on the lawn. Miller Plaza is on Market Street in downtown Chattanooga between 7th and 8th Street.
