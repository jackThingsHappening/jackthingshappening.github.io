Alicia Blue brings Americana, folk, and alt-country to the free Nightfall stage at Miller Plaza. Her songs carry real emotional depth and honest storytelling. Blue is a Nashville-based singer-songwriter with releases on independent labels. Her voice sits in a clear, direct register and her live performance is focused and unhurried. This free concert at Miller Plaza is open to all ages. Music starts at 7pm. Bring a chair or blanket and find a spot on the lawn. The Nightfall series runs every other Friday at Miller Plaza in downtown Chattanooga.
