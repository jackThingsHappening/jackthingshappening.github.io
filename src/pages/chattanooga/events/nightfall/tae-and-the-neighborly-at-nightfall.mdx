Tae and the Neighborly headline this free Nightfall concert at Miller Plaza. The group blends soul, R&B, pop, and rock with vibrant instrumentation. Tae is a Chattanooga-area vocalist who has built a local following through consistent live performance. The Neighborly is the full band that backs the project. Their live show is energetic and crowd-engaged. This free concert at Miller Plaza is open to all ages. Music starts at 7pm. Bring a chair or find space on the lawn. The Nightfall series runs every other Friday through the season.
