Sinkane headlines this free Nightfall concert at Miller Plaza. The band blends Afrobeat-inspired rock, funk, electronica, and jazzy pop into one bright, danceable package. Ahmed Gallab leads the project. He was born in Khartoum, Sudan, raised in Salt Lake City, and built his career in New York. Sinkane has released multiple albums on City Slang and other labels. This concert is free and open to all ages at Miller Plaza in downtown Chattanooga. Music starts at 7pm. Bring a chair or find space on the lawn.
