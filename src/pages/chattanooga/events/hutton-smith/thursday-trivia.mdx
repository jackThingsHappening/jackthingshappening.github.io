Free trivia every Thursday night at 7pm at Hutton and Smith Brewing on the Southside. Bring a crew, grab a pint, and see what you know. Questions run across categories. Hutton and Smith Brewing is on Williams Street in the Southside neighborhood of Chattanooga. The trivia night is free and runs about two hours. No ticket required. Walk in and form a team of up to six. The brewery pours its own craft beers alongside seasonal and limited releases. Check the Hutton and Smith social pages for any themed trivia nights or special events on the weekly schedule.
