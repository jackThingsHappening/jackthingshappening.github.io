More than 140 vendors fill Camp Jordan Arena for three days of indoor holiday shopping. Friday runs 2pm to 7pm, Saturday 10am to 5pm, and Sunday 10am to 4pm. Admission is $8. Kids 12 and under get in free. Photo ops with Santa, the Grinch, and Cindy Lou Who are included at no extra charge. Camp Jordan Arena is at 323 Camp Jordan Pkwy in East Ridge, about 10 minutes from downtown Chattanooga via I-24 East.
