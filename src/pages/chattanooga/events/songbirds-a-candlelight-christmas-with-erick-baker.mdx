Erick Baker is an Emmy-winning folk soul singer-songwriter from Knoxville, Tennessee. His sound sits at the meeting point of folk, rock, country, and soul. Critics compare his voice to Ray LaMontagne and Amos Lee. This candlelit show features Christmas classics and his heartfelt songwriting. The set starts at 7:00 PM at Songbirds.
