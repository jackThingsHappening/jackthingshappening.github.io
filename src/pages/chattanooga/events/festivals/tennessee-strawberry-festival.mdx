Dayton, TN has been doing this for 79 years. A full week in downtown built around fresh strawberries: grand parade, pageants, carnival rides in Washington Park, art show, baking contest, cruise-in, and food vendors. The Kentucky Headhunters headline Saturday night.

About 45 minutes north of Chattanooga. The week runs Monday May 4 through Saturday May 9. Saturday is the big day, starting with a pancake breakfast at 7:30am and the Strawberry Chase 5K at 8am before the headliner closes it out.
