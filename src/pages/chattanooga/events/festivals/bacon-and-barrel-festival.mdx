Twenty-plus local restaurants compete for the Golden Hog award. Bulleit, Buffalo Trace, Crown Royal, and 17 more distilleries are pouring samples. General Admission covers 12 whiskey samples and unlimited food. All-Inclusive ($95) is unlimited everything plus commemorative glassware and VIP seating. Live music, 5–8pm.

Strictly 21+. Proceeds benefit The Chattanooga Market nonprofit. Tickets through chattanoogafestivals.com/bacon, non-refundable.
