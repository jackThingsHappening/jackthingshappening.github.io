South Pittsburg is the historic home of Lodge Cast Iron, and every last full weekend of April the town leans into it hard. The National Cornbread Cook-Off, Cornbread Alley where civic groups hand out creative variations, live music, a 5K, and vendor shopping fill two days in town.

About 30 miles west of Chattanooga. Saturday 8am–6:30pm, Sunday 8am–5pm. Free parking with shuttle from outer lots. Rain or shine, no pets. Presale tickets through TicketSpice.
