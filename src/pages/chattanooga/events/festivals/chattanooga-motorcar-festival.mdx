Vintage racing on track, a Concours d'Elegance drawing entries from across the country, automobile displays, and concerts on the downtown riverfront. The festival debuted in 2019 and ran every fall until a postponement in 2025. The 2024 Best in Show went to a 1935 Swallow Sidecar SS1.

Returning Fall 2026. Specific dates haven't been announced yet. Check chattanoogamotorcar.com for updates.
