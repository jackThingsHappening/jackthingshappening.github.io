Free bluegrass on the riverfront, two days, rain or shine. Chattanooga Presents books local, regional, and national acts every fall at Ross's Landing. Rhonda Vincent is confirmed for 2026.

Lawn chairs and blankets welcome. No outside food, drink, or coolers. Vendors on site with food, beer, and wine. Leashed dogs OK. Free electric shuttle runs until 11pm. Downtown hotels are a short walk if you're making a weekend of it.
