Main Street on the Southside goes car-free for 24 hours every first Saturday of December. What started as a handful of events has grown to 120+: parade (voted best in Chattanooga three years running), live music across multiple stages, art installations, food, and community events from midnight to midnight. MAINx24 is Chattanooga's signature end-of-year community event. The South Broad Main Street corridor closes to cars from midnight Saturday to midnight Sunday. Free to attend. Check the MAINx24 website for the full event map, participating businesses, and entertainment schedule. Reflection Riding participates as a community partner.
