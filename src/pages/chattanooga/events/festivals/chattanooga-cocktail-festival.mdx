Your ticket is a passport for 12 mini cocktails from local distilleries and bartenders. Ketel One, Bulleit, Empress Gin, tequilas, and more in the mix. Mixology competitions, live entertainment, and food trucks on site. Proceeds benefit the Chattanooga Market nonprofit.

Strictly 21+, physical ID required. Presented by Chattanooga Festivals, the same crew behind the Bacon & Barrel event.
