Michigan's Greensky Bluegrass headline The Signal on September 9 during their 2026 evening-with tour — two full sets, no opener. The Kalamazoo five-piece play acoustic stomp with the rule-breaking energy of rock and roll, rooted in jam-band tradition but never predictable. Their 2025 anniversary album XXV is a good starting point if you're new to them. Music at 8pm.
