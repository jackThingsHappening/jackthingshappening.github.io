Shane Torres headlines the opening show of the Lookout Comedy Festival's JJ's Bohemia series. He's a New York comic best known for a deadpan Conan bit defending Nickelback. Torres performs at clubs and festivals nationally and has appeared on major late-night programs. His style is dry and precise with a deadpan delivery. JJ's Bohemia is a Chattanooga venue and long-standing community institution. The 20th anniversary series runs two nights at JJ's as part of the larger Lookout Comedy Festival. Check the festival website for ticket details.
