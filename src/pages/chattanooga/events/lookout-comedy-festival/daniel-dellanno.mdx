Daniel Dellanno started doing stand-up at 18 in Atlanta. His material draws from his Southern upbringing and his own life. He made his Netflix debut on Kevin Hart's LOL Network. Dellanno performs at clubs and festivals nationally. His style combines storytelling with sharp crowd work. This show is part of the Lookout Comedy Festival, which runs multiple nights across Chattanooga venues in October. Check the festival website for ticket details, venue location, and the full festival schedule.
