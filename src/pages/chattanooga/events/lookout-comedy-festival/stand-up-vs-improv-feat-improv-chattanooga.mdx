Comics and Improv Chattanooga face off at Barlery. One side writes their jokes. The other builds scenes from audience suggestions on the spot. General seating is first come, first served. This event is part of the annual Lookout Comedy Festival, which runs multiple nights across Chattanooga venues in October. Barlery is a bar in downtown Chattanooga. The festival features national and local acts across a range of formats. Check the Lookout Comedy Festival website for ticket details, the full festival schedule, and other events happening during the run.
