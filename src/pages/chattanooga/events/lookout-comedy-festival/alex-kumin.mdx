Alex Kumin headlined Comedy Central Standup, NBC's "That Clip Show: Holiday Edition," and the Don't Tell Comedy series. Time Out named her a Comics to Watch pick. Her style is sharp, specific, and often self-deprecating. Kumin performs at clubs and festivals nationally. This show is part of the Lookout Comedy Festival, which runs multiple nights across Chattanooga venues in October. Check the festival website for ticket details, venue location, and the full festival schedule.
