Kyle Kinane closes the festival with the second of two 20th anniversary shows at JJ's Bohemia. He tells long, dark stories with dry wit. His career spans more than 20 years of stand-up, including Comedy Central specials and regular appearances on late-night programs. Kinane is considered one of the best working storytellers in stand-up. JJ's Bohemia is a Chattanooga venue celebrating its 20th year with this two-night comedy series as part of the Lookout Comedy Festival. Check the festival website for ticket details.
