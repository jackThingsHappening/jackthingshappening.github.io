Ryan Goodcase won the World Series of Comedy in 2019 and the San Francisco International Comedy Competition in 2021. His dry writing style earned him a spot in the Netflix Is a Joke Fest "Introducing..." showcase. His special "Maybe They're Dead" has over 200,000 YouTube views, and one clip topped 50 million plays. Two shows at The Comedy Catch: 7pm and 9:15pm.
