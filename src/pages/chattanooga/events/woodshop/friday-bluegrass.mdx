Regional musicians rotating through The Woodshop every Friday night in St. Elmo. Traditional bluegrass in a small listening room with a close stage. The rotating format means the lineup changes week to week, drawing from the strong Chattanooga-area acoustic music community. Expect banjo, fiddle, mandolin, guitar, and upright bass in tight bluegrass arrangements. The Woodshop on St. Elmo Avenue has hosted weekly bluegrass nights for years. Doors open at 5pm. Music typically starts at 6pm. Check the venue's social pages for the weekly artist announcement.
