Max Gomez plays The Woodshop in St. Elmo on December 9. The Taos, New Mexico songwriter writes Americana and folk with a southwestern edge. Gomez grew up between New Mexico and Missouri and has released several albums including work with producer Shooter Jennings. His songs pull from classic country, folk, and acoustic rock. He has toured with artists including Brandi Carlile and Neko Case. The small room at The Woodshop puts the audience within a few feet of his guitar. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
