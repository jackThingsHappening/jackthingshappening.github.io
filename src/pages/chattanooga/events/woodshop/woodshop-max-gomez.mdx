Max Gomez plays The Woodshop in St. Elmo on December 9. The Taos, New Mexico songwriter writes Americana and folk with a Western lean. Critics compare him to Jackson Browne and John Prine. His album Memory Mountain hit number one on the Alt-Country chart. Joe Bryson opens the night. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
