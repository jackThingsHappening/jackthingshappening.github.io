Bird and Byron play The Woodshop in St. Elmo on January 28. Blake Bird and Nick Byron are a Nashville indie rock and soul duo. They write warm, harmony-driven songs with a haunting edge. The pair reached the America's Got Talent quarterfinals and draw more than 180,000 monthly Spotify listeners. Their close vocals suit this small listening room well. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
