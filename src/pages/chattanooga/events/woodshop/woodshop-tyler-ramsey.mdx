Tyler Ramsey plays The Woodshop in St. Elmo on January 21. He spent nearly a decade as lead guitarist in Band of Horses. He played on their Grammy-nominated album Infinite Arms before going solo in 2017. Ramsey writes fingerstyle indie folk with pedal steel and quiet, open arrangements. His latest record, New Lost Ages, came out in 2024. He builds pieces where melody, bass, and rhythm all come from one guitar. That kind of detailed playing rewards a small room. The close stage at The Woodshop fits his sound well. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
