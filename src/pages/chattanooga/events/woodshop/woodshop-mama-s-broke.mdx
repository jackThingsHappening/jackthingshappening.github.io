Mama's Broke are Canadian Folk Music Award winners known for haunting vocal harmonies rooted in older folk traditions. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm. The duo is Lisa Marie Bogert and Amy Lou Sherrill. They draw from Celtic, Appalachian, and Eastern European folk music. Their vocals interlock in a way that is rare in the genre. They have toured internationally and performed at major folk festivals across Canada and the United States. The Woodshop in St. Elmo is a small listening room where their harmonies carry across every corner. Check the venue for ticket details.
