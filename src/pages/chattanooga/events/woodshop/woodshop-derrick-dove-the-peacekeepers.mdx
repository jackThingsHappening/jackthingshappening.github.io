Derrick Dove & The Peacekeepers play The Woodshop in St. Elmo on March 20. The South Georgia band mixes blues, soul, classic rock, and Southern spirit. Derrick Dove leads on guitar and vocals. In January 2026 the band won the International Blues Challenge in Memphis. Dove also took Best Guitarist honors there. Their albums Rough Time and Burn It Down topped the iTunes blues chart. They have shared stages with ZZ Top and Blackberry Smoke. Expect a high-energy set in the small room. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
