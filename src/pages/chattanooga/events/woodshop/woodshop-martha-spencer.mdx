Martha Spencer is an Appalachian roots musician who plays guitar, fiddle, banjo, bass, and dulcimer. Her music draws on old-time mountain traditions, from ballads to string-band dance tunes. The Woodshop is a quiet listening room in St. Elmo, a good fit for songs this rooted and this detailed. Doors at 6pm. See the venue page for ticket details.
