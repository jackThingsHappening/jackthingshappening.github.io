Martha Spencer is an Appalachian roots musician who plays guitar, fiddle, banjo, bass, and dulcimer. Her music draws on traditional mountain music from the Virginia and North Carolina highlands. Spencer grew up in Whitetop, Virginia, in a family with deep roots in Appalachian music. She has performed at the Kennedy Center and appeared on recordings with Ralph Stanley and other traditional artists. She teaches and advocates for the preservation of old-time music. The Woodshop in St. Elmo is a close listening room that suits traditional acoustic performance. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
