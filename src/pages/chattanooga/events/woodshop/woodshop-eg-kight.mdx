E.G. Kight plays The Woodshop in St. Elmo on February 6. Known as the Georgia Songbird, she sings blues, soul, and roots music. Her grandmother taught her guitar at age five. She turned to the blues after hearing Koko Taylor. Kight has shared stages and worked with B.B. King, Taj Mahal, and Pinetop Perkins. Her 2008 album It's Hot in Here topped the roots blues chart. She still lives on family land in Dublin, Georgia. The small room suits her sweet, Southern voice. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
