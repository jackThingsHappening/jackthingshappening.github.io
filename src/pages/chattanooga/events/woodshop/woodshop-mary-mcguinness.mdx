Mary McGuinness plays The Woodshop in St. Elmo on December 10. The Nashville singer blends folk-Americana, Laurel Canyon warmth, and classic country. Her voice carries clear and soul-deep across quiet songs. She trained in opera and later toured with Kenny Loggins and Shawn Colvin. The small room suits her delivery. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
