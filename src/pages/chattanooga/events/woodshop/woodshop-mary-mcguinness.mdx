Mary McGuinness plays The Woodshop in St. Elmo on December 10. The Nashville singer blends folk-Americana, Laurel Canyon, and singer-songwriter traditions. McGuinness writes songs built around clear images and direct emotion. Her voice sits in a warm, mid-range register. She has released recordings and toured the Southeast and Midwest. The Woodshop is an intimate listening room on St. Elmo Avenue where her acoustic sound fills the space. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm. Check the venue for ticket details.
