Bryant "Bass Hitter" Russell and The Prophets play a special New Year's Eve edition of Scenic City Soul Night at The Woodshop on December 31. Doors open at 6pm and the show starts at 7pm. The night includes Southern hors d'oeuvres and a champagne toast at midnight. The Woodshop's intimate listening room setting makes this a different kind of NYE option from the typical bar crawl. The venue has limited seating, so reservations are strongly recommended. The Woodshop is at 5500 Saint Elmo Avenue in St. Elmo, with street parking available on the block.
