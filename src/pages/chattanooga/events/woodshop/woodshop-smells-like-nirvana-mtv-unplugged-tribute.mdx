Smells Like Nirvana plays The Woodshop in St. Elmo on January 24. The Chicago tribute band recreates the Nirvana sound with no click tracks or backing tracks. The set pulls from Nevermind, In Utero, Bleach, and rare deep cuts. Frontman Paul Wandtke leads a raw, grunge-fueled performance. The intimate listening room fits the stripped-down MTV Unplugged style. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
