Mid America pairs Mia Carruthers and Curtis Roush for a western folk rock set steeped in '70s-influenced songwriting and high desert imagery. Carruthers is a New Mexico singer-songwriter with roots in Albuquerque. Roush is a guitarist and producer based in Nashville. Their songs move between gentle folk and driving Americana. The duo recorded their debut at a studio in the Southwest. The Woodshop in St. Elmo is a close, intimate room where their harmonies carry well. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
