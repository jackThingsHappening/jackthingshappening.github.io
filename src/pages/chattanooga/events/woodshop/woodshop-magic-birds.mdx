Magic Birds are an Americana duo who pull from classic country, bluegrass, folk, and soul. Their songs mix old-time roots with a modern pop sensibility. The duo is based in Nashville and has released multiple recordings. Their vocal harmonies sit at the core of the arrangements. Magic Birds have played listening rooms and festivals across the Southeast and Midwest. The Woodshop in St. Elmo is a close venue where their blend of traditional and contemporary Americana comes through without amplification barriers. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
