Toronzo Cannon is a Chicago blues guitarist and singer on Alligator Records. He plays hard-driving electric blues with strong original songwriting. Cannon spent years driving a city bus in Chicago while building his music career on the South Side blues circuit. He signed to Alligator Records and released to strong reviews. His guitar work is aggressive and rooted in the West Side Chicago style. The Woodshop in St. Elmo is a small listening room where his sound fills every corner. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
