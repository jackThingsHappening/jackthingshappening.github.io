A Lord of the Rings themed party with Innman & The Prancing Ponies, a costume contest, a hobbit-inspired menu, and elven cocktails at The Woodshop in St. Elmo. This is the second year of the event. The band plays original music inspired by Tolkien's world alongside traditional folk and Americana. Cash prizes go to the best costumes. The menu runs all evening alongside a full bar. The Woodshop is a small listening room on St. Elmo Avenue in Chattanooga. Check the venue for tickets and start time.
