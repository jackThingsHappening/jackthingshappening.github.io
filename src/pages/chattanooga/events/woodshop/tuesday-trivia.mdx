Free trivia every Tuesday at 8pm at The Woodshop in St. Elmo, hosted by Rebekah Cecil. Prizes and food specials on the night. The trivia format runs multiple rounds covering music, pop culture, history, and general knowledge. Teams of up to six compete for prizes. Cecil has hosted the weekly night for multiple seasons and keeps the rounds fresh. The Woodshop is a small listening room on St. Elmo Avenue that doubles as a neighborhood bar on trivia nights. No cover. Walk in, form a team, and play.
