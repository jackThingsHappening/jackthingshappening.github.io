Mike Zito is a blues and blues-rock guitarist with a heavy, soulful touch. He has won multiple Blues Music Awards over his long career. His songs mix raw electric blues with roots rock. Zito's live show hits hard in the small room. He has recorded for Ruf Records and Forty Below Records, collaborating with artists like Walter Trout and Devon Allman. His biography includes time in both St. Louis and New Orleans, and both cities shaped his sound. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm. Check the venue for ticket details.
