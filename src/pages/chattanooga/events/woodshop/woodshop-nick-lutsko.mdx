Nick Lutsko plays The Woodshop in St. Elmo on January 9. The Chattanooga musician built a following online with viral comedy songs and absurd parodies. His albums lean toward psychedelic indie rock and sharp character studies. He plays multiple instruments and shifts between humor and honest reflection. Jordan Burchel and award-winning banjo player Randy Steele join the bill. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
