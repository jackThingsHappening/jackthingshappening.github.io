Goldpine is a Nashville husband-and-wife duo trading bold harmonies in what they call 'aggressive Americana.' The pair is Jamie Lin Wilson and Jonny Burke. Wilson is a Texas singer-songwriter and longtime member of The Trishas. Burke is a Texas-born guitarist and producer. Together they write songs that sit at the intersection of classic country and Americana. The small room at The Woodshop in St. Elmo suits their sound well. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
