Bryant 'Bass Hitter' Russell and his band bring the groove every Thursday at The Woodshop in St. Elmo. The setlist runs through classic soul, funk, and R&B. Russell is a Chattanooga-area musician and bandleader known for his driving bass lines and energetic live shows. The Thursday night residency pulls from acts like James Brown, Sly Stone, and Al Green. The Woodshop is an intimate listening room on St. Elmo Avenue. The small stage puts the band close to the crowd. Check the venue for cover charge and start time.
