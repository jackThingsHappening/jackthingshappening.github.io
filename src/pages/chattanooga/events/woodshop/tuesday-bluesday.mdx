Jhett Black plays blues every Tuesday night at The Woodshop in St. Elmo. Small room, close sound, doors at 8pm. $10 cover. Black is a Chattanooga-area guitarist and vocalist who plays electric blues and soul. The Tuesday night residency gives local blues fans a consistent weekly option in a listening room setting. The Woodshop is an intimate venue on St. Elmo Avenue. The cover includes the music and the room. Bar service runs all night. No reservations needed - walk in and find a table or lean against the wall near the stage.
