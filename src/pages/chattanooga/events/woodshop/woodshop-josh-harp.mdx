Josh Harp is a Chattanooga-born blues musician who also works as an actor and educator. He plays soulful blues rooted in the Delta and Chicago traditions. Harp has performed in theater productions and appeared in film and television roles. On stage as a musician, he focuses on acoustic and electric blues with strong original songwriting. His live shows are known for warmth and audience engagement. The Woodshop in St. Elmo is an intimate listening room where his storytelling lands close to the crowd. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
