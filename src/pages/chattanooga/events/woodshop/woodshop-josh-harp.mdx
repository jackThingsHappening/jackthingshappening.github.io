Josh Harp is a Chattanooga-born blues musician who also works as an actor and educator. He plays soulful blues rooted in the local tradition. His homecoming show brings that sound back to St. Elmo. The intimate room suits his storytelling style. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
