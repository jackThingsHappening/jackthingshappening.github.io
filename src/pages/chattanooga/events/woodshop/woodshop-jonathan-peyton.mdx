Jonathan Peyton is an independent Georgia singer-songwriter who leans on emotionally resonant storytelling. Hunter Blalock opens the show. Peyton writes in the folk and Americana tradition, with songs built around specific moments and places. His voice is warm and direct. Blalock is a Chattanooga-area singer-songwriter who has opened for numerous national acts at The Woodshop. This is a seated listening room show on St. Elmo Avenue. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
