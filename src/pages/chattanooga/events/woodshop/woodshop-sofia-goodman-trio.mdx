Sofia Goodman is a Nashville drummer and composer who leads her own trio. Her music sits in modern, melodic jazz with strong rhythmic foundations. Goodman studied at Berklee College of Music. Her compositions draw on jazz standards and original work. The trio format keeps the arrangements lean and allows space for improvisation. The Woodshop in St. Elmo is an intimate listening room suited to small jazz ensembles. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm. Check the venue for ticket details.
