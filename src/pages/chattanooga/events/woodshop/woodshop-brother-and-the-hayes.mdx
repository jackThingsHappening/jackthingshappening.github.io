Brother and The Hayes play The Woodshop in St. Elmo on January 31. The Texas-born, Nashville-based duo pairs siblings Jeb and Seth Hayes with tight harmony and Americana songwriting. Their songs draw from country, folk, and gospel roots. The brothers have toured nationally and built a following in the Texas and Tennessee listening room circuit. The Woodshop is an intimate venue on St. Elmo Avenue that suits close, harmony-based performances. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm. Check the venue for ticket details.
