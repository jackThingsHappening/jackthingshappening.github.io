Brother and The Hayes play The Woodshop in St. Elmo on January 31. The Texas-born, Nashville-based duo pairs siblings Jennie Hayes Kurtz and David Bingaman. Their songs mix Americana, folk, country, and blues with western swing. They draw on the storytelling of Guy Clark and the soul of Bonnie Raitt. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
