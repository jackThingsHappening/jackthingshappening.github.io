Jake Blount is both a performer and a scholar of Black folk music, with stops at Carnegie Hall and the Newport Folk Festival. He plays The Woodshop in St. Elmo. Blount plays banjo and fiddle and builds his sets from the African American string band tradition. His album Spider Tales won the IBMA award for Emerging Artist. He has studied and performed traditional music alongside ethnomusicological research. The Woodshop is a small listening room in St. Elmo that suits this kind of close, careful playing. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm. Check the venue for ticket details.
