A Sun Records Tribute Show comes to The Woodshop in St. Elmo on January 2. The night honors the Memphis label that Sam Phillips founded in 1952. Sun blended blues, country, gospel, and rhythm and blues into early rock and roll. The lineup features Emerald Butler, Mario Monterrosso, Jhett Black, and Joel Beaver. Butler is a fiddler and songwriter who has recorded at Sun Studio. Black is an awarded, internationally touring bluesman. Monterrosso is an Italian guitarist and singer who tours worldwide. The small room puts these players close to the crowd. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
