The Family brings together four Muscle Shoals musicians - Dylan LeBlanc, James LeBlanc, Angela Hacker, and Bay Simpson - for a collaborative set at The Woodshop in St. Elmo. Dylan LeBlanc is a singer-songwriter with releases on Rough Trade Records. James LeBlanc is a Nashville guitarist and producer. Hacker and Simpson are session musicians rooted in the Muscle Shoals tradition. Together the four blend Southern soul, country, and Americana. The small room on St. Elmo Avenue is well suited to this kind of collaborative performance. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
