Ashes & Arrows play The Woodshop in St. Elmo on February 11. The trio formed at an Asheville open mic in 2021. Members come from North Carolina and Arrowtown, New Zealand. Their sound blends country, Americana, and folk with tight three-part harmonies. They reached the semi-finals of America's Got Talent in 2024. The small listening room suits their close vocal blend. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
