Hayde is a seven-piece group from Norway that blends bluegrass with Nordic folk. Their sound pairs tight harmonies with driving acoustic strings. The band won a 2022 IBMA vocalist award. Their live show brings big energy to the small St. Elmo room. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
