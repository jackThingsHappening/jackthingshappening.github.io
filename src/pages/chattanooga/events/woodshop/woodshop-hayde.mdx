Hayde is a seven-piece group from Norway that blends bluegrass with Nordic folk. Their sound pairs tight harmonies with fiddles, mandolin, and guitar in arrangements that feel both old and new. The group has toured Europe and the United States, playing festivals and listening rooms. Their albums have earned praise from both Scandinavian folk and American Americana audiences. The Woodshop in St. Elmo is a small, close room where the band's layered arrangements come through clearly. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm. Check the venue for ticket details.
