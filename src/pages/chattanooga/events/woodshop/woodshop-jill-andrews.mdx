Jill Andrews plays The Woodshop in St. Elmo on January 22. The Nashville singer-songwriter writes indie folk and Americana. She co-founded the band The Everybodyfields in Knoxville before going solo in 2009. That band played Bonnaroo and the Kennedy Center and toured with the Avett Brothers. Her solo records build broader arrangements on top of her roots sound. Her songs have appeared in shows like Grey's Anatomy and Nashville. The close listening room suits her clear voice and detailed writing. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
