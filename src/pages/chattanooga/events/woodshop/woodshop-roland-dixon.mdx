Roland Dixon plays The Woodshop in St. Elmo on December 18. The Knoxville singer-songwriter writes weathered, literary songs about love, loss, and everyday absurdity. His sound sits near Tom Waits, Leonard Cohen, and old Appalachian murder ballads. He delivers the words with dark wit and plain honesty. Dixon has four albums, including the 2026 release Songs For Dogs. He is a familiar voice across East Tennessee and has played WDVX's Blue Plate Special. The small listening room suits his close, story-driven style. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
