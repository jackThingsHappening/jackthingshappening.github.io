Robbie Fulks plays The Woodshop in St. Elmo on March 31. The Chicago songwriter helped define alt-country in the 1990s. His 2016 album Upland Stories earned two Grammy nominations. He has released 15 records across a career spanning more than 30 years. Fulks mixes folk, bluegrass, and country with dark humor and sharp lyrics. His 2023 album Bluegrass Vacation leaned fully into acoustic picking. He is a strong guitarist and a funny, direct performer between songs. The listening room format puts his words front and center. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
