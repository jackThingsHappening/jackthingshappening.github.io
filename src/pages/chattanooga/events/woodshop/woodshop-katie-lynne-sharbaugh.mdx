Katie Lynne Sharbaugh plays The Woodshop in St. Elmo on January 8. The Nashville singer-songwriter fuses pop with jazz and R&B. She studied at Berklee College of Music and plays violin, viola, and piano. Her songs pair the nostalgia of 1970s soul with modern storytelling. She first learned Irish fiddle tunes by ear at a summer home in Ireland. Sharbaugh released her debut EP Little Blue Beetle Volume 1 in 2021. Her later work includes the concept album Upstaged. The intimate stage fits her cinematic, melodic sound. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
