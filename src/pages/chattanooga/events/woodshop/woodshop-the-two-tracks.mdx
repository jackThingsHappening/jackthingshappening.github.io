The Two Tracks are a husband-and-wife duo who lead a full band through bluegrass, Americana, and folk-rock. Their sound builds on traditional string band roots and extends into rock territory. The duo writes original songs and covers classic Americana material. Their live show moves between quiet moments and full-band arrangements. The Woodshop in St. Elmo is a small listening room where the full band still fits comfortably on the stage. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm. Check the venue for ticket details.
