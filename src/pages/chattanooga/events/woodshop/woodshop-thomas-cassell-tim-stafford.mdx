Thomas Cassell and Tim Stafford bring a bluegrass evening to The Woodshop. Cassell is a sharp young mandolin player and songwriter from East Tennessee. Stafford co-founded Blue Highway, the five-time IBMA Group of the Year. Together they play traditional bluegrass and original Americana in the close confines of the St. Elmo listening room. Stafford's experience in the bluegrass world brings depth to the pairing, and Cassell's writing adds fresh material. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm. Check the venue for ticket details.
