The Fox & The Flame is the project of Chattanooga singer-songwriter Caleb Lange. His music blends folk and Americana with a cinematic, atmospheric edge. The songs build slowly and fill the room. The Woodshop's listening setup fits the sound well. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
