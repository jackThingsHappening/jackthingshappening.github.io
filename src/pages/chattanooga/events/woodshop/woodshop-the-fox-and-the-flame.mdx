The Fox & The Flame is the project of Chattanooga singer-songwriter Caleb Lange. His music blends folk and Americana with atmospheric production. Lange records and performs locally and has built a following on the Chattanooga music scene. His songs move between quiet introspection and driving full-band arrangements. The Fox & The Flame has played local venues and festivals and continues to grow beyond the region. The Woodshop in St. Elmo is an intimate listening room well suited to this kind of songwriting. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm. Check the venue for ticket details.
