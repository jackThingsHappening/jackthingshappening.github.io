Laid Back Country Picker is David Prince, a singer-songwriter from Louisa, Kentucky. His sound blends '70s guitar rock, honky-tonk, and country punk. He plays with his partner Honey and brings a loud, driving show. Prince has played the Ryman, Bonnaroo, and Red Rocks, and he spins on Sirius Outlaw Country Radio. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
