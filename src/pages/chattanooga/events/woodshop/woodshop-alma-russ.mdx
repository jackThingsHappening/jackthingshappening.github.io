Alma Russ plays The Woodshop in St. Elmo on January 29. The western North Carolina musician blends folk, bluegrass, and mountain ballads. She plays fiddle, claw-hammer banjo, and guitar. Russ learned Scotch-Irish murder ballads as a child and took up fiddle at age twelve. She auditioned for American Idol in 2017 and earned praise from all three judges. Lionel Richie compared her to Dolly Parton. She released her first full album, Next Town, in 2020. Her Appalachian sound fits the intimate stage. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
