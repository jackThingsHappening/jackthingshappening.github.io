The New Quintet plays The Woodshop in St. Elmo on January 16. The five-piece band honors traditional folk, ragtime, old-time, and early country. The lineup pairs guitar, fiddle, upright bass, clarinet, and lap steel, with shared vocals. Nicholas Edward Williams leads the group as a storyteller and musicologist. The band formed in 2020 and has toured across the Southeast. In 2026 they reached the quarterfinals on Season 21 of America's Got Talent. Their warm, acoustic sound fits the small listening room well. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
