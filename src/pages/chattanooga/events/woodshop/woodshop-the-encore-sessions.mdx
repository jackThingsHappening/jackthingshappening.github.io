Local artists share the stage for a free benefit night in St. Elmo. The show supports the Musicians Mental Health Initiative and raises awareness of mental health resources for working musicians. The Encore Sessions feature a rotating lineup of Chattanooga-area performers. Artists play short sets and pass the stage. The Woodshop is a small listening room on St. Elmo Avenue. No cover charge. Donations go directly to the initiative. Check the venue's social pages for the full artist lineup closer to the date.
