LVNDR plays two holiday nights at The Woodshop in St. Elmo on December 12 and 13. The Chattanooga band blends neo-soul, R&B, and psychedelic funk under vocalist Maria Jordania. They competed on Season 21 of America's Got Talent and drew praise from every judge. A holiday menu runs both nights, and cocktail attire is encouraged. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
