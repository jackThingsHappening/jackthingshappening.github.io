Jason Tinney performs this original one-man play in St. Elmo. The piece mixes storytelling, live music, and film into an Appalachian folk and alt-country road story. Kashun Parks directs and Jason Hackbarth scores the music. The work earned an ArtsBuild Artists Work in Partnership grant. Choose the matinee with doors at 1pm or the dinner show with doors at 6pm.
