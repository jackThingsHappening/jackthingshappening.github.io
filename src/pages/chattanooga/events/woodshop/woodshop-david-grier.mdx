David Grier is widely regarded as one of the finest flatpick guitarists around - a three-time IBMA Guitar Player of the Year. He plays The Woodshop in St. Elmo. Grier grew up in a musical family in Virginia and has recorded multiple solo albums. His playing blends traditional bluegrass with jazz and swing influences. He has toured and recorded with Alison Krauss and others in the acoustic music world. The small room at The Woodshop puts the audience close to the guitar. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
