Joe Pug plays The Woodshop in St. Elmo on January 30. The Austin songwriter writes acoustic folk and Americana built on sharp lyrics and plaintive harmonica. His songs echo the country-folk tradition of Bob Dylan and John Prine. Steve Earle mentored him early on. Brother and The Hayes open the show. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
