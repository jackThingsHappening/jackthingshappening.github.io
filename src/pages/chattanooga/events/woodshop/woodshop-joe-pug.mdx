Joe Pug plays The Woodshop in St. Elmo on January 30. The Austin songwriter writes acoustic folk and Americana built on strong lyrical craft. Pug has released multiple albums on Nashville's Lightning Rod Records and toured with artists including Josh Ritter and Neko Case. Critics have drawn comparisons to early Bob Dylan and John Prine for his plainspoken precision. His live show is solo or small band and works best in intimate rooms. The Woodshop on St. Elmo Avenue holds a small crowd close to the stage. Doors at 5pm, show at 6pm.
