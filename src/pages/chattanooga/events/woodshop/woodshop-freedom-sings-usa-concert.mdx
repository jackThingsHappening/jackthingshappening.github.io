Freedom Sings USA hosts a benefit concert at The Woodshop. The lineup features DANIMAL, The Essentials, Blake Worthington, and others. Freedom Sings USA is an organization that uses music to advocate for First Amendment rights. Each show raises funds for programs that connect students with working musicians and free speech education. The Woodshop on St. Elmo Avenue is a small listening venue with a close stage. This show spans multiple artists and runs most of the evening. Check the venue for ticket details and start time.
