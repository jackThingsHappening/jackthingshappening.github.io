Christkindlmarkt returns to The Commons in Collegedale for a two-day holiday market. The event draws on European Christmas market traditions. Vendors sell artisan gifts and festive food across the outdoor plazas. Warm Glühwein and twinkling lights set the Old-World mood.

The market runs Saturday from 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM. It reopens Sunday from 11:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Admission is free. Bring cash and arrive early, since the plaza fills up on weekend afternoons.
