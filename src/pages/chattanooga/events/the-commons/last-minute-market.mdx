The Last Minute Market gives holiday shoppers a final chance to buy local gifts before Christmas. Vendors fill The Commons with artisan goods and festive treats. Live music plays through the market. Shoppers can find handmade presents and stocking stuffers in one stop.

The market opens Saturday from 11:00 AM to 5:00 PM. It runs again Sunday from 12:00 PM to 5:00 PM. Admission is free. The date falls days before Christmas, so plan for a busy crowd.
