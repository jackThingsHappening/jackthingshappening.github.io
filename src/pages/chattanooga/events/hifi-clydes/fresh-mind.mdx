Reggae rhythms, rock, jazz, and blues all share one set. Fresh Mind comes from the greater Chattanooga area. Frontman James Snider was born in Jamaica and brings the reggae tradition into the band's sound. The group has performed across the region and recorded multiple sets. Hi-Fi Clyde's is a live music venue on Main Street in Chattanooga's South Broad District. Check the venue website for ticket details and start time. The room holds a few hundred people and has a dedicated dance floor in front of the stage.
