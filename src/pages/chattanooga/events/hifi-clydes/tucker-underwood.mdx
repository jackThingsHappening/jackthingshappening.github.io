Tucker Underwood plays modern country with a Southern rock edge. He lives in Chattanooga and writes songs built on melody and storytelling. His sound draws comparisons to Luke Combs, Koe Wetzel, and HARDY. His songs cover chasing a dream, heartbreak, and Friday nights. Hits 96 named him a rising country act on its Proximity Playlist. Hi-Fi Clyde's is a live music venue on Main Street in the South Broad District. This show is 21 and up with a $10 cover. Doors and start time are listed on the venue website.
