Big brass rock fills the room. Chattanooga Horn Authority is an eight-piece local band. The group plays horn-driven covers of Chicago, Blood Sweat & Tears, Steely Dan, and Huey Lewis. The members bring decades of stage experience. Late crowds pack the floor for these sets. Doors are 21 and up. Cover is $10.
