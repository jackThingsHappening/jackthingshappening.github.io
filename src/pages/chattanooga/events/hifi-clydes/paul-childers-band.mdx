Soul, funk, and blues rock guitar drive this Nashville set. Paul Childers sings, writes, and plays lead. His grooves lean on a Motown feel. He is a Songbirds Guitar Museum ambassador. He has opened for Vince Gill, B.B. King, and Robert Cray. His band brings that sound to Main Street. Doors are 21 and up. Cover is $10.
