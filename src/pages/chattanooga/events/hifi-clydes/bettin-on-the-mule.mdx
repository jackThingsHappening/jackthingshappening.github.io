Southern rock meets classic rock and country in this set. Bettin' On The Mule formed in 2017 in Rockmart, Georgia. Jacob Wilson leads the group and writes most of its songs. The band calls its blend hometown rock. They have won the Smiley's Georgia Music Showcase twice. Doors are 21 and up. Cover is $10.
