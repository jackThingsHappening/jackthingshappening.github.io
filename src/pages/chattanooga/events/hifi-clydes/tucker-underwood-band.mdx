Modern country and Southern roots shape this set. Tucker Underwood grew up in the South and lives in Chattanooga. Melody and storytelling lead his songs. He plays guitar and writes original material that sits at the intersection of modern country and Americana. The Tucker Underwood Band includes a full band backing him. Hi-Fi Clyde's is a live music venue on Main Street. Check the venue website for ticket details and start time. The room holds a few hundred people. Underwood also headlines Barrelhouse Ballroom and plays across the Chattanooga regional circuit.
