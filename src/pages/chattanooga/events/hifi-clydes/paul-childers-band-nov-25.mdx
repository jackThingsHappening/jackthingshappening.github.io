Pop, R&B, and soul mix with blues rock leads. Paul Childers returns to Hi-Fi Clyde's with his band. His grooves lean on a Motown feel. He plays as a Solo act and with his full band. Hi-Fi Clyde's is a live music venue on Main Street in Chattanooga's South Broad District. Check the venue website for ticket details and start time. The room holds a few hundred people. This November show is a return engagement from earlier in the year. Childers is a Nashville-based guitarist who brings a strong set of original soul and blues rock material to the stage.
