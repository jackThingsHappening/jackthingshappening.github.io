Five miles sits between a 5K and a 10K, which makes the Trail of Tears 5 Miler a solid mid-December workout for runners building through the holidays. The CTC handles chip timing and course certification. The race falls the same weekend as Christmas at the Choo-Choo, so downtown parking can be tighter than usual. Check the CTC calendar for start location and current registration fees.
