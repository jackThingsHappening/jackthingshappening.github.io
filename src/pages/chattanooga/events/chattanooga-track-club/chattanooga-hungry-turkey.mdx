The Chattanooga Hungry Turkey runs the Saturday after Thanksgiving, two days after the Fitness Central Turkey Trot. It gives runners back-to-back holiday race options without leaving the region. Registration is through RaceRoster. The November timing means cold morning temperatures, so dress in layers and expect a crowd of post-holiday runners. The CTC handles timing and certification. Check the CTC calendar for distance, start location, and current registration fees.
