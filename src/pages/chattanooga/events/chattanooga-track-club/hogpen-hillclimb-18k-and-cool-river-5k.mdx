Now in its 48th year, the Hogpen Hillclimb is one of the oldest races on the Chattanooga calendar. The 18k course carries significant elevation gain. A 5k Cool River Tubing option gives less-experienced runners a way in. Proceeds benefit Habitat for Humanity. January conditions mean cold mornings and potentially icy trails. The charity connection and long history make this one a fixture for local runners. Check the CTC calendar for start location and registration.
