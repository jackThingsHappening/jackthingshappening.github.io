Thanksgiving morning in Chattanooga means road racing before the turkey hits the table. The Fitness Central Turkey Trot is a CTC-sponsored event with chip timing and a certified course. The club has run events like this for decades and the logistics are well organized. These races fill up fast in November. Check the CTC calendar for distance, start time, and registration link.
