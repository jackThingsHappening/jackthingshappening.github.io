The Wauhatchie Trail Race heads into the valley near Lookout Mountain on wooded single-track with uneven terrain. February conditions can run from firm to muddy depending on recent rain. Trail shoes are worth the investment for this course. Registration is through UltraSignUp. The race draws a mix of Chattanooga trail regulars and runners from across the region. Check the CTC calendar or UltraSignUp for distance options and current fees.
