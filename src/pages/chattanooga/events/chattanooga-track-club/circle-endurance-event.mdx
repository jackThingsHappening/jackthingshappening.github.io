Pick your suffering: 3, 6, 12, or 24 hours on a looped course, covering as many miles as you can before time runs out. The format rewards patience over pace. There are no place finishes based on distance covered. You set your own target and adjust on the fly. Volunteers can sign up through UltraSignUp if you want to support without racing. Check the CTC calendar for course location and start time.
