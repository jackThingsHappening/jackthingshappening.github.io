Chattanooga Cars and Coffee meets on the 3rd Sunday of each month at Chattanooga State. The October show runs 8am to noon. All makes, models, and eras are welcome. The event draws collectors and enthusiasts from across Chattanooga and the surrounding region. Free to attend. No ticket required. Coffee is available on site. October is one of the most popular months for the show as the fall weather brings out the community and the light is good for photos. Check the event social pages for any special categories or guest appearances.
