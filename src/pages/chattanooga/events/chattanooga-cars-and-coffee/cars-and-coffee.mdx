Chattanooga Cars and Coffee meets at Chattanooga State on the 3rd Sunday of each month from March through November. Hours run 8am to noon. The show is free and open to the public. Enthusiasts bring everything from daily drivers to show cars. Each month often has a loose theme. Past focuses have included Italian marques, BMW M cars, JDM imports, and American muscle. The lot at 4501 Amnicola Hwy fills up early, so arriving before 9am gets you the best view of the lineup.
