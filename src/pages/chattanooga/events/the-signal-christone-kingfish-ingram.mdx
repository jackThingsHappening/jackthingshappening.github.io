Christone "Kingfish" Ingram brings his 2025 album Hard Road to The Signal on November 12. The Clarksdale, Mississippi guitarist picked up a guitar at age six and built a modern Delta blues voice that earned two Grammy nominations before he turned 25. His own Red Zero Records released Hard Road as his most personal work yet. Show at 8pm.
