Coolidge Park hosts Chattanooga's free New Year's Eve celebration on December 31. The park sits on the North Shore riverfront, across the Tennessee River from downtown. Family Fun Zones and a Kids Stage open in the afternoon. A Kid's Countdown runs near 5:55pm for families with young children who will not make midnight. The Main Stage brings live music from 6pm until the ball drops. Food and beverage vendors from around the city line the park. At midnight the crowd counts down together and fireworks light up the river. The City of Chattanooga hosts the event each year, and admission is free.
