Cody Smith headlines The Comedy Catch. Comedic Cody left pharmacy to chase comedy and now tours the world. He has more than seven million combined followers and over a billion streams online. His laid-back act leans on clever wordplay and sharp takes on adult life. He is known for his partnership with Skrewball Peanut Butter Whiskey. This show starts at 7:00 PM on Thursday. It is rated R, so plan an adult night out downtown.
