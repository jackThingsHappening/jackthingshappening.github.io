Ryan Goodcase headlines a Lookout Comedy Festival show at The Comedy Catch. Goodcase writes tight jokes and delivers them with dry, deadpan calm. He became the youngest winner of the World Series of Comedy in 2019. He later won the San Francisco International Comedy Competition. A festival clip of his set drew tens of millions of views. Shows run at 7:00 PM and 9:15 PM. The show is rated R.
