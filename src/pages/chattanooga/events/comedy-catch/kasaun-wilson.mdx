Kasaun Wilson headlines The Comedy Catch. He is a comedian and writer who earned Emmy and WGA nominations for The Problem with Jon Stewart. Rolling Stone recognized his work on that show. He began comedy at Howard University and has since played the Apollo Theater and Netflix Is a Joke. His stand-up blends sharp storytelling with honest observation. He has also appeared on Last Comic Standing and Laff Tracks. Shows run Friday and Saturday at 7:00 PM and 9:15 PM. This show is rated PG-18.
