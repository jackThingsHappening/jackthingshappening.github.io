The Now You See Us Comedy Tour comes to The Comedy Catch. The show features Mason Bird, Chris Silio, and JJ Alexander. All three earned Golden Tickets on the Kill Tony podcast. Chris Silio is a blind comic from Miami known for brutally honest jokes. The night brings outrageous stories, sharp observations, and unpredictable humor. This show starts at 7:00 PM on Thursday. It is rated R, so plan an adult night out downtown.
