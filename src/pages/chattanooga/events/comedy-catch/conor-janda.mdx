Conor Janda headlines The Comedy Catch. The Philadelphia native and New York transplant writes sharp, personal jokes. His set covers a stay-at-home dad, an all-boys Catholic school, and getting sober. A Just for Laughs New Face, he has appeared on Comedy Central and in Kevin Hart's Funny AF on Netflix. He also co-created the web series Boys Club. This show starts at 6:00 PM on Sunday. It is rated R and LGBTQ-friendly.
