Jimmy Sowell headlines a Lookout Comedy Festival show at The Comedy Catch. Sowell is a Chattanooga comic known online as NERDORK. His viral pullover sketches have drawn hundreds of millions of views across social media. He pairs silly energy with adult stoner humor and nearly a decade of stand-up experience. This show starts at 7:00 PM. The show is rated R, so come ready for an adult set.
