Steve Hofstetter is a New York comedian with a Columbia degree, a Fox television series, and nearly 290 million YouTube views. His style is fast, smart, and built on observational comedy, social commentary, and crowd work. He became widely known online for dealing with hecklers -- clips of those moments went viral and landed him a TV deal with Fox. His stand-up covers American culture, politics, religion, and everyday ignorance with a sharp, unsentimental eye. He also authored the memoir Ginger Kid. This show is a rare single-night stand at The Comedy Catch. It runs Monday Nov 2, 2027 at 7:00pm. The venue holds a small crowd, so tickets tend to move fast.
