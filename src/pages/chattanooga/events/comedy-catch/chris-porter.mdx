Chris Porter brings raw, caustic stand-up to The Comedy Catch for a special engagement. The Kansas City comic was a finalist on Last Comic Standing and a regular on The Bob & Tom Show. He works clean of props and gimmicks, drawing hard laughs from everyday life. Shows run Friday at 7:00 PM and Saturday at 7:00 PM and 9:15 PM. Rated R, so come ready for an adult night out.
