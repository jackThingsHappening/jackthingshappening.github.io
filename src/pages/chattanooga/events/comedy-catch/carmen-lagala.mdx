Carmen Lagala headlines a special engagement at The Comedy Catch. The New York comic grew up in small-town Vermont and plays that charm against smart, dark jokes. She made her network debut on The Late Show with Stephen Colbert in 2018. Her special Sweet Batch has passed 300,000 views online. Shows run Saturday and Sunday at 7:00 PM and 9:15 PM. This show is rated R, so plan an adult night out.
