Rocky Dale Davis headlines The Comedy Catch. The Brookwood, Alabama native has racked up over 500 million views online. He has appeared on NBC's Bring the Funny and Comedy Central's Hart of the City. He jokes about his redneck family, his Brooklyn neighbors, and sober dating. He has opened for Bert Kreischer and Tom Segura. Shows run Wednesday and Thursday at 7:00 PM. This show is rated R, so plan an adult night out downtown.
