Shapel Lacey headlines The Comedy Catch. The Mesa, Arizona native is a paid regular at the world-famous Comedy Store. A former competitive cheerleader and punk rocker, he turns personal chaos into comedy. His material runs sharp, honest, and unexpectedly heartwarming. He made the 2026 Netflix shortlist and performs alongside Bill Burr and Matt Rife. Shows run Friday and Saturday at 7:00 PM and 9:15 PM. This show is rated R, so plan an adult night out.
