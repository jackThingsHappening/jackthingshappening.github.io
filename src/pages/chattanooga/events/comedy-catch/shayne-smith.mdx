Shayne Smith headlines The Comedy Catch. The Utah-raised comic is one of the most tattooed performers in comedy. His act blends sharp storytelling with an offbeat, self-aware sense of humor. His special Prison for Wizards has passed 32 million views online. He also sings for the band Painted Devils. Shows run Friday and Saturday at 7:00 PM and 9:15 PM. This is a clean show that works for a night out downtown.
