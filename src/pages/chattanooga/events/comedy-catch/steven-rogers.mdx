Steven Rogers headlines The Comedy Catch. The Syracuse native and New York comic made his TV debut on The Late Show with Stephen Colbert. He opens for Brian Regan in theaters across the country. His act blends wit, vulnerability, and observational jokes about everyday life. His album Before He Was Super hit number one on iTunes. Shows run Friday through Sunday. This is a clean show that works for a family night out.
