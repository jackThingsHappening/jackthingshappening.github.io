Tony Tone headlines The Comedy Catch. He is a veteran comedian and actor who has performed since 1990. He appeared on HBO's Def Comedy Jam, BET's Comic View, and Comics Unleashed. From 2006 to 2013 he served as Ringmaster for the UniverSoul Circus. His act mixes sharp impressions with stories about family and everyday life. He has shared the stage with Dave Chappelle, Steve Harvey, and Chris Rock. Shows run Friday and Saturday at 7:00 PM and 9:15 PM. This show is rated R.
