Rob Coleman headlines two nights at The Comedy Catch. The Cleveland comic has worked the stage for more than a decade. He won the 2025 World Series of Comedy. Coleman keeps his material clean and clever, which lets him play clubs, colleges, and churches alike. Shows run Friday and Saturday at 7:00 PM and 9:15 PM. Come downtown to 29 Station Street for the show.
