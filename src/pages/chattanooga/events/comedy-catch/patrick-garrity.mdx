Patrick Garrity headlines a special engagement at The Comedy Catch. The comic runs a nonstop road schedule he calls the Neverending Tour. He packs his act with wild characters, sharp impressions, and off-the-wall stories. Fans sign his white touring car after every show. His material draws on the military, Irish culture, and life as the youngest sibling. This show is rated R, so plan an adult night out.
