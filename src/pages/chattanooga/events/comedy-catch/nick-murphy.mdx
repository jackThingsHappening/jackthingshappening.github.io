Nick Murphy headlines a weekend run at The Comedy Catch. The Atlanta-raised, Austin-based comic made his debut on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon. Netflix selected him for its 2025 Introducing showcase after sets at the Netflix Is A Joke and Moontower festivals. His observational comedy draws on years of watching and writing. Shows run Friday and Saturday, with 7:00 PM and 9:15 PM sets on Saturday. This show is rated for adults.
