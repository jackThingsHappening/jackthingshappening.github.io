Luke Null headlines The Comedy Catch. The Cincinnati comic was a cast member on Saturday Night Live for its 43rd season. He builds his act around a guitar, pairing witty jokes with catchy songs. He trained in improv at the iO Theater in Chicago before SNL. His special Pretty Songs, Dirty Words came out in 2025. This show starts at 7:00 PM on Thursday. It is rated R, so plan an adult night out.
