Billy Sorrells plays a special engagement at The Comedy Catch. He was born in Chattanooga and grew up in Houston. Sorrells built a following on Wild 'N Out and BET Comic View. He also went viral online with his Peaches character. This one-night show starts at 7:00 PM. The show is rated R, so leave the kids at home and come for an adult evening of stand-up.
