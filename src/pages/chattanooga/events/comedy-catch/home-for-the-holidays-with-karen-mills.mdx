Karen Mills headlines a holiday show at The Comedy Catch. The touring comic has more than 30 years on stage and played the Grand Ole Opry. She was an All-American basketball player at UTC, and her jersey hangs retired. Her honest, relatable jokes cover aging, menopause, and daily life. A cancer survivor, she tours with Leanne Morgan and appears in the Netflix series Leanne. This show starts at 6:00 PM on Sunday. It is rated PG-13.
