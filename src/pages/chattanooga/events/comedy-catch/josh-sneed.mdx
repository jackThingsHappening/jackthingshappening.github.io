Josh Sneed headlines The Comedy Catch. The Cincinnati comic left a job at Procter and Gamble to chase stand-up. He has a Comedy Central Presents special and a Last Comic Standing run. His clean, relatable act draws on life as a husband and father. He is one of the most-played comics on Sirius XM and hosts the Detention podcast. Shows run Friday and Saturday at 7:00 PM and 9:15 PM. This show is rated PG-13.
