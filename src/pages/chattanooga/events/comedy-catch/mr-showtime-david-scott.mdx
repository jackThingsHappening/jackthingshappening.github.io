David Scott headlines The Comedy Catch as Mr. Showtime. The New York native holds the Guinness World Record for the longest solo stand-up set at 40 hours and 8 minutes. His act channels Rat Pack style and pure stage energy. The finale runs the crowd through four decades of music. He has appeared on HBO and Comedy Central. Shows run Friday and Saturday, with 7:00 PM and 9:15 PM sets on Friday. This show is rated for adults.
