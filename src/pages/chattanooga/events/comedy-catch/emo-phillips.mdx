Emo Phillips headlines The Comedy Catch. The Chicago-born comic became a cult figure in the 1980s with surreal, offbeat one-liners. His act uses a wandering falsetto and a confused, childlike delivery. Critics have likened his style to Pee-wee Herman mixed with Steven Wright. He slips dark twists into innocent setups when the crowd least expects it. This show starts at 7:00 PM on Wednesday. It is rated for adults, so plan a night out downtown.
