Chris Cope headlines The Comedy Catch. The Florida-raised, Los Angeles-based comic started in 2003 and tours nationally. His Dry Bar special Babyface has passed six million views. He has appeared on Jimmy Kimmel Live, Tosh.0, and The Conan O'Brien Show. He also won the 2015 Big Sky Comedy Festival. Shows run Friday and Saturday at 7:00 PM and 9:15 PM. This show is rated R, so plan an adult night out downtown.
