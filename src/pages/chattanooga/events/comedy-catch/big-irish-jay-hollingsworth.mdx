Big Irish Jay Hollingsworth headlines two nights at The Comedy Catch. The comic came up in Boston, where readers once named him the city's best comedy export. His charmingly brutal style has drawn more than 100 million online views. His debut special Green Monster streams on Amazon Prime. Shows run Tuesday and Wednesday at 7:00 PM and 9:15 PM. This show carries an adult rating.
