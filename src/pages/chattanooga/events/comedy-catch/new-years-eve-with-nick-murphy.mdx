Nick Murphy headlines New Year's Eve at The Comedy Catch. The Atlanta-raised, Austin-based comic made his debut on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon. Netflix picked him for its 2025 Introducing showcase after sets at major festivals. He credits his stage skill to years of observation and hard work. Ring in the new year with two shows at 7:00 PM and 10:00 PM. This show is rated R, so plan an adult night out.
