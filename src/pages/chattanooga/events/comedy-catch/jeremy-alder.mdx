Jeremy Alder plays a special engagement at The Comedy Catch. The North Carolina comic grew up homeschooled by missionary parents and once worked as a preacher. He now mines that upbringing for sharp, thoughtful jokes. His Dry Bar special Purity Pants has drawn millions of views. He has opened for Tom Papa and Demetri Martin. This show starts at 7:00 PM and is rated PG-13.
