Alex Velluto headlines a clean comedy night at The Comedy Catch. The Salt Lake City comic is known for sharp observational jokes and a tidy delivery. He has recorded three Dry Bar Comedy specials and toured more than 100 cities. Nate Bargatze picked him to perform on the series The Showcase. This show is clean, so it works for a wide crowd. Doors open downtown at 29 Station Street.
