Moody Molavi headlines a high-energy night at The Comedy Catch. The Tennessee-based comic grew up as an Iranian Southern Baptist and mines that mashup for laughs. He calls himself a blend of kebabs and grits. His Dry Bar special Remove Before Flight has passed 10 million views. His act runs on animated storytelling and family stories. This show is rated PG-13. Find the room downtown at 29 Station Street.
