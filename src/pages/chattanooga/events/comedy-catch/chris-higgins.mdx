Chris Higgins plays two nights at The Comedy Catch. You can grab the 7:00 PM or 9:15 PM show on either date. His comedy is rated R, so expect an adult crowd and mature material. Higgins has performed at clubs and festivals nationally and appeared on regional comedy circuits. His stand-up draws on personal experience and sharp observational writing. The Comedy Catch is on Chestnut Street in downtown Chattanooga. Check the venue website for ticket prices. Each show runs about 75 minutes. Doors open 30 minutes before showtime.
