Etta May is a Southern comedy character created by actress Brenda Ferrari. She goes by the "Queen of Southern Sass" and plays it straight -- a fictional housewife from Bald Knob, Arkansas with a deadpan command of Southern domestic life. Her set covers family, motherhood, and the low-stakes chaos of everyday routines. It is clean and crowd-friendly without being soft. She won the American Comedy Award for Stand-Up Comic of the Year and has appeared on Oprah, Showtime, CMT, and CBS Sunday Morning. The Comedy Catch runs four shows across two nights: Thursday Feb 19 at 6:30pm and 8:45pm, and Friday Feb 20 at 6:00pm and 8:30pm. Doors open 30 minutes before show time.
