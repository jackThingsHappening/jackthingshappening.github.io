Alex Kumin headlines a Lookout Comedy Festival show at The Comedy Catch. The New York comic came up through Chicago and Boston before settling in the city. She writes bold, sharp stand-up about gender and social life. She is a regular at the Comedy Cellar and topped a 2025 comics-to-watch list. Shows run at 7:00 PM and 9:15 PM. The show is rated R.
