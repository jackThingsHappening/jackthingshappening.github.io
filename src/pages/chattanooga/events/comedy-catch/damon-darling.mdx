Damon Darling headlines The Comedy Catch. The Ohio comic built a huge online following with crowd-work clips and his Got a Dollar series. He has more than 2.7 million TikTok followers and hundreds of millions of video views. His comedy runs observational and down-to-earth, with stories from his oil-rig days. He started stand-up during his sobriety journey. Shows run Thursday at 7:00 PM and 9:15 PM. This show is rated for adults.
