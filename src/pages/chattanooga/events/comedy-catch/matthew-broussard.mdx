Matthew Broussard headlines The Comedy Catch. The Atlanta-raised comic studied applied math at Rice before stand-up. He has performed on The Tonight Show with Jimmy Fallon and Conan. He also filmed a Comedy Central Half Hour and competed in Roast Battle. His set digs into personal stories plus sidebars on grammar and statistics. Shows run Friday at 7:00 PM and 9:15 PM, Saturday at 6:00 PM and 8:30 PM. This show is rated for adults.
