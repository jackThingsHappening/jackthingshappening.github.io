Brent Terhune headlines The Comedy Catch. The Indianapolis comic built 65 million-plus views with his satirical online character. Variety named him one of its 10 Comics to Watch for 2020. He has written daily topical jokes for the syndicated Bob and Tom Show for over a decade. His style gets called a blue-collar take on The Colbert Report. Shows run Friday and Saturday at 7:00 PM and 9:15 PM. This show is rated R.
