Caleb Synan grew up as a preacher's kid in a small Southern town before moving to Los Angeles to pursue comedy. He has performed on Conan twice, appeared on Last Comic Standing, and released his Comedy Central special "30" in 2022. His style is low-key and sharp -- measured pacing, smart writing, and material rooted in family, religion, and millennial life. He finds the weird angle in ordinary situations without pushing hard for it. The result feels less like a show and more like a good conversation with someone who notices things. He performs Thursday Feb 26 at 7:00pm and Saturday Feb 27 at 7:00pm and 9:15pm. Doors open 30 minutes before showtime.
