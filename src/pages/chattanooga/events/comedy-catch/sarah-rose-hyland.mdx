Sarah Rose Hyland headlines The Comedy Catch. The Louisville native started at Second City Chicago before moving to Los Angeles. She trained at UCB and The Groundlings and now performs at the Comedy Store. She landed series-regular roles on MTV's Punk'd and Fox's MADtv. She has opened for Margaret Cho and Bobby Lee. This show starts at 7:00 PM on Thursday. It is rated R, so plan an adult night out downtown.
