Greg Morton headlines The Comedy Catch. The Canadian-born impressionist and voice actor reached the semifinals on America's Got Talent. His act blends rapid-fire voices, pop-culture satire, and storytelling. He has voiced more than 20 cartoons and played Just for Laughs four times. He has shared stages with Dave Chappelle and Chris Rock. Shows run Friday and Saturday at 7:00 PM and 9:15 PM. This is a clean show that works for the whole family.
