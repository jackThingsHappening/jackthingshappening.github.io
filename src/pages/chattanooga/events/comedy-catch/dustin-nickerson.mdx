Dustin Nickerson headlines a special engagement at The Comedy Catch. The Seattle-area comic built his name on clean, observational jokes about marriage, parenting, and aging. He married young and raised three kids, and that chaos drives his act. He has performed on The Tonight Show and in the Netflix Is a Joke Festival. Shows run Saturday and Sunday at 7:00 PM and 9:15 PM. This show is rated PG-13.
