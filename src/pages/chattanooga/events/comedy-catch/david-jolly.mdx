David Jolly headlines The Comedy Catch. The Florida comic built a following through the Kill Tony podcast. He calls himself America's Favorite Uncle and leans on warm, relatable storytelling. His set runs raw and fearless, with unfiltered stories and sharp wit. He speaks openly about his sobriety on stage and online. This show starts at 7:00 PM on Thursday. It is rated for adults, so plan a night out downtown at 29 Station Street.
