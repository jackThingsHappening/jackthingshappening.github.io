Billy Wayne Davis headlines a special engagement at The Comedy Catch. The comic grew up in Crossville, Tennessee, and built his act on small-town stories. He has appeared on Last Comic Standing and WTF with Marc Maron. He toured with Ralphie May and opened for Sturgill Simpson. His album Testify arrived in 2022. This show is rated R, so plan an adult night out downtown at 29 Station Street.
