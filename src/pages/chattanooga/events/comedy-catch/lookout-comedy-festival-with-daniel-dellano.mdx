Daniel Dellanno headlines a Lookout Comedy Festival show at The Comedy Catch. The Atlanta comic started stand-up at age 18 and made his Netflix debut on Kevin Hart's Funny AF. His style blends quick wit with honest jokes about dating and city life. He has also played the Comedy Store in Los Angeles. This show starts at 7:00 PM. The show is rated R.
