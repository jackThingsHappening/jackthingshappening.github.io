Two nights of adult stand-up from Claude Stuart hit The Comedy Catch. Shows run at 7:00 PM and 9:15 PM on both dates. Plan a grown-up night out downtown. Stuart is a veteran stand-up comedian with years of touring experience across clubs nationally. His material is adult-rated and leans on experience, observation, and character work. The Comedy Catch is on Chestnut Street in downtown Chattanooga. Check the venue website for ticket prices. Doors open 30 minutes before showtime. Each show runs about 75 minutes.
