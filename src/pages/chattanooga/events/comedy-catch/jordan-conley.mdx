Jordan Conley headlines The Comedy Catch. He is a stand-up comedian from Garden Grove, California. He won the Wildcard spot on Season 17 of America's Got Talent and reached the semifinals. Simon Cowell gave his set a standing ovation. His comedy covers movies, superheroes, theme parks, and married life. He brings high energy and a rapid-fire delivery to the stage. Shows run Friday and Saturday at 7:00 PM and 9:15 PM. This show is rated PG-18.
