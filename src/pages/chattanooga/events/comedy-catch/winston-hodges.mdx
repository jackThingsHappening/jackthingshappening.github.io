Winston Hodges plays a special engagement at The Comedy Catch. The comic grew up in Richmond, Virginia and now tours full time from Washington, D.C. His high-energy sets mix Southern charm with edgy, pointed jokes about race and religion. He reached the top six on Kevin Hart's Funny AF for Netflix. This show starts at 7:00 PM and is rated R.
