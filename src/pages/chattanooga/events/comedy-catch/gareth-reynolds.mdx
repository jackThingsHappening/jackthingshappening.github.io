Gareth Reynolds headlines The Comedy Catch. The Milwaukee comic co-hosts the hit history podcast The Dollop, which pulls millions of downloads each month. His debut album Riddled With Disease hit number one on the Billboard comedy chart. He has written for or appeared on New Girl, Arrested Development, and You're the Worst. He also co-hosts We're Here to Help with Jake Johnson. This show starts at 7:00 PM on Thursday. It is rated for adults.
