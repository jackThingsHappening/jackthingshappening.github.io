DJ Sandhu headlines two nights at The Comedy Catch. The Sacramento comic mixes absurd observations with sharp takes on culture, travel, and family. He has performed at the Edinburgh Fringe and toured dozens of cities each year. He has also shared stages with Russell Peters and Theo Von. Shows run Tuesday and Wednesday at 7:00 PM and 9:15 PM. This show carries an adult rating.
