Sean Patton headlines a special engagement at The Comedy Catch. The New Orleans comic built his name in New York with restless, physical storytelling. He has twice performed on Conan and released the Peacock special Number One. He also stars on the FX series English Teacher. Shows run Monday and Tuesday at 7:00 PM and 9:15 PM. This show is rated R, so plan an adult night out.
