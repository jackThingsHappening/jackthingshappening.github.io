Mario Tory headlines The Comedy Catch. The Atlanta comic is known for quick storytelling and crowd-savvy timing. He was the first comedian featured on Kevin Hart's Hart of the City on Comedy Central. Hart then produced his half-hour special for The Next Level. He has also performed on 50 Cent's BET show 50 Central. This show starts at 7:00 PM on Saturday. It is rated R, so plan an adult night out downtown.
