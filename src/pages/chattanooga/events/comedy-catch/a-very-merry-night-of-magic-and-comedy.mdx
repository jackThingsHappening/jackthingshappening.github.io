Magicians Lance Johnson and Lawrence James team up for a holiday comedy magic show. The two performers bring more than 60 years of combined experience to the stage. Their act mixes sleight of hand with laughs and Christmas cheer. They have played comedy clubs, college campuses, and Fortune 500 events across the country. This show is clean and works for a family night out. It starts at 7:00 PM at 29 Station Street.
