Four courses with award-winning pitmaster Chris Prieto - smoked brisket, farm-sourced sides, specialty bourbon cocktails, and live music against the sunflower fields. Happy Valley Farms hosts this dinner on its property. Prieto is known for his work in competition barbecue and farm-to-table barbecue cooking. The dinner format pairs his food with bourbon pairings selected for each course. Live music plays during the meal. Register through Happy Valley Farms. Check the site for cost and the specific date. Space is limited.
