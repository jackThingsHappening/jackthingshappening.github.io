Paint the countryside with nationally acclaimed watercolorist Alan Shuptrine. The workshop runs five hours with live demonstrations and personal instruction on capturing fall landscapes in watercolor. Shuptrine is a Chattanooga-based painter known for large-scale, highly detailed watercolor works. His paintings of the Appalachian region have been published and exhibited nationally. Happy Valley Farms provides the setting. Register through Happy Valley Farms. Check the site for cost and the specific date. All skill levels are welcome but participants should bring their own watercolor supplies if they have them.
