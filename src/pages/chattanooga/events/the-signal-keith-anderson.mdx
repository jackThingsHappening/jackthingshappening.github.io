Country veteran Keith Anderson plays The Signal on September 18. He co-wrote Garth Brooks and George Jones's "Beer Run," penned Big & Rich's number one "Lost in This Moment," and built a solo career on gold-certified hook-heavy country. His 2008 Top 5 hit "I Still Miss You" still anchors his live sets. Music at 8pm.
