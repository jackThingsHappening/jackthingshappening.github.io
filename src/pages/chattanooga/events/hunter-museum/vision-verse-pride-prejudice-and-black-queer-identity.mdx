Vision + Verse marks Chattanooga Pride Week at the Hunter Museum on October 15. Marcus Ellsworth, River Abraxas, Janelle Jezebelle, and other local Black creatives lead performances and discussion. The program ties to the exhibition Free as they want to be, now on view at the museum. It suits anyone who wants to explore Black queer identity through art and open conversation.
