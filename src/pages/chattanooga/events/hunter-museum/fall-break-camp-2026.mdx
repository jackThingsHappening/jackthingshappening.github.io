Kids ages 6 to 12 spend fall break making art at the Hunter, October 12 to 16. Each of the five days brings a new project, from sculpture and textiles to prints. Campers explore the museum collection and draw ideas from the current exhibitions. The museum supplies all art materials and snacks. Pack a nut-free lunch and a water bottle each day. Check the museum site for daily times and how to register.
