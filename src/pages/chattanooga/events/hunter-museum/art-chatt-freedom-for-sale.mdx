SOBI Connect, a local creative collective, leads this evening program at the Hunter Museum. The program responds to the exhibition Free as they want to be: Artists Committed to Memory. Dancers, musicians, and poets perform throughout the night. You also make your own art and talk about the show's themes. The event runs from 6 to 8 PM as part of Throwback Thursday. Admission is free.
