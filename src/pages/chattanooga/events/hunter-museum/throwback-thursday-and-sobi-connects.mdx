The Hunter opens its doors for free on November 5 from 4 to 8 PM. Visitors explore the permanent collection and the exhibition Free as they want to be: Artists Committed to Memory. SOBI, a collective of local Black creatives, leads a collaborative art program that responds to the show. The Art Bridges Foundation Access for All program sponsors the free admission. Drop in anytime during the evening.
