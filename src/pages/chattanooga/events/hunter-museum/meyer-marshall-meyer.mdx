String Theory brings three string players to the Hunter Museum on January 26 at 6:30pm. Bassist Edgar Meyer performs with his son George Meyer and mandolin master Mike Marshall. The trio blends classical, bluegrass, and folk in one evening. Their sound crosses genre lines with ease. The concert takes place inside the museum, part of the String Theory chamber music season. Seats are limited for this intimate setting. Check the String Theory or Hunter Museum site for ticket prices and how to buy. Arrive early to find your seat before the music starts.
