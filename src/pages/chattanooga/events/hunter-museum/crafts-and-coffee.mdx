Crafts and Coffee gives adults ages 18 and up an afternoon of art-making and conversation on November 6. Makers create a hands-on craft while they sip complimentary coffee or tea. The relaxed setting pairs creative work with easy social time in the museum. EBT cardholders enter for free. The program runs from 2 to 4 PM on the first Friday of the month.
