Wreaths and Art at Walden Peak Farm takes the museum outdoors on September 19. Spend the day making a wreath and looking at art on the farm. It is a hands-on trip into the countryside. Walden Peak Farm is an agricultural property in the Chattanooga region. The Hunter Museum brings art interpretation to this off-site location. All wreath-making materials are provided. Check the Hunter Museum website for cost and registration. This is a full-day program that combines craft, art appreciation, and a visit to a working farm.
