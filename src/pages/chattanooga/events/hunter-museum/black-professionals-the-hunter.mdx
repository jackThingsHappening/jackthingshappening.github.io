This bimonthly gathering brings area Black professionals together for community, culture, and connection on October 5. The October event honors BettyeLynn Smith and features live performances, food, and time to network. Guests can view the exhibition Free as they want to be and take part in a new career development session led by Unum and the Urban League. An after-hours social follows the main program.
