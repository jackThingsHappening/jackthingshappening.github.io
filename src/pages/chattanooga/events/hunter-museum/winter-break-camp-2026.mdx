Rising 1st through 6th graders spend winter break making art at the Hunter, December 28 to 31. Campers work with a wide range of art materials and tools across the week. Each day includes hands-on projects and guided walks through the galleries and sculpture garden. Kids draw ideas from the museum collection of American art. Camp runs 9am to 4pm each day. Non-members pay $300 for the week. Passport-level members and above pay $240. Check the museum site for lunch rules and how to register.
