Art After Dark gives teens ages 12 to 18 after-hours access to the Downtown Chattanooga Library on October 10. The night pairs art-making with snacks and a relaxed social setting. This month's activity teaches clay mask techniques. Teens must pre-register and bring a signed guardian permission form to take part.
