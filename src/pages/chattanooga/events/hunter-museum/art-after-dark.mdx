Art After Dark gives teens ages 12 to 18 after-hours access to the Downtown Chattanooga Library on October 10. The night pairs art-making with snacks and a relaxed social atmosphere. The Hunter Museum co-presents this event. Art supplies and materials are provided. The Downtown Chattanooga Library is on Broad Street. This is a free event for teens. No registration is required. The evening is designed to give young people a creative and social outlet in a welcoming space. Check the Hunter Museum or Chattanooga Public Library websites for the specific start time and program details.
