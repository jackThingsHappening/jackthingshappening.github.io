Crafts & Coffee gives you a slow morning at the Hunter Museum on September 4. Grab a hot cup, sit down at a table, and make something small with your hands. It is a low-pressure creative morning. The Hunter Museum is on the bluff above the Tennessee River on Bluff View. Crafts & Coffee runs periodically as part of the museum's community programming. Supplies are provided. No art experience is required. Check the Hunter Museum website for cost and registration. The museum is open to the public and admission fees may apply for access to the galleries.
