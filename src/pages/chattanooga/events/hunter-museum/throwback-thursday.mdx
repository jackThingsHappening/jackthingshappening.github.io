Throwback Thursday opens the Hunter Museum for free on December 3 from 4 PM to 8 PM. Visitors can explore the permanent collection at no charge. The current exhibition "Free as they want to be: Artists Committed to Memory" is also open. Cheryl Finley, Ph.D., and Deborah Willis, Ph.D., curated that show. The Art Bridges Foundation supports this program through its Access for All initiative.
