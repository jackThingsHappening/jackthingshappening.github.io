Chery Jordan and the Runway Rebels bring a fashion show into the galleries on October 29. Models walk a runway that celebrates Black style and creative expression. Music and dance fill the space alongside the looks. The show ties into the exhibition Free as they want to be: Artists Committed to Memory, which traces identity and cultural memory through art. Regular museum admission applies.
