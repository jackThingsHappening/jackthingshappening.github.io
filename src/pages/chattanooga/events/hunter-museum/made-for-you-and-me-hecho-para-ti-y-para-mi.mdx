This program runs in both Spanish and English on September 24. Families look at art together and then make something with their own hands. The Spanish title, Hecho Para Ti y Para Mí, tells you the museum wants every visitor to feel at home. Bring the kids and share the gallery in the language you speak best. Check the museum site for the time and how to sign up.
