Dr. Deborah Willis speaks at the Hunter Museum on October 22 as part of the Art Wise series. She is a MacArthur Fellow and chairs the Department of Photography and Imaging at New York University. She ranks among the nation's leading historians of African American photography. Her talk draws on the exhibition Free As They Want To Be: Artists Committed To Memory. This event is free and open to all.
