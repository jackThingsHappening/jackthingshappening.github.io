The Hunter turns Throwback Thursday into a music night on September 3. Live sound fills the galleries and ties into the Saya Woolfalk exhibition. Wander with the music as your guide. The Saya Woolfalk exhibition features large-scale installations that blend identity, mythology, and science. Throwback Thursday is a recurring Hunter Museum program that invites visitors into the galleries in a social, after-hours format. Check the Hunter Museum website for admission details and start time. The museum is on the bluff above the Tennessee River on Bluff View in Chattanooga.
