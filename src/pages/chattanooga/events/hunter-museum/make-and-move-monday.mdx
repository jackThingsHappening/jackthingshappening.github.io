Make and Move Monday mixes hands-on art with a little bit of moving around on September 7. Kids build something at the table, then get up and shake out the wiggles. The Hunter Museum hosts this family-friendly Monday program. It is designed for young children with adult accompaniment. Art supplies are provided. The museum is on the bluff above the Tennessee River on Bluff View. Check the Hunter Museum website for cost, registration, and age recommendations. The Make and Move format alternates between seated art activities and movement exercises to keep young participants engaged.
