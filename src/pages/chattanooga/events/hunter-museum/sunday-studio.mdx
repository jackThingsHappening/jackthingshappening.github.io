Sunday Studio fills the Hunter Museum with drop-in art activities on October 4. Kids and families work through hands-on projects led by museum staff, the Downtown Public Library, and the Houston Museum. The program celebrates the new exhibition Free as they want to be and marks the reopening of the Walnut Street Bridge. Outdoor activities run alongside the indoor tables.
