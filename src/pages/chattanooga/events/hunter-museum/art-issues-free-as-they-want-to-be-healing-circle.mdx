This Art+Issues healing circle invites Black men to a space for honest conversation and reflection on December 10. Anthony M. Wiley Jr. of Welcome to Today leads the gathering. The session draws on the exhibition "Free as they want to be: Artists Committed to Memory." Participants explore freedom, identity, and healing. The program runs from 6 PM to 7 PM and admission is free.
