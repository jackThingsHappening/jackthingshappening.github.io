Fragments of Becoming runs at the Hunter Museum on December 3 from 6 to 7 PM. Artist Karla Bivens and designer Vee Rhodes lead this program. They explore how people and communities hold memory, identity, absence, and belonging. The work shows how we save, break, and rebuild our memories over time. The program responds to the exhibition Free as they want to be: Artists Committed to Memory. Admission is free and open to all.
