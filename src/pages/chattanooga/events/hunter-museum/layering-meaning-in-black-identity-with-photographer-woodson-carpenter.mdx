Photographer and poet Woodson Carpenter leads this hands-on workshop on October 8. It opens with a guided walkthrough of the exhibition Free as they want to be. Each guest brings a personal photograph to swap with another attendee, then responds with paint, pencil, poetry, or prose. Everyone leaves with a piece of collaborative art, and no advance registration is required.
