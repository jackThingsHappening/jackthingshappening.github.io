Vision + Verse: Posing as Past runs at the Hunter Museum on November 19 from 6 to 7 PM. Local performers respond to art through dance, poetry, voice, and music. The program connects to the exhibition Free as they want to be: Artists Committed to Memory. Each piece asks how we picture the past and who gets to pose within it. Admission is free and open to everyone.
