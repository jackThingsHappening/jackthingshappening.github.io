Students from STEM School share their own readings of works in the Hunter's East Wing galleries on October 8. The evening spotlights how young creative and technical minds see the museum collection. Teachers, friends, and families attend free after checking in at Guest Services. The program centers on student presentations rather than a general museum tour.
