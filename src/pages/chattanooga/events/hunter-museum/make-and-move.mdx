Make and Move is a monthly program for preschool and homeschool families on November 2. Each session blends a gallery experience, a hands-on art activity, and dance or movement. Kids build something at the table, then get up and move their bodies. One adult joins each family for free, and total family cost stays at or below $25. It runs from 1 to 2 PM.
