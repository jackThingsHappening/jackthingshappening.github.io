Friday nights, TailGate Brewery on Market St puts a movie on the big screen at 7pm. Grab a fresh pint from the taproom. Find a seat and settle in. The show runs about two hours. TailGate Brewery is a Nashville-based craft brewery with a taproom on Market Street in the Southside neighborhood of Chattanooga. The movie selection rotates weekly and is announced on social media. No ticket required - walk in and order a beer. The taproom pours TailGate's core lineup and seasonal releases. Check the social pages for the weekly film announcement.
