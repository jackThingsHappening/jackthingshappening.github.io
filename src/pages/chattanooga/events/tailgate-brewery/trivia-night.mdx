Thursday nights belong to trivia at TailGate Brewery on Market St. Questions kick off at 7pm. Round up a team or grab a seat with strangers. Order a fresh pint and work through the rounds together. TailGate Brewery is a Nashville-based craft brewery with a taproom on Market Street in the Southside neighborhood of Chattanooga. The trivia format covers general knowledge, pop culture, sports, and current events across multiple rounds. No ticket required - walk in and join a table. Check the TailGate social pages for any themed trivia nights or changes to the schedule.
