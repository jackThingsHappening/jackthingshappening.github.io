Jay Buchanan plays Songbirds on Saturday, September 19. Called one of the most commanding voices in modern rock, he pulls deep soul influence into every song. This one starts a little later - doors and set at 8pm. Buchanan fronts Rival Sons and has toured with deep-pocket blues players worldwide. His solo sets strip back the production and focus on voice and guitar. Songbirds is an intimate room that suits that format. Check the venue site for tickets.
