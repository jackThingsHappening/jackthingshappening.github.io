Memphis hip-hop legend Project Pat takes The Signal stage on September 25. He's the older brother of Juicy J and a foundational figure in Three 6 Mafia's Dirty South movement — his 2001 anthem "Chickenhead" still lands. Paranoia-laced street rap with heavy bass production: this is classic Memphis. Show at 8pm.
