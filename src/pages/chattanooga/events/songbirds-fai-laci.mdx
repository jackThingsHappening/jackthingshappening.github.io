Boston rock band Fai Laci play Songbirds on Tuesday, September 22 behind their debut album Elephant in the Room, produced by Dan Auerbach of The Black Keys. The five-piece blend punk urgency and glam stomp with the swagger of classic rock — loud and cathartic live. Music starts at 7pm in the intimate downtown room.
