The zoo marks wildlife conservation milestones with extra programming across the grounds. Conservation Awareness Days celebrate SAFE initiatives. SAFE stands for Saving Animals From Extinction. Each event runs from 11am to 3pm. Zoo keepers add extra talks on featured animals. Find information stations, themed animal enrichment, and arts and crafts at each stop. Everything is included with standard admission. Arrive by 11am to catch the first keeper chat.
