Hundreds of hand-crafted illuminated lanterns line the zoo grounds each night through late January. Displays reach up to 16 feet tall and cover wildlife including tigers, polar bears, red pandas, and elephants. Nearly 50 distinct scenes are spread across the zoo. Some installations include interactive elements like swings and seesaws. Indoor habitats will be closed and most resident animals won't be visible during the festival. Tickets run $23.50 for adults and $17.50 for children ages 3-12. Zoo members pay $19 for adults and $14 for kids. The festival runs Wednesday through Sunday, 5:30 to 9:30pm.
