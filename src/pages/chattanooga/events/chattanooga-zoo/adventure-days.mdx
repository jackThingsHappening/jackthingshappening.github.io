Four times a year, the Chattanooga Zoo opens free to library cardholders age 18 and under. Bring your Chattanooga Public Library card to the ticket booth. Library staff run hands-on activities all day. A librarian leads story time at 11am and again at noon. Adults without a library card pay standard zoo rates. Gates open at 9am. The zoo sits inside Warner Park off North Holtzclaw Avenue.
