A 1924 memorial auditorium on McCallie Avenue seating around 2,000. Named for Hamilton County's World War I veterans. Hosts Broadway tours, classic rock, country, comedy, and other large live events. The building is a registered historic landmark in Chattanooga. The main hall has a traditional horseshoe balcony layout and strong acoustics for large productions. Parking is available in the surrounding McCallie Avenue area. Check the venue website for the current event calendar. The auditorium is managed by the City of Chattanooga. It has hosted major acts and touring productions for 100 years.
