A 1924 memorial auditorium on McCallie Avenue that seats around 2,000. Named for veterans of World War I, it books a wide range of touring acts — Broadway runs, classic rock, country, and comedy. Bigger and more traditional than the Walker Theatre a few blocks away on Broad Street.
