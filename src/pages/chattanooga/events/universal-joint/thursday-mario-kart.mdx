Mario Kart goes up on the big screen at Universal Joint every Thursday. Race night draws a mix of regulars and first timers to the bar on Lookout St. Grab a beer and pick your driver. The screen is big enough to watch from across the room. Races run all evening and anyone at the bar can join in. Entry is free.
