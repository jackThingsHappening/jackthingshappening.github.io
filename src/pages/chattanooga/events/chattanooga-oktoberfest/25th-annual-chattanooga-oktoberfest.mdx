Chattanooga's longest-running Oktoberfest lands at First Horizon Pavilion for two days. Live Oompah music plays under the main tent all weekend. The Biergarten pours from local, regional, and international breweries. Saturday runs 10am to 8pm and Sunday runs 11am to 5pm. Contests include brat toss and stein hoisting. Kids get face painting and balloon animals. Free admission, card-only payments for food and beer.
