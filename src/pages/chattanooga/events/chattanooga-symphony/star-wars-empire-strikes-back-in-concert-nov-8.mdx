The CSO performs John Williams' score live while the film plays on screen. The Empire Strikes Back introduced the Imperial March and the Cloud City theme. This is the second of two performances, following the Friday show. The CSO plays the full orchestral score in sync with the film at Memorial Auditorium. Live-to-picture concerts allow audiences to experience a beloved film with live orchestral accompaniment. Check the CSO website for ticket prices and seating. Memorial Auditorium seats about 2,000 on McCallie Avenue.
