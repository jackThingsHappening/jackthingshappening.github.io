The CSO performs John Williams' score live while the film plays on screen. The Empire Strikes Back introduced the Imperial March and the battle themes that defined the trilogy's sound. The film runs full-length with the orchestra playing every cue. An evening performance also runs November 7 at 7:30 PM.
