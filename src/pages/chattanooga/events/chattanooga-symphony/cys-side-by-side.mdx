Youth Symphony students perform side by side with professional CSO musicians at the Tivoli. Each student shares a music stand and a part with a CSO player. The program is both a performance and a mentorship experience. The CYS Side-by-Side concert is one of the highlights of the youth orchestra season. The program typically features a substantial orchestral work. Admission is free or low cost. Check the CSO and CYS websites for program details and any ticket requirements. The Tivoli Theatre is on Broad Street and seats about 1,700.
