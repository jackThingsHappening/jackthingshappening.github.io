Students from across the region perform full orchestral works at Memorial Auditorium. The Chattanooga Youth Symphony trains young musicians in a professional orchestral setting. The fall concert is the ensemble's first full performance of the season. The program typically includes one large orchestral work and several smaller pieces. The orchestra is conducted by professional musicians. Admission is free or low cost. Check the CYS website for the program and any ticket requirements. Memorial Auditorium is on McCallie Avenue and seats about 2,000. This is a free or low-cost alternative to the professional CSO season.
