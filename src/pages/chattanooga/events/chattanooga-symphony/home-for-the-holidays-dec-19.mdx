The CSO's holiday pops show brings carols, film music, and seasonal orchestral favorites to Memorial Auditorium. The program is the orchestra's end-of-year send-off for the community. A matinee performance follows December 20 at 3:00 PM. This is the evening performance on December 19. The program includes audience sing-alongs, holiday readings, and a full orchestral program of seasonal music. Check the CSO website for ticket prices and seating. Memorial Auditorium on McCallie Avenue seats about 2,000. This is one of the most popular shows in the CSO season.
