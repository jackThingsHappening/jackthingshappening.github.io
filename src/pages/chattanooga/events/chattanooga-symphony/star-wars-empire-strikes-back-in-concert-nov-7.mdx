The CSO performs John Williams' score live while the film plays on screen. The Empire Strikes Back introduced the Imperial March and the Cloud City theme. The CSO plays the full orchestral score in sync with the film at Memorial Auditorium. This format is called a live-to-picture concert. The orchestra plays in the pit while the film runs above. John Williams' score for The Empire Strikes Back is considered one of the greatest in film history. Check the CSO website for ticket prices and seating. Memorial Auditorium seats about 2,000 on McCallie Avenue.
