Big band arrangements fill the Tivoli for Valentine's weekend. The program pulls from the swing era: Duke Ellington, Count Basie, Glenn Miller, and others. The CSO's brass and rhythm sections drive the arrangements in a pops concert format. The Tivoli Theatre is a restored 1921 venue on Broad Street that seats about 1,700. Big Band's Best is an annual Valentine's season tradition. Check the CSO website for ticket prices and seating. Doors open 30 minutes before curtain. The Tivoli's acoustics and intimate scale make it a natural home for this kind of program.
