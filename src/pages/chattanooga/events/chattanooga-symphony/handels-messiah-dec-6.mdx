The CSO and full chorus perform Handel's Messiah, including the Hallelujah chorus. The oratorio runs about two and a half hours with an intermission. This is the second performance, following the December 5 show. The matinee runs at 3:00 PM on Sunday. Check the CSO website for ticket prices and seating. Memorial Auditorium on McCallie Avenue seats about 2,000. The Sunday matinee is a good option for those who prefer afternoon programming. The Hallelujah chorus traditionally prompts the audience to stand.
