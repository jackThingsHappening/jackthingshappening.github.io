The CSO's holiday pops show brings carols, film music, and seasonal orchestral favorites to Memorial Auditorium. The program is the orchestra's end-of-year send-off for the community. This is the Sunday matinee at 3:00 PM, following the Saturday evening performance. The program includes audience sing-alongs, holiday readings, and a full orchestral program of seasonal music. Check the CSO website for ticket prices and seating. Memorial Auditorium on McCallie Avenue seats about 2,000. This matinee is a popular option for families and those who prefer afternoon concerts.
