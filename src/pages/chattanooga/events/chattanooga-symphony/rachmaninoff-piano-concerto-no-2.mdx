Rachmaninoff's Second opens with solo piano chords before the full orchestra enters. The lyrical second movement has appeared in dozens of films and recordings. The concerto is one of the most performed in the standard repertoire. This CSO Classics concert pairs the concerto with another orchestral work. Check the CSO website for the full program, soloist, and ticket details. Concerts take place at the Tivoli Theatre on Broad Street. The Tivoli seats about 1,700 and is one of the best-sounding rooms in the region for large orchestral works.
