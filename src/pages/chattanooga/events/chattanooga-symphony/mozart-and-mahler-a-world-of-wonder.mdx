The season's final Classics concert pairs Mozart and Mahler on the same program. Mozart's tight, clear writing sets up a sharp contrast with Mahler's full orchestra scale. Both composers worked in the German-Austrian tradition a century apart. Tickets at chattanoogasymphony.org.
