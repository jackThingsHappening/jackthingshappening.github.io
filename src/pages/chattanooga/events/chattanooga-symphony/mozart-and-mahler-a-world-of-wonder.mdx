The season's final Classics concert pairs Mozart and Mahler on the same program. Mozart's tight, clear writing sets up a contrast with Mahler's vast, emotional orchestration. Mahler's works typically require large forces and run 60 to 90 minutes. The pairing is musically and intellectually interesting. Check the CSO website for the specific works and any guest soloists on the program. Concerts take place at the Tivoli Theatre on Broad Street. The Tivoli seats about 1,700. Doors open 30 minutes before curtain. This is the closing concert of the CSO Classics season.
