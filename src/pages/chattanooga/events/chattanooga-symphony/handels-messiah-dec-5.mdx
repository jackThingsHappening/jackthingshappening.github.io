The CSO and full chorus perform Handel's Messiah, including the Hallelujah chorus. The oratorio runs about two and a half hours with an intermission. Messiah was first performed in Dublin in 1742. The Hallelujah chorus traditionally prompts the audience to stand. The December 5 performance runs at 7:30 PM at Memorial Auditorium. Check the CSO website for ticket prices and seating. Memorial Auditorium is on McCallie Avenue and seats about 2,000. The CSO performs Messiah annually as part of the holiday season programming.
