The CSO Chorus leads this Front Row program, performing choral works drawn from traditions of struggle and survival. The series format puts the audience a few feet from the singers, with time for talk between pieces. This is the Saturday evening performance. A Sunday matinee follows March 21 at 3:00 PM.
