Students from across the region perform full orchestral works at the Tivoli. The winter program covers pieces the ensemble has prepared through the fall semester. The Chattanooga Youth Symphony trains young musicians from across the region in a professional orchestral setting. The winter concert marks the midpoint of the season. Admission is free or low cost. Check the CYS website for the program and any ticket requirements. The Tivoli Theatre is on Broad Street and seats about 1,700. This is a good opportunity to hear the next generation of Chattanooga musicians.
