The CSO Chorus leads this Front Row program, performing choral works drawn from traditions of struggle and survival. The Front Row series puts the audience close to the performers. The program for Voices Unbroken covers spirituals, protest songs, freedom songs, and other works connected to the history of resilience. This is the Saturday performance, following the Friday show. Check the CSO website for ticket details. Front Row concerts take place at the Tivoli Theatre on Broad Street. The Saturday show is the final performance of this program.
