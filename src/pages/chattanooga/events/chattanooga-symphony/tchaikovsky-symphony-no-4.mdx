The CSO plays its first Tivoli concert this season with Tchaikovsky's Fourth Symphony. The work opens with a fate motif, then moves through three more movements of increasing urgency and power. The finale is one of the loudest endings in the standard repertoire. The CSO Classics series brings professional orchestral programming to the Tivoli Theatre on Broad Street. Check the CSO website for the full program, any guest soloists, and ticket details. The Tivoli seats about 1,700. Doors open 30 minutes before curtain.
