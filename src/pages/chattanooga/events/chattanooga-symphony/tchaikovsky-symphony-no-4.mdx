The CSO plays its first Tivoli concert this season with Tchaikovsky's Fourth Symphony. The work opens with a fate motif, then drives toward a finale built on a Russian folk song. This is the homecoming concert after a half-season run at Memorial Auditorium. Tickets at chattanoogasymphony.org.
