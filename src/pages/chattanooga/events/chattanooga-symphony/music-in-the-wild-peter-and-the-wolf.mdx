The CSO performs Prokofiev's Peter and the Wolf outdoors at Reflection Riding. Before the main concert, musicians pop up along the trail introducing each instrument and character from the story. The piece was composed in 1936 for children and assigns each character a specific instrument - the duck is an oboe, the wolf is horns. The outdoor format puts the audience in the landscape while the music plays. Check the CSO and Reflection Riding websites for ticket details. This event is family-friendly. Bring a blanket or low chair for the main performance.
