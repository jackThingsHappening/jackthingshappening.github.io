The CSO performs Prokofiev's Peter and the Wolf outdoors at Reflection Riding. Before the main concert, musicians pop up around the grounds and the nature center runs animal encounters and activities. In the piece, each instrument voices a different character in the story. Kids can walk away knowing what a bassoon sounds like.
