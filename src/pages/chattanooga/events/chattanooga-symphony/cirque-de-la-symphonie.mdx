Aerialists, acrobats, and contortionists perform live with the orchestra at this Pops show. The Cirque de la Symphonie touring production coordinates circus performance with live orchestral music. The acts perform above and around the stage while the CSO plays. This is not a typical concert format. The combination of live acrobatics and live orchestra is visually and musically impressive. Check the CSO website for ticket prices and seating. The Pops series tends to draw wider audiences than the Classics series. Memorial Auditorium seats about 2,000 on McCallie Avenue.
