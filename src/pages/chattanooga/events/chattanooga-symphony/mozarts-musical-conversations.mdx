The Front Row series puts the audience a few feet from the players. This program covers Mozart's writing, with performers explaining musical decisions and structure between pieces. Front Row concerts combine live performance with informal commentary from the musicians. The audience asks questions and the players respond. This format turns a concert into a conversation about how music works. Check the CSO website for ticket details and the specific program. Front Row concerts take place at the Tivoli Theatre on Broad Street. The series runs several times through the season.
