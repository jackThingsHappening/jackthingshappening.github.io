  <div class="pb-5">Chattanooga's craft brewing scene expanded steadily through the 2010s. The downtown cluster around Market Street and East MLK Boulevard anchors the scene. Southside, St. Elmo, and East Brainerd added taprooms over the years. Wanderlinger, Oddstory, and Hutton &amp; Smith are the names locals mention most. Several Southside taprooms also host live music on weekends. The <a href="/chattanooga/guides/live-music">live music guide</a> covers those venues in detail.</div>
  <div class="pb-5">The North Georgia day trip is a different kind of outing. The Cartecay River valley near Ellijay runs through Georgia wine country. Mercier Orchards covers the cider side of that trip. Cartecay Vineyards and Engelheim are within a few miles of each other. Fall weekends fill up fast in that area.</div>

    <h2 class="text-3xl mb-2" style="font-family: 'Libre Baskerville', serif;">North Shore &amp; Frazier Ave<span class="color-pink ml-1"><b>.</b></span></h2>
    <p class="text-xl">Brewhaus sits on Frazier Ave a short walk from Coolidge Park and the Walnut Street Bridge. It's a bar rather than a brewery, but the tap selection and location make it the obvious North Shore stop for a post-walk pint.</p>
    <div class="flex flex-wrap gap-4 mt-4">
      <a href="/chattanooga/guides/outdoors" class="page-item-link text-logoDarkGreen underline">Outdoor adventures guide</a>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="place-description hidden mb-10 mt-10 p-4 border border-black rounded-lg" data-place="southside">
    <h2 class="text-3xl mb-2" style="font-family: 'Libre Baskerville', serif;">Southside &amp; East Main<span class="color-pink ml-1"><b>.</b></span></h2>
    <p class="text-xl">East Main and the Southside corridor hold some of the city's most active taprooms, with outdoor beer gardens and a live music presence on weekends. Easy to string together with dinner on the same stretch.</p>
    <div class="flex flex-wrap gap-4 mt-4">
      <a href="/chattanooga/guides/live-music" class="page-item-link text-logoDarkGreen underline">Live music guide</a>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="place-description hidden mb-10 mt-10 p-4 border border-black rounded-lg" data-place="blue-ridge-ga">
    <h2 class="text-3xl mb-2" style="font-family: 'Libre Baskerville', serif;">Blue Ridge &amp; Ellijay, GA<span class="color-pink ml-1"><b>.</b></span></h2>
    <p class="text-xl">About 55 minutes south of Chattanooga, the Cartecay River valley runs through some of Georgia's best wine country. Mercier Orchards anchors the cider side of the trip; Cartecay and Engelheim vineyards cover the wine trail. Blue Ridge's downtown strip has its own brewery and enough restaurants to make a full day of it. Go early on weekends. The town fills up fast in fall foliage season.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="place-description hidden mb-10 mt-10 p-4 border border-black rounded-lg" data-place="cleveland-tn">
    <h2 class="text-3xl mb-2" style="font-family: 'Libre Baskerville', serif;">Cleveland &amp; Charleston, TN<span class="color-pink ml-1"><b>.</b></span></h2>
    <p class="text-xl">About 25–30 minutes north of Chattanooga on 75. Morris Vineyard in Charleston is the closest winery to the city in this guide. Mash &amp; Hops in downtown Cleveland covers the craft beer side. Pair the two for a low-key Tennessee day trip that doesn't require crossing into Georgia.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="place-description hidden mb-10 mt-10 p-4 border border-black rounded-lg" data-place="st-elmo">
    <h2 class="text-3xl mb-2" style="font-family: 'Libre Baskerville', serif;">St. Elmo<span class="color-pink ml-1"><b>.</b></span></h2>
    <p class="text-xl">Historic neighborhood at the base of Lookout Mountain. The Tap House fits naturally into a St. Elmo afternoon: Incline Railway, a walk down Tennessee Ave, then a pint before heading back up the ridge.</p>
    <div class="flex flex-wrap gap-4 mt-4">
      <a href="/chattanooga/guides/outdoors?tags=lookout-mtn" class="page-item-link text-logoDarkGreen underline">Lookout Mountain outdoors</a>
      <a href="/chattanooga/guides/coffeeshops?tags=st-elmo" class="page-item-link text-logoDarkGreen underline">St. Elmo coffee</a>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="place-description hidden mb-10 mt-10 p-4 border border-black rounded-lg" data-place="jasper-tn">
    <h2 class="text-3xl mb-2" style="font-family: 'Libre Baskerville', serif;">Jasper Highlands, TN<span class="color-pink ml-1"><b>.</b></span></h2>
    <p class="text-xl">About 35 minutes northwest of Chattanooga on the Cumberland Plateau above the Tennessee River Gorge. Top of the Rock is a brewpub with plateau views that make it a destination in its own right. Plan an afternoon.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="place-description hidden mb-10 mt-10 p-4 border border-black rounded-lg" data-place="guild-tn">
    <h2 class="text-3xl mb-2" style="font-family: 'Libre Baskerville', serif;">Guild, TN<span class="color-pink ml-1"><b>.</b></span></h2>
    <p class="text-xl">A short drive west of downtown on US-41, just past Raccoon Mountain. Lookout Winery sits on a bluff with Tennessee River views. It earns its own afternoon.</p>
    <div class="flex flex-wrap gap-4 mt-4">
      <a href="/chattanooga/guides/outdoors?tags=raccoon-mtn" class="page-item-link text-logoDarkGreen underline">Raccoon Mountain outdoors</a>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="place-description hidden mb-10 mt-10 p-4 border border-black rounded-lg" data-place="ringgold-ga">
    <h2 class="text-3xl mb-2" style="font-family: 'Libre Baskerville', serif;">Ringgold, GA<span class="color-pink ml-1"><b>.</b></span></h2>
    <p class="text-xl">Six miles south of Chattanooga at I-75 Exit 350. The Georgia Winery is the closest winery to the city in this guide. It is barely a day trip.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="place-description hidden mb-10 mt-10 p-4 border border-black rounded-lg" data-place="dalton-ga">
    <h2 class="text-3xl mb-2" style="font-family: 'Libre Baskerville', serif;">Dalton, GA<span class="color-pink ml-1"><b>.</b></span></h2>
    <p class="text-xl">About 30 minutes south on I-75. Not a craft beer destination yet, but Dalton Brewing holds its own for a city that size. Easy add-on if you're already heading south.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="place-description hidden mb-10 mt-10 p-4 border border-black rounded-lg" data-place="east-brainerd">
    <h2 class="text-3xl mb-2" style="font-family: 'Libre Baskerville', serif;">East Brainerd<span class="color-pink ml-1"><b>.</b></span></h2>
    <p class="text-xl">Chattanooga's fast-growing east side, about 20 minutes from downtown. Mean King Brewing on Jenkins Road is the first craft brewery to plant a flag out here, so it stands on its own for now. Worth a stop if you live nearby or are heading toward Ooltewah.</p>
  </div>
</div>
