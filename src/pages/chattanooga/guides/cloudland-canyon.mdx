    <p class="text-xl">Yes. The Visitor Center rents mountain bikes for $35 half day or $55 full day. Call ahead to confirm availability before making the drive.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="mb-6">
    <h3 class="text-2xl mb-2">Can you bring dogs to Cloudland Canyon?</h3>
    <p class="text-xl">Dogs are welcome on most trails on a leash no longer than 6 feet. The Waterfalls Trail is not recommended for dogs. The 600-plus metal grate stairs can be painful and difficult for them to navigate.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="mb-6">
    <h3 class="text-2xl mb-2">When do the cave tours run?</h3>
    <p class="text-xl">Guided wild cave tours of Sitton's Cave run in April, June, July, September, and October. Each tour lasts about 2 to 2.5 hours and costs $45 per person, ages 10 and up. Call 706-657-4050 to book. Experienced cavers can get a permit for Case Cave, which requires ropes and harnesses.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="mb-6">
    <h3 class="text-2xl mb-2">What waterfalls are in Cloudland Canyon?</h3>
    <p class="text-xl">The park has two main waterfalls: Hemlock Falls and Cherokee Falls. Both are reached via the Waterfalls Trail, which branches off the West Rim Loop. Flow varies with rainfall and is strongest after a heavy storm.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="mb-6">
    <h3 class="text-2xl mb-2">Can you swim at Cloudland Canyon?</h3>
    <p class="text-xl">There is no designated swimming area in the park. The waterfalls and creek pools are not set up for swimming. The fishing pond is stocked for fishing only.</p>
  </div>
