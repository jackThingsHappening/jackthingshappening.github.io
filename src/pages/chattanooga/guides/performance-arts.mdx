    <h3 class="text-2xl mb-2">What Broadway shows come to Chattanooga?</h3>
    <p class="text-xl">Broadway touring productions typically come to the Tivoli Theatre on Broad Street, operated by the Tivoli Foundation. The Tivoli reopened October 2026 after a four-year, $75 million restoration. It now includes an expanded performance center in the adjacent Fowler Brothers building. Check tivolichattanooga.com for the current season lineup.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="mb-6">
    <h3 class="text-2xl mb-2">Where can I see free live theatre in Chattanooga?</h3>
    <p class="text-xl">Scenic City Shakespeare performs free outdoor productions at Greenway Farms in Hixson, typically in spring and fall on Friday and Saturday evenings. No tickets or reservations are required. Bring a lawn chair and snacks. Check sceniccityshakespeare.org for the next production date.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="mb-6">
    <h3 class="text-2xl mb-2">What are the best family-friendly performances in Chattanooga?</h3>
    <p class="text-xl">Seven venues in this guide offer family-friendly programming. In December, both Chattanooga Ballet and Chattanooga Dance Theatre stage Nutcracker productions. Chattanooga Theatre Centre runs family musicals and youth shows year-round on the North Shore. Scenic City Shakespeare is free and outdoor. UTC Theatre Co. stages affordable public shows four times a year.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="mb-6">
    <h3 class="text-2xl mb-2">Does the Chattanooga Symphony play anything besides classical music?</h3>
    <p class="text-xl">Yes. The Chattanooga Symphony and Opera runs pops programming alongside the classical season. They have played live film scores in past seasons, including the Star Wars scores performed live to picture. Check chattanoogasymphony.org for the current season.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="mb-6">
    <h3 class="text-2xl mb-2">What is the Nooga Nutcracker?</h3>
    <p class="text-xl">The Nooga Nutcracker is a Chattanooga-specific spin on the classic ballet, staged by Chattanooga Dance Theatre each December at UTC's Roland Hayes Concert Hall. It replaces the standard characters with local stand-ins: the Tennessee River, aquarium fish, and the Chattanooga Lookouts. It features 60+ performers. Tickets go on sale in October through the UTC box office.</p>
  </div>
</section>
