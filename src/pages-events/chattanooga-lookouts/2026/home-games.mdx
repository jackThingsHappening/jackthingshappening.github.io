The Chattanooga Lookouts play Double-A ball at AT&T Field on the riverfront, one of the better minor league setups in the South. The stadium sits right along the Tennessee River with Lookout Mountain in the backdrop — worth showing up early just for that.

Recurring promos to know: **Thirsty Thursday** runs drink specials at the Right Field Bar every Thursday home game. **Fireworks Friday** follows every Friday night game. **Saturday Live Music** means pre-game concerts near the stadium bar. **Family Sunday Funday** ends with kids on the field running bases and a post-game catch open to everyone.
