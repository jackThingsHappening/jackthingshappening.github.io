The Chattanooga Market sets up at the Tennessee Aquarium Plaza every Saturday along the riverfront. Browse from 10:00 am to 5:00 pm. The outdoor plaza sits right on the water — one of the more scenic spots to shop in the city.

<br />

Find fresh produce, local art, food trucks, and handmade goods. Live music plays throughout the day. Pets are welcome.

<br />

Plan your meals before you go. Knowing what you need makes the most of the vendor selection.
