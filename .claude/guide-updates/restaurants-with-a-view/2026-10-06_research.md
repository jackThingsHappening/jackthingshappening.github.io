# Research: restaurants with scenic views, rooftop dining, waterfront restaurants, and scenic eating spots in Chattanooga, TN

## 2026-10-06

1. **Iris Rooftop Bar (Waymark Hotel)** — NEW for 2026. Billed as Chattanooga's tallest/highest rooftop bar. Set in a restored 1927 bank building downtown, with lush tropical greenery and a wrap-around terrace. Panoramic views of the downtown skyline and Lookout Mountain. Chef Wilbur Cox. Likely MISSING. (timesfreepress.com, therooftopguide.com)

2. **Stratus Rooftop Lounge (Caption by Hyatt Chattanooga Downtown)** — NEW for 2026, the city's first Hyatt. 360° rooftop views over the skyline, Tennessee River valley, and Lookout Mountain. Likely MISSING. (parade.com, therooftopguide.com)

3. **Chattanooga FC Rooftop Bar (Project Starball)** — NEW, launched Jan 2026 atop CFC's six-story headquarters. Commanding views of Finley Stadium and downtown. Unusual sports-view angle. Likely MISSING. (therooftopguide.com)

4. **Whisky Thief (The Edwin Hotel)** — Rooftop bar atop the Edwin Hotel overlooking the Bluff View Art District, Walnut Street Bridge, and the Tennessee River. Well-established, premium. Probably already covered.

5. **High Rail Chattanooga (The Chattanoogan Hotel, Curio by Hilton)** — Rooftop restaurant/bar atop the Chattanoogan with wide city views. Full food and drink menu. On OpenTable. Probably covered. (highrailchattanooga.com)

6. **Paloma Bar de Tapas (The Westin, West Village)** — Rooftop tapas bar atop the Westin. Views of west Chattanooga and Lookout Mountain. Possibly covered.

7. **WXYZ Bar (Aloft Hotel, Hamilton Place)** — Rooftop bar near Hamilton Place with views of surrounding mountains and ridges. East-side option away from downtown. Possibly MISSING.

8. **Pickle Barrel** — Historic flatiron building on Market Street with a roof deck; "best-kept secret" views of downtown skyline, ridgelines, and Lookout Mountain on clear days. Casual/affordable. Possibly MISSING.

9. **Boathouse Rotisserie & Raw Bar** — On Riverside Drive near the Choo Choo complex. One of the most expansive outdoor decks in the city, at near water level on the Tennessee River. Seafood. Probably covered.

10. **Scotties on the River** — On Riverfront Parkway near the Tennessee Aquarium area; patio with unobstructed river view and a fire pit for cooler evenings. Probably covered.

11. **Overboard Bar & Grille (Pier 2)** — On the second floor of Pier 2, sits ~40 feet above the Tennessee River with indoor and outdoor seating right on the water. Tied to Chattanooga Riverboat. Possibly MISSING. (chattanoogariverboat.com)

12. **Rembrandt's Coffee House (Bluff View Art District)** — Perched on a limestone bluff high above the river; outdoor terrace looks over the river, Walnut Street Bridge, and North Shore. Casual coffee/pastry, approachable price. Possibly MISSING.

13. **Tony's Pasta Shop & Trattoria (Bluff View Art District)** — Italian restaurant with courtyard garden seating overlooking the bluff toward the river, European-village feel. Possibly MISSING.

14. **STIR (Chattanooga Choo Choo complex)** — Open-air concept with a large heated outdoor patio; oysters, scratch cooking, lunch/dinner/weekend brunch. Patio dining in the historic complex. Possibly MISSING. (choosechatt.com)

15. **1885 Grill (St. Elmo)** — Southern coastal seafood and steaks at the base of the Incline Railway; patio with Lookout Mountain views. St. Elmo neighborhood angle. Possibly MISSING. (tripadvisor.com)

16. **Terminal Brew House (Southside)** — Brewpub with a rooftop patio/beer garden near the Choo Choo; good food and house beer with elevated views. Possibly MISSING.

17. **Little Coyote (Lookout Mountain)** — Sits atop a hill overlooking Lookout Mountain; wood-fired meats, house-made corn tortillas, 100+ agave spirits. Scenic mountain setting. Possibly MISSING. (seerockcity.com)

18. **The Tap House & Empyreal Brewing Co. (Lookout Mountain)** — Pub food and rotating local/house beer with a porch for taking in mountain views. Lookout Mountain option. Possibly MISSING.

19. **Massey's Kitchen (Lookout Mountain)** — Full-service lunch/dinner/weekend brunch in the historic Massey's general store spot; open-air spaces with mountain atmosphere. Possibly MISSING.

20. **Ruby Falls Cafe (Lookout Mountain)** — Casual cafe inside the 1929 limestone castle at Ruby Falls; dining surrounded by history and nature, pairs with Point Park / Ochs Observatory views nearby. Attraction-adjacent scenic eating. Possibly MISSING. (rubyfalls.com)
