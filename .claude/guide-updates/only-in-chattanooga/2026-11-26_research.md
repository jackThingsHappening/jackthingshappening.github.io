# Research: unique, one-of-a-kind shops, bars, and experiences found only in Chattanooga, TN

## 2026-11-26

1. **Reading Room Bar** — Bar + used bookstore hybrid at 3210B Brainerd Rd. Wed-Sun 12pm-12am. All books $8 or less. Hosts craft nights, writer's groups, trivia, bingo, author readings. Food service ends at 10pm. Subscribe to "The Reading Room Rag" newsletter for event updates. Website: readingroombar.com

2. **Hart & Hive Provisions** — Gourmet grab-and-go market at 1301 Dorchester Rd #113 (Riverview). Chef-made meals, rotating soups, pimento cheese, artisan candles, jewelry. Mon-Thu 10am-6pm, Fri 10am-5pm, Sat 10am-4pm, Sun closed. Also has Queen's Kitchen location at 1308 Hanover St (Tue-Sat 11am-4pm). Website: hartandhiveprovisions.com

3. **Rosie Mae's Alpaca Farm** — [PASS B] Coffee shop + alpaca farm in Wildwood, GA, 15 minutes from downtown Chattanooga. Free to visit; $2 for a bag of alpaca feed. Coffee from Mean Mug, drinks named after the 9 alpacas. Kids drinks, pastries, boutique with alpaca products. Insider: the basic visit is free — the $130 Experience Chattanooga photography tour is optional. Website: rosiemaesalpacafarm.com

4. **Understory Tea House** — Tea house + herbal apothecary at 400 E Main St Suite 170. Tue-Sun 10am-7pm (Sun closes at 5pm). Matcha, traditional teas, herbal preparations, small bites. Regular workshops: herbal salve-making, sound baths, ornament crafting. Many workshops use sliding-scale pricing. Cauldron Club membership available. Website: understorytea.com

5. **Naughty Cat Cafe** — Chattanooga's first cat cafe at 3742 Tennessee Ave Suite 100. Wed-Sat 11am-5pm, Sun 10am-4pm. 30+ adoptable cats in 2,000 sq ft of custom cat lounges. Gourmet coffee + local craft beer. Advance tickets required — book online before you go. Website: naughtycatcafe.com

6. **Infinity Flux** — Chattanooga's largest independent comic and game shop at 3643 Hixson Pike. Mon-Thu 12-7pm, Fri 12-8pm, Sat-Sun 11am-7/8pm. Two distinct spaces: one for comics, one for RPG/fantasy. 2026 Will Eisner Spirit of Comics Retailer Award nominee. Newsletter signup gets 10% off first online order. Website: infinityflux.net

7. **Game On Chatt** — Tabletop and trading card game store at 7510 Lee Hwy. Mon-Thu 2-8pm, Fri 2pm-midnight, Sat 12-8pm, Sun closed. Magic: The Gathering, Yu-Gi-Oh, Pokemon, D&D, Warhammer, board games, miniature paints. Regular tournaments. Hosts game nights at local breweries. Phone: (423) 602-5800

8. **4th Floor Makerspace (Chattanooga Public Library)** — [PASS B] 12,000 sq ft free public makerspace at 1001 Broad St. No library card required for most equipment. 3D printers, laser cutters, vinyl cutters, iMacs with design software. Tool lending program in partnership with Elder's Ace Hardware. Some equipment requires a free one-hour training session. Website: chattlibrary.org/4th-floor

9. **Scenic City Hot Sauce** — Hot sauce specialty shop at 133 Frazier Ave, North Shore, across from the Walnut Street Bridge. Phone: (423) 654-9624. Website: sceniccityhotsauces.com

10. **Bleu Fox Cheese Shop** — Artisan cheese and charcuterie shop at 324 E Main St. Mon-Sat 11am-7pm, Sun 12-6pm. Cheese club subscription available for regular pickups. Phone: (423) 498-1977. Website: bleufoxcheeseshop.com

11. **Yellow Racket Records** — Independent vinyl shop at 2311 E Main St. Mon/Tue/Thu/Sat 11am-6pm, Wed/Fri 11am-8pm, Sun 12-4pm. New releases, reissues, pre-loved vinyl, limited RSD titles. Comfy chairs, knowledgeable staff. Free shipping on orders over $75. Participates in RSD Black Friday (600+ limited editions). Website: yellowracketrecords.com

12. **Good Kinsmen** — Premier kitchen and knife store at 521 Cherokee Blvd (North Shore). Mon-Sat 10am-6pm, Sun closed. Kitchen knives, Mac knives, custom blades, knife sharpening service. Good stop if you are already on the North Shore strip. Phone: (423) 309-4170. Website: goodkinsmen.com

13. **Tokushu Knife** — [PASS B] Japanese chef knife and custom handle store at 3974 Norcross Rd. Very limited hours: Tue 10am-5pm, Sat 12-4pm only. Insider: confirm hours before making a special trip — this is not a walk-in-anytime shop. Website: tokushuknife.com

14. **Frazier Avenue Vintage District** — North Shore walkable stretch with 4+ dedicated vintage shops. Ugly Duck Vintage (30 Frazier Ave, buy/sell/trade, inventory turns fast), Electric Crocodile (20+ vendors, neon yellow exterior, clothes + vinyl + decor), The Local Dive (Cherokee Blvd, 30+ vendors, clothing + housewares), Collective Clothing (vintage + Inherent Records inside). Best visited as a half-day crawl.

15. **Winder Binder Mercantile & Bookstore** — [PASS B] Frazier Ave hybrid shop mixing new and used books with folk art, pottery, photography, vinyl from Chad's Records inside, and a deep Chattanooga local history section. The vinyl angle is not obvious from the name — worth knowing before you go.

16. **District Leaf Supply** — Premium cigar lounge on the North Shore with boutique feel and comfortable seating. One of several cigar lounges in Chattanooga; this one stands out for its North Shore location near the Frazier Ave retail strip.

17. **Improv Chattanooga** — Live improv comedy troupe performing at Plant Bar (202 Manufacturers Rd Suite B, North Shore). Also runs shows at The Chattery (1800 Rossville Ave Suite 108). Check Facebook (facebook.com/improvchattanooga) for current show schedule — performances are not on a fixed weekly calendar.

18. **Electric Crocodile** — [PASS B] Frazier Ave vintage shop with over 20 different vendors. The neon yellow exterior makes it unmissable. Mix of vintage clothes, vinyl, and decor. Worth a separate mention from the broader antique district because of its size and vendor variety.

19. **Poor Taste** — Vintage shop on East Main Street with one-of-a-kind goods. After hours, the backyard hosts live music events. Dual use as a shop by day and venue by night makes it one of the more unusual spots in the Southside.

20. **Blue Skies** — Independent gift and lifestyle shop on the North Shore with thousands of items: artisanal jewelry, cards, home goods, whimsical finds. Positioned as a destination for locals and visitors who want something that is not chain-made. Good adjacent stop to the Frazier Ave vintage crawl.
