# Research: breweries, craft beer taprooms, wineries, and cideries in and around Chattanooga, TN

## 2026-09-15

1. **Hutton & Smith Brewing Co.** — Widely called the gold standard of Chattanooga craft beer. On MLK Boulevard. Balanced IPAs, clean stouts. Taproom open 7 days a week, 21+, bar seating across from the brewhouse plus a room with shuffleboard. Likely already covered.
2. **OddStory Brewing Co.** — Known for funky sours and pastry stouts. Plant-filled taproom, family-friendly outdoor patio. Downtown. Likely already covered.
3. **Five Wits Brewing Co.** — Southside district downtown. Broad craft beer selection, comfort food, regular live music. Potentially covered.
4. **TailGate Brewery** — Historic Southside building near the Chattanooga Choo Choo (former Terminal Brewhouse). 7-barrel Belgian/farmhouse brewhouse, 30 drafts, scratch pizza kitchen, double-decker patio. Opened June 2022. Potentially missing.
5. **Chattanooga Brewing Co.** — One of the region's oldest, brewing since 1890. Downtown. Likely already covered.
6. **Wanderlinger Brewing Company** — Excels at traditional European styles plus sours made with locally-grown fruit. Downtown. Potentially covered.
7. **The Tap House (St. Elmo)** — Veteran-owned neighborhood spot. 30+ rotating taps: local beer, cider, kombucha, gluten-free and non-alcoholic options. Good for the St. Elmo neighborhood filter. Potentially missing.
8. **Naked River Brewing Company** — In one of Chattanooga's oldest buildings, natural light and vintage details. Texas-style BBQ, live music Thu-Sat, Sunday brunch. Kid- and pet-friendly. Potentially missing.
9. **Monkey Town Brewing Company** — Opened 2014 in Dayton, TN (~45 min north). Recently opened a second location in Chattanooga. Good regional + city coverage. Likely missing.
10. **Mean King Brewing** — One of Chattanooga's newest breweries, East Brainerd area off Jenkins Road. Expands neighborhood coverage beyond downtown. Likely missing.
11. **Heaven & Ale Brewing Co.** — CLOSED / no longer in business (was on E. Main St., Southside). If currently listed in the guide, it should be REMOVED. Verify.
12. **Lookout Winery** — Uses ancient gravity/siphon techniques to move wine, per owner Chuck Bordogna. Distinctive story worth a description line. Potentially missing.
13. **Lady Naps Winery (Paris Winery of Chattanooga)** — Playful blends, warm atmosphere, founded by cousins Pamela McKinley and Nikki Nicholson. Listed on Tennessee Wines. Likely missing.
14. **Ocoee Winery** — ~40 miles east of downtown. Sweet wines are visitor favorites; white muscadine from Native American muscadine grapes, Bronze at the South Regional Wine Competition. Regional winery. Likely missing.
15. **Morris Vineyard (Charleston, TN)** — Local winery known for blueberry wines and exclusive flavors, green panorama. ~30 min north. Regional coverage. Likely missing.
16. **Savannah Oaks Winery (Delano, TN)** — Family-owned, blackberry wine, view over the Hiwassee River. Day-trip winery. Likely missing.
17. **Apple Barn Winery / Cider House** — Partners on regional beverage trails alongside Mountain Winery and Mill Bridge Winery. Adds cider coverage to a beer/wine guide. Verify distance and hours.
18. **Chattanooga Beer Fest 2026** — Annual craft beer festival (chattanoogabeerfest.com). Worth an events/festival mention for beer lovers planning a visit. Likely missing.
19. **The walkable brewery circuit** — Southside, downtown, and the MLK corridor put Hutton & Smith, Chattanooga Brewing Co., OddStory, TailGate, Five Wits, and Wanderlinger within walking distance. Useful intro/context framing for the guide. Content angle, not a new item.
20. **Cider / non-alcoholic options callout** — Several taprooms (The Tap House especially) carry cider, kombucha, gluten-free, and NA options. A guide covering "cideries" should flag where cider drinkers and non-drinkers are well served. Content angle.
