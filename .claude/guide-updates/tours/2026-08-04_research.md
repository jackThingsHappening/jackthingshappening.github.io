# Research: guided tours, walking tours, boat tours, adventure tours, and sightseeing in Chattanooga, TN

## 2026-08-04

1. **Southern Belle Riverboat** — Chattanooga's signature riverboat; daily sightseeing, lunch, and dinner cruises on the Tennessee River with live narration and an open-air deck. Departs from the downtown riverfront. Website: chattanoogariverboat.com. Likely already covered but a must-have anchor for a tours guide.
2. **Chattanooga Ducks** — Narrated tour aboard a renovated WWII amphibious Duck Boat that starts on downtown streets then splashes into the Tennessee River. Family-favorite, fun angle on the city. Website: chattanoogaducks.com. Potentially missing.
3. **Blue Heaven River Tours** — Smaller-boat river tours in downtown Chattanooga and the Tennessee River Gorge. Website: blueheavenrivertourschattanooga.com. Likely missing; good complement to Southern Belle.
4. **Chattanooga Ghost Tours** — Nightly walking ghost tours (weekends in winter) from a haunted steampunk shop at 57 E 5th St. Also offers ghost hunts with paranormal equipment. Classic downtown evening tour. Potentially missing.
5. **Ghost & Booze Tour (Haunted Pub Crawl)** — 21+ small-group tour through the Historic District pairing haunted history with the city's best bars. Bookable via Viator. Distinct from the family ghost tour. Likely missing.
6. **Chatt Taste Food Tours** — Downtown Chattanooga food tour showcasing local restaurants, murals, and history; offers the city's only Brunch Food Tour. Progressive-meal format. Potentially missing.
7. **ChattaBrew Tour** — Behind-the-scenes brewery experience visiting four Chattanooga craft breweries over ~4 hours, tasting signature and seasonal brews with brewmasters. Likely missing.
8. **Chattanooga Brew Tours** — Guided craft-beer tours of local breweries/taprooms; runs twice daily Fri/Sat/Sun (1 & 5 p.m.). Pairs with ChattaBrew as brewery-tour options. Potentially missing.
9. **Chattanooga Rydables Tours (formerly Segway Bike Tours)** — Guided ~1-hour tours on whimsical motorized stuffed-animal scooters (unicorns, dragons) crossing Walnut Street Bridge through downtown, Bluff View Art District, and riverfront. Also offers e-bike and classic Segway tours. Website: chattanoogasegwaytours.com. Potentially missing; a notable evolution of the old Segway tour brand.
10. **Guided Electric Bike City Tour / All Wheels Electric** — Fully guided e-bike tours on Specialized Como cruisers along the riverfront trails, covering city history and Tennessee River ecology. Website: allwheelselectric.com. Likely missing.
11. **Discover Chattanooga Walking Tour** — 90-minute narrated walking tour of downtown City Center and Riverfront along Broad and Market Streets with professional guides. Good entry-level orientation tour. Bookable via Viator. Potentially missing.
12. **Chattanooga Guided Adventures** — Beginner-friendly guided outdoor tours and classes: outdoor rock climbing, kayaking, mountain biking, backpacking, hiking, and navigation. Also runs the Nickajack Bat Cave Kayak Tour and Fall Colors Kayak Tour in the Tennessee River Gorge. Website: chattanoogaguidedadventures.com. Strong adventure-tour anchor; likely missing.
13. **Nickajack Bat Cave Kayak Tour** — Eco kayak tour to watch endangered gray bats emerge from a cave over Nickajack Lake at dusk (summer); winter version features migratory sandhill cranes. Run by Chattanooga Guided Adventures. Unique bucket-list experience; likely missing.
14. **Ruby Falls** — Guided cavern walk descending 260 ft via glass-front elevator to the tallest underground waterfall open to the public in the U.S.; ~2–2.5 hr visit. Also home to High Point ZIP Adventure and a 40-ft climbing tower. Lookout Mountain. Website: rubyfalls.com. Iconic; likely already covered.
15. **Rock City Gardens** — Self-guided walking trail atop Lookout Mountain (1,700 ft) through ancient rock formations and gardens with 400+ native plants; see seven states from Lover's Leap. Website: seerockcity.com. Iconic; likely already covered.
16. **Lookout Mountain Incline Railway** — One of the world's steepest passenger funiculars ("America's Most Amazing Mile"), operating since 1895; rides up Lookout Mountain to an observation deck. ~1 hr visit. Website: ridetheincline.com. Iconic; likely already covered.
17. **High Point ZIP Adventure (at Ruby Falls)** — Zipline course through the treetops of Lookout Mountain plus a 40-ft climbing tower with varied routes. Adventure-tour option distinct from the cave. Likely missing.
18. **Raccoon Mountain Caverns — Crystal Palace Tour** — 45–55 min guided walking tour through the first quarter-mile of the developed cave (lights, pathways, handrails); wild/extended caving tours also available with helmets and gear. Guided-tour depth beyond Ruby Falls. Potentially missing.
19. **Battles for Chattanooga Museum** — Civil War museum at 1110 East Brow Road, Lookout Mountain, using projection-mapping, 3D animation, and a digital panoramic display to bring the November 1863 battles to life. Pairs with Point Park / Cravens House. Potentially missing.
20. **Chickamauga & Chattanooga National Military Park (Point Park, Cravens House)** — America's oldest and largest national military park; Point Park atop Lookout Mountain offers a visitor center, walking trails, and city overlooks, with the 1854 Cravens House (only surviving Civil War structure on the mountain). Ranger-led and self-guided history tours. Website: nps.gov/chch. Potentially missing.
