# Research: guided tours, walking tours, boat tours, adventure tours, and sightseeing in Chattanooga, TN

## 2026-11-17

1. **Southern Belle Riverboat** — Chattanooga's signature riverboat since 1985. Daily sightseeing (1.5-hr cruise with 45 min live narration + Moon Pie), dinner cruises (dinner, cheese/charcuterie, 4 wines), seasonal Fall Foliage cruises to the Tennessee River Gorge, and New Year's Eve cruises. 151 Riverfront Parkway, Chattanooga, TN 37402. (423) 266-4488. chattanoogariverboat.com. Anchor entry — likely already covered.
2. **Chattanooga Guided Adventures** — Locally owned outdoor recreation company. Beginner-friendly kayak, SUP, rock climbing, mountain biking, backpacking, and hiking tours. Flat-water kayak tour to Nickajack Bat Cave, Burgess Falls waterfall kayak tour, self-guided SUP (driven 5 miles upstream, paddle back through downtown). Launch at Coolidge Park near Walnut Street Bridge. 3929 Forest Highland Dr, Chattanooga, TN 37415. chattanoogaguidedadventures.com. Likely missing — strong adventure-tour addition.
3. **ChattaBrew Tour** — Ranked #1 Food & Drink tour in Chattanooga. Visits four craft breweries over four hours on a climate-controlled beer bus; tastings at each stop, bottled water, and a Brewing 101 lesson. Great brewery-tour entry. Likely missing.
4. **Chatt Taste Food Tours** — Named a Top 10 Food Tour in the country (USA Today poll). Three options: West Village, Taco Tuesday, and Art & Southern Cuisine. Walking food tours of downtown Chattanooga. Likely missing — strong culinary-tour addition.
5. **Ruby Falls** — Guided tours descend 260 ft via glass-front elevator into Lookout Mountain to the tallest public-access underground waterfall in the U.S. Tour options: Classic Waterfall Tour, Lantern Tour, Extended Cavern Experience. rubyfalls.com. Likely already covered but essential.
6. **Lookout Mountain Incline Railway** — "America's Most Amazing Mile." Steepest passenger railways in the world (72.7% grade); carrying riders up Lookout Mountain since 1895. Scenic ride with mountaintop views. Likely already covered.
7. **Murder & Mayhem Haunted History Walking Tour** — Ghost/history walking tour of Chattanooga's spookiest and most storied spots with a local professional guide. 4.6 avg rating, 92% recommend (335 reviews). Likely missing — popular walking tour.
8. **Chattanooga Guided Walking Tour (J.D. Harper)** — Led by historian/author J.D. Harper. Covers the Tennessee Riverwalk, Tivoli Theatre, and Historic Read House Hotel. Great historic-walking-tour entry. Likely missing.
9. **Raccoon Mountain Caverns — Crystal Palace Walking Tour** — 45–55 min guided walk through the first quarter-mile of cave; guide explains cave formation and points out stalactites, stalagmites, soda straws, and rare shield/helictite formations. raccoonmountain.com. Likely missing — good caving tour.
10. **Lookout Mountain Flight Park (Hang Gliding)** — Certified flight school offering tandem hang gliding, paragliding, and ridge soaring on Lookout Mountain. Premier aerial adventure near Chattanooga. flylookout.com. Likely missing — unique adventure tour.
11. **Nickajack Bat Cave Kayak Tour** — Guided 3-mile paddle around Nickajack Lake to the bat-cave entrance; summer roost for up to ~100,000 endangered gray bats that emerge at dusk; chance of osprey and bald eagle sightings. Seasonal (summer). Offered by Chattanooga Guided Adventures. Likely missing — signature seasonal tour.
12. **Chickamauga & Chattanooga National Military Park — Ranger-Guided Tours** — Free 90-min ranger-led overview tours of the Battle of Chickamauga, Sat & Sun at 10am and 2pm; car-caravan format following a ranger. nps.gov/chch. Likely missing — major Civil War history tour, and free.
13. **Chickamauga Battlefield Bicycle Tour** — Free ranger-led historical bike tours of the battlefield, roughly 3–4 miles on flat to moderately hilly terrain. Outdoor Chattanooga's Mobile Bicycle Fleet provides free loaner bikes/helmets. nps.gov/chch. Likely missing — free active history tour.
14. **Chattanooga Segway Tours** — Guided Segway tours of the North Shore and downtown with historical commentary; also runs the "Boo on Wheels" ghost tour (1.5-hr rydable route through art alleys, past Civil War-era buildings, to the Aquarium, across Walnut Street Bridge). chattanoogasegwaytours.com. Likely missing.
15. **Historic Southside Food Tour** — Chef-crafted tastings from celebrated restaurants paired with stories of Chattanooga's shift from industrial railroad town to Southern culinary destination. Good neighborhood food-tour entry. Likely missing.
16. **ChattaBrunch Food Tour** — Chattanooga's only brunch-themed food tour. Niche but distinctive culinary-tour addition. Likely missing.
17. **Downtown Chattanooga Food Tour** — Guided walking food tour connecting visitors with authentic Southern cuisine across downtown stops. Widely booked on Viator/Tripadvisor. Likely missing.
18. **GoBoat Rentals / Electric Boat Rental** — Self-captained electric boat rentals to explore the Tennessee River and Chickamauga Lake at your own pace; quiet, eco-friendly, no license required. Self-guided boat-tour alternative to the Southern Belle. Likely missing.
19. **North Chickamauga / Ocoee / Nantahala Whitewater** — Guided whitewater rafting and kayaking on the Ocoee (1996 Olympic course), Nantahala, Suck Creek, and North Chickamauga rivers. Classic regional adventure-tour day trip from Chattanooga. Likely missing — rounds out water-adventure options.
20. **Chattanooga Crypts and Chills Ghost Tour** — 1-hr walking ghost tour along the Tennessee Riverwalk uncovering forgotten local stories, including the lore of "Room 311." Alternative to the Segway ghost tour for walkers. Likely missing.
