# Research: dessert shops, bakeries, ice cream, candy, and sweet treats in Chattanooga, TN

## 2026-07-27

1. **Clumpies Ice Cream Co.** — Handcrafting ice cream in Chattanooga since 1999; classic and seasonal small-batch flavors plus coffee/pastries. Multiple locations: Northshore by Coolidge Park, Southside across from the Choo Choo, and St. Elmo at the bottom of the Incline. Website: clumpies.com. Likely already covered — a Chattanooga cornerstone.

2. **The Hot Chocolatier** — Handmade artisan chocolates, pastries, truffles, macarons, opera cake and famed hot chocolate, small batches since 2008. Opened a second St. Elmo location in early 2023. Website: thehotchocolatier.com. Likely already covered.

3. **The Ice Cream Show** — At the south end of the Walnut Street Walking Bridge, next to the Hunter Museum and across from the Edwin Hotel; markets 17,000+ flavor combinations. Website: theicecreamshow.com. Possibly missing — strong tourist-district pick.
