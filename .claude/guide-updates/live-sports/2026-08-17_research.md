3. **Chattanooga Red Wolves SC** — Professional soccer, founding member of USL League One (Division 3). Play at CHI Memorial Stadium. As of late July 2026, regular-season record 9-7-1. Website: chattanoogaredwolves-sc.com. Likely covered.

4. **CHI Memorial Stadium** — Tennessee's first professional soccer-specific stadium; 5,500 seats. Home of the Red Wolves. 5. **Chattanooga FC** — Independent professional soccer club, joined MLS NEXT Pro in 2024. Plays at Finley Stadium (since 2009). In 2025 became the first independent club in league history to host a playoff match. Home opener typically early March. Website: chattanoogafc.com. Likely covered.
