# Research: weekend itineraries, hotels, things to do, and hosting visitors in Chattanooga, TN

## 2026-07-28

1. **Erlanger Park (new Chattanooga Lookouts stadium)** — Brand-new 8,032-seat multi-use ballpark in the South Broad District, built on the old US Pipe & Foundry site with the former Power House as the entrance. Home opener was April 14, 2026 vs. the Montgomery Biscuits; the Tennessee Riverwalk greenway runs through the venue. Replaces AT&T Field (2000–2025). A marquee weekend addition — likely MISSING from the guide. (erlangerpark.com; milb.com)

2. **The Waymark Hotel, Tapestry Collection by Hilton** — Newly opened in the historic 1927 Chattanooga Savings Bank & Trust building at 736 Broad St, vacant for nearly two decades. Features the Iris Rooftop Bar and The Vault, a speakeasy inside the building's original bank vault. A fresh downtown lodging option — likely MISSING. (waymarkhotel.com; irischattanooga.com)

3. **Tivoli Theatre reopening** — The "Jewel of the South" reopens mid-2026 after renovation, adding a 190-seat cinema and studio, expanded lobby, a restaurant, six bar spaces, and additional event areas plus a modernized marquee. Notable new nightlife/arts option — likely MISSING. (tn.gov/tourism)

4. **Walnut Street Bridge — CLOSED for restoration** — The iconic pedestrian bridge is closed for restoration until late September 2026. Critical caveat for any weekend itinerary; the guide should flag this so hosts don't send visitors to a closed bridge. Likely needs an UPDATE. (findingtheuniverse.com)

5. **The Hotel Chalet at the Choo Choo** — Refurbished boutique hotel at the Choo Choo complex; guests can also stay in refurbished Pullman train cars. Core to the "Choo Choo Edition" theme — likely already covered but verify current status/details. (choochoo.com)

6. **Terminal Station / Choo Choo complex** — Free to explore even for non-guests; historic train station with restaurants, boutiques, comedy club, Gate 11 micro-distillery, rose gardens, and live music along Station Street. Includes a restaurant co-owned by actor Norman Reedus. Central to the guide theme. (choochoo.com/explore)

7. **The Read House Hotel** — Historic downtown hotel with a restored lobby, fine dining, and a swanky cocktail bar (Bar & Billiards). A classic "host your visitors here" pick in the heart of downtown. Likely a good addition if not present. (tripadvisor boutique-hotels)

8. **The Dwell Hotel** — 16-room mid-century-modern boutique hotel in a brick-and-limestone building downtown, walkable to the Aquarium and Walnut Street Bridge. Distinctive design-forward stay. (thedwellhotel.com)

9. **The Edwin Hotel, Autograph Collection** — Riverfront boutique hotel between the Aquarium and the Hunter Museum, with the Whiteside rooftop bar overlooking the Tennessee River. Strong "restaurant/hotel with a view" option for hosting. (marriott)

10. **The Kinley Chattanooga Southside** — Boutique hotel in the vibrant Southside district near the Choo Choo, with restaurant, fitness center, and concierge. Ties directly to the Southside/Choo Choo neighborhood. (booking.com/southside)

11. **The Chattanoogan Hotel, Curio Collection by Hilton** — Upscale Southside hotel with spa, Noogan's Coffee Market, the Forge restaurant, and a rooftop bar. Good larger-group/convention hosting base. (curio)

12. **Tennessee Aquarium** — 13,000 animals, 700+ species across the River Journey and Ocean Journey buildings, plus an IMAX theater; touch stingrays and lake sturgeon. Anchor downtown attraction three blocks from the Creative Discovery Museum. (tnaqua.org)

13. **Creative Discovery Museum** — Children's museum (UnEarthed fossil dig, STEM Zone rockets, ArtSpace, TreeHouse Adventure). Hours: Sun–Fri 10a–5p, Sat 9a–5p. Best family/kids stop for hosting visitors with children — "Park and Play All Day" pairs with the Aquarium. (cdmfun.org)

14. **Ruby Falls** — Tallest underground waterfall open to the public in the US; descend 260 ft by glass-front elevator, guided cavern walk, ~2–2.5 hours. On Lookout Mountain, ~15 min from downtown. Classic day-trip for a weekend visit. (lookoutmountain.com)

15. **Rock City Gardens** — Lookout Mountain trail through massive rock formations, 400+ native plant species, and the "See 7 States" Lover's Leap overlook. Iconic photo stop. (lookoutmountain.com)

16. **Lookout Mountain Incline Railway** — Open since 1895; billed as the world's steepest passenger railway at a 72.7% grade, ~1 hour visit. Ties the "train" theme to a real Chattanooga rail experience — natural fit for the Choo Choo edition. (lookoutmountain.com)

17. **Coolidge Park & the historic carousel** — Northshore waterfront park with a restored 100-year-old carousel, interactive fountain, and open lawns under the Walnut Street Bridge. Free, family-friendly riverfront stop. (usadventurer.com)

18. **Tennessee Riverwalk** — 13-mile riverside greenway/promenade with city and river views, connecting downtown, the Southside/South Broad (now running through Erlanger Park), and beyond. Great for walking/biking during a weekend stay. (findingtheuniverse.com)

19. **CARTA Free Electric Shuttle** — Free electric shuttle linking downtown, the Northshore, and the Southside, letting visitors get around car-free. Practical logistics tip that strengthens a hosting itinerary. (tripadvisor)

20. **Bluff View Art District** — Walkable clifftop district above the river with the Hunter Museum of American Art, gardens, and cafes, near Coolidge Park and the Riverwalk. Rounds out a cultural half-day for visitors. (advcollective travel guide)
