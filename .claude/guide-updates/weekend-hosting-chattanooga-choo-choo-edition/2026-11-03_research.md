# Research: weekend itineraries, hotels, things to do, and hosting visitors in Chattanooga, TN

## 2026-11-03

1. **Hotel Chalet at the Chattanooga Choo Choo** — The on-site boutique hotel; guests can book restored vintage train cars or standard rooms. 4.8-star rated, large pool, walkable Southside location. The core "Choo Choo edition" anchor. Likely already covered. thehotelchalet.com
2. **Chattanooga Choo Choo complex (Southside)** — Former 1909 Terminal Station; now a district with dining, shopping, Comedy Catch club, Glenn Miller Gardens, and an ~11,000 sq ft dog park with pool and wash stations. Central hub for a hosting weekend. Likely covered.
3. **The Edwin Hotel** — Luxury boutique on the Tennessee River between the Hunter Museum and Bluff View Art District. Rooftop bar (Whiskey Thief) and spa. Great premium recommendation for visitors. Possibly missing.
4. **The Dwell Hotel** — 16-room adults-only 1909 boutique; each room unique with bold design. Includes Matilda Midnight tiki cocktail lounge off the lobby, breakfast, valet. A distinctive stay option. Possibly missing.
5. **The Waymark Hotel** — NEW: revitalized landmark inside a historic 1927 bank building downtown, with a subterranean speakeasy called The Vault. Fresh 2026 opening worth adding. Likely missing.
6. **Kinley Chattanooga Southside (Tribute Portfolio)** — Boutique hotel with art gallery featuring local female artists, restaurant, cocktail lounge, cafe, fitness center. Walkable to Southside. Possibly missing.
7. **The Chattanoogan, Curio Collection by Hilton** — Highly rated full-service hotel, friendly staff, strong downtown location near the Southside. Solid mid-premium pick. Possibly covered.
8. **Embassy Suites Chattanooga Downtown** — Newer build steps from the Tennessee Aquarium; indoor pool, hot tub, free breakfast, rooftop dining, evening reception. Good family hosting base. Possibly missing.
9. **Tennessee Aquarium** — World-class two-building aquarium (freshwater + ocean), IMAX, butterfly garden, river otters to penguins. Plan ~2 hours. Riverboat cruises depart from the plaza. Day-1 essential. Likely covered.
10. **Tennessee Riverwalk** — 16+ mile paved path along the river; the Coolidge Park to Ross's Landing stretch is ~30 min and scenic. Easy morning activity for guests. Likely covered.
11. **Walnut Street Bridge & Coolidge Park (North Shore)** — One of the world's longest pedestrian bridges connecting downtown to North Shore; Coolidge Park has an 1894 Dentzel carousel and interactive fountain. Classic walkable combo. Likely covered.
12. **Lookout Mountain trio — Incline Railway, Rock City, Ruby Falls** — Incline is the steepest passenger railway (72.7% grade, since 1895); Point Park "see seven states"; Ruby Falls 145-ft underground waterfall; Rock City gardens and Enchanted Trail. Day-2 staple. Likely covered.
13. **Bluff View Art District** — Walkable riverside arts enclave with Hunter Museum, sculpture garden, and Rembrandt's Coffee House with river views. Good for a relaxed morning. Possibly missing.
14. **Frazier Avenue / North Shore** — Main drag of boutiques, vintage shops, galleries, and brunch spots. Pairs with Coolidge Park and the Walnut Street Bridge. Possibly covered.
15. **Chattanooga Whiskey Experimental Distillery** — Across from the Choo Choo; tours and tastings, named a must-visit distillery. Natural walkable add for a Choo Choo-anchored weekend. Possibly missing.
16. **The Trinity (Southside)** — NEW Aug 2026: New Orleans-inspired restaurant at 1278 Market St in the historic former St. John's building. Close to the Choo Choo. Likely missing.
17. **Bona Fide (Southside)** — NEW July 2026: chef-driven seasonal neighborhood restaurant at 55 Johnson St with housemade pastas and small bites. Walkable from Choo Choo. Likely missing.
18. **Alleia (Southside)** — Local-favorite Italian with housemade pasta, wood-fired dishes, and a strong wine list. Reservations smart on weekends. Great dinner pick near the Choo Choo. Possibly missing.
19. **The Reading Room** — NEW 2026: Chattanooga's first combined bookstore-and-bar with floor-to-ceiling shelves, craft cocktails, and quiet reading nooks. Novel evening stop. Likely missing.
20. **Erlanger Park (new ballpark)** — Opening April 2026: new baseball stadium downtown with field boxes, cabanas, and a berm for family picnics; also hosts concerts. Fresh weekend-entertainment option. Likely missing.
