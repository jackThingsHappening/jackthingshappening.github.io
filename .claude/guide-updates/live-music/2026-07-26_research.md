# Research: live music venues, bars, festivals, and events in Chattanooga, TN

## 2026-07-26

1. **The Signal** — Chattanooga's premier mid-size concert hall (~1,000 capacity) on Chestnut Street, adjacent to the Chattanooga Choo Choo. Books national touring acts across genres before they graduate to arenas; good sightlines/sound throughout. Site: thesignaltn.com. Likely already covered.

2. **JJ's Bohemia** — Legendary Southside/downtown dive bar with a tiny room and barely-elevated stage; the venue local musicians name when they talk about Chattanooga. Close-quarters shows, wide range of local/touring acts. Likely already covered.

3. **Songbirds** — Music hall inside the historic Chattanooga Choo Choo; intimate ~250-person setting tied to the guitar museum, hosting local and international artists. Site: songbirdsfoundation.org. Likely covered.

4. **The Woodshop Listening Room** — St. Elmo neighborhood bar/dinner-show room in a converted woodworking shop, run by Jhett Black & Callie Sioux. Themed nights: Tuesday Bluesday (blues/roots), Wednesday jazz + trivia, Thursday soul & BBQ, Forever Bluegrass Fridays (Pinion family), monthly St. Elmo Songwriting Series. Low/no cover Tue–Fri; ticketed Saturdays. Site: thewoodshoplisteningroom.com. Potentially missing — strong add.

5. **Barking Legs Theater** — Intimate concert hall (76 fixed seats, up to ~150) known for jazz, folk, and experimental programming. "Wednesday Jazz" featuring local musicians. Address: 1307 Dodds Ave. Site: barkinglegs.org. Possibly missing.

6. **Nightfall** — Chattanooga's original free outdoor concert series, Friday nights at Miller Plaza/Miller Park downtown, roughly May–July (May 1–July 31, 2026). Music starts ~7 p.m.; diverse lineups. Site: nightfallchattanooga.com. Should be covered — seasonal free series.

7. **TVFCU Riverfront Nights** — Free Saturday-night summer concert series at Ross's Landing on the riverfront, Memorial Day to Labor Day. Site: riverfrontnights.com. Should be covered.

8. **Cherry Street Tavern** — Downtown bar (open since 2021) with live music most nights: open mics, jazz nights, a monthly artist-in-residence series, and booked local artists. Potentially missing.

9. **HiFi Clyde's** — Retro-style honkytonk bar on Main Street in the Southside; energetic live shows every weekend. Usually no cover on weeknights; weekend cover ~$5–10. Potentially missing.

10. **Sluggo's North Vegetarian Cafe** — Laid-back vegan cafe/bar on Cherokee Blvd (Northshore) that hosts shows in its downstairs concert space; punk/underground leaning. Potentially missing.

11. **Wanderlinger Brewing** — Southside taproom that programs live music seriously — jazz, folk, bluegrass, and singer-songwriter acts in an intimate setting. Possibly missing; note dual brewery/music role.

12. **Soldiers & Sailors Memorial Auditorium** — Downtown 1924 hall seating ~3,800; has taken over much of the Tivoli's larger live music events during renovation, hosting acts like Joe Bonamassa and Tedeschi Trucks Band. Part of Tivoli Foundation. Site: tivolichattanooga.com/memorial_auditorium. Possibly missing.

13. **Tivoli Theatre** — Historic 1921 "Jewel of the South" on Broad Street (~1,750 seats), under renovation with a planned reopening/expansion in 2026. Broadway, concerts, comedy. Site: tivolichattanooga.com. Update status note if covered.

14. **Walker Theatre** — Smaller Tivoli Foundation venue (~851 seats) in the Memorial Auditorium building; mid-size concerts and performances. Possibly missing.

15. **Barrelhouse Ballroom** — Live music venue/ballroom cited among the city's notable rooms where the local scene is growing. Verify address/programming. Potentially missing.

16. **Tremont Tavern** — Northshore neighborhood tavern frequently listed among Chattanooga bars with regular live music. Verify current schedule. Potentially missing.

17. **Sunset Amphitheater at The Bend** — Planned 6,000–12,500-capacity outdoor amphitheater on the former industrial Westside "Bend" site, developed by Colorado-based Venu; construction potentially starting Q1 2027 to bring major festivals/concerts back. Forward-looking note, not yet open. Missing (future).

18. **IBMA World of Bluegrass** — Major two-day bluegrass festival hosting top global artists, with convention, tradeshow, awards show, and showcases across downtown Chattanooga. Site: worldofbluegrass.org. Possibly missing — big annual draw.

19. **Forever Bluegrass Fridays** — Weekly Chattanooga bluegrass tradition curated by Cindy Pinion & family (legacy of Boxcar Pinion's festival) every Friday at the Woodshop in St. Elmo. Recurring event worth a callout. Possibly missing.

20. **Jfest** — Large one-day Christian rock & worship festival at the Tennessee Riverpark (2026 date May 16). Free/family-oriented. Site: jfest.com. Possibly missing — niche but big annual event.
