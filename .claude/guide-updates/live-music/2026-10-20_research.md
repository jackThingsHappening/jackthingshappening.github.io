# Research: live music venues, bars, festivals, and events in Chattanooga, TN

## 2026-10-20

1. **The Signal** — Premier mid-size concert hall inside the Chattanooga Choo Choo complex. 21 Choo Choo Avenue, Chattanooga, TN 37402. Concert Hall holds 1,500 (1,300 standing floor + 200 seated mezzanine). The Parlour holds up to 500 standing or 280 seated. Books national touring acts across genres. Likely already covered.
2. **Barrelhouse Ballroom** — Mid-size venue that fills the gap between club shows and concert halls. 1501 Long Street, Chattanooga, TN 37408. Opened September 2022. Capacity ~500 on the main stage, thirty-foot stage. Books rock, country, indie, hip-hop. barrelhouseballroom.com. Likely covered or a strong add.
3. **Songbirds** — Guitar-focused venue now at 206 W Main Street (moved from the old 35 Station St location). Hosts a wide genre range — folk, rock, roots, tribute acts. venue.songbirds.org. Likely covered; verify current address.
4. **JJ's Bohemia** — Legendary tiny dive bar at 231 E Martin Luther King Blvd. Open as a music room since 2006. Barely elevated stage, books punk, indie, experimental. Thursday nights are comedy open mic. Likely covered.
5. **The Woodshop Listening Room** — Neighborhood listening-room venue and bar in St. Elmo. 5500 Saint Elmo Avenue, Chattanooga, TN 37409. Phone 423-803-6165. Intimate, songwriter-focused. thewoodshoplisteningroom.com. Possibly missing — strong add.
6. **Wanderlinger Brewing** — Southside taproom that programs live music seriously — jazz, folk, bluegrass, singer-songwriter in an intimate setting. Announces shows on Instagram first. Possibly missing.
7. **Nightfall Concert Series** — Free outdoor Friday series at Miller Park and Miller Plaza, May 1 through July 31, 2026 (14 weeks). Gates 6 p.m., local opener 7 p.m., headliner 8 p.m. Moves to Miller Park stage once a month. Art market, food trucks, kids' activities. NightfallChattanooga.com. Strong add if missing.
8. **IBMA Bluegrass Live!** — Major bluegrass festival downtown, October 23–24, 2026. 2026 lineup includes Sierra Ferrell, Leftover Salmon, Larry Sparks, Michael Cleveland & Friends, Lonesome River Band, Della Mae. Presented by Chattanooga Tourism Co. The 37th IBMA Bluegrass Music Awards are at Memorial Auditorium October 22. Strong festival add.
9. **3 Sisters Bluegrass Festival** — Free bluegrass festival, October 2–3, 2026. 2026 lineup: Sam Bush, Rhonda Vincent, Dan Tyminski Band, The Travelin' McCourys, Appalachian Road Show, East Nash Grass, Big Richard. Long-running Chattanooga tradition. Strong add.
10. **Chattanooga Jazz Fest** — Second annual, free, held at the Chattanooga Choo Choo. 10+ local performers including Stephen Powers Sextet, Jim Crumble Quartet, Dexter Bell and Friends, Monday Night Big Band, UTC Jazz Band. Good add for genre breadth.
11. **Tivoli Theatre** — Historic "Jewel of the South" downtown. Reopens mid-2026 after renovation adding a 190-seat cinema/studio, expanded lobby, restaurant, six bar spaces, modernized marquee. Hosts touring concerts (e.g. Billy Strings Oct 20–21, 2026). tivolichattanooga.com. Note the reopening.
12. **Soldiers & Sailors Memorial Auditorium** — Large downtown hall for bigger touring shows and events (NEEDTOBREATHE, IBMA Awards). Part of the Tivoli Theatre Foundation. Worth covering as the big-room option.
13. **Walker Theatre** — Intimate theatre next to Memorial Auditorium, part of the Tivoli Foundation. Books comedy and music — Keb' Mo', Charlie Berens, Matt McCusker on the fall 2026 calendar. Possibly missing.
14. **Sunset Amphitheater (coming)** — Announced $300M open-air amphitheater at The Bend by Urban Story Ventures. Planned 12,500 capacity — would be Chattanooga's first large-scale open-air venue. Still in development. Note as a forward-looking "what's coming."
15. **Erlanger Park** — New downtown baseball stadium opening spring 2026 in the Riverfront District. Will also host concerts and special events. Emerging live-music option.
16. **Parkway Pourhouse** — Downtown bar booking live music several nights a week, focused on local and regional acts — jazz, Americana, rock. Possibly missing.
17. **Barking Legs Theater** — Long-running intimate performance space for eclectic music, dance, and experimental acts. A quieter local institution. Possibly missing.
18. **HiFi Clyde's** — Bar with regular live music programming downtown. Casual nightlife-plus-music spot. Possibly missing.
19. **The FEED Co. Table & Tavern** — Southside restaurant/tavern that regularly books live local music. Food-plus-music crossover venue. Possibly missing.
20. **Charles & Myrtle's Coffeehouse** — Alcohol-free, all-ages listening room for acoustic and folk acts. Fills the family-friendly / sober live-music niche. Possibly missing.
