# Research: Cloudland Canyon State Park hiking trails, camping, waterfalls, and activities near Chattanooga, TN

## 2026-08-10

1. **West Rim Loop Trail** — The park's signature hike. A 5-mile moderate loop with canyon-edge overlooks. Named a Top 10 hike in America by Backpacker magazine. Takes 2.5–3.5 hours. Likely already covered.
2. **Waterfalls Trail** — Strenuous 2-mile round-trip with steep metal stairs down to Cherokee Falls and Hemlock Falls. About ½ mile to Cherokee, a steeper ½ mile further to Hemlock. Likely already covered.
3. **Cherokee Falls & Hemlock Falls** — The park's two main waterfalls. They spill over curved rock outcrops into the boulder-filled canyon. The core scenic draw. Likely already covered.
4. **Overlook Trail** — Short, mostly flat 1-mile trail to the park's most iconic canyon view. Good for families and quick visits. Takes about 30 minutes. Likely already covered.
5. **Sitton's Gulch Trail** — 6-mile round-trip strenuous trail from the valley floor. Follows a tall wooden bridge and creek upstream past cascades and hemlocks. Starts from a gravel lot at ~800 ft elevation. Possibly missing.
6. **Sitton's Cave wild cave tours** — Naturalist-led tours of a wild cave (no lights or handrails). Offered April, June, July, September, October. 2–2.5 hours, $45/person, ages 10+, groups of 2–8. Register at 706-657-4050. Likely a strong addition.
7. **Five Points Recreation Area** — Former coal-mining area now with 23 miles of trails for hiking, mountain biking, and horseback riding. Separate trailhead (Ascalon). $5 parking drop box, exact change. Possibly missing.
8. **Cloudland Connector Trail** — Multi-use trail linking park areas. Part of 30+ miles of mountain-biking singletrack, e-bikes, and multi-use trails. Possibly missing.
9. **Mountain bike rentals** — Available from the Visitor Center. $35 half-day, $55 full-day. Practical detail for bikers. Possibly missing.
10. **Disc golf course** — 18-basket course weaving through fields and forest. $4 per person per round. Possibly missing.
11. **Yurt village** — 10 unique yurts with open floor plans, heat, and bunk beds. Shared playground and bathrooms near the West Rim Trail. Rustic lodging option. Possibly missing.
12. **Cottages / cabins** — 18 cottages with kitchens, fireplaces, living areas, and screened porches. Full-comfort lodging. Likely already covered.
13. **Campgrounds** — 75 tent/trailer/RV sites with water and electric, 30 walk-in tent sites, 13 backcountry sites, 4 pioneer sites with Adirondack shelters. 119 total. Likely already covered.
14. **Backcountry camping** — 13 backcountry sites for hike-in overnight trips. Appeals to more serious campers. Possibly missing.
15. **Horseback riding trails** — 16 miles of equestrian trails, largely in the Five Points area. Possibly missing.
16. **Geocaching** — More than 10 geocaches hidden across the park for treasure-hunting visitors. A fun family add-on. Possibly missing.
17. **Interpretive Center** — Open Saturday–Sunday 10 a.m.–5 p.m. (weekdays seasonally). Good rainy-day and orientation stop. Possibly missing.
18. **Accessibility features** — All-Terrain Georgia Action Trackchair for mobility access on rugged terrain, plus EnChroma color-blind viewing glasses. Notable inclusive amenities. Likely missing.
19. **Park logistics** — 3,538 acres on Lookout Mountain's western edge. Address: 122 Cloudland Canyon Park Road, Rising Fawn, GA 30738. Park hours 7 a.m.–10 p.m. $5 daily parking (ParkPass). Office 706-657-4050. Reservations 800-864-7275. Core reference facts.
20. **Case Cave** — A technical wild cave requiring harnesses and rope, open only to experienced cavers. Contrasts with the guided Sitton's Cave tours. Niche mention. Possibly missing.
