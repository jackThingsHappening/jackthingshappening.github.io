# Research: theaters, performing arts venues, dance, opera, comedy, and live performances in Chattanooga, TN

## 2026-11-10

1. **Tivoli Theatre (reopened)** — BIG update. Reopened October 20, 2026 after a four-year, $75 million restoration. Billy Strings played two sold-out opening shows plus a free Broad Street block party. "Jewel of the South," Beaux Arts 1920s movie palace, gold leaf and ornamental plaster restored to 1921 look, modern seats/lighting. Expanded into neighboring building — five theater spaces, movie theater, concessions, lounges. 709 Broad St. Likely already covered but status/facts need major update (was closed for renovation).

2. **Chattanooga Theatre Centre** — Founded 1923, community theatre, year-round live productions. 102nd/103rd seasons. 2026–2027 season themed around the nation's 250th: Come from Away (Sept 11–27, 2026), See Rock City (Nov 6–22, 2026, Circle Theatre), Harry Connick Jr.'s The Happy Elf (Dec 4–20, 2026), Frozen (summer). 400 River St. theatrecentre.com. Likely covered.

3. **Chattanooga Symphony & Opera (CSO)** — Nation's first combined professional resident symphony and opera company (merged 1985). Season Sept–May. 2026-27 highlights: Star Wars: Empire Strikes Back in Concert (Nov 7–8), Rachmaninoff Piano Concerto No. 2 (Nov 19), Handel's Messiah (Dec 5–6), Home for the Holidays (Dec 19–20). Returning to the renovated Tivoli this season. chattanoogasymphony.org. Likely covered.

4. **Chattanooga Ballet** — Founded 1973, professional dancers + advanced students, classical and contemporary. CEO/artistic director Brian McSween. Opened season with "ART / Bridges" (Oct 8–10 at UTC Fine Arts Theater) honoring Walnut Street Bridge reopening. The Nutcracker Dec 8–10 at Memorial Auditorium with live CSO. 2026-27 closes with an original work on Jackie Mitchell at the renovated Tivoli in April. chattballet.org. Possibly covered.

5. **Soldiers & Sailors Memorial Auditorium** — Major venue, up to 3,500 seats. Hosts Broadway tours and ballet. Spring/summer 2026 Broadway: The Wiz (Apr 28–May 3), Mrs. Doubtfire (Jun 12–14). 399 McCallie Ave. Likely covered.

6. **Robert Kirk Walker Theatre** — Intimate theater within the Memorial Auditorium complex, smaller-scale performances. Likely covered or worth adding.

7. **The Comedy Catch** — Chattanooga's established stand-up comedy club, touring national comedians, hosts Improv Comedy Night. thecomedycatch.com. Likely covered.

8. **Improv Chattanooga** — Local improv comedy organization. Free monthly show the last Friday of each month at the Downtown Library. Also does Drunk Storytelling (improvisers recreate history as told by inebriated storytellers). Performs at venues across the city. improvchattanooga.com. Likely MISSING — good add.

9. **Barking Legs Theater** — Intimate venue for theatre, dance, live music; local, regional, national talent, contemporary and traditional. 1307 Dodds Ave. barkinglegs.org. Likely covered.

10. **Ensemble Theatre of Chattanooga (ETC)** — Chattanooga's only regularly producing theatre that financially compensates its artists. Produced RENT (Apr 24–26, 2026). ensembletheatreofchattanooga.com. Possibly MISSING — good add.

11. **UTC Department of Theatre / UTC Theatre Co.** — University productions at the Fine Arts Center, 752 Vine St, Room 330. 2026-27 productions: The Revolutionists (Oct 6–10), A Midsummer Night's Dream (Nov 17–21), Big Fish (Apr 20–24). Affordable, public. Possibly MISSING — good add.

12. **Signal Mountain Playhouse** — Community theater founded 1972 at Mountain Arts Community Center, 809 Kentucky Ave, Signal Mountain. One winter adult play + an outdoor summer musical (all four July weekends) with youth and adults. smph.org. Likely MISSING — good regional add.

13. **Mountain Arts Community Center (MACC)** — 809 Kentucky Ave, Signal Mountain. Studios, gallery, theater, dance and ballet rehearsal space, event hall, pottery studio. Hosts Signal Mountain Playhouse. Worth a mention as a venue.

14. **The Barrelhouse Ballroom** — Downtown live-performance / music venue listed among Chattanooga performance venues. Worth confirming and possibly adding.

15. **The Signal** — Mid-size live music and performance venue in the Southside. Listed among Chattanooga performance venues. Possibly covered under live-music but relevant.

16. **MuzArDanZ (Music + Art + Dance)** — Local organization blending music, visual art and dance performance. muzardanz.org. Niche but a genuine local dance/performance group — potential add.

17. **The Chattery** — Community learning space that hosts Improv Comedy Shows (e.g., Mar 7 and Aug 22, 2026, 7–9 PM). thechattery.org. Niche venue for comedy nights.

18. **Lookout Comedy Festival** — Annual multi-night Chattanooga comedy festival; 2026 edition included an "Improv vs. Stand-Up" show. Festival-level comedy event worth noting for a comprehensive guide.

19. **Walker Theatre / Tivoli / Memorial Auditorium combined management** — All three historic downtown houses are operated together (tivolichattanooga.com handles ticketing). Useful context for readers navigating which venue hosts what.

20. **CHA Ballet "The Nutcracker" with CSO** — Annual holiday staple pairing Chattanooga Ballet with the live symphony, ticketed through the Tivoli/Memorial system. A signature recurring performing-arts event worth highlighting seasonally.
