# Research: theaters, performing arts venues, dance, opera, comedy, and live performances in Chattanooga, TN

## 2026-07-29

1. **Tivoli Theatre** — The 1921 "Jewel of the South" (100 E 11th St, ~1,750 seats) is reopening Oct 20–21, 2026 after closing in 2022 for a ~$75M restoration. Billy Strings is booked for the first shows back. Major news hook — any guide should reflect it's LIVE again after years dark. Likely covered but needs status update.

2. **Tivoli Performing Arts Center / Bobby Stone Theatre** — New addition in the adjacent Fowler Brothers Co. building, connected to the Tivoli on every floor. Features the Bobby Stone Theatre, a patrons lounge, year-round restaurant, rooftop bar, and flexible event spaces. Projected to open by end of 2026 with programming in early 2027. Likely MISSING — brand new.

3. **Soldiers & Sailors Memorial Auditorium** — 399 McCallie Ave, capacity ~3,800. Chattanooga's big touring-act and concert hall; served as CSO's home during the Tivoli renovation. Run by the Tivoli Theatre Foundation. Likely covered.

4. **The Walker Theatre** — 399 McCallie Ave, 37402 (part of the Memorial Auditorium complex). Smaller, more intimate space for performances and events; sells through Ticketmaster. Possibly missing or under-detailed.

5. **Chattanooga Theatre Centre** — Founded 1923, one of the oldest community theatres in the country; year-round live stage productions. theatrecentre.com. Core institution — likely covered.

6. **Barking Legs Theater** — Intimate performing-arts venue known for contemporary dance, experimental jazz, poetry slams, singer-songwriters, film nights, and local storytelling. barkinglegs.org. A local favorite — should be covered.

7. **Ensemble Theatre of Chattanooga** — Chattanooga's only regularly producing professional theatre committed to paying its artists; new productions every 6–8 weeks, with a short-play festival (Jan–Feb) and summer musical concerts. ensembletheatreofchattanooga.com. Possibly missing.

8. **Chattanooga Symphony & Opera (CSO)** — Region's flagship orchestra + opera. 2025–26 season performed at Memorial Auditorium during the Tivoli reno; hopes to return to the Tivoli for 2026–27. Box office (423) 267-8583, chattanoogasymphony.org. Should be covered — update venue note.

9. **The Comedy Catch** — 29 Station St, Southside, 37408. Chattanooga's dedicated comedy club with shows most nights (stand-up + improv); doors ~1 hr before. thecomedycatch.com. Should be covered.

10. **Improv Chattanooga** — Local improv-comedy troupe/scene (improvchattanooga.com). Adds a homegrown comedy angle beyond touring headliners. Possibly missing.

11. **Lookout Comedy Festival** — Annual fest scheduled Oct 14–17, 2026 with shows at The Comedy Catch, JJ's Bohemia, Cherry St Tavern, and other Southside venues. lookoutcomedyfestival.com. Likely MISSING — good recurring event.

12. **Chattanooga Ballet** — Celebrated its 50th anniversary (season "Art / Legacy," 2024); performs in the Roland Hayes Concert Hall at UTC. chaballet.org. Anchor for the dance section. Possibly missing.

13. **Chattanooga Dance Theatre** — Studio + company that stages the annual "Nooga Nutcracker" (60+ performers, full set) at the Roland Hayes Theatre, UTC Fine Arts Center. chattanoogadancetheatre.com. Possibly missing.

14. **UTC Fine Arts Center / Roland W. Hayes Concert Hall** — Corner of Vine & Palmetto on the UTC campus; recently renovated 505-seat hall named for tenor Roland Hayes. Hosts ballet, dance, and community events (factickets.evenue.net). Possibly missing — key dance/classical venue.

15. **Bessie Smith Cultural Center** — 200 E MLK Blvd. History/cultural institution (Chattanooga African American Museum + Bessie Smith Performance Hall, honoring the "Empress of the Blues"). Hosts live events, galleries, and performances. bessiesmithcc.org. Possibly missing.

16. **Signal Mountain Playhouse** — Est. 1972, an outdoor "theater under the stars" amphitheater (corner of Rolling Way & James Blvd). Summer production runs weekends in July; winter show at the Mountain Arts Community Center. smph.org. Likely MISSING — distinctive seasonal venue.

17. **Songbirds Venue** — Intimate ~250-person concert venue tied to the Songbirds Foundation (music education nonprofit) with an attached guitar museum viewable during shows/rentals. venue.songbirds.org. Possibly missing.

18. **The Signal** — Larger modern live-music venue bringing high-energy touring acts to Chattanooga; frequently listed among the city's best concert halls. Possibly missing.

19. **CSO "Star Wars: A New Hope In Concert"** — Chattanooga Symphony & Opera performs the full film score live to picture starting Jan 31, 2026. Example of the CSO's film-in-concert programming — good concrete "what to see" item. Event-level detail.

20. **CSO 2026–27 season opener — West Side Story & American Groove** — Opens at Memorial Auditorium (Sept 24, 2026, 7:30 PM) with Ellington's "Black, Brown & Beige" and Symphonic Dances from West Side Story; season finale was Verdi's Requiem (200+ musicians, free 6:45 Spotlight Talk). Shows the CSO's range for the guide. Event-level detail.
