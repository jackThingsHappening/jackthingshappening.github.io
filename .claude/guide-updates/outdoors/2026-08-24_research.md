# Research: outdoor activities, hiking, climbing, kayaking, parks, trails, and nature in Chattanooga, TN

## 2026-08-24

1. **Sunset Rock (Lookout Mountain)** — Iconic overlook with panoramic Tennessee River views, famed for sunsets. Moderate trail on Lookout Mountain, part of the National Park network. Also a top summer climbing crag (shady most of the day). Likely already covered.
2. **Stringer's Ridge** — 92-acre urban wilderness in North Shore with about 7 miles of multi-use singletrack and downtown skyline views. Beginner-friendly, close to downtown. Likely covered.
3. **Snooper's Rock (Prentice Cooper State Forest)** — Highest-rated local trail (4.7 stars, 2,100+ reviews). Roughly 0.5 mi to an overlook of the Tennessee River Gorge horseshoe. Free. Potentially missing.
4. **Foster Falls (South Cumberland State Park)** — 60-foot waterfall with a turquoise plunge pool, ~40 min from downtown. Swimming, sport climbing (Mammy 5.9, Narcissism 5.10b), and hiking. Potentially missing.
5. **Lula Lake Land Trust / Lula Falls** — Turquoise lake, sandstone formations, and a 100-foot lower waterfall on Lookout Mountain. Limited open days — check the land trust calendar. Potentially missing.
6. **Fiery Gizzard Trail (South Cumberland)** — Epic point-to-point route ~45 min out, shaded and challenging, popular with backpackers and day hikers. Potentially missing.
7. **Tennessee Riverwalk / Coolidge Park** — Paved multi-use greenway along the Tennessee River; Coolidge Park in North Shore offers kayak launch, bike paths, and downtown views. Free. Likely covered.
8. **Stone Fort (Little Rock City)** — Prime bouldering destination on a golf course; hundreds of classic sandstone problems from V1 to V12+. Day-use fee. Potentially missing.
9. **Tennessee Wall (T-Wall)** — Two-mile sandstone cliff line along the Tennessee River with 600+ trad routes. World-class traditional climbing. Potentially missing.
10. **Reflection Riding Arboretum & Nature Center** — 300+ acres of gentle family walks, native plants, and wildlife (red wolves). Admission charged. Lookout Mountain foothills. Potentially missing.
11. **Raccoon Mountain** — Premier mountain biking area with beginner-to-advanced technical singletrack; also caves and reservoir. Free trail access. Potentially missing.
12. **Lookout Mountain Flight Park** — Tandem hang gliding and paragliding with bird's-eye views of Lookout Mountain; no experience needed. Paid tandem flights. Potentially missing.
13. **Ocoee River whitewater rafting** — Class III-IV rapids on the 1996 Olympic course, ~1 hr east. Guided trips through multiple outfitters. Seasonal (dam-release). Potentially missing.
14. **L2 Outside** — Downtown water-sports outfitter renting kayaks, tandems, and stand-up paddleboards for the Tennessee River. Central launch point. Potentially missing.
15. **Tennessee River Blueway** — 45 miles of scenic flatwater paddling between Chickamauga and Nickajack dams; half-day to multi-night routes with public launches. Free/self-guided. Potentially missing.
16. **Glen Falls (Lookout Mountain)** — One of the most accessible waterfalls to downtown; ~0.5-mile hike to the falls. Free. Good family/beginner option. Potentially missing.
17. **Greeter Falls (South Cumberland)** — ~2-mile loop with three falls (Upper, Lower, Boardtree); Lower Greeter's plunge pool is a top swimming hole. Potentially missing.
18. **Chickamauga Battlefield** — ~10,000 acres of preserved landscape 30 min south with roughly 50 miles of hiking/biking trails. Free National Military Park. Potentially missing.
19. **North Chickamauga Creek Pocket Wilderness** — Local favorite full-day hike with creek scenery, cascades, and swimming holes near Soddy-Daisy. Free. Potentially missing.
20. **South Chickamauga Creek Greenway** — Paved greenway connecting neighborhoods to nature; note a section near US-75 by Camp Jordan Park is closed for maintenance until July 2027. Free. Potentially missing.

**Sources:** AllTrails, Outside Online, Outdoor Chattanooga, 57hours, Climbing.com, EPB, Choose Chattanooga, Times Free Press, TrailLink, Chattanooga.gov, Lookout Mountain Flight Park, L2 Outside.
