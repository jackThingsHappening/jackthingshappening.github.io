# Research: museums, aquariums, galleries, exhibits, and educational attractions in Chattanooga, TN

## 2026-09-29

1. **Tennessee Aquarium** — Anchor attraction on the Tennessee River downtown. Two buildings: River Journey and Ocean Journey. Home to 13,000+ animals across 700+ species. Includes an IMAX 3D Theater. Likely already covered. Key facts: 1 Broad St, Chattanooga, TN 37402. tnaqua.org.

2. **Scuttlebutt Reef (Tennessee Aquarium, NEW Spring 2026)** — New gallery in the Ocean Journey building. A whimsical collection of exhibits showing nature as playful and unexpected. Likely MISSING — recent addition worth adding as an update note.

3. **Tennessee Aquarium IMAX 3D Theater 30th anniversary** — The IMAX theater celebrates 30 years in 2026. Bean Sprouts Café is new in 2026, serving breakfast/lunch/snacks plus coffee for adults. Fresh facts to fold into the aquarium entry.

4. **Creative Discovery Museum** — Downtown children's museum, hands-on, four floors of interactive exhibits (build, dig, paint, experiment, climb). Voted Best Place to Take Kids four years running. Likely already covered. 321 Chestnut St, Chattanooga, TN 37402. cdmfun.org.

5. **Hunter Museum of American Art** — Home to the Southeast's largest collection of American art. Sits on an 80-foot limestone bluff over the river. Campus blends a 1904 Classical Revival mansion, a 1970s brutalist addition, and a 2005 contemporary wing. Admission $20 adults, $18 seniors 65+, free for kids 17 and under. Likely covered. 10 Bluff View Ave. huntermuseum.org.

6. **Houston Museum of Decorative Arts (Anna Safley Houston Museum)** — One of the largest antique glass and china collections in the Southeast. Across the street from the Hunter Museum in the Bluff View Art District. NOTE: reopening in 2026 at a restored building, 201 High Street — currently closed for restoration. Likely covered but the 2026 reopening + new address is a critical update. thehoustonmuseum.org.

7. **Bluff View Art District** — Concentrated neighborhood of art, food, and gardens on the bluffs above the river. Cobblestone paths, European-style gardens, gallery spaces. Ties Hunter and Houston museums together. Likely covered.

8. **Tennessee Valley Railroad Museum** — Interactive exhibits, vintage train rides through the Missionary Ridge Tunnel; Hiwassee River Rail Adventures between Copperhill and Etowah. Likely covered. 4119 Cromwell Rd, Chattanooga, TN 37421. tvrail.com.

9. **Bessie Smith Cultural Center** — Preserves African American history in Chattanooga and the Tennessee Valley. Named for blues singer Bessie Smith, born in Chattanooga in 1894. Newly renovated with new exhibits, video presentations, and interactive technology; walks visitors from Native Americans and slavery through Civil Rights to today. On MLK Blvd. Likely covered — renovation note is a fresh update. bessiesmithcc.org.

10. **Coolidge National Medal of Honor Heritage Center** — Downtown interactive center on the Medal of Honor from the Civil War to today. Coolidge Theater immersive film about local hero Charles H. Coolidge dodging tank fire in WWII France. Possibly MISSING. mohhc.org.

11. **Battles for Chattanooga Museum** — At the top of Lookout Mountain, ~5 miles from downtown. Civil War history with an immersive digital projection on a 3D diorama of the battles. Possibly missing or underdeveloped.

12. **International Towing & Recovery Hall of Fame & Museum** — Quirky niche museum of restored antique wreckers and tow-truck history (Chattanooga invented the tow truck). Good "lesser-known" entry. Possibly missing. 3315 Broad St. internationaltowingmuseum.org.

13. **Songbirds Guitar Museum** — Guitar and pop-culture museum plus concert venue. As of 2026 located at 1407A Market St (corner of 14th St). Displays guitars from famous musicians; open Wednesday–Sunday; hosts live indie/folk/rock shows. Possibly missing or needs updated address. songbirds.org.

14. **ICA Chattanooga (Institute of Contemporary Art at UTC)** — University contemporary art institute. 2026 exhibitions include "Affrilachia: Testimonies" by Chris Aluka Berry (Oct 15–Dec 4, 2026) and a Juried Student Show (Dec 11, 2026–Jan 22, 2027). Likely MISSING — strong contemporary-art addition. utc.edu/arts-and-sciences/ica.

15. **Association for Visual Arts (AVA)** — Nonprofit gallery and hub for the local visual-arts scene; publishes the Chattanooga Art Guide. Good pointer entry for the galleries section. Possibly missing. avarts.org.

16. **Reflection Riding Arboretum & Nature Center** — 317-acre botanical garden and nature center. Animal experiences with hawks and endangered red wolves; walking trails. Educational/nature attraction. Possibly missing. 400 Garden Rd, Chattanooga, TN 37419. reflectionriding.org.

17. **Ruby Falls** — Guided cavern walk to the tallest underground waterfall open to the public in the U.S.; descend 260 feet via glass-front elevator. Stalactites, stalagmites, flowstone. Educational geology attraction on Lookout Mountain. Likely covered elsewhere but relevant. rubyfalls.com.

18. **Rock City Gardens** — Enchanted Trail with rock formations, caves, gardens, and the "seven states" panorama on Lookout Mountain. Educational/nature. Likely covered elsewhere. seerockcity.com.

19. **Lookout Mountain Incline Railway** — Historic railway operating 125+ years, one of the world's steepest passenger railways; panorama of the city, river, and Appalachians. Historic/educational transportation attraction. ridetheincline.com.

20. **Tennessee Triennial for Contemporary Art (2026)** — Statewide contemporary-art event; the Hunter Museum is part of the consortium leading it, uniting Tennessee's major art museums. A timely 2026 hook for the galleries/exhibits section. brooksmuseum.org / huntermuseum.org.
