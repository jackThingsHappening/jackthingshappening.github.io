# Research: coffee shops, cafes, espresso bars, and specialty coffee in Chattanooga, TN

## 2026-09-22

1. **Pennington's Books** — New bookstore, coffee shop, and art gallery on the Southside. Grand opening April 11, 2026. Founded by Spencer Pennington. Blends specialty coffee, new/used books (literary fiction focus), and local art (prints, originals, pottery); developing a painting class program. Near UTC campus. LIKELY MISSING (opened after most guide builds). Source: timesfreepress.com, penningtonsbooks.com, theutcecho.com.

2. **Hungry Mother** — Cozy café on historic Houston St, downtown/City Center. Opened 2025. Known for adventurous drinks — watermelon mint cold brew, salted maple and tahini lattes. Friendly owner-led menu walk-throughs. Uses Joe Coffee ordering. LIKELY MISSING. Source: joe.coffee, hungry-mother.localoria.com.

3. **New Wave Coffee** — Roaster founded late 2020 by the (Be)Caffeinated team and award-winning roaster Luke Pigott, who has won national roasting medals. Opening a roasting and education center in the White Oak community of Red Bank (announced July 2025). Address noted as 14 W Kent St, North Shore. POSSIBLY MISSING or needs update. Source: timesfreepress.com, newwave-chatt.com.

4. **Milk & Honey** — 135 N Market St, North Shore-adjacent downtown. Open 6am–3pm daily. Small-batch coffee, in-house biscuits and croissants, gelato station, grab-and-go counter; carries some Niedlov's items. Craft-meets-comfort brunch café. POSSIBLY COVERED. Source: milkandhoneychattanooga.com, tripadvisor.com.

5. **Velo Coffee Roasters** — 509 E Main St (main roastery, since 2010) plus North Shore location at 205 Manufacturers Rd. Roasting visible from the café; roasts every weekday; precision-focused (grind, water temp tuned daily). Founders Andrew and Jessica Gage. One of the original specialty pioneers. LIKELY COVERED — verify second location. Source: nelya.net, roastlocal.com.

6. **Mad Priest Coffee Roasters** — 1900 Broad St espresso bar/retail on Southside; roastery on Cherokee Blvd. Founded 2015 by Michael and Cherita Rice as a specialty roaster and social enterprise; partners with Bridge Refugee Services to hire displaced people. SCA certified, Golden Bean and Good Food Award recognized. LIKELY COVERED — verify address/mission detail. Source: madpriestcoffee.com, roastlocal.com.

7. **Mean Mug Coffee** — Original on W Main St (Southside), 1,100+ reviews; flagship on North Shore with full breakfast/lunch, in-house baking, and their own roasting program (launched 2017). St. Elmo location known for house-made Apple Butter Cinnamon Rolls; Fort Oglethorpe location has drive-thru. Chattanooga coffee institution. LIKELY COVERED — verify all locations. Source: nelya.net, exploringchatt.com.

8. **Goodman Coffee** — Warehouse Row (11th St rail-warehouse district) plus Market Street. Founder Ian Goodman previously ran Greyfriar's Coffee & Tea for a decade. Single-origin focus, curated pastry selection, modern design. Also supplies Sunnyside Cup drive-thru. LIKELY COVERED. Source: roastlocal.com, noogafinder.com.

9. **Sleepyhead Coffee** — Started as a coffee cart in 2018, grew to brick-and-mortar. Pink-and-green, plant-filled rooms; vegan food/pastry menu, gluten-free options, inclusive community focus. Distinctive niche. POSSIBLY COVERED. Source: noogafinder.com.

10. **Oaks Coffee House** — Silverdale Rd (East Brainerd), 601 reviews at 4.7 stars. Suburban neighborhood cafe with actual parking; go-to remote-work spot with good wifi and food service. POSSIBLY MISSING (suburban). Source: oakscoffee.com, noogafinder.com.

11. **Rembrandt's Coffee House** — Bluff View Art District. Outdoor terrace overlooking the Tennessee River, cozy indoor seating, standout pastries and chocolates. Scenic destination cafe. LIKELY COVERED. Source: noogafinder.com.

12. **Niedlov's Bakery & Cafe** — 215 E Main St, 37408. Legendary handcrafted breads; takes coffee as seriously as bread. Bakery-cafe hybrid. LIKELY COVERED. Source: niedlovs.com, joe.coffee.

13. **The Camp House** — Downtown. Café charm with a creative, open-doors community vibe; welcomes remote workers and artists; hosts live events/music. LIKELY COVERED — verify. Source: coffeeplume.com.

14. **Stone Cup Roasting Co.** — North Shore cafe pouring exclusively Stone Cup beans; upstairs lounge seating and a patio. Own roasting program. POSSIBLY COVERED. Source: nelya.net.

15. **Frothy Monkey** — Southside location. Nashville-based; favorite study spot with lots of room and free wifi; full food menu, blends vibrant aesthetic with cozy seating. POSSIBLY COVERED. Source: noogafinder.com.

16. **(Be)Caffeinated** — Dayton Blvd, Red Bank, plus North Shore and Rossville locations. Bright, welcoming space with reliable wifi; well-made coffee; parent brand of New Wave roasting team. POSSIBLY MISSING. Source: noogafinder.com, exploringchatt.com.

17. **Sunnyside Cup** — Drive-thru at 3008 Broad St pouring locally roasted Goodman Coffee. Quick-service option for the Broad St / Southside corridor. POSSIBLY MISSING. Source: exploringchatt.com.

18. **Lola Beans** — Drive-thru inspired by Alaskan coffee huts; fun/unique coffee drinks plus energy drinks and teas; friendly staff. Niche drive-thru option. POSSIBLY MISSING. Source: exploringchatt.com.

19. **Chattanooga Coffee Company (Chattz)** — Founded Sept 11, 2002; reopened on Broad St (2021) after Market St. One of the older independent roasters; beloved longtime destination. POSSIBLY MISSING. Source: chattanoogacoffeecompany.com, roastlocal.com.

20. **The Daily Ration** — Tucked-away Northshore brunch café, mostly locals. Quiet Sunday-morning spot for a latte and a filling breakfast. POSSIBLY MISSING (brunch-leaning). Source: nelya.net. Also noted: **Protagonist Coffee Roasters** and **Xozio** — smaller local-market roasters that could warrant a mention if they have public-facing cafes (unverified storefronts). Source: roastlocal.com.
