Backlink Outreach — Expectations and Benchmarks


WHY VENUES LINK BACK

You've given them a free SEO asset. Their venue page on your site can rank
for searches like "Barrelhouse Ballroom events Chattanooga." Linking back
to it reinforces that ranking and drives ticket buyers directly to their
upcoming shows. Most venues want more event discovery. This is free
distribution for them with no downside.


ON HARSH CRITICISM

Expect it routinely. Common responses:
- "Your domain authority is too low" (they're protecting their own DA)
- "Not enough traffic" (they want GSC data you don't have yet)
- "We only link to established publications"
- No response at all — the majority case

This isn't personal and it isn't necessarily accurate. A lot of paid placement
brokers use these as leverage to charge more or filter out sites they don't want
to manage. A local events and guides site at this stage is a legitimate target
for local outreach. The criticism often comes from people who deal in high-volume
generic link sales, not local relevance.


HOW MANY BACKLINKS FOR A LIFT

For a local niche site targeting city-level queries ("things to do in
Chattanooga"), the bar is lower than people think. The competition is other
local sites, most of which also have thin link profiles.

Realistic expectations:
- 3-5 quality local links (Chattanooga news sites, local blogs, Chamber of
  Commerce, tourism boards) can move rankings on low-competition queries
- 10-15 starts to matter for mid-competition queries like "Chattanooga events
  this weekend"
- Quality beats quantity hard at this scale. One link from a Chattanooga news
  outlet is worth more than 20 from generic directories.


HIGHEST-LEVERAGE TARGETS

- Chattanooga Times Free Press
- Local TV station websites
- Chattanooga Area Chamber of Commerce
- Tennessee tourism resources
- Local neighborhood blogs

These are the hardest to get but move the needle the most.

Paid directory listings (not random link farms — actual directories like local
Chamber membership pages) are low-risk and give citation value on top of the link.


HOW MANY EMAILS TO GET 5 BACKLINKS

No reliable number exists. Conversion depends more on targeting and pitch
than volume.

Cold email to random bloggers: 2-5% response rate, maybe 1 in 50 converts
to a link. That puts you at 250+ emails for 5 links.

Warm outreach to local contacts (people who know Chattanooga, have attended
events you cover, follow the Facebook page): 20-30% response rate, much
better conversion. 20-30 emails is a realistic path to 5 links.

The honest shortcut: reach out to local business owners whose venues or
businesses you've already written about. You have 41 venue pages and 13
guides with named businesses. An email that says "we built a page about your
venue / mentioned you in our guide" converts far better than cold blogger
outreach. 50 targeted emails with a realistic shot at 5-10 links.
